# Darnes

**Summary**: Individual on [[csabai|Csabai]] targeted for assassination by the militant Psalmist extremist cell known as [[the-doves|The Doves]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Target of The Doves

Darnes is a person of interest on [[csabai|Csabai]] identified in the encrypted intelligence archives of slain broker [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] (Session 24). 

On Day 89-1105, files recovered by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] from Singh's starship at [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] revealed that a radical Psalmist splinter cell known as [[the-doves|The Doves]] had actively placed Darnes under lethal surveillance and prepared an operational plan to assassinate them, operating in tandem with a scheme to seize the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (Session 24).

---

## Archival Inquiries by BIAS CCR

On Days 90–91-1105, [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] conducted comprehensive intelligence searches for Darnes across the municipal datanets of [[apex|Apex]] and the restricted noble archives of [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]], finding the individual completely scrubbed from public registries (Session 25). Operatives also fed the name to royal attaché [[margaux-evans|Margaux Evans]] as Singh's dying whisper (Session 25).

## Identity as the "Witchfinder General"

On Days 91–92-1105, investigations conducted by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] across [[apex|Apex]] unmasked Darnes as a feared corporate intelligence interrogator originating from [[nesvorny-university|Nesvorny University]] and [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]], widely known as the **"Witchfinder General"** (Session 26).

Employed by [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], Darnes was infamous for the "Darnes Incident," where employees were summarily dismissed following silent interviews with him (Session 26). Operative [[beck|Beck]] deduced that Darnes is an unregistered corporate telepath utilizing [[psionics|Psionics]] to detect internal corporate treason and dissent (Session 26). His role made him a prime target for assassination by [[the-doves|The Doves]], prompting LSP Corporate Security to ambush and detain Beck when she sought an interview with him (Session 26).

## Covert Operative of Erebus Power

On Day 93-1105, electronic penetration of [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] secure files by [[asher|Asher]] revealed a major intelligence breakthrough: Darnes was not an authentic LSP employee, but was merely subcontracted to the megacorp (Session 27). His primary, permanent corporate employer was **[[erebus-power|Erebus Power]]**—the identical corporation that ran the illicit fast-cloning laboratories broken up by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] at [[morella-station|Morella Station]] (Session 27). The files also confirmed Darnes maintained a residence within [[apex|Apex]] and coordinated his activities through two primary handlers in Corporate Security and HR (Session 27).

## Telepathic Inquisitor Against The Doves

As revealed by LSP Corporate Security on Day 93-1105, Darnes was specially deployed by [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]] and LSP as an unregistered telepathic inquisitor to detect and purge embedded [[the-doves|Dove]] infiltrators throughout corporate facilities (Session 28). His role made him a primary assassination target for Dove terror strikes (Session 28).
