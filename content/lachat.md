# Lachat

**Summary**: System 1030 in Solo Sector Subsector J, base of [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]]'s advanced computing operations.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Lachat (1030) carries the UWP profile E200401-A ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## Industrial Activity

The system is a center of operations for [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]], which unveiled humanity's largest computer archive and AI advisory system, [[alexandria-ai|Alexandria AI]], in early 1105 ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## Cultural Customs

Natives of Lachat maintain a distinct cultural tradition of coloring their fingernails with specific pigments, where variations in color communicate personal heritage, social status, or caste affiliations (recognized off-world by fellow natives such as [[sarik|Sarik]]) (Session 17).
