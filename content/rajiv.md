# Rajiv

**Summary**: Hired guard at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch near Hampton]], killed by [[sarik|Sarik]] in [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s ambush on Day 236-1105; his [[nanite-belt|Nanite Belt]] was taken.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-23

---

Rajiv was one of the guards hired to hold [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] on [[tutin|Tutin]]. He patrolled the surrounding terrain with [[rene-desjardins|René Desjardins]], wearing cloth armor beneath his clothes (Session 85).

## Death (Day 236-1105)

The crew ambushed the patrol as it set out, west of the ranch. [[sarik|Sarik]]'s knife attack badly wounded Rajiv without damaging his radio, but left him conscious. Rajiv fled; [[isaiah|Isaiah]] ran him down and grappled him. After [[asher|Asher]] warned that he could regenerate, Sarik killed and decapitated him (Session 85).

René said the guards' regeneration came from belts containing nanites. Rajiv wore one, and the crew recovered his [[nanite-belt|Nanite Belt]] (Session 85).

## His Belt After the Ambush (Session 86)

The crew questioned René about Rajiv's belt, asking whether it detected life signs or could be monitored at the ranch. [[sarik|Sarik]] adjusted it to fit and wore it (Session 86).

René also said he could imitate Rajiv's voice, and that the two usually spent hours on patrol together; the patrol was due back within a couple of hours (Session 86).
