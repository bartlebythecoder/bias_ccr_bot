# Harry Proud

**Summary**: Corrupt commercial shuttle pilot at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] who accepted a Cr 30,000 bribe to smuggle the crew off [[mirassol|Mirassol]], known for his bitter rivalry with [[sky|Sky]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Shuttle Operations & Bribery

Harry Proud was an orbital transport and shuttle pilot operating out of [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]] (Session 8). On Day 35-1105, he was approached by [[sarik|Sarik]] offering Cr 30,000 for covert passage off-world (Session 8). While initially demanding Cr 60,000, Proud was successfully negotiated down to Cr 30,000 by [[isambard|Isambard]] under the promise of future commercial business (Session 8).

## Rivalry with Sky

During negotiations, Proud expressed intense personal and professional contempt for station flyer pilot [[sky|Sky]] (Session 8). He cautioned the crew against associating with her, disparaging her flight capabilities and disclosing her reputation as an erratic or deadly flyer known by the callsign **"Killer"** (Session 8).

Proud piloted the expedited emergency medical shuttle carrying Liam's corpse and the concealed crew on their 30-hour transit to [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 8).

## Theft, Patricide & Motive

Upon landing on [[romentino|Romentino]] on Day 37-1105, Proud betrayed the crew by covertly swapping his identical company flight bag for [[sky|Sky]]'s bag, stealing both the crated [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] and Sky's Cr 20,000 in cash (Session 10). Proud's motive was desperate and paternal: he intended to use the stolen wealth to take his severely addicted son, [[screetch|Screetch]], off-world for rehabilitation (Session 10).

On Day 38-1105, Proud rendezvoused with Screetch at the [[blue-parrot|Blue Parrot]] in GAS town (Session 10). When Proud refused to surrender his cash for Screetch to buy [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]], Screetch flew into a rage and stabbed Proud to death in a nearby alleyway, fleeing with the bag (Session 10).
