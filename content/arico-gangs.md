# Gangs of Arico

**Summary**: Overview of the criminal organizations of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico]] system, from the street gangs of Jonestown on [[freeport|Freeport]] to the syndicates of the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] and [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Organized crime in the Arico system is concentrated in two places: the **Jonestown** entertainment district on the Freeport Torus, and the lawless outer belt of the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]]. Two street gangs fight over Jonestown, while two larger regional organizations operate out in the belt and supply or rival them.

## Freeport Street Gangs (Jonestown)

Both gangs make their money from belters arriving on Freeport for their downtime.

### [[primero-cartel|The Primero Cartel]]
- **Leader**: **"Boss" Kaelen Varr**, a charismatic and ruthless former belter with a sharp head for business. He understands what belters want when they come off a long haul, and keeps a tight network of distributors.
- **Territory**: The upscale, heavily trafficked entertainment zones around the major docking bays and pleasure hubs where belters first arrive, with a strong presence in the **Neon Alley** and **Gravity Gardens** districts.
- **Operations**: Distributes **Sycorax**, supplied directly by the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]], along with high-end stimulants and custom-blended narcotics. It also runs "belter services" (escorts, rigged gambling dens, and "debt consolidation" backed by intimidation) and extorts protection money from smaller businesses in its territory.
- **Relationship with the Meinhoff Brigade**: The cartel is the Brigade's main distribution arm on Freeport. The arrangement is stable but transactional; Varr pays his dues and is careful not to step on the Brigade's toes.

### [[zero-g-vipers|The Zero-G Vipers]]
- **Leader**: **"Serpent" Rix**, younger and more volatile than Varr, known for aggressive tactics and the fierce loyalty of his core crew. He cares less about business than about respect and territory.
- **Territory**: The grittier, older, labyrinthine parts of Jonestown near the industrial zones and secondary docking ports. The Vipers keep trying to muscle into the Primero Cartel's prime locations.
- **Operations**: Cheaper street drugs, including locally synthesized variants, bought from independent smugglers or taken by hijacking cartel shipments, sometimes even Meinhoff shipments meant for the cartel. They also run muggings, bar fights, and small heists, and sell information, especially about Primero Cartel movements, to anyone who pays.
- **Rivalry**: The Vipers resent the cartel's "corporate" style and its reliance on the Meinhoff Brigade, and the two gangs are locked in constant skirmishes, turf wars, and sabotage.

## Regional Criminal Organizations

- **[[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyer Syndicate]]**: A militarized group of miners operating in the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]]. They push the limits of the law, raiding rival asteroid installations and attacking ships that approach their claims. Bank CEO [[orslo-jollynn|Orslo Jollynn]] was revealed to have been secretly leading the syndicate before her death.
- **[[meinhoff-brigade|The Meinhoff Brigade]]**: Based on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] and led by the [[demon-princes|Demon Prince]] [[yandee|Yandee]]. The Brigade is the main supplier of narcotics, including Sycorax, to the Primero Cartel on Freeport.
