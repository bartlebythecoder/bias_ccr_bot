# Reshka

**Summary**: Ethnic-Irklan starport mechanic on Csabai who acted as an underworld commercial go-between for Irklan assassination contracts and sold key intelligence to BIAS CCR.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Underworld Cutout Role

Reshka is an ethnic-[[irklan|Irklan]] starport technician and starship mechanic stationed within [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 27). Leveraging his heritage and ties to the reclusive Irklan mountain enclaves, Reshka runs an illicit side business acting as a commercial broker and intermediary, arranging "invitations" for wealthy off-worlders and local syndicates seeking to contract [[irklan|Irklan]] operatives for martial contracts and wetwork (Session 27).

## The Cr 75,000 Transaction

On Day 92-1105, street fixer [[tina|Tina]] identified Reshka as the central cutout for Irklan contracts on Csabai (Session 27). Operative [[sarik|Sarik]] approached Reshka at Starport Alpha to uncover who had commissioned the assassin who murdered [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] and ambushed [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] (Session 27):
- Reshka initially demanded Cr 100,000 for the client's identity, eventually settling for Cr 75,000 in cash (Session 27).
- Reshka turned over a surveillance photograph of a man in his early 20s captured inside the starport lounge and revealed that the individual was affiliated with the local [[psalm-134|Psalmist]] establishment, directing the operatives toward the [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] in [[basin-city|Basin City]] (Session 27).
