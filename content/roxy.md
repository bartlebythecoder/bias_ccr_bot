# Roxy

**Summary**: [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] street worker who sold BIAS CCR [[katarin|Katarin]]'s address for Cr 50.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

**Roxy** is a resident and street-level worker on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] in the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] (Session 41).

## Informant on Katarin's Address
On Day 119-1105, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] approached Roxy while investigating the poker winning streak of [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]] (Session 41). In exchange for a payment of Cr 50, Roxy provided the operatives with the residential address of Sargon's girlfriend, [[katarin|Katarin]], in Section 2 (Session 41).
