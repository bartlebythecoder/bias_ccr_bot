# Tricia Schelling

**Summary**: Base Manager at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]], recognized for her eccentric doll collection and bureaucratic reliance on incoming corporate investigators.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Administrative Authority

Tricia Schelling serves as the Base Manager and primary corporate executive at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on the frozen moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]] in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system (Sessions 7–8). She exercises executive authority over base logistics, personnel operations, and off-world communications, maintaining an office distinguished by an extensive personal doll collection (Session 8).

## Operational Oversight & Deception

Following the disappearance of Field Support supervisor [[sophia|Sophia]], Schelling met with returning operatives [[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isambard|Isambard]], [[sarik|Sarik]], and [[sky|Sky]] (Session 8). She accepted their false cover story that Sophia could not be found, expressing relief that incoming corporate security auditors would arrive shortly to resolve the incident (Session 8).

Shortly thereafter, Schelling was swayed by [[beck|Beck]]'s manufactured warning that deceased mechanic [[liam|Liam]]'s corpse represented a hazardous contagion risk, authorizing an immediate expedited shuttle launch to [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 8). Once corporate investigators arrived at K.C. Station, a panicked Schelling contacted the shuttle mid-transit to demand confirmation of stowaways (Session 9), which enabled the operatives to slip off the moon with the stolen [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (Session 8).
