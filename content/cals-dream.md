# Cal's Dream

**Summary**: Berthed starship belonging to information broker [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] at Berth 17 of [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]], where his murdered crew was discovered.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Vessel Overview & Berth 17

*Cal's Dream* is a light interstellar starship owned and operated by the late information broker [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] (Session 25). In early 1105, the vessel completed a Jump transit to [[csabai|Csabai]], mooring at Berth 17 within [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] (Sessions 24–25).

## Crime Scene & Intelligence Recovery

- **The Exfiltration & Counter-Ambush**: On Day 89-1105, following Singh's assassination outside [[henrys-tavern|Henry's Tavern]], operatives from [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] entered *Cal's Dream* using Singh's berthing key, allowing [[asher|Asher]] to copy encrypted video logs regarding [[the-doves|The Doves]] before [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] eliminated an [[irklan|Irklan]] assassin outside the airlock (Session 24).
- **Discovery of the Murdered Crew**: On Day 90-1105, police investigator [[lt-tony-mcintyre|Lt. Tony McIntyre]] cordoned off the vessel as an active crime scene after forensic units found the murdered bodies of three of Singh's crew members hidden onboard, systematically executed by the Irklan to suppress Singh's intelligence (Session 25). The site was subsequently visited by royal attaché [[margaux-evans|Margaux Evans]] (Session 25).
