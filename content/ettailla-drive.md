# Ettailla Drive

**Summary**: Extremely expensive [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]] hard drive surgically and permanently attached to the side of the user's head for computer-like processing and memory. Informally called the "LaChat Hard Drive", after [[lachat|Lachat]].

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

## Overview

The Ettailla Drive, informally known as the "LaChat Hard Drive", is a substantial computer hard drive physically mounted on the side of its user's head. Its nickname comes from the [[lachat|Lachat]] system, home of its maker, [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]].

## Installation

Fitting the drive is a permanent surgical procedure; once installed, it cannot simply be removed. The drive is extremely expensive.

## Capabilities

The drive is expected to give its user computer-like processing power and memory.

## Sightings

[[duquette|Duquette]], boss of [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch near Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]], has equipment attached to his head that he uses for communications and calculations, possibly an Ettailla Drive. A former ranch worker took it as a sign that he is an android (Session 84).

[[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]], ruler of [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], also has equipment that could be an Ettailla Drive.
