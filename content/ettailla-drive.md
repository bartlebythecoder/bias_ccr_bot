# Ettailla Drive

**Summary**: Extremely expensive cybernetic hard drive surgically and permanently attached to the side of the user's head, meant to give computer-like processing and memory. Informally called the "LaChat Hard Drive", after [[lachat|Lachat]].

**Last updated**: 2026-10-28

---

## Overview

The Ettailla Drive, informally known as the "LaChat Hard Drive", is a substantial computer hard drive physically mounted on the side of its user's head. Its nickname comes from the [[lachat|Lachat]] system.

## Installation

Fitting the drive is a permanent surgical procedure; once installed, it cannot simply be removed. The drive is extremely expensive.

## Capabilities

The drive is expected to give its user computer-like processing power and memory.
