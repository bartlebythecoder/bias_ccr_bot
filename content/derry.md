# Derry

**Summary**: Commercial sector and sushi bar on Suliman where BIAS CCR met secretly with security officer Hector.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Derry** refers to both a commercial sector and a popular sushi bar situated on the asteroid settlement of [[suliman|Suliman]] in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system (Session 34).

## Undercover Meeting Site
The sushi bar is known to be frequented by corporate personnel, including security officer [[hector|Hector]], who was a close friend of the missing scientist [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]]. [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] tracked Hector here after 6 PM on instructions from union leader [[carl-parker|Carl Parker]]. After overcoming Hector's initial reluctance and navigating surveillance cameras, the team held a clandestine meeting in a secluded corner, extracting vital intelligence regarding Wu-Ketai's uplift timeline (Session 34).
