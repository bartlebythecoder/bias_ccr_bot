# Silas (Suliman)

**Summary**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] security officer at [[suliman|Suliman]]'s arrivals checkpoint who interrogated [[isambard|Isambard]] under his Humphrey DuPont cover.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-16

---

**Silas** is a corporate security officer stationed at the arrivals checkpoint of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] asteroid settlement [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 34).

*(Not to be confused with [[silas|Silas]], the independent sub-AI inside Metropolis Station.)*

## Interrogation of Humphrey DuPont

On Day 115-1105, Officer Silas conducted the individual security interrogation of [[isambard|Isambard]], who was posing as *Humphrey DuPont*, spokesman for a workers' rights lobby group. Isambard kept his composure, framing the visit as a fact-finding mission with scheduled meetings with union organizer [[carl-parker|Carl Parker]], and cleared security (Session 34).

His colleague [[anya-suliman|Anya]] questioned [[beck|Beck]] at the same checkpoint (Session 34).
