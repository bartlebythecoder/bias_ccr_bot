# Asher

**Summary**: Brilliant chemist and elite hacker originating from the impoverished world of Pharr and educated on Nesvorny, serving as scientific and cyber infiltration specialist for the crew.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Background

Asher was born on [[pharr|Pharr]] (0928), recognized as the poorest planet in the subsector (Sessions 1–5). Possessing exceptional intellectual acumen and drive, he escaped severe poverty by winning admittance to the prestigious university system on nearby [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] (1028), completing advanced training in chemical sciences (Sessions 1–5).

After losing his employment, he turned to high-risk covert intelligence operations with [[beck|Beck]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[sarik|Sarik]] (Sessions 1–5).

## Technical & Infiltration Expertise

- **Chemistry Specialist**: Highly skilled in laboratory analysis, chemical synthesis, and material diagnostics. Posed as a research chemist in the Science Building of [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]] (Sessions 1–5).

While his general routine administrative IT skills are standard, he possesses an extraordinary, intuitive talent as a hacker, capable of breaching secure databases, cracking protected firewalls, and bypassing corporate encryption protocols (Sessions 1–5).

Guarded the rear rank during the subterranean breach (Session 6).
