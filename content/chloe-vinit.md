# Chloe Vinit

**Summary**: Student at Freeport University on Csabai and mercenary girlfriend of Jacob Godard who surrendered his comm-phone and wilderness location for a cash bribe.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Academic Life & Complicity

Chloe Vinit is an enrolled student attending [[freeport-university|Freeport University]] on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 29). She maintained a romantic relationship with [[jacob-godard|Jacob Godard]], the courier who fled the [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] with a confidential data wafer (Session 29).

## The University Bribe

On Day 94-1105, operatives from [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] confronted Chloe within the university library (Session 29). While [[beck|Beck]] attempted to elicit cooperation through empathetic appeals, operative [[isambard|Isambard]] perceived Chloe's mercenary disposition (Session 29). When offered a cash payout, Chloe promptly handed over Godard's personal comm-phone and disclosed his hiding place inside a cavern in the [[mushroom-woods|Mushroom Woods]] (Session 29).
