# Primero Cartel

**Summary**: Major organized crime syndicate operating across the upscale entertainment districts of Freeport, led by Kaelen Varr and trafficking Sycorax from Eternity Station.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

The Primero Cartel is the dominant criminal cartel in the primary entertainment corridors of Jonestown on [[freeport|Freeport]]. Led by **Boss Kaelen Varr**, a charismatic yet ruthless former belter, the cartel specializes in catering to belters arriving off long mining hauls.

## Operations & Alliances

- **Territory**: Controls prime commercial docking bays, Neon Alley, and Gravity Gardens on Freeport.
- **Narcotics Pipeline**: Primary distributor of **Sycorax**, a potent narcotic supplied directly by the Meinhoff Brigade based on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]].
- **Illicit Services**: Operates rigged gaming parlors, escort networks, protection rackets targeting belter businesses, and coercive debt consolidation schemes.
- **Rivalries**: Engaged in a violent turf war with the [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]], who continually attempt to ambush shipments and encroach upon Primero territory.
