# Pressure Crystals

**Summary**: Rare crystalline minerals formed deep within the high-gravity atmosphere of the gas giant Nazca, vital for contra-grav drives and driving major futures market speculation.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Gravitational Synthesis

Pressure crystals are unique, high-density mineral matrices synthesized under extreme gravitational pressures within the deep atmospheric envelopes of select gas giants:
- **Nazca Gas Giant**: In the [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] system (1022), the gas giant Nazca is the sole celestial body producing these crystals.
- **Technical Demands**: Deep-atmosphere skimming, extraction, and stabilization require advanced **Tech Level A** infrastructure and heavy contra-grav lifters.

## Strategic & Economic Value

- **Gravitics Manufacturing**: Pressure crystals are an indispensable raw material required for building contra-grav vehicles, artificial gravity plating, and starship inertial compensators.
- **Market Speculation**: Following the resolution of the Sanlucar revolution in 1105, pressure crystal futures experienced an unprecedented speculative boom on the [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] Stock Exchange ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).
