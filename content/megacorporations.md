# Megacorporations

**Summary**: Astronomical interstellar commercial enterprises operating at Imperial scale, wielding regional political authority, sovereign planetoid leases, and private armies.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Structure and Astronomical Scale

Within the Third Imperium, millions of corporate entities conduct business, but only a small handful operate at true Imperial scale. Known as **Megacorporations**, their annual profits, total share capital, and personnel counts exceed the economic output of entire planetary sectors.
- **Disconnected Boards**: Central executive boards establish broad strategic policy across distant sectors, leaving practical governance to powerful regional managers.
- **Regional Autonomy**: Regional directors frequently command greater local authority, security resources, and diplomatic leverage than planetary nobility or Imperial planetary governors.

## Imperial Sovereignty and Covert Competition

Megacorporations are legally bound to respect Imperial sovereignty and local planetary taxes:
- Because direct militarized confrontation with the Imperium would be commercially fatal, disputes with Imperial authorities or rival megacorps are prosecuted through covert action, mercenary proxies, and industrial espionage.
- Many megacorporations maintain dedicated private paramilitary forces equipped with high-tech weaponry ([[susag|SuSAG]]).
