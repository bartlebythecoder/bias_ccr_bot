# Freeport University

**Summary**: Prominent academic institution and campus on Csabai attended by student Chloe Vinit, site of the intelligence shakedown.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Campus & Infrastructure

Freeport University (also referenced as *University of Freeport*) is a major collegiate institution located on [[csabai|Csabai]], serving thousands of undergraduate students, researchers, and technical scholars (Session 29). The campus includes extensive multi-tier research libraries, athletic recreation complexes, and lecture halls (Session 29).

## Investigation of Day 94-1105

On Day 94-1105, during the manhunt for fugitive courier [[jacob-godard|Jacob Godard]], operatives from [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] entered the university library (Session 29). Locating student [[chloe-vinit|Chloe Vinit]], the operatives paid a cash bribe in exchange for Godard's comm-phone and the coordinates of his wilderness retreat in the [[mushroom-woods|Mushroom Woods]] (Session 29).
