## Leadership of the Hunglungs

Imogen Kane leads the [[hunglungs|Hunglungs]], the resistance movement formed by residents of [[doptown|Doptown]] aboard [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]. The Hunglungs are organizing to free themselves from [[the-choke|The Choke]], the scant-oxygen under-region where those unable to pay the daily tax are confined.
