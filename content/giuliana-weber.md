# Giuliana Weber

**Summary**: President of the Commonwealth States of Sanlucar and revolutionary commander of the Aridush Freedom Fighters, driven by historical trauma from the 1089 Civil War.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Early Life and the 1089 Trauma

Giuliana Weber is the daughter of a senior leader in the former Khanate of Aridush on [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]]. During the [[sanlucar-civil-war|Sanlucar Civil War]] (1089–1090), an assault raid struck her family compound, slaughtering her entire family and leaving her as the sole survivor ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]). Weber has consistently maintained that clandestine Imperial Marines executed the raid on behalf of the [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]], an event that fueled her lifelong determination to restore Aridush sovereignty.

## Revolutionary Leadership (1105)

Weber founded and led the [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]], harnessing deep working-class anger toward Valentian mining monopolies and their ties to the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement:
- Orchestrated hit-and-run guerrilla strikes and sabotage against Valentian infrastructure.
- Led the early 1105 general offensive that forced the unconditional surrender of the Valentian armed forces ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## Presidency and Statesmanship

Assuming the presidency of the newly founded [[commonwealth-states-of-sanlucar|Commonwealth States of Sanlucar]], Weber demonstrated pragmatic diplomatic resolve:
- Initially held firm on demands for Imperial Marine war crime tribunals, prompting a diplomatic stalemate with [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).
- On 034-1105, concluded a historic settlement: dropped war crime charges and pardoned Psalmist detainees in exchange for official interstellar recognition from Kidderminster and Green travel clearance from the Imperial Navy ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).
- Shifted focus toward post-war reconstruction, appealing across the subsector for heavy machinery and relief aid ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).
