# Dr. Pearson

**Summary**: Academic researcher at [[nesvorny-university|Nesvorny University]] whose published papers describe ancient [[romentino|Romentino]] civilizations and specifically reference Ancient Orbs.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Academic Research & Publications

Dr. Pearson is an archaeological scholar and faculty researcher at [[nesvorny-university|Nesvorny University]] on [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] (1229) (Session 11). Pearson's scholarly work focuses on pre-human civilizations and xenological archaeology within Sector Solo-6 (Session 11).

## The Ancient Orb Breakthrough

On Day 39-1105, operative [[asher|Asher]] discovered academic monographs authored by Dr. Pearson that explicitly document an enigmatic pre-human culture on [[romentino|Romentino]] and discuss "their [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orbs]]" (Session 11). This publication represents the first confirmed historical and scientific reference to the artifact recovered from the [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]], establishing a compelling scientific motive for the crew to journey to Nesvorny (Session 11).
