# Caliban's Heart

**Summary**: Mysterious center of [[the-choke|The Choke]] on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], the heart of the station's scant-oxygen under-region.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-20

---

## Location & Purpose

Caliban's Heart is the center of [[the-choke|The Choke]], the scant-oxygen under-region aboard [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] where residents who cannot pay the daily tax are sent. The station itself is a quasi-legal habitat in the lawless [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] belt, governed by the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]).

## Unknown Nature

What lies at Caliban's Heart, and why it is regarded as the center of The Choke, remains a mystery.
