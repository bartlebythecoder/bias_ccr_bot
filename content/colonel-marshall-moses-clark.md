# Colonel Marshall Moses Clark

**Summary**: Marine colonel commanding the Subsector Navy contingent stationed in [[hampton|Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]].

**Last updated**: 2026-10-20

---

## Command at Hampton

Colonel Marshall Moses Clark is a marine colonel serving as head of the Subsector Navy contingent in [[hampton|Hampton]], the town on [[tutin|Tutin]] that serves as the entry and exit point for all workers on the planet. His command places him at the gateway through which every worker passes on arrival and departure.
