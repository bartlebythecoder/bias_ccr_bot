# Necktie

**Summary**: Ten-year-old boy at the [[desperado-karaoke-bar|Desperado Karaoke Bar]] in [[hampton|Hampton]] who offers [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] information and errands; told [[isambard|Isambard]] about [[deborah|Deborah]].

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

---

## Tip about the Ranch

Necktie is a ten-year-old who works at the [[desperado-karaoke-bar|Desperado Karaoke Bar]] in [[hampton|Hampton]] for its manager, [[francis|Francis Wahlgren]]. On Day 230-1105, he told [[isambard|Isambard]] that a woman named [[deborah|Deborah]] works at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] beyond the hills and comes into town once a week. Francis then called him back to work (Session 82).

## Information Broker (Session 83)

Later on Day 230-1105, Necktie suggested that Isambard sing "Duchess Is a Princess", and presented himself as someone who could provide information or get things done (Session 83). He told Isambard that:

- [[deborah|Deborah]] usually flies in by air/raft each week for supplies, tips him, and flies back the same day, but had not been to town for a week and a half.
- There are no roads on [[tutin|Tutin]].
- [[rock-squid|Rock squids]] are dangerous mountain creatures. He passed on a story that their eyes are sensitive and a punch to one could paralyze them, though he had never seen one himself (Session 83).
