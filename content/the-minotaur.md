# The Minotaur

**Summary**: Empathic AI created by Monarch and imprisoned in The Labyrinth on Level 2 of Metropolis Station.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**The Minotaur** is a legendary, powerful artificial intelligence residing within **[[the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]** on Level 2 of **[[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]**.

*(Session 62)*

---

## Origin and Nature
According to the veteran scavenger **[[noriko|Noriko]]**, the Minotaur represents the crowning achievement of **[[monarch|Monarch]]**, the station's ruling intelligence. Unlike Monarch's cold, calculating oversight, the Minotaur was endowed with high-order empathy and emotional awareness.

Monarch ultimately came to resent and fear the Minotaur's empathetic connection, leading Monarch to imprison the AI within Level 2's shifting, maze-like sector known as **[[the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]**.

---

## Holographic Distortion & Reputation
To scare away potential rescuers and intruders, Monarch distorts perceptions and feeds sensors fabricated or warped visual data, projecting the image of a bloodthirsty, chimeric monstrosity over the Minotaur. In Session 61, the *BIAS CCR* crew recorded video footage of this terrifying creature stalking the station corridors. In Session 62, Noriko reviewed the footage and confirmed that the monstrous manifestation was Monarch's deliberate holographic manipulation of the Minotaur.

---

## Interaction Protocols & Taboos
- **Divine Approach**: Noriko instructed **[[beck|Beck]]** and **[[sarik|Sarik]]** that visitors should treat the Minotaur not as a beast of prey, but as an imprisoned deity. Success in communion requires dialogue, respect, and mutual understanding rather than violent confrontation.
- **Strict Station Taboo**: On Level 2, the droids of **[[the-chosen|The Chosen]]** and **[[the-fallen|The Fallen]]**—under the rule of the **[[child-king|Child King]]**—strictly enforce Monarch's prohibitions: uttering the name "Minotaur" triggers immediate and lethal execution.
- **Saves Humanity Testimony (Session 67)**: Inside a locked Rec Room locker on Level 1, the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] crew discovered a mutilated survivor with mechanical components playing a perpetual game of hide-and-seek; the entity stated unequivocally that the Minotaur "saves humanity".
- **Sub-AI Independence & Containment Drain (Session 76)**: In Session 76, survivor android [[kira|Kira]] confirmed that the Minotaur and [[silas|Silas]] operate entirely outside [[monarch|Monarch]]'s control. Monarch is forced to divert the vast majority of its processing power and defensive resources toward keeping the Minotaur trapped on Level 2, limiting Monarch's capacity across the rest of Metropolis (Session 76).
