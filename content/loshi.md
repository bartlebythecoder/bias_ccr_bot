# Loshi

**Summary**: Dockmaster at Eternity Station who admitted BIAS CCR on their stolen Wu-Ketai launch.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Loshi** is the official dockmaster stationed at the main arrival berths on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] in the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico Belt]] (Session 40).

## Encounter with BIAS CCR
On Day 118-1105, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] landed at Eternity Station aboard a stolen [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] launch craft carrying two uplifted simians, [[isaiah|Isaiah]] and [[thomas|Thomas]] (Session 40). Meeting with the operatives in his docking office, Loshi expressed deep skepticism over [[isambard|Isambard]]'s claim that they had won the corporate vessel in a card game. Loshi revealed that local information broker [[angus|Angus]] had already alerted him that the newcomers were suspected of being corporate spies or radical simian liberationists (Session 40).

Accepting the team's cover credentials under [[petow|PETOW]], Loshi assessed the standard station oxygen tax and docking deposit, issuing the group a strict 24-hour pass. Because the operatives required more time to await extraction by pilot [[sky|Sky]], Loshi directed them to Teamster leader [[reidmar|Reidmar]] to negotiate a pass extension (Session 40).
