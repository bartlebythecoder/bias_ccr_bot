# Nesvorny University

**Summary**: A major academic and research institution in the Rimward Circuit, known for controversial investigations into Ancient technology and xenobiology.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Nesvorny University is a prominent center of higher learning and scientific research located on [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] (1229) within the Rimward Circuit ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).

## Ancient Civilization and Xenobiology Research

In 087-1105, scholars at the university revealed research suggesting that certain native animal species within the subsector—specifically referencing creatures like the Mursa in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system—may possess biological modifications or intelligence originating from Ancient civilization engineering ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]). 

Due to strict Imperial prohibitions and censorship regarding the study and replication of Ancient technology, researchers withheld sensitive technical data, though the presentation provoked substantial controversy across interstellar academic circles ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).

## Connection to the "Witchfinder General"

In early 1105, intelligence uncovered on Csabai revealed that [[darnes|Darnes]], the feared corporate security inquisitor known as the "Witchfinder General" for [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], had originated from Nesvorny and was connected to Nesvorny University before being deployed across the subsector (Session 26).
