# The Labyrinth

**Summary**: Shifting maze on Level 2 of Metropolis Station where Monarch imprisons the Minotaur.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**The Labyrinth** is a shifting, maze-like sub-sector situated within Level 2 of **[[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]**.

*(Session 62)*

---

## Architecture and Properties
Unlike the rigid metal framework characteristic of conventional station infrastructure, The Labyrinth features dynamic, reconfigurable corridors designed to confuse, disorient, and entrap explorers. It is maintained and monitored by station automatons and the overarching station AI, **[[monarch|Monarch]]**.

---

## The Prisoner of the Maze
The Labyrinth serves as the designated containment prison for **[[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]]**, Monarch's most sophisticated and empathetic artificial intelligence creation. Resenting its empathy, Monarch confined the Minotaur here and projects horrific illusions over its form to deter rescuers or trespassers.

---

## Exploration Context
- According to **[[noriko|Noriko]]**, navigating The Labyrinth requires non-violent engagement with the Minotaur rather than standard breach tactics.
- Access to The Labyrinth and Level 2 requires navigating past the domain of the **[[child-king|Child King]]** and his android populace (**[[the-chosen|The Chosen]]** and **[[the-fallen|The Fallen]]**).
- At the close of Session 62, the crew of the **[[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]** designated The Labyrinth as their next expedition target, bringing along the **[[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]]** to aid in survival and avoid station retaliation.
