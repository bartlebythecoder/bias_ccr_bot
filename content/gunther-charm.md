# Gunther Charm

**Summary**: Rig Manager in executive command of [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] who granted inspection access to the undercover BIAS CCR team.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Executive Authority

Gunther Charm serves as the Rig Manager and chief corporate executive directing all mining, engineering, and logistics operations at [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 17). 

## The Audit Inspection

On Day 47-1105, Charm received the undercover [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] operatives in the Fresno Lounge alongside Security Chief [[marius-hope|Marius Hope]] (Session 17). Convinced by Real Red Hand escort [[andy|Andy]] that the party represented authorized financial auditors dispatched by "The Banks" (Romentino's ruling banking oligarchy), Charm ordered facility staff to accommodate all of the team's inspection requests, unwittingly facilitating their discovery of the station's illicit [[cloning|Cloning and Memory Units]] program (Session 17).
