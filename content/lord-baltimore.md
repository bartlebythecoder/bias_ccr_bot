# Lord Baltimore

![[lord_baltimore.png|259]]

**Summary**: Imperial noble and Redshirt naval intelligence officer for [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]] who bought BIAS CCR's artifacts at [[the-cove|The Cove]] and hired them to rescue [[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]]; confidant of [[cooper|Cooper]].

**Last updated**: 2026-10-27

---

## Aristocratic Standing & Estate

Lord Baltimore is an influential Imperial aristocrat maintaining an opulent estate, Baltimore House, within the Noble Quarter of [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 11). He hosts exclusive high-society galas attended by planetary governors, megacorporation executives, and naval commanders (Session 11).

## Alliance with Cooper & Reception of the Crew

On Day 39-1105, Baltimore extended an exclusive dinner invitation to [[beck|Beck]] and her retinue (Session 11). At a private post-gala dinner, he revealed that the team's covert patron, [[cooper|Cooper]], had specifically reached out requesting that Baltimore provide oversight and protection for Beck during her stay on Romentino (Session 11).

## Geopolitical Theories & Contracts

- **Imperial Succession War**: Baltimore proposed that the Third Imperium faces an imminent dynastic war between the Great Houses ([[house-aledon|House Aledon]] vs. [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]) (Session 11). He noted that the deployment of the advanced anagathic [[deleon|DeLeon]] has rendered the Emperor functionally immortal, freezing normal aristocratic succession and pushing ambitious houses toward war (Session 11).
- **The Red Hand Rescue Contract**: Introduced the operatives to [[christine|Christine]], whose brother was kidnapped by [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]], encouraging the crew to execute a rescue operation for an MCr 1.0 bounty (Session 11).

## Evacuation Yacht to Csabai

On Day 47-1105, coordinating with [[tres-capulet|Tres Capulet]], Lord Baltimore offered [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] immediate transport off Romentino aboard his private luxury star yacht (the Getaway) bound for [[csabai|Csabai]] (1626), shielding the operatives from impending corporate and syndicate retaliation (Session 18).

## Exfiltration of BIAS CCR to Csabai

On Day 47-1105, Lord Baltimore intercepted BIAS CCR at an old port sector outside [[gas-town|GaS Town]] to warn them of planetary arrest warrants issued by [[the-banks|The Banks]] (Session 19). Baltimore offered immediate refuge aboard his [[gsbag|GSbAG]] luxury yacht docked at the starport beyond local banking jurisdiction, successfully ferrying the operatives, [[tina|Tina]], [[sky|Sky]], and the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] out of Romentino orbit and setting course for [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 19).

## Hosting BIAS CCR to Csabai

Aboard his luxury yacht during the Jump transit to [[csabai|Csabai]], Lord Baltimore hosted the BIAS CCR operatives, [[tina|Tina]], and [[sky|Sky]] (Session 20). Baltimore spent extensive personal time with Sky, which generated friction with his devoted executive assistant, [[pamela-hooverly|Pamela Hooverly]] (Session 20).

## Csabai PR Campaign and Tina's Warning

During the 43-hour approach to Csabai on Day 56-1105, Lord Baltimore heavily publicized BIAS CCR's heroism on Romentino, orchestrating a massive media reception at the starport (Session 21). Simultaneously, an unstable female patron approached the team seeking to spy on Baltimore's private comms, prompting [[tina|Tina]] to warn the team that Baltimore was deceptively dangerous and "more than he claims" (Session 21).

## Palace Patronage on Csabai

On Day 57-1105, Lord Baltimore leveraged his high-society standing to introduce BIAS CCR to [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] at [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]], facilitating their retention for the recovery of the stolen [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] (Sessions 22–23).

## In-System Warning & Kidderminster Belt Safehouse (Session 66)

On Day 191 (Session 66), Lord Baltimore re-established contact with the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] crew via encrypted in-system communications while they were operating in the Orion Belt around [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Session 66):
- **Warning of Psalmist Pursuit**: Baltimore urgently warned that [[psalm-134|Psalmists]] and religious fanatics are hunting [[sarik|Sarik]] across the system, having detected traces or movement of the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]].
- **Safehouse Offer**: Baltimore offered the crew access to an unlisted, secure asteroid safehouse in the Kidderminster Belt to evade bounty hunters, naval patrols, and zealots.
- **Banter with Beck**: When Baltimore inquired into the status of the Ancient Orb, [[beck|Beck]] playfully parried and deflected his questions.

## Redshirt Intelligence & The Artifact Deal (Session 80)

Between Days 209 and 211, BIAS CCR flew to Baltimore's asteroid base, [[the-cove|The Cove]] in the Arico belts, where his assistant [[pamela-hooverly|Pamela Hooverly]] received them (Session 80).

- **How he knew**: Over dinner Baltimore explained that [[cooper|Cooper]], who had hired the crew on [[mirassol|Mirassol]], had asked him to watch them. He had traced the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] through law-enforcement reports on Psalmist spies, who had bugged the hotel he arranged for the crew on [[romentino|Romentino]]; he apologized for it. He also knew of the price on [[sarik|Sarik]]'s head and had recognized the crew in footage of the simians' release in Solomon (Session 80).
- **Redshirt naval intelligence**: Baltimore disclosed that he works in Redshirt naval intelligence for [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]]. [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]], now with the Redshirts, confirmed her own account of leaving [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] (Session 80).
- **The artifacts**: Baltimore warned that the artifacts were illegal and that the Blackshirts expected them to be surrendered (Session 80). He offered MCr 1 for them, with [[fagan|Fagan]] assessing their value and seized equipment making up any amount above that. The appraisal valued the eight [[heavy-marbles|Heavy Marbles]] at Cr 10,000 each, the pseudoflesh at Cr 100,000 and the data wafer at MCr 20; the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] had no established price (Session 80). The crew handed over all of the artifacts, including the Orb, a beetle artifact, and the [[exact-replica-of-the-duchess|Exact Replica of the Duchess]] (Sessions 80–82).
- **A ship**: Baltimore cannot cover payments on a ship, but offered ownership of one of five navy-seized vessels, each carrying a mortgage. The crew first favored an Ambush Frigate, with 63 remaining payments of about MCr 1.36 per month, but finally chose a Light Reconnaissance Ship, to be collected on [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]]. The MCr 1 for the artifacts will be paid with the ship (Session 80).
- **Navy consulting jobs**: Baltimore offered two confidential navy jobs. [[asher|Asher]], [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] chose the MCr 4 rescue of a hostage held on [[tutin|Tutin]] for more than a year, with Cr 400,000 paid up front (Session 80).

## The Tutin Assignment (Session 81)

The hostage is [[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]], held at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|a wilderness ranch near Hampton]]. The assignment is a covert Red Shirt job: the team may not identify themselves as Red Shirts or arrest anyone, ordinary Red Shirts will not recognize them as allies, and if things go wrong they have a password and orders to submit to arrest (Session 81). Baltimore authorized killing defenders if necessary, but warned that many deaths could become a public massacre if the authorities arrived first (Session 81).

Baltimore also promised to use his influence to secure the favor [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]] owes the crew for the return of her body, and to brief her personally. The favor could bring an audience with her, or help if the team is jailed (Session 81).
