# The 134 Psalms

**Summary**: The canonical 134-verse sacred text of the Psalmist faith, promoting personal enlightenment, peaceful coexistence, and universal harmony.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Canonical Structure & Content

The 134 Psalms comprise an ancient collection of poetic verses, aphorisms, and spiritual guidelines revered across the Imperium by adherents known as Psalmists ([[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]):
- **Core Progression**: The texts begin with self-compassion (*"1. Be kind to yourself"*), move through personal discipline and social harmony (*"51. Love is the answer; 53. Live in harmony with nature"*), cultivate resilience (*"98. You are a warrior; 100. You are victorious"*), and culminate in universal transcendence (*"132. Be one with the universe; 133. Be the love you wish to see in the world; 134. Just be"*).
- **Theological Function**: Adherents study, recite, and meditate upon these verses, believing they contain the spiritual keys to unlocking self-realization and personal truth ([[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]).

## Social and Political Impact

While the verses preach non-violence, love, and peaceful coexistence ([[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]), the political application of Psalmist teachings has provoked fierce resistance on resource-heavy frontier worlds like [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] and corporate worlds like [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]; [[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
