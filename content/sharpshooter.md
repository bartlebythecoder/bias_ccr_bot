# Sharpshooter

**Summary**: 300-ton Wu-Ketai patrol gunboat on interdiction duty around Metropolis Station.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The *Sharpshooter* is a 300-ton [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] naval patrol gunboat deployed in the Orion Belt / [[metropolis-station|Metropolis]] interdiction sweep alongside an accompanying troop transport.

## Operational History
- **Day 191 Metropolis Sweep**: Under the command of [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]], *Sharpshooter* and its transport consort swept the perimeter around [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], issuing direct hailing queries to the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] regarding their disabled ship transponder and scavenging activities.
- **Station Interdictions**: [[arkady|Arkady]] cautioned the crew that *Sharpshooter* and Wu-Ketai naval officers frequently confiscate recovered salvage and high-value artifacts, though subordinate officers like Officer Conway have reputations for taking bribes.
- **Target of [[monarch|Monarch]]**: The station AI [[monarch|Monarch]] later proposed a deal to the crew of the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]], tasking them with delivering an unidentified device left in Metropolis Level 2 lobby directly to Commander Kilroy aboard his patrol operations in exchange for an ancient Orb-locating device.
