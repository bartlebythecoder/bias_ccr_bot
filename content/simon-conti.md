# Simon Conti

**Summary**: Civilian cook formerly employed at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]] who unexpectedly recognized the undercover crew in [[ricci-plaza|Ricci Plaza]] on [[romentino|Romentino]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Background & Cover Compromise

Simon Conti is a short, talkative civilian caterer and cook who previously worked at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on the frozen moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]] (Session 14). 

On Day 44-1105, while the [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] operatives were engaged in a delicate underworld summit with mob boss [[eminshi-moth|Eminshi Moth]] in [[ricci-plaza|Ricci Plaza]] on [[romentino|Romentino]], Conti unexpectedly entered the cafe and loudly greeted the operatives, reminiscing about their shared time working at K.C. Station (Session 14). Highly alarmed by the breach of their cover identities in front of senior underworld figures, the crew quickly dismissed and shooed Conti away (Session 14).
