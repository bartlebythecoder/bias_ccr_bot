# David Orana

**Summary**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] research technician on [[suliman|Suliman]] who pointed BIAS CCR toward [[shailok|Shailok]] and the Care Facility in the Lundquist case.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**David Orana** is a corporate research technician employed by [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] in the research division on the asteroid settlement [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 37).

## Connection to George Castle and Richard Lundquist
Orana is a colleague of [[george-castle|Dr. George Castle]], known to regularly take breakfast at 10 AM in the station concourses. On Day 117-1105, Castle directed [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] to interview Orana regarding missing researcher [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]] (Session 37).

Orana characterized Lundquist as an overly inquisitive figure who routinely inquired into prohibited corporate projects. Orana disclosed the critical lead that Lundquist had repeatedly visited an elderly patient named [[shailok|Shailok]] in the station's Care Facility. When pressed further about specific research programs, Orana grew visibly anxious, terminated the conversation, and was subsequently shadowed by [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] (Session 37).
