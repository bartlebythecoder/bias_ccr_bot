# Cafe Dimitri

**Summary**: Fortified members-only clubhouse of the [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]] in [[freeport|Freeport]]'s Jonestown district, where BIAS CCR found the occupants already executed.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Cafe Dimitri** (also known as *Dimitri's*) is an establishment located in the Jonestown district of [[freeport|Freeport]] that serves as a fortified, members-only clubhouse for the [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]] street gang (Session 51).

## Operational History

* **Vipers Clubhouse**: In late 1105, investigative journalist [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]] and informant [[rico-mathers|Rico Mathers]] identified Cafe Dimitri as the primary operational headquarters and hangout for the Zero-G Vipers, linked particularly to a gang figure named [[montanez|Montañez]] (Session 51).
* **The Triple Execution (Day 132-1105)**: When [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] operatives [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] cased the establishment at 7:30 PM on Day 132-1105, they found blacked-out windows, loud blaring music, and an ajar entrance. Inside, Sarik discovered three murdered men—one tied to a chair amidst a pool of blood and two riddled with gunshot wounds. While unobserved by planetary authorities, the site was under hostile electronic surveillance by unknown operatives equipped with psionic shield headsets. Sarik recovered an unpowered laptop and commercial invoices before using a foam fire extinguisher screen to escape (Session 51).
