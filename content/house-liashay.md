# House Liashay

**Summary**: Ruling noble house of the Hess subsector, elevated by the Emperor following the defeat of House Gaval in the Hessian Revolt, led by Duke Shiral Vash Liashav.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Elevation post-Hessian Revolt

House Liashay (also rendered Liashav) was granted sovereign rule over the **Hess subsector** directly by Imperial decree following their staunch loyalist service during the [[hessian-revolt|Hessian Revolt]]. They replaced the treasonous [[noble-houses|House Gaval]], which was stripped of title and eradicated from the noble roll ([[noble-houses|Noble Houses]]).

## Leadership & Corporate Power

- **Leadership**: Headed by **Duke Shiral Vash Liashav** ([[noble-houses|Noble Houses]]).
- **Megacorporate Holdings**: Owns an impressive 18% equity stake in [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]], alongside 8% stakes each in [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], and [[susag|SuSAG]].
- **Ideology**: Vocal, active opponent of the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement across the spinward border.
