# Commonwealth States of Sanlucar

**Summary**: The internationally recognized sovereign government on the moon Sanlucar, formed by the Aridush coalition under President [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Formation and Recognition

The Commonwealth States of Sanlucar was established following the overthrow of the [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]] by the [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]]. On 034-1105, the planetary government of [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] formally extended diplomatic recognition to the Aridush-led administration as the legitimate government of the moon ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).

## Governance and Division of Authority

Under the diplomatic accord negotiated in 1105:
- The Aridush coalition controls the moon's primary civic infrastructure and vital hydrogen/gas mining facilities ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).
- The defeated Valentian administration was permitted limited civilian operations in designated isolated outposts, guarded under Imperial Subsector Naval oversight ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).

## Reconciliation Measures

Under President [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]], the Commonwealth government agreed to pragmatic concessions to normalize interstellar trade:
- Dropped formal demands for war crimes tribunals against Imperial military officers ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).
- Released political detainees associated with the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).
- Resulted in the Imperial Navy lifting travel warnings and restoring Green travel status to [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).
