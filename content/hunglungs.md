# Hunglungs

**Summary**: Resistance movement of [[doptown|Doptown]] residents on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] actively organizing to free themselves from [[the-choke|The Choke]].

**Last updated**: 2026-10-20

---

## Overview

The Hunglungs are residents of [[doptown|Doptown]], the low-oxygen debtor's slum within [[the-choke|The Choke]] aboard [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]. They are actively forming a resistance movement aimed at freeing themselves from The Choke.

## Resistance Movement

The Hunglungs are organizing against the conditions that hold them in [[the-choke|The Choke]]: scant oxygen, short lives, and confinement for those unable to pay the daily tax. Their goal is escape from the under-region itself.

## Leadership

The Hunglungs are led by [[imogen-kane|Imogen Kane]].
