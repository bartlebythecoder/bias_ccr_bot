# Eminshi Moth

**Summary**: Ruthless underworld loan shark and mob boss in Startown Liberty on Romentino who held Elin Butler's Cr 500,000 casino debt and enforces the syndicate ban on Starlight smuggling.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Underworld Dominance

Eminshi Moth is an established underworld figure and loan shark operating across the commercial and gaming sectors of [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 13). Controlling high-stakes credit lines and gambling debt enforcement at venues like [[rickeys-casino|Rickeys Casino]], Moth operates under the syndicate peace framework established by [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]] (Sessions 12–13).

## Financial Extortion of Elin Butler

Following heavy gambling losses, [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]] and his companion [[marcus|Marcus]] accrued a staggering Cr 500,000 debt to Moth (Session 13). Under pressure from Moth's enforcers, Elin's sister [[christine|Christine Butler]] transferred the initial ransom sum under the guise of an abduction payment to satisfy Moth's demand (Session 13).

## Lethal Enforcement of Syndicate Truce

Moth strictly enforces the organizational ban prohibiting the off-world trafficking of [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] (Session 13). Lower-level operatives like [[joey|Joey]] and street dealers like [[tina|Tina]] live in mortal fear of Moth, knowing that any unauthorized export to systems such as [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] carries an immediate summary death sentence if uncovered (Session 13).

## Ricci Plaza Breakfast Summit

On Day 44-1105, Moth met with [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] in [[ricci-plaza|Ricci Plaza]], presenting internal findings that The Red Hand was uninvolved in the Butler kidnapping and providing the safehouse location of the [[real-red-hand|Real Red Hand]] (Session 14). During the summit, operative [[asher|Asher]] confronted Moth regarding rogue subordinate [[joey|Joey]], prompting Moth to promise an internal inquiry (Session 14).

## Romance with Sophia Baker & Vendetta against BIAS CCR

Decrypted communications recovered from [[sophia|Sophia Baker]]'s cyber-deck revealed that Moth was her secret romantic partner and handler, and that the two planned to elope together off-world using the proceeds of Ancient artifact thefts (Session 15). Prior to her disappearance, Sophia transmitted surveillance photos of [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] to Moth, warning they were undercover operatives (Session 15). Believing the party murdered or captured Sophia, Moth deployed his personal enforcers to stalk and abduct the [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] operatives across Startown Liberty (Session 15).
