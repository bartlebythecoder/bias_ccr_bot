# Noriko

**Summary**: Retired scavenger-diver aboard [[the-bell|The Bell]], the lone survivor of her crew, with rare knowledge of [[monarch|Monarch]] and [[the-minotaur|the Minotaur]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Noriko** is a veteran deep-space scavenger and diver stationed aboard **[[the-bell|The Bell]]**. She possesses exceptional insight into the internal ecology and artificial intelligences of **[[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]**.

*(Session 62)*

---

## Background & History
Noriko formerly led an expedition crew exploring "The Deep" (Metropolis Station). Approximately one year prior to Session 62, her entire crew was killed during an ill-fated treasure expedition inside the station. Shattered by the loss, Noriko retired from active diving and remained aboard *The Bell*, harboring rare, first-hand knowledge about the station's core entities.

---

## Revelations Regarding the Minotaur & Monarch
In Session 62, following a tip from **[[ghost-eater|Ghost Eater]]**, **[[beck|Beck]]** and **[[sarik|Sarik]]** sought out Noriko:
- **The True Nature of the Minotaur**: Noriko revealed that the entity known as **[[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]]** is actually **[[monarch|Monarch]]**'s greatest technological creation—an immensely powerful artificial intelligence endowed with genuine empathy.
- **Monarch's Resentment**: Monarch grew deeply jealous and resentful of its own creation's emotional capacity and imprisoned the Minotaur within the shifting corridors of **[[the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]** on Level 2.
- **Visual Deception**: When Beck and Sarik displayed the sensor and video footage of the terrifying beast they had encountered on Metropolis, Noriko identified it immediately as the Minotaur. She explained that Monarch projects horrific sensory distortions over the Minotaur's appearance to terrify, repel, and deceive any intruders.
- **Counsel for Approach**: Noriko warned the crew not to engage the Minotaur violently. Instead, she advised approaching it as one would approach a deity—prioritizing dialogue, respect, and emotional connection over weapons.
- **Station Protocol**: She clarified that Metropolis's defensive systems generally remain passive unless explorers actively provoke Monarch by breaching security protocols, picking secured systems, or forcing locked blast doors.

---

## Lore of the Murals & Ishmael's Memorial (Session 64)
In Session 64 (Session 64):
- **Interpretation of Drain Room Murals**: When Beck consulted Noriko regarding the murals discovered in the Level 2 Drain Room, Noriko explained they depicted **[[monarch|Monarch]]** ushering its "chosen ones" to safety away from internal annihilation.
- **Ishmael's Memorial**: When asked about **[[ishmael|Ishmael]]**, Noriko revealed that he was a fellow scavenger-diver who entered Metropolis long ago and never returned. In his honor, Noriko maintains and tends hundreds of memorial bonsai trees aboard *The Bell*.

## Devotion to the Minotaur (Session 65)
In Session 65 (Session 65), **[[magnus|Magnus]]** disclosed to Isambard and Sarik that Noriko had become spiritually devoted to **[[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]]**, viewing the empathetic AI as a divine savior figure rather than a technological artifact.
