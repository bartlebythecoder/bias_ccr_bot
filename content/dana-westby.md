# Dana Westby

**Summary**: [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] resident who guided BIAS CCR, introduced them to Teamster leader [[reidmar|Reidmar]] and explained the station's syndicate politics.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

**Dana Westby** is a resident, guide, and intermediary on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] familiar with union leadership and station syndicate dynamics (Session 40).

## Guiding BIAS CCR
On Day 118-1105, Dana introduced herself to [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] following their arrival meeting with dockmaster [[loshi|Loshi]] (Session 40). Inquiring keenly about rumors of [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] genetic experiments on uplifted simians, she offered to guide the operatives through the station to meet Teamster union head [[reidmar|Reidmar]].

## Exposing Station Power Dynamics
Dana briefed the team on the deep factional divide governing Eternity Station, detailing how the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] organized crime syndicate had systematically infiltrated the Teamsters Local 315F union leadership. She subsequently led the operatives through the tunnels of **The Icebox**, introducing them to local syndicate etiquette and cybernetic cultures before delivering them to Reidmar at the **Stellar Burn** entertainment complex (Session 40).
