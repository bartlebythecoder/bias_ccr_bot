# Doptown

**Summary**: Low-oxygen slum within [[the-choke|The Choke]] on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], where the station's indebted are held and cannot leave without clearing their debts and paying the mandatory oxygen tax.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-20

---

## Location & Purpose

Doptown is a low-oxygen slum aboard [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], the quasi-legal habitat in the lawless [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] belt governed by the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]). It forms part of [[the-choke|The Choke]], the wider scant-oxygen under-region where residents who cannot pay the daily tax are sent.

## Debtor's Prison

Doptown serves as a debtor's prison. Its inhabitants cannot leave without clearing their debts and paying the mandatory "oxygen tax" ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]). The [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] maintains its iron grip over station commerce by extracting tribute through "The Court" and condemning indebted belters to Doptown's low-oxygen squalor ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]).

## Resistance

The [[hunglungs|Hunglungs]] are Doptown residents actively forming a resistance movement to free themselves from [[the-choke|The Choke]].
