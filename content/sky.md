# Sky

**Summary**: Commercial flyer pilot at K.C. Station, former Imperial Marine, and Cooper's local contact who joined the crew's raid on the Mirassol Pyramid for an equal share of artifacts.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role & Background

Sky served as a local transport and shuttle pilot stationed at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on the frozen moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]] in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system (Session 6). Unknown to base management, Sky operated as the local planetary liaison and contact point for corporate espionage handler [[cooper|Cooper]] (Session 6).

Like crew operative [[sarik|Sarik]], Sky is a hardened veteran of the Imperial Marines, possessing combat training and tactical discipline (Session 6).

## The Mirassol Pyramid Raid

On circa Day 32-1105, Sky noticed the covert preparations of [[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[sarik|Sarik]] following the disappearance of [[sophia|Sophia]] (Session 6). Rather than reporting the irregular activity, she leveraged her flight capabilities to demand an equal fifth share of whatever precursor relics the team extracted from the [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]] (Session 6).

Piloting the crew south in a station flyer, Sky entered the subterranean complex and took the front-rank point scout position alongside fellow veteran marine [[sarik|Sarik]] (Session 6).

- **Air-Raft Extraction**: Piloted the air-raft extraction from the [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]], successfully flying the crew and the recovered [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] back toward [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] while navigating the aftermath of the mission (Session 7).

## Reputation & The "Killer" Moniker

Within base pilot circles, Sky faced sharp professional hostility from rival shuttle pilot [[harry-proud|Harry Proud]], who labeled her incompetent and disclosed to the crew that she was notoriously known by the callsign **"Killer"** (Session 8).

## The Romentino Smuggling Operation

Upon touchdown on [[romentino|Romentino]] on Day 37-1105, Sky undertook the highest-risk role in the extraction plan: she covertly slipped away from the shuttle carrying the crated [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], evading an armed [[ling-standard-products|LSP]] security cordon while the remaining four operatives surrendered as decoy stowaways (Session 9). Following the release of the crew from corporate interrogation, Sky held the artifact awaiting a rendezvous (Session 9).

## The Bag Switch

In the Startown Plaza on Day 38-1105, Sky discovered that rival shuttle pilot [[harry-proud|Harry Proud]] had tricked her during their flight by swapping their identical company duffel bags, absconding with both the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] and Sky's Cr 20,000 personal savings (Session 10).

## Financial Advance by Beck

On Day 42-1105, facing severe financial distress after the theft of her savings, Sky pressed to liquidate the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (Session 12). [[beck|Beck]] provided her a Cr 20,000 cash advance against her share of the artifact, easing tensions and integrating Sky into the ongoing [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] investigations (Session 12).

## Interrogation and Departure for Csabai

On Day 47-1105, following the Morella Station raid, planetary security forces from [[the-banks|The Banks]] raided BIAS CCR's leased condo in the Noble Quarter, detaining Sky, [[tina|Tina]], and [[sydney|Sydney]] for corporate espionage questioning (Session 19). Released without charges, Sky contacted [[sarik|Sarik]] via comms (Session 19). On Sarik's orders, Sky returned to the condo to retrieve the concealed [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], paid Sydney for her services, and escorted Tina to rendezvous with the team aboard [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s yacht for Jump transit to [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 19).

## Jump Transit, Baltimore's Attention, and the Orb's Warning

During the Jump voyage from Romentino to [[csabai|Csabai]] aboard [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s luxury yacht, Sky spent considerable time in the personal company of Lord Baltimore, drawing open animosity from his assistant [[pamela-hooverly|Pamela Hooverly]] (Session 20). While aboard, Sky handled the flashing [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] and experienced a terrifying psionic premonition of danger awaiting the team on Csabai, which she urgently communicated to BIAS CCR (Session 20).

## Starport Patron and the Pink Valise Secret

Upon touching down at Csabai starport on Day 56-1105, Sky helped introduce a patron who wanted the crew to scan [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s computers for references to her (Session 21). In a confidential farewell note left by [[tina|Tina]] for [[asher|Asher]], Sky was revealed to be concealing a critical, dangerous secret inside her pink valise (Session 21).

## Forensic Log Analysis at Csabai Palace

On Day 57-1105, Sky assisted [[asher|Asher]] in auditing the sensor logs of [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]], identifying the key timestamp anomaly where [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]] spent ten unmonitored minutes with [[lord-edgar|Lord Edgar]] before drugging [[lord-julian|Lord Julian]] (Sessions 22–23).

## Starport Errands and Interrogation

On Day 89-1105, while running errands in [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]], Sky was caught in a wave of suspicion when BIAS CCR discovered that a radical Psalmist cult called [[the-doves|The Doves]] knew of their possession of the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (Session 24). Interrogated by the operatives upon her return, Sky convinced the team of her innocence and integrity (Session 24).

On Day 115-1105, as the team prepared at [[freeport|Freeport]] for the three-hour transit flight to the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] mining asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]], the group deliberated whether Sky should accompany the landing team under Asher's fabricated cover story, aligning on a cohesive narrative before departure (Session 34).

## Social Diversion at The Blue Moose
On Day 116-1105, Sky operated alongside [[beck|Beck]] at [[the-blue-moose|The Blue Moose]] tavern, sharing drinks with two off-duty asteroid laborers occupying the surveillance blind spot (Session 36). Her engaging conversation kept the workers thoroughly occupied—eventually drinking them into complete incapacitation—while Asher and Sarik breached the secret utility corridor. Equipped with a discrete communications earpiece from Sarik, Sky maintained perimeter watch and communications link from the tavern as the infiltration team entered the core labs (Session 36).

## Extraction of Romero to Freeport
On Day 117-1105, during the base-wide alert triggered by the simian breakout and chrono lab breach, Sky received an emergency radio communication from the team inside the core labs (Session 39). Per their instructions, Sky located underworld informant [[romero|Romero]], fulfilling BIAS CCR's promise to help him escape the asteroid, and escorted him aboard an outbound shuttle bound for [[freeport|Freeport]] (Session 39).

## Transit to Eternity Station
On Day 118-1105, while BIAS CCR navigated docking and high-stakes poker challenges on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] under a strict 24-hour pass, Sky mobilized to fly out to the Dark Crescent to extract the team and their new companions (Session 40).

## Freeport Advance Base & Coordinating with Ridpath
On Day 120-1105, as Sky was en route to Eternity Station, she received updated orders from the team to reverse course and establish a base on [[freeport|Freeport]] while the crew underwent a week of cybernetic augmentation surgery (Session 45). Sky was tasked with watching for cryptic anonymous transmissions from a local informant regarding [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] and coordinating a meeting with [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]]'s Freeport lawyer, [[ridpath|Mr. Ridpath]]. When the operatives arrived on Freeport on Day 130-1105, Sky successfully introduced them to Ridpath to begin the [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] homicide investigation (Session 45).

## Stardust Casino Reconnaissance with Isambard
On the evening of Day 130-1105, Sky accompanied [[isambard|Isambard]] to the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]] coffee shop in North Lux (Session 46). Backing Isambard during his meeting with broker [[stadler|Stadler]], Sky assisted in securing the video recording of suspect [[david-parker|David Parker]]'s street kidnapping by [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]].

## Acoustic Triangulation & Staking Out Julie's Building

Following the abduction of [[sarik|Sarik]] by the telepathic operative [[julie|Julie]] on Day 131, Sky was designated to execute the active portion of Beck and Asher's acoustic triangulation plan (Session 48). Equipping herself with a psionic protection helmet, fireworks, an air horn, and party noisemakers, Sky conducted systematic sweeps across the Freeport LRT tram lines (Session 48). Triggering loud horn blasts at successive tram stations while Beck and Asher analyzed Sarik's boosted earpiece audio feed, Sky's noise sweeps successfully allowed Asher to isolate Sarik's coordinates to an LRT station facility (Session 48).

Around 6:00 PM, as Sarik was crated for transit, Sky converged with [[beck|Beck]] outside Julie's building, taking up tactical perimeter surveillance to track any rideshare or courier attempting to move the crate while Asher and Ridpath engaged Julie in ransom negotiations (Session 48).

## Condo 8th Floor Breach & Blade Standoff

Equipped with a psi helmet and carrying only her combat blade, Sky accompanied Beck into the condo tower and ascended to the 8th floor following Asher's camera tracking (Session 49). The pair debated ambush versus negotiation tactics, securing a hallway fire extinguisher as an improvised weapon. 

Upon breaching the apartment, Sky backed Beck during the tense confrontation with Julie, who was holding a live dead-man trigger. When the negotiations concluded and Julie prepared to leave, tensions flared and Sky drew her combat knife in a brief standoff; however, Beck ordered her to stand down, allowing Julie to depart unharmed to fulfill her side of the bargain (Session 49).

## Surveillance of Ortega Frey

On the afternoon of Day 132-1105, Sky established mobile surveillance over Stardust Casino owner [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]] across North Lux (Session 51). Sky systematically mapped Frey's daily routines, noting that Frey traveled in a small personal grav car with a single bodyguard and frequently entered shops unaccompanied while her guard waited outside, identifying critical vulnerabilities for potential isolation or interception (Session 51).

## Weapons Procurement & Park 32 Interception

Later that evening, Sky was dispatched to the Stardust Casino to negotiate an emergency black-market weapons deal with [[stadler|Stadler]] (Session 52). Paying Cr 14,000 to cover previous debts and expedited delivery fees, Sky secured two assault pistols and ballistic cloth armor by 9:00 PM, rearming the operatives before their raid on Starka Pramerski's residence.

Rushing to Park 32 following an anonymous tip, Sky tracked a suspicious older man in a business suit approaching the park perimeter. Intercepting him just before he boarded a tram, Sky forcefully seized his comms handset as he fled. An active incoming call labeled *"Boss"* connected to an unidentified woman who abruptly disconnected. Armed with her handgun and the stolen device, Sky took up position in the shadows as Hunter's Heroes cordoned the park (Session 52).

## Disarming the Gunman & Securing Burner Phone

At Park 32 on Day 132-1105, Sky executed a clean interception of the fleeing armed operative who had discarded his weapon upon encountering her in the shadows (Session 53). After securing the operative's discarded firearm and answering the brief call from the mysterious female "Boss", Sky evaded the cordon established by [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]]. She successfully rendezvoused with the crew and handed the seized burner phone over to [[asher|Asher]] for cryptographic decryption, initiating the trail to [[winslow-leach|Winslow Leach]] and Orion Docks (Session 53).

## Mall Crime Scene Overwatch & Spotting the Hitman at Jollynn Trust

On Day 133-1105, Sky deployed armed with concealed assault weaponry and tranquilizers alongside Beck and Sarik to the Freeport shopping mall (Session 54). She maintained tactical security while Beck disproved the suicide/overdose staging on [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]].

Later in the day, when the crew investigated [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]]:
- Sky set up a surveillance stakeout outside the bank tower in the financial district, observing perimeter and lobby security.
- Sky made a breakthrough identification: she spotted [[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]]—the very same operative she had intercepted and disarmed outside Park 32—acting openly as part of bank CEO [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn's]] personal security detail (Session 54).
- Sky prepared with the team to infiltrate Jollynn's evening speaking engagement to facilitate the theft and cloning of Jollynn's phone (Session 54).

## Stardust Charity Gala Infiltration & Covert Overwatch

At the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]] charity gala on the evening of Day 133-1105, Sky operated under strict concealment protocols to prevent [[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]] from recognizing her from their Park 32 fight (Session 55):
- **Security Distraction**: Sky engaged casino security guards in extended casual conversation in the foyer, extracting details on event logistics and guard shifts while allowing Sarik to slip unnoticed into the employees-only corridor.
- **Ballroom Overwatch**: Taking a seat at an inconspicuous back table, Sky monitored venue security and police presence during the speeches, ready to provide cover fire or tactical extraction if the team's tablet-snatch operation was compromised (Session 55).

## Breaching the Jollynn Mansion & Neutralizing Richard (Session 56–57)

During the direct assault on Marie Orslo Jollynn's gated mansion on Day 133-1105, Sky served as point scout and stealth infiltrator (Sessions 56–57):
- **Perimeter Breach**: Sky led the team over the estate's perimeter fence while the gatehouse guard was distracted, locating the unlocked service entrance into the mansion.
- **Maid's Room Skirmish**: Entering an interior room, Sky surprised an armed guard and housemaid [[isabel|Isabel]]. Sky slashed the guard, sustained a gunshot wound in return, and assisted Thomas in neutralizing him.
- **Takedown of Richard**: When chief bodyguard Richard ([[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]]) entered the residence calling for Isabel, Sky ambushed him in tandem with Thomas, stabbing Richard in the neck and incapacitating him without gunfire.
- **Mansion Sweep & Tunnel Escape**: Outfitted in captured guard flak armor, Sky cleaned foyer evidence and assisted in securing the perimeter while Beck blasted open the safe, safely extracting through the underground escape tunnel into a public park (Sessions 56–57).

## Permanent Contract & Project Metropolis Transit (Session 58)
During the crew's seven-week sabbatical on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], Sky negotiated a permanent formal contract with [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]], securing a guaranteed base salary supplemented by hazard pay bonuses for high-risk combat operations (Session 58).

Following the installation of a new double turret and laser drill on their starship, Sky piloted the vessel outbound from Eternity Station on a 3.5-day transit toward [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]], navigating the approach vectors to thread through the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] patrol net during the final approach hour (Session 58).

## Running the Wu-Ketai Patrol & Infiltration (Session 59)
On Day 188-1105, Sky served as co-pilot alongside [[isambard|Isambard]] at the helm of the ***[[raconteur|Raconteur]]*** as they approached Project Metropolis (Session 59). When an interdicting [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] fighter engaged them with pulse lasers, Sky maintained tactical flight discipline under fire until the pursuer broke off near the station. Sky docked the ship at ***[[the-bell|The Bell]]*** and accompanied the crew during the six-hour dive insertion into **A00 Docking Bay** (Session 59).

## Comms Overwatch & Surveillance of Asher (Session 62)
During operations around *The Bell* and Metropolis (Session 62):
- **Shipboard Comms**: Maintained continuous radio contact with the boarding party from the *BIAS CCR* vessel alongside the uplifted simians.
- **Watching Asher**: When [[isambard|Isambard]] privately voiced deep concerns that [[monarch|Monarch]] may have scanned their brains to generate synthetic duplicates, Sky agreed to keep a vigilant, discreet watch over [[asher|Asher]] for anomalous behavior while he continued technical operations and accompanied the strike team into the station.

## Lord Baltimore Message Thread (Session 64)
Aboard the *BIAS CCR* starship following the team's extraction from Level 2 (Session 64):
- **Baltimore Transmission**: Sky received and displayed a confidential message thread from **[[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]**, disclosing that she was currently operating on an active assignment on his behalf.

## The One-Handed Man Alert & Baltimore In-System (Session 65)
Aboard the starship across Days 189–190 (Session 65):
- **Overnight Security**: Confirmed with the operatives that neither she nor the uplifted simians had departed the vessel overnight.
- **Relaying Dana Westby's Alert**: Relayed an urgent transmission from [[dana-westby|Dana Westby]] on Eternity Station warning that a **[[one-handed-man|One-Handed Man]]** had arrived hunting Sarik. Sky aggressively proposed mounting an immediate preemptive strike to eliminate the pursuer before he tracked them to Metropolis.
- **Lord Baltimore In-System Comms**: Received a direct transmission from [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]], who arrived in-system and inquired whether BIAS CCR still had possession of the **[[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]]**.
