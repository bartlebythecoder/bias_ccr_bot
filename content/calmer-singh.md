# Calmer Singh

**Summary**: Information broker on [[csabai|Csabai]] assassinated by an [[irklan|Irklan]] poisoned dart outside [[henrys-tavern|Henry's Tavern]], whose data logs exposed [[the-doves|The Doves]]' conspiracy.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Intelligence Career & Death

Calmer Singh was a private intelligence broker operating across [[csabai|Csabai]] and the Lower Circuit (Session 24). Conducting trade out of his berthed starship at Berth 17 of [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]], Singh maintained commercial ties with merchant broker [[wesley-val|Wesley Val]] (Session 24).

On Day 89-1105, Singh arranged an operational rendezvous with [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] at [[henrys-tavern|Henry's Tavern]] in Starport Alpha (Session 24). Just prior to the meeting, Singh was targeted in the concourse by an assassin from the [[irklan|Irklan]] sect, who struck him down with a lethal envenomed dart (Session 24). Singh collapsed outside the tavern, whispering faint parting words before dying in the presence of [[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], and [[sarik|Sarik]] (Session 24).

## The Legacy of Singh's Logs

Searching Singh's body, the operatives recovered his docking pass for Berth 17 (Session 24). Infiltrating his ship, Asher extracted encrypted video archives revealing that Singh had tracked a militant Psalmist cell calling themselves [[the-doves|The Doves]], who were preparing to assassinate [[darnes|Darnes]] and execute BIAS CCR to seize the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (Session 24).

---

## The Slaughter of Singh's Crew

On Day 90-1105, forensic sweeps conducted by [[lt-tony-mcintyre|Lt. Tony McIntyre]] aboard Singh's vessel, ***[[cals-dream|Cal's Dream]]***, uncovered the bodies of three crew members who had been methodically assassinated by the [[irklan|Irklan]] to suppress the intelligence data Singh carried (Session 25).
