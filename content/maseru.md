# Maseru

**Summary**: System 1224 in Solo Sector Subsector J, known for its extensive prison moons and penal colonies.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Maseru (1224) is cataloged under UWP profile C6A5587-9 ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]).

## Penal Infrastructure

The system serves as a major detention destination in the subsector, housing numerous prison moons. In 035-1105, authorities reported record surges in inbound inmates, resulting in critical overcrowding concerns that have been compounded by administrative and construction delays on scheduled penal colony expansions ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]).
