# The Backways

**Summary**: Unmapped, labyrinthine subterranean network of abandoned tunnels and waterways beneath Startown Liberty, inhabited by Romentino's destitute underclass and scavengers.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Structure & Demographics

The Backways comprise a sprawling, decaying underground subterranean sector lying beneath [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 19). Originating from decommissioned utility conduits, structural drainage lines, rusty waterways, and forgotten starport access passages, the Backways are completely unmonitored by corporate authorities or the constabulary of [[the-banks|The Banks]] (Session 19).

The district is populated almost exclusively by Romentino's forgotten underclass: homeless scavengers, destitute workers cast out by corporate downsizings, and desperate mobs surviving under squalid, hazardous conditions (Session 19). The lower reaches connect into the toxic refuse processing sector that houses colonies of orphaned street urchins, including [[sydney|Sydney]], [[marco|Marco]], and [[paolo|Paolo]] (Session 19).

## The Day 47 Ambush

On Day 47-1105, while evading planetary corporate espionage warrants issued by [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] engaged the guide services of young urchin [[marco|Marco]] to bypass public transit corridors and reach [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s yacht at the starport (Session 19).

While navigating a narrow corridor beside a rusted industrial waterway, the operatives were trapped and ambushed from two sides by a mob of twenty starving inhabitants wielding improvised melee weapons (Session 19). In the ensuing melee:
- Operatives [[beck|Beck]] and [[asher|Asher]] sustained serious physical injuries (Session 19).
- Operatives neutralised nine of the assailants (killing several and gravely injuring others), finally breaking the mob's resolve and scattering the remainder (Session 19).
- Beck rendered rapid field medical care to stabilize Asher and dress their wounds before guiding the team through the refuse processing sector toward safety (Session 19).
- A hidden cache within the tunnels yielded emergency rations, fresh water, and an illicit [[ai-spider|AI Spider]] (Session 19).
