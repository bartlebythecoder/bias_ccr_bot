# Sanlucar Crisis

**Summary**: The 1105 revolution on [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] in which the [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]] under [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]] overthrew the [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The **Sanlucar Crisis** was a major political upheaval on [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] (1022), the first moon of the gas giant Nazca, home to about four million people in underground and orbital cities. In 1105 the moon erupted into fighting not seen since the [[sanlucar-civil-war|civil war of 1089–1090]]. The [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]] forced the surrender of the [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]], until then the largest state on Sanlucar and the power that controlled most of its economy ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## Background

- Sanlucar has a history of political instability and disputes over its mining wealth, going back to the destruction of the Khanate of Aridush in the [[sanlucar-civil-war|1089–1090 civil war]].
- The Valentian Republic dominated the economy, and resentment over how resources were shared grew among working people.
- The Valentian leadership's association with the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement deepened that resentment.

## The Revolution

- The Aridush Freedom Fighters waged a guerrilla campaign of hit-and-run raids and sabotage against Valentian forces.
- A final offensive forced the surrender of the Valentian military.
- A provisional government was set up under [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]], focused on redistributing the moon's resources.
- The Imperial Navy stayed neutral, and neighboring systems watched without intervening. Sanlucar's travel code went to Red during the worst of the fighting.

## Giuliana Weber

Weber emerged as the movement's leader. She is the daughter of a senior leader of the old Aridush Khanate and the sole survivor of the raid that killed the Aridush leadership during the 1089–1090 civil war. She blames the Imperial Marines for her family's deaths and publicly vowed to pursue those responsible for past actions against the Aridush people.

## Aftermath

- The Aridush held military control but were not equipped to run the moon's bureaucracy, and Sanlucar's future was uncertain.
- The Imperial Navy, with a strong presence in the system, worked with all sides toward a sustainable government, and the travel code returned to Green.
- After talks with [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]], Weber won formal recognition for the new [[commonwealth-states-of-sanlucar|Commonwealth States of Sanlucar]] in exchange for dropping war-crimes prosecutions against Imperial officers and granting amnesty to Psalmist detainees ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).
- Reconstruction was slowed by shortages of contra-grav lifters, atmospheric processors, and modular housing, while the new stability set off a speculative boom in [[pressure-crystals|pressure crystal]] futures on the Kidderminster exchange ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).
