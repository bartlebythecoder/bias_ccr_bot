# Csabai Palace

**Summary**: The seat of government and ancestral grand residence of Viscount Rittenhouse on Csabai, site of the Beaugard painting theft and high-society palace balls.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Architectural & Security Overview

The Csabai Palace is the opulent administrative citadel and private ancestral estate of [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]], the ruler of [[csabai|Csabai]] (Sessions 22–23). The estate features expansive art galleries, grand ballrooms, extensive staff quarters, and high-tech private medical augmentation facilities (Sessions 22–23).

The palace maintains a specialized internal security architecture:
- Automated optical surveillance cameras are installed throughout common halls and public galleries (Sessions 22–23).
- To respect aristocratic privacy, sensors are programmed to automatically shut off camera feeds whenever a member of the Rittenhouse noble family enters an area, reactivating only when they depart (Sessions 22–23).
- Household discipline and domestic logistics are governed by head butler [[sterling|Sterling]], while protective security was historically directed by [[morgan|Morgan]] (Sessions 22–23).

## The Beaugard Theft

On Day 57-1105, the historic [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] on loan from [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] was stolen from the gallery during a vigil kept by [[lord-julian|Lord Julian]] (Sessions 22–23). [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] conducted a discrete forensic audit of the palace system, uncovering that [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]] and [[lord-edgar|Lord Edgar]] exploited the privacy shut-off protocol to drug Julian and conceal the artwork within the palace suites (Sessions 22–23).

---

## The Library Assassination Attempt

On Day 91-1105, the palace library became an assassination ambush site when lead housekeeper [[agnes-tuttle|Agnes Tuttle]], working as a covert operative for [[the-doves|The Doves]], attempted to paralyze and murder [[asher|Asher]] via waste incinerator disposal to steal the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (Session 26).
