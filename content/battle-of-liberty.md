# Battle of Liberty

**Summary**: Decisive fleet clash in the 1064/1084 Hessian campaigns halting House Gaval, fought within a 300,000-year-old Ancient war debris belt; La Libertad remains an interdicted graveyard.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Ancient Origin of the Battlefield

The La Libertad (0926) Asteroid Belt is not a natural celestial formation, but is believed to have been shattered into being more than **300,000 years ago** during an apocalyptic space battle between rival battle fleets of [[the-ancients|The Ancients]] ([[the-ancients|The Ancients]]).

## Strategic Engagement (Hessian Revolt)

Millennia later, the La Libertad belt served as the battlefield for the decisive naval engagement of the Hessian Revolt:
- Imperial subsector fleets from [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] and [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]] intercepted and broke the advance of House Gaval's invasion force.
- Overextended supply lines prevented the Hessians from advancing further, preserving Imperial subsector integrity.
- Both sides sustained devastating losses. An Imperial defeat would have triggered the total collapse of the Kidderminster subsector.

## Capital Ship Losses & Black Tech

- ***Allamu (BC-9516)***: Kinunir-class cruiser held the vanguard for four hours under continuous fire before screen failure destroyed the ship with all hands; name memorialized for future Imperial dreadnoughts ([[kinunir-battle-cruiser|Kinunir-Class Battle Cruiser]]).
- ***Ninkur Sagga (BC-9521)***: Destroyed on the flank with all hands due to either a magazine detonation or a catastrophic [[black-globe-generator|Black Globe Generator]] screen failure ([[kinunir-battle-cruiser|Kinunir-Class Battle Cruiser]]).

## Modern Hazard Status

Layered with both Ancient precursor wreckage and modern starship hulks, La Libertad is littered with unexploded ordnance, reactor debris, and lethal radiation, placed under permanent travel interdiction by the Travellers' Aid Society (TAS).
