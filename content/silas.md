# Silas

**Summary**: Independent sub-AI inside [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] that operates outside [[monarch|Monarch]]'s control.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Silas** is an autonomous station sub-artificial intelligence operating within [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] beyond the direct command and control of the rogue AI [[monarch|Monarch]] (Session 76).

## Nature & Threat Profile

According to intelligence provided by survivor android [[kira|Kira]], Silas is one of two high-order sub-AIs functioning independently inside Metropolis—the other being **[[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]]** imprisoned in [[the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]].

While Monarch concentrates substantial processing and military bandwidth on containing the Minotaur, Silas operates covertly from other sections of the station network. Station schematics recovered by the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] crew identified a dedicated **Silas Control Terminal**, though the crew hesitated to solicit Silas as an ally against Monarch due to unpredictable motives and the acute danger of rogue AI cyberattacks.

## Pseudo-Flesh Farm Management & Role Clarification (Session 77)

In Session 77 (Session 77):
- **Distinct from Damien**: When the crew questioned whether Silas was an alias or persona used by [[damien|Damien]], Kira clarified that Silas is completely separate: an autonomous infrastructure sub-AI specifically engineered to supervise and direct the biological **pseudo-flesh farms** across Metropolis Station.
- **Support for Infiltration Androids**: Silas's generation of organic tissue directly feeds into the **Infiltrator-Android Area** adjacent to Micro-Hangar C-118, where synthetic bodies (such as the [[exact-replica-of-the-duchess|Exact Replica of the Duchess]]) are grown and prepped for personality downloads from the Infiltration Personalities Databank.
