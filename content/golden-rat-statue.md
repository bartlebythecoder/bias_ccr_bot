# Golden Rat Statue

**Summary**: Gilded rat idol found on a ritual shrine in the Level 1 kitchen of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], flanked by crucified [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] guards.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The **Golden Rat Statue** is a gilded zoomorphic idol discovered upon an altar-like kitchen prep counter on Level 1 of [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 60).

## The Kitchen Shrine
On Day 188-1105, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] entered the Level 1 Kitchen while sweeping the station, encountering a disturbing ritualistic scene:
- **Flanking Crucifixions**: Flanking the counter where the statue sat, the decomposing corpses of two [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporate security personnel were mounted in crucifixion postures against the wall (Session 60).
- **Ritual Offerings**: The Golden Rat Statue was arranged in the center of the prep table, surrounded by deliberate offerings of preserved food supplies, medical packs, and mechanical salvage.
- **Significance**: The arrangement suggests that scavenging groups, surviving personnel, or aberrant synthetic servitors operating under [[monarch|Monarch]]'s domain have developed ritualistic, cultic practices or totemic superstitions within the isolation of the derelict station (Session 60).
