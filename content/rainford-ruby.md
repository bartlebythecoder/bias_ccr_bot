# Rainford Ruby

**Summary**: [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] industrialist and cousin of [[kino-yuba-foust|Kino Yuba Foust]] who organized the failed heist on the [[foust-refinery|Foust Refinery]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Rainford Ruby is a wealthy industrialist on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]], owner of the [[ruby-refinery|Ruby Refinery]], and the cousin and rival of [[kino-yuba-foust|Kino Yuba Foust]] (Sessions 31–32).

## Feud with Kino Yuba Foust
After his refinery was robbed by mercenaries hired by Foust a year earlier, Ruby planned an elaborate scheme of corporate revenge. When Foust secured a massive refining deal with Hexham, Ruby infiltrated operatives into Foust's facility and used his wealth to tie up all cargo lifters across Marcolandia, forcing a dangerous stockpile of rare metals at the [[foust-refinery|Foust Refinery]]. He then hired mercenary leader [[larry-mckarney|Larry McKarney]] to poison Foust and execute an inside heist, which was ultimately defeated by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] (Sessions 31–32).
