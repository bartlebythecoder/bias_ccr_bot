# Hill Monkey

![[hill-monkey.png]]

**Summary**: Small shy nocturnal herbivore native to the high elevations of [[tutin|Tutin]], 25 to 30 centimetres tall, not generally aggressive but a frequent disease carrier and a persistent campsite thief.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-21

---

## Biology & Behavior

The hill monkey is a herbivore that grows to between 25 and 30 centimetres tall. It is shy and nocturnal, and is mostly seen at night.

The animal is not generally aggressive toward humans.

## Habitat & Range

The hill monkey is native to the high elevations of [[tutin|Tutin]] (1624), one of only two breathable worlds in the subsector. Tutin has been administered by the [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] government as a restricted nature reserve since the [[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]] of 1077–1083.

## Human Interaction

Hill monkeys are a known nuisance to travellers in the Tutin highlands. They are known to sneak into campsites and steal equipment from hikers and campers.

They are also a health hazard. The animals often carry diseases, so they should be avoided, and any bite from one should be treated.
