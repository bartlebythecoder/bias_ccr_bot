# Jean Luc Cartier

**Summary**: Young Csabai nobleman and nephew of Viscount Rittenhouse who eloped to Marcolandia aboard the Raconteur.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Jean Luc Cartier is a young nobleman belonging to the prominent Cartier family on [[csabai|Csabai]] and the nephew of [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse]] (Session 30).

## Elopement to Marcolandia
Defying the severe social objections of his noble family, Jean Luc arranged passage aboard the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] under [[isambard|Isambard]] to elope with [[jocelyn-gauthier|Jocelyn Gauthier]], a former household servant. His identity and motives were uncovered through investigative background checks conducted by [[beck|Beck]] prior to departure from [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 30).
