# News Dispatch — Volume 1

**Summary**: News dispatches covering the first month of 1105 across Solo Sector Subsector J, featuring the Aridush uprising on Sanlucar, genetic uplifting on [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]], [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]]'s Alexandria supercomputer announcement on [[lachat|Lachat]], humanitarian aid from [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] to Pharr, and shipping travel restrictions in [[romentino|Romentino]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Dispatches and Timeline

### 001-1105: Uplifted Simians on Arico El Nuevo
- **Location**: [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] (1324)
- **Actor**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]]
- Genetic tech corporation Wu-Ketai announced the successful breeding of uplifted simians in laboratory conditions.
- Commercial availability is anticipated within years, explicitly marketed as organic subservient labour to avoid the legal and physical risks of AI-driven robotics and androids.

### 003-1105: Revolution on Sanlucar de Barram
- **Location**: [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] (1022)
- **Actors**: [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]], [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]], [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]], [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]
- The Aridush Freedom Fighters, led by Giuliana Weber, overthrew the Valentian Republic government across the gas-mining moon of Sanlucar following a lightning offensive and surrender of Republic forces.
- Weber declared a provisional government, vowing to end corruption, redistribute resources, and personally hunt down all individuals responsible for atrocities against her family and the Khanate of Aridush.
- Imperial forces and neighboring systems have remained silent regarding external intervention.

### 018-1105: Alexandria Supercomputer Unveiled
- **Location**: [[lachat|Lachat]] (1030)
- **Actors**: [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]], [[byron-falcon-power|Sir Byron Falcon Power]], [[alexandria-ai|Alexandria AI]]
- Sir Byron Falcon Power of Erebus Power announced "Alexandria," a supercomputer data repository and artificial intelligence advisor.
- Designed strictly as an advisory system with no operational implementation powers to comply with Imperial AI safety regulations.

### 025-1105: Nesvorny Humanitarian Aid Package
- **Location**: [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] (1229) -> [[pharr|Pharr]] (1130)
- **Actors**: [[rimward-circuit-collective|Rimward Circuit Collective]]
- The Nesvorny government confirmed a MCr 45.0 care package funded by the Rimward Circuit Collective to relieve the humanitarian crisis on Pharr.

### 033-1105: Romentino Line Company Travel Access
- **Location**: [[romentino|Romentino]] (1428)
- Imperial officials expanded permitted inbound travel to include major Line Companies, while maintaining a strict ban on Free Traders and independent vessels due to ongoing security concerns.
