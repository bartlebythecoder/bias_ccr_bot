# Delgado Trading

**Summary**: The youngest Imperial megacorporation, founded in 997, known for pioneering electronic miniaturization, military hardware, antiquities, and publishing.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Corporate Origins

Founded in **997**, Delgado Trading, LIC is the youngest of the Imperial megacorporations operating within Sector Solo-6. The firm originated as a specialized manufacturer of advanced military hardware, establishing an unrivaled interstellar reputation for extreme technological miniaturization.

## Modern Conglomerate Holdings

Over its century of expansion, Delgado diversified aggressively across disparate economic sectors:
- **Resource Extraction**: Extensive heavy mineral mining and refining operations.
- **Publishing & Media**: Subsector communications, educational texts, and interplanetary media publishing.
- **Exotic Markets**: High-value interstellar antiquities brokering and large-scale consumer toy manufacturing.
