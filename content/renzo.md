# Renzo

**Summary**: Ghost of a Wu-Ketai crew member in Metropolis Station's warhead chamber, obsessed with destroying the station and Monarch.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Renzo** was a [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] crew member aboard [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] whose dying memories formed a persistent, agitated holographic station ghost within the zero-gravity [[nuclear-warhead|Nuclear Warhead]] chamber (Sessions 78–79).

## Nature & Encounters

First encountered in Session 77–78 perched atop the strategic nuclear warhead muttering cryptic poetry, Renzo was identified as a corrupted holographic memory projection. She was obsessed with the complete annihilation of the station to wipe out the rogue AI [[monarch|Monarch]].

- **The Lost Diary**: During psychic contact with [[asher|Asher]] in Session 78, Renzo revealed that she had hidden a personal diary in the Disassembly Area on Level 2 prior to her death. [[isambard|Isambard]] successfully pacified her during the initial terminal hack by presenting [[beck|Beck]]'s psychological profiling book as her lost diary.
- **Psychic Assault on Asher (Session 79)**: As [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] prepared to evacuate the warhead chamber after setting the 4.5-hour delay, Renzo launched a severe psychic attack against Asher's mind, blunting his intellectual sharpness and instilling a heightened, acute empathy for androids before Isambard suppressed her with an EMP grenade.
- **Destruction**: Renzo was vaporized alongside the rest of Metropolis Station when the nuclear warhead detonated at 20:30 hours on Day 208.
