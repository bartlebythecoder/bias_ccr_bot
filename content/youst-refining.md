# Youst Refining

**Summary**: An industrial refining corporation on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] that secured an exclusive processing monopoly with [[hexham-mining|Hexham Mining]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Youst Refining Inc (also referred to colloquially in local dispatches as Foust Refinery) is a mineral and chemical processing enterprise operating on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] (1425) ([[news-1105-vol-5.1|News Dispatch — Volume 5.1]]).

## Exclusive Hexham Contract & Labor Expansion

In 070-1105, Youst announced a lucrative contract with [[hexham-mining|Hexham Mining]], becoming the exclusive refiner for all Hexham extraction output ([[news-1105-vol-5.1|News Dispatch — Volume 5.1]]). 

The rapid tripling of capacity required an urgent hiring drive across all technical and service categories. To bridge the severe labor shortage, the company contracted convict work crews from the [[ouro-correction-department|Ouro Correction Department]] ([[news-1105-vol-5.1|News Dispatch — Volume 5.1]]).
