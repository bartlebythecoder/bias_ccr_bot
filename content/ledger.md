- **Class / Type:** Imperial Type-S Scout/Courier (100–200 tons).
- **Status:** Operational (In flight in deep asteroid belt; transponders cold).
- **Ownership:** On official long-term loan to [[isambard|Isambard]] from the Imperial Scout Service.
- **Key Modifications:**
  - Forward heavy laser-drill refit (installed at Eternity Station for asteroid penetration).

During the Month Sabbatical on Csabai (Days 57–88-1105), all four principals underwent advanced clinical augmentation funded by Viscount Rittenhouse:

- **[[beck|Beck]]:** Cellular regeneration therapy; psychological dampening filters; charisma/composure tuning.
- **[[sarik|Sarik]]:** Sub-dermal muscle grafting; heavy bone-density reinforcement; joint stabilization to mitigate past trauma.
- **[[isambard|Isambard]]:** Neural reflex booster; fine-motor ocular implants; cyber-assisted mechanical grip.
- **[[asher|Asher]]:** Cognitive neuro-link; neural coprocessor; chemical sensory filtration.
