# Incubu Asteroid Colony

**Summary**: Asteroid research colony in the Arico belt funded by House Ilethian, focused on genetic engineering and populated by Psalmist factions divided over biological manipulation.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Incubu is a small asteroid outpost in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] belt (1324) with a population of roughly 500 inhabitants, sponsored and funded by the noble house [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]. The colony operates as a bare-bones Science Research Center centered on experimental genetic engineering.

## Internal Factionalism & Ideology

The population consists almost entirely of adherents to the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement living in primitive, austere conditions. The settlement is torn by an intense internal conflict:
- **Traditionalists**: Oppose artificial intervention into biological nature, citing spiritual purity.
- **Modernists**: Seek to actively advance human potential and resilience through genetic engineering.

## Colony Leadership

- **Owen Baker**: Overall colony director navigating ideological rifts.
- **Tarek Ibrahim**: Respected leader of the Psalmist traditionalist faction.
- **Dr. Marcus Novak**: Chief medical officer managing the laboratory facilities.
