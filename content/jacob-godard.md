# Jacob Godard

**Summary**: Young Psalmist courier on Csabai who fled the Basin City center with a data wafer containing wiped evidence of the Irklan hit, captured in the Mushroom Woods.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## The Evacuation of the Data Wafer

Jacob Godard was a junior courier and administrative operative stationed within the [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] in [[basin-city|Basin City]] on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 29). On Day 93-1105, as operatives [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] breached the facility, Godard initiated an emergency computer purge, copying the wiped institutional archives onto a physical data wafer before fleeing out a service exit minutes ahead of the shotgun shootout with [[guillaume-sterling|Guillaume Sterling]] (Session 29).

## Manhunt and Capture

Godard went into hiding, warning his martial arts trainer [[sammy-petit|Sammy Petit]] at [[sammys-dojo|Sammy's Dojo]] and leaving his comm-phone with his girlfriend [[chloe-vinit|Chloe Vinit]] at [[freeport-university|Freeport University]] before retreating to a secluded cavern within the [[mushroom-woods|Mushroom Woods]] (Session 29). 

On Day 94-1105, [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] tracked Godard to his cave, lured him out, and seized the data wafer (Session 29). Operative [[asher|Asher]] scrubbed all mentions of the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] from the wafer before turning it over to planetary police, securing the legal exoneration of BIAS CCR while Godard was remanded into custody (Session 29).
