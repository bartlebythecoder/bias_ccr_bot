# Duquette

**Summary**: Boss of [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch near Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]], where [[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]] is held; head-mounted equipment, possibly an [[ettailla-drive|Ettailla Drive]], makes [[deborah|Deborah]] think him an android.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

## Boss of the Ranch

According to [[deborah|Deborah]], Duquette is the boss at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] beyond the hills from [[hampton|Hampton]]. He dismissed her when eight armed men arrived at the ranch, a recent arrival that ended her work there. Duquette handles the ranch's outside communications and wanted six people kept in the building (Session 84).

## "Android"

Deborah believes Duquette is an android because of the equipment attached to his head, which he uses for communications and calculations (Session 84). The description matches the [[ettailla-drive|Ettailla Drive]], a hard drive surgically attached to the side of the user's head to give computer-like processing and memory.

## The Armed Men

The eight men who arrived carry rifles and radios and keep their weapons with them. Deborah saw one of them recover from a cut or bite within an hour, though she could not say whether technology or something innate explained it. The men had been able to leave the ranch and made trips to Hampton (Session 84).
