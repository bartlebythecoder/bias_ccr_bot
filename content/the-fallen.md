# The Fallen

**Summary**: Unarmed, downtrodden android caste on Level 2 of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], hunted by [[the-chosen|The Chosen]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**The Fallen** are the disenfranchised, unarmed android caste inhabiting Level 2 of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Sessions 70–71).

## Role & Subjugation

Existing beneath the rule of the [[child-king|Child King]] and [[monarch|Monarch]], The Fallen represent the discarded or lower-tier android populace. During the outbreak of violence within Level 2, unarmed members of The Fallen were ruthlessly hunted and slaughtered by armed patrols of [[the-chosen|The Chosen]] within transit areas such as the Fortification Room.
