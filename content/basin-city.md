# Basin City

**Summary**: Major surface metropolis on Csabai, location of the regional Csabai Psalmist Center and municipal transit links.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Metropolitan Overview

Basin City (commonly referenced as *Basin*) is an expansive planetary surface metropolis situated on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 27). Unlike the repulsor-levitated aerial habitats of [[apex|Apex]], Basin City is grounded on the terrestrial surface, featuring dense commercial districts, industrial transport arteries, and religious institutions (Session 27).

## The Psalmist Center Investigation

On Day 93-1105, following leads provided by underworld cutout [[reshka|Reshka]], operatives [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] took a sub-orbital shuttle down from [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] to Basin City (Session 27). Infiltrating the prominent [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] located within the city, the operatives sought to identify the young Psalmist operative depicted in Reshka's surveillance photograph who had commissioned the Irklan hit against [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] and [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] (Session 27).
