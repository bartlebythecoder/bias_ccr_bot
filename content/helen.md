# Helen

**Summary**: Ghostly projection of a K.C. Station fixer that BIAS CCR met and disrupted with EMP grenades in Metropolis Station's Tool Shed.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Helen** was a fixer from [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] whose floating cognitive apparition was encountered by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] within the looted Tool Shed on the lower machinery decks of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Session 72).

## Encounter in the Tool Shed

In the Tool Shed, Helen appeared as an agitated, floating ghostly projection demanding the return of her tools, despite no tools being visible. As tensions rose and the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] registered a threat spike, [[isambard|Isambard]] threw an EMP grenade; an initial miss triggered an explosive spray of nails and screws from workshop debris that damaged Isambard and [[asher|Asher]]'s combat suits.

A follow-up EMP grenade thrown by Isambard struck Helen's projection directly, disrupting the image and causing her to flicker and vanish. This confirmed she was an external projection transmitted into the shed from an unknown source rather than a physical occupant. As the crew scavenged and retreated into the corridor, Helen's apparition resumed broadcasting calls for assistance, prompting the party to send maintenance android [[raihan|Raihan]] back inside to tend to her.
