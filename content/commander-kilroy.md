# Commander Kilroy

**Summary**: Wu-Ketai naval commander of the interdiction sweep around Metropolis Station, determined to destroy Monarch.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Commander Kilroy** is a military or station leader operating in connection with the conflict inside [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 61).

## War Against Monarch & Naval Patrol Operations (Session 61, 66)
According to intelligence provided by shuttle diver [[arkady|Arkady]], Commander Kilroy leads a dedicated faction committed to destroying the rogue alien AI **[[monarch|Monarch]]** (Session 61). Kilroy's forces contend not only with Monarch's robotic swarms and mind-copying networks, but also with compromised, synthetic, or cloned survivors operating within the station's decks.

On Day 191 (Session 66), Commander Kilroy commanded an interdiction sweep outside [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] aboard the 300-ton gunboat **[[sharpshooter|Sharpshooter]]** and an accompanying troop transport (Session 66):
- **Interrogation of BIAS CCR**: Kilroy directly hailed the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]], interrogating the crew regarding their disabled ship transponder and scavenging activities.
- **Reputation**: Arkady noted that while subordinate officers under Kilroy (such as Officer Conway) are susceptible to bribes, Kilroy himself strictly impounds unauthorized salvage and confiscated artifacts.
- **Target of Monarch's Bargain**: Inside the station, Monarch offered a contract to the BIAS CCR team: deliver an unknown device from the Level 2 lobby directly to Commander Kilroy in exchange for an Ancient Orb tracking device (Session 66).

## Armed Boarding & Alliance on The Bell (Session 67–68)
As [[arkady|Arkady]]'s extraction shuttle returned to ***[[the-bell|The Bell]]*** carrying the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] crew and their recovered salvage, Commander Kilroy and her armed naval troops boarded the habitat, having already subdued [[magnus|Magnus]] and establishing an interdiction quarantine prohibiting any artifacts from leaving while Monarch lives (Sessions 67–68).

In Session 68, the BIAS CCR team presented a forthright alliance proposal:
- **Nanite Immunity**: Kilroy safely inspected Monarch's courier tablet device using illegal internal nanite technology, which shielded her from Monarch's neural scanning matrices.
- **Trojan Cables Warning**: Kilroy warned the crew that their salvaged precursor alloy cables were a trap—while doubling computing speed, they allow Monarch to seize control of host networks.
- **Intel on The Cube**: Disclosed that scientist [[blavatsky|Blavatsky]] had engineered an anti-Monarch virus called **[[the-cube|The Cube]]**, currently located on Level 4 with [[damien|Damien]].
- **Arming the Strike Team**: Pledging not to detain the crew after Monarch's destruction, Kilroy equipped the team with combat armor, EMP grenades, gauss rifles, and station tactical maps.

## Loading Dock Ultimatum & Summit (Session 75)
In Session 75 (Session 75):
- **Ultimatum to the Raconteur**: Following the arrival of a hostile [[the-doves|Psalmist]] fleet blockading Level 1, Kilroy hailed the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] on an encrypted channel, delivering an ultimatum demanding the surrender of all recovered precursor artifacts in exchange for respite.
- **Summit on the Dock**: Meeting Isambard and Beck on neutral ground in the Loading Dock flanked by four armed naval marines, Kilroy evaluated the dual threats of Monarch and the Psalmist fleet.
- **Intelligence on Damien & Kira**: Received critical intelligence that [[damien|Damien]] is positioned on **Level 3** under an alias, and that survivor android [[kira|Kira]] possesses immunity from previous wipe cycles.
- **Pact & Support**: Kilroy permitted the crew to retain their clamshell diagnostic analyzer and agreed to furnish replacement vacuum suits, grenades, and heavy ordnance for their planned incursion into Level 3.
