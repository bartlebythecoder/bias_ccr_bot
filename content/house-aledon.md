# House Aledon

**Summary**: One of the Three Great Houses of the Imperium, ruling the Pilar subsector under Duke Norris, holding 18% of [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], and fiercely opposing [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview and Dominance

House Aledon is an Imperial Great House governing the **Pilar subsector**. Commanded by **Duke Norris**, the house commands vast military fleets and interstellar commercial power, standing alongside House Alkalikoi and House Ilethian as the dominant triumvirate of Charted Space ([[noble-houses|Noble Houses]]).

## Megacorporate Equity & Industry

Aledon exercises direct industrial control through deep equity stakes:
- **[[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]**: Owns 18% of LSP, cementing their joint hegemony over electronics, weapons, and heavy industry across the subsectors ([[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]).
- **[[susag|SuSAG]]**: Holds 5% ownership in the chemical and pharmaceutical giant ([[susag|SuSAG]]).

## Political Ideology & Rivalries

- **Rivalry with House Ilethian**: Engaged in a bitter, multi-generational cold war against [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]], competing for interstellar prestige, naval contracts, and megacorporate influence.
- **Anti-Psalmist Stance**: Outspoken opponent of the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement, coordinating with LSP lobbies to restrict Psalmist spread across industrial worlds ([[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]).
