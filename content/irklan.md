# Irklan

**Summary**: An austere, insular mountain culture on [[csabai|Csabai]] renowned for extreme physical discipline, rejection of advanced technology, and lethal close-quarters martial prowess.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Culture and Philosophy

The Irklan are an ancient, insular human society inhabiting the harsh, isolated mountain highlands of [[csabai|Csabai]]. Characterized by deep religious privacy and an intentional resistance to high-technology machinery, their culture centers entirely upon self-reliance, physical austerity, and mental fortitude.

## Combat Prowess & Wilderness Survival

Through lifelong conditioning in unforgiving mountain climes, Irklan warriors possess close-quarters combat skills, sensory reflexes, and endurance that consistently exceed standard Imperial military infantry benchmarks:
- Archival reports detail extraordinary combat efficiency in desert, mountain, and vacuum terrain.
- While deeply suspicious of outsiders, Irklan individuals occasionally contract their services as discreet wilderness guides, bodyguards, or private enforcers.

## Assassination Strikes in Starport Alpha

On Day 89-1105, an Irklan operative assassinated information broker [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] using a poisoned envenomed dart outside [[henrys-tavern|Henry's Tavern]] at [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] (Session 24). A subsequent Irklan strike team sent to eliminate [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] at Berth 17 was thwarted when [[asher|Asher]] experienced a precognitive warning from the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], enabling [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] to kill the Irklan assassin in a counter-ambush (Session 24).

## Martial Ethics and Witness Elimination

On Days 90–91-1105, Irklan cultural intermediary [[inkulan|Inkulan]] revealed foundational tenets of the sect's martial doctrine to [[sarik|Sarik]] at [[theos-bar|Theos Bar]] (Session 25). Irklan operatives view contracted assassinations solely as tests of martial skill and personal honor, operating strictly alone without clan-wide blood feuds (Session 25). Furthermore, Irklan assassins systematically eliminate any witnesses who share proximity to secrets they are hired to suppress, accounting for the ruthless slaughter of [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]]'s three starship crew members aboard ***[[cals-dream|Cal's Dream]]*** (Session 25).
