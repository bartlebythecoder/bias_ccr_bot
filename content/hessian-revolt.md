# Hessian Revolt

**Summary**: Major 11th-Century rebellion where House Gaval seceded from the Third Imperium and invaded [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] subsector, sparking interplanetary war between noble houses.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Outbreak & Rebellion

The Duke of Hess of House Gaval renounced Imperial allegiance and launched a surprise invasion into the [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] subsector (dated 1064 / 1069–1070) ([[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]]). The insurrection sought to capitalize on regional isolation before the Emperor could intervene.

## Miscalculations and Noble Realignment

House Gaval relied on several flawed strategic assumptions regarding rival houses:
- **House Aledon**: Gaval claimed an understanding that Aledon would secede; Aledon rejected this, declared war on Gaval, but guarded its own borders.
- **House Yetrina**: Expected to surrender immediately; instead, Yetrina mobilized and offered fierce resistance.
- **House Ilethian**: Expected to observe Imperial protocol and hold its fleet in Pleasant Hill; instead, Ilethian broke Imperial protocol by preemptively dispatching warships to fight alongside Yetrina.

## Climax and Legacy

The campaign culminated in the titanic [[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]], which broke House Gaval's invasion fleet and permanently altered regional balance of power ([[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]]).
