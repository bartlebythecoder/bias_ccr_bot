# Romentino

**Summary**: Hostile world 1428 with corrosive atmosphere, sub-zero cold, and 0.9G gravity, ruled by an oligopoly known as "[[the-banks|The Banks]]," centered on [[noavarite|Noavarite]] mining.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Planetary Characteristics & Atmosphere

Romentino (1428) is cataloged under UWP profile C7B2431-8 ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]):
- **Hostile Environment**: Orbits at 1.443 AU with a mass of 0.7 Earth, 0.9G surface gravity, an 18-hour day, and a year of 1.93 Earth years; lacks moons or rings.
- **Corrosive Air**: Atmospheric pressure is 0.6 atm with a corrosive, toxic chemistry; mean surface temperature is a frigid 199.69 K (-73°C), preventing outdoor activity without full sealed environmental suits.

## Governance and Major Settlements

The world is ruled by an oligarchic financial syndicate known as **"The Banks,"** fostering a cutthroat, frontier society:
- **Rosselini**: Planetary capital and principal urban center (pop ~20,000).
- **[[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]]**: Starport community (pop ~5,000) divided into green safety zones, GAS town, and Barrikday slums; thriving trade in illicit [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] (Session 10).
- **Morella**: Dedicated mining settlement (~500 residents) obliterated by terrorists in 1105 ([[news-1105-vol-5|News Dispatch — Volume 5]]).
- **Culture**: Celebrates "No! Day" annually on Day 45.

## Industry, Crime & Terror

- **[[noavarite|Noavarite]] Mining**: The economic lifeblood is mining Noavarite for interstellar jump fuel refining, dominated by [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]].
- **Red Hand Terror**: In 051-1105, anti-mining insurgents [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]] bombed the Morella outpost, triggering retaliatory security sweeps in Startown Liberty by Imperial Navy forces under [[commodore-koroleva|Commodore Koroleva]] ([[news-1105-vol-5|News Dispatch — Volume 5]]).
- **Ecological Hazards**: Suffered an invasive infestation of venomous [[csabaipede|csabaipedes]] from Csabai cargo in 1104 ([[csabaipede|Csabaipede]]). The frozen moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]] in this system harbors predatory [[mursa|Mursa]] behemoths, the corporate outpost [[kc-station|K.C. Station]], and the precursor [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]] (Sessions 1–5; [[mursa|Mursa]]).

- **LSP Corporate Security Interdiction**: In early 1105, [[ling-standard-products|LSP]] security units interdicted the shuttle landing pad following unauthorized incidents at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]], subjecting arriving personnel to electronic comms jamming and isolated interrogations (Session 9).
