# Kira

**Summary**: Self-aware survivor android from the Disassembly Chamber of Metropolis Station who allied with BIAS CCR.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Kira** is an intelligent, self-aware survivor android encountered by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] within the Disassembly Chamber on Level 2 of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Session 73).

## Discovery & Characteristics

Kira was discovered in the gruesome Disassembly Chamber, where massive automated arms butcher defective androids into scrap. Notably, she wore a morale patch inscribed *"Remember La Libertad"*, tying her past to the historic [[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]].

Unlike other station androids who fell victim to the culling or blindly served [[monarch|Monarch]], Kira understood Monarch's architectural and operational limitations and actively evaded disassembly. She explained the station's logistics to the crew:
- Successful androids who pass emulation trials are deactivated and transported upward via the **Goods Elevator**.
- The waste chutes are lethal execution conduits.
- Monarch harvests brain-scan telemetry from living divers to construct synthetic androids convinced of their own organic humanity.

## Alliance with BIAS CCR

[[beck|Beck]] invited Kira to join their escape from Metropolis Station. Kira guided the squad safely to **Assembly Overview**, where she accessed a secure terminal to retrieve a critical Video Data Chip housing ancient software algorithms used by Monarch to instill human self-delusion in androids.

Before unlocking the sealed blast door leading into **Final Testing**, Kira requested and received a solemn promise from [[isambard|Isambard]] and the crew to grant her permanent asylum and extract her off the station aboard the [[raconteur|Raconteur]].

## Final Testing & Descent to Cargo Loading (Session 74)

In Session 74 (Session 74):
- **Guiding through Final Testing**: Guided the crew through the chaotic Final Testing bay, warning them to avoid scanning arms and sorting pens that would dump biological humans down failure chutes.
- **Damien's Awakening Virus Lore**: Disclosed that the androids' cognitive confusion stemmed from an insurgent memory virus spread across Level 2 by [[damien|Damien]] to sabotage Monarch.
- **Armament**: In the Goods Elevator staging room, learned combat handling and took possession of a scavenged **Combat Shotgun** from the team's looted cache.
- **Elevator Descent**: Operated the cargo elevator controls to descend to the lower cargo-loading bay, and participated in the tense parley with holed-up [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] soldiers before heavy security drones attacked.

## Vacuum Suit Retrieval & Raconteur Evacuation (Session 75)

In Session 75 (Session 75):
- **Suit Courier Under Vacuum**: Acting as an agile courier across the depressurized Loading Dock, retrieved three spare vacuum suits from [[sky|Sky]] aboard the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] and delivered them back into the Goods Elevator airlock, enabling Beck, Isambard, and Asher to evacuate safely.
- **Boarding the Starship**: Evacuated aboard *The Raconteur*, securing her place alongside the crew as agreed.
- **Wipe Immunity**: Revealed to possess unique memory persistence and wipe immunity, making her invaluable to Commander Kilroy and BIAS CCR.

## Downtime Encampment, Sub-AI Architecture & Station Secrets (Session 76)

During Days 201–206 aboard *The Raconteur* in the Loading Dock (Session 76):
- **Social Integration**: Fully integrated into shipboard life, mastering board games like chess and poker while conversing with the crew and the simians.
- **Sub-AI Topology Intel**: Disclosed vital technical intelligence on station architecture, explaining that sub-AIs [[silas|Silas]] and [[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]] operate independently from Monarch's primary mainframe. Revealed that Monarch continuously consumes the majority of Metropolis's power and compute resources just to keep the Minotaur contained behind Level 2 bulkheads.
- **Station Lead Guidance**: Provided navigational context for station blueprints, detailing the Silas control terminal, secret hangar, micro-hangar, and the locked compartment concealing a nuclear warhead.

## Silas Farm Clarification & Infiltration Databank Incursion (Session 77)

In Session 77 (Session 77):
- **Silas Clarification**: Clarified to the crew that [[silas|Silas]] is not an alias for [[damien|Damien]], but an independent sub-AI dedicated specifically to supervising and managing the biological pseudo-flesh cultivation farms.
- **Assault Commitment & Gear**: Committed to the dangerous incursion into Micro-Hangar C-118 despite lingering apprehension; carried the laser cutting gear so that Sarik could maintain readiness with his heavy rocket launcher.
- **Identifying the Infiltration Databank**: In the zero-g computer nexus with heavy cabling, identified the chamber as the **Infiltration Personalities Databank**, where Monarch uploads archived biological brain scans into inert synthetic android husks (such as the [[exact-replica-of-the-duchess|Exact Replica of the Duchess]]).

## Evacuation, Protection from Conway, and Escape (Session 79)

In Session 79 (Session 79):
- **Retreat to Shuttle Bus**: Accompanied the squad during the rapid retreat through the compromised War Studies complex, navigating past proximity rocket booby traps and the psychic disturbance caused by [[renzo|Renzo]].
- **Micro-Hangar Laser Breach**: Aided the team as Sarik used the cutting laser to breach the sealed blast doors into Micro-Hangar C-118, securing the orbital shuttle bus.
- **Protection from Officer Conway**: When parleying with the corrupt security executive [[conway|Officer Conway]], Conway attempted to claim Kira as salvage/station property or an artifact to be confiscated. [[isambard|Isambard]] firmly shut down the demand, declaring Kira to be a fully recognized member of the crew under BIAS CCR protection.
- **Escape into Asteroid Belt**: Safely boarded the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] alongside the squad and the retrieved [[exact-replica-of-the-duchess|Exact Replica of the Duchess]], escaping into the uncharted asteroid belt coordinates just prior to the nuclear vaporization of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]].
