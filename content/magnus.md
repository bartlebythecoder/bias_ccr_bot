# Magnus

**Summary**: Scavenger-diver aboard [[the-bell|The Bell]] and survivor of [[saltpeter|the Saltpeter]] crew, which tore itself apart after finding a relic in Metropolis.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Magnus** is an independent scavenger and expedition diver operating out of **[[the-bell|The Bell]]**. He is a surviving crew member of the scavenger vessel **[[saltpeter|Saltpeter]]** alongside **[[lars|Lars]]**.

*(Session 62)*

---

## Background & Scavenging History
Magnus and his crew ventured into the depths of **[[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]** in search of high-value relics. During their deep incursion, the crew recovered an alien/station artifact that sparked catastrophic paranoia and madness, causing the crew to violently turn on each other until only Magnus and Lars were separated and scattered.

Magnus managed to escape back to *The Bell*, leaving his salvaged relics secured in **[[arkady|Arkady]]**'s safe while awaiting Lars's return.

---

## Interactions with the BIAS CCR Crew
- **Anxious Vigil for Lars**: Following the *BIAS CCR* crew's initial return from Metropolis in Session 62, Magnus approached them in zero-g frantically seeking news of Lars. When the crew recounted discovering a woman's body hung in the reception area, Magnus was overcome with grief and anxiety and retreated to his quarters.
- **Intelligence on Level 2 & Level 3**: In a later interview with **[[beck|Beck]]** and **[[sarik|Sarik]]**, Magnus provided crucial navigational lore regarding Metropolis:
  - Infiltrating **Level 3** requires passing through the android society on **Level 2**.
  - The droids on Level 2 are split into two rival factions: **[[the-chosen|The Chosen]]** and **[[the-fallen|The Fallen]]**.
  - Both factions are ruled by the **[[child-king|Child King]]**, an adult android suffering the delusion that it is human, notorious for unpredictable cruelty and an obsession with outsiders.
  - Magnus endured a tense interrogation by the Child King before being allowed access to a hidden vent leading down to Level 3.
  - On Level 3, Magnus's party was ambushed in the dark by aggressive security automatons.
  - **The Minotaur Warning**: Magnus explicitly warned the crew never to mention the name "Minotaur" while on Level 2, as uttering it results in immediate summary execution by the droids.
  - He pleaded with the party to relay a message to Lars if found, reassuring Lars that he would never leave *The Bell* without him (unaware that Lars had suffered a mental collapse and was shot by Arkady).
- **The Bends & The Child's Photograph (Session 65)**: In an emotional interview on Day 190 with **[[isambard|Isambard]]** and **[[sarik|Sarik]]**, a trembling Magnus confessed that he had not dared enter the station for three weeks. He described BIAS CCR as his last hope for recovering Lars. Magnus warned them against trusting the "half-robot" **[[ghost-eater|Ghost Eater]]** and revealed that **[[noriko|Noriko]]** had become religiously devoted to **[[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]]**. Furthermore, Magnus revealed a faded physical photograph of a young child that he had salvaged from a ventilation duct on a previous dive. Arkady later diagnosed Magnus as suffering from severe station-induced psychological trauma ("The Bends") and recommended the crew take him far away from the system when they leave.

## Relationships
- **[[lars|Lars]]**: Partner and fellow survivor of the *Saltpeter*; Magnus remains steadfastly loyal to him.
- **[[arkady|Arkady]]**: Stationmaster of *The Bell*, who safeguards Magnus's recovered artifacts in his vault and diagnosed his "Bends."
- **[[the-chosen|The Chosen]] & [[the-fallen|The Fallen]]**: Droid factions Magnus navigated on Level 2.
- **[[child-king|Child King]]**: Android ruler who interrogated Magnus.
- **[[ghost-eater|Ghost Eater]] & [[noriko|Noriko]]**: Fellow residents of *The Bell* whom Magnus deeply distrusts.
