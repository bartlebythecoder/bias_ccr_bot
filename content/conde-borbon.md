# Conde Borbon

**Summary**: Independent narcotics dealer on [[freeport|Freeport]] tied to both the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] and the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]], sought by several rival parties.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**Conde Borbon** is an underworld figure operating in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system, linked through investigative intelligence to both the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] and the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]] on [[freeport|Freeport]] (Sessions 47–48).

---

## Operational History

### Cryptic Tip & The Mercenary Race (Day 131-1105)
On Day 131-1105, during the unfolding investigation into the assassination of [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]], an anonymous tipster sent a cryptic query to [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]: *"Where is Conde Borbon?"* (Session 47). Immediately afterward, rival mercenary unit [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] messaged the operatives, implying they had already located him.

Investigative reporter [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]] later confirmed that Borbon held direct organizational ties linking the Meinhoff Group with the Primero Cartel, connecting him directly to the vessel ***[[belladonna|Belladonna]]*** docked in Freeport's starport (Session 48).

### The Stardust Casino Contract & True Target Revelation (Day 132-1105)
On Day 132-1105, rogue telepath [[julie|Julie]] sent an encrypted message claiming that [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]], proprietor of the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]], had attempted to contract her to assassinate Conde Borbon (Session 50). 

Investigating in Jonestown, [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] interviewed Borbon's close friend [[rico-mathers|Rico Mathers]] at the Homestead Apartments. Rico confirmed that Borbon had split from the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]] to distribute narcotics independently, and reported that an operative in an orange suit (suspected to be [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]]) had already been hunting for him (Session 50).

Simultaneously, [[asher|Asher]] analyzed surveillance footage outside [[club-strata|Club Strata]] from the night of [[louise-soto|Louise Soto's]] murder. Asher discovered that Conde Borbon had been standing directly in front of Soto at the exact instant the bullet struck, demonstrating that Borbon was the actual intended target of the assassination and Soto was tragic collateral damage (Session 50).
