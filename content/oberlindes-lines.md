# Oberlindes Lines

**Summary**: Major interstellar shipping line in the Kidderminster subsector, established post-Hessian Revolt by Roxanne Hault Oberlindes and now majority-owned by SuSAG.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Post-War Origins

Oberlindes Lines was founded in the aftermath of the Hessian Revolt by Kidderminster commercial magnate **Roxanne Hault Oberlindes**.
- In the wake of the war, numerous subsector worlds were cut off from interstellar supply chains.
- Oberlindes acquired surplus Imperial naval transport vessels at low cost, rapidly creating an expansive logistics network to supply isolated planets with trade goods.

## Megacorporate Acquisition

While the Oberlindes dynasty retains substantial equity, operating control was acquired by megacorporation [[susag|SuSAG]], which maintains majority ownership and integrates the fleet into its pharmaceutical and chemical distribution channels ([[susag|SuSAG]]).
