# Hexham Mining

**Summary**: An extraction enterprise on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] that partnered with [[youst-refining|Youst Refining]] to resolve long-standing refining bottlenecks.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Hexham Mining Inc is a primary resource extraction company based on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] (1425) ([[news-1105-vol-5.1|News Dispatch — Volume 5.1]]).

## Deal with Youst Refining

Historically hampered by internal processing delays and transport inefficiencies when attempting to refine its own ore, Hexham concluded a multi-million-credit agreement in 070-1105 naming [[youst-refining|Youst Refining]] as its sole refinery for all mining output, effectively outsourcing all downstream refining ([[news-1105-vol-5.1|News Dispatch — Volume 5.1]]).
