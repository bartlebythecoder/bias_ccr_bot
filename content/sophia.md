# Sophia

**Summary**: Field Support supervisor at K.C. Station on Mirassol who was coerced by Blue Lou Boyle to breach the Ancient pyramid, currently missing.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Station Role

Sophia Baker served as the Field Support supervisor (Session 12) at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on the frozen moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]], directing surface operations, outdoor maintenance expeditions, and overseeing field workers including operative [[sarik|Sarik]] (Sessions 1–5).

## Coercion & Disappearance

Sophia was covertly contacted and coerced by the infamous off-world criminal boss [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]] (Sessions 1–5). Boyle pressured Sophia and station mechanic [[liam|Liam]] to break into the buried [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]] to extract valuable [[the-ancients|Ancient]] artifacts (Sessions 1–5).

Following an illicit entry into the site, Liam was killed under unconfirmed circumstances, and Sophia vanished completely, prompting an emergency response and triggering a corporate investigation (Sessions 1–5).

## Quarters Search & Recovered Gear

Following her disappearance into the [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]], Sophia's living quarters at K.C. Station were covertly burglarized by operative [[isambard|Isambard]], who recovered her locked portable safe, a thud (needle) gun, an electronic tracker monitoring an exterior ATV crawler, and Cr 10,000 in cash (Session 8).

## Decrypted Transmissions

Following the recovery of her cyber-deck, decryption by [[asher|Asher]] revealed an urgent message from an off-world handler ordering Sophia to *"not waste anymore time and do whatever she has to do to go into the ancient pyramid"* (Session 12).

## Secret Romance & Elopement with Eminshi Moth

Comprehensive decryption of Sophia's computer on Day 044-1105 confirmed that her criminal handler was Red Hand captain [[eminshi-moth|Eminshi Moth]], who was also her romantic partner (Session 15). The pair had planned to elope together once the [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]] was looted (Session 15). Before disappearing, Sophia had alerted Moth to [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[isambard|Isambard]]'s true identities, sending surveillance photos that sparked Moth's revenge vendetta against the crew (Session 15).
