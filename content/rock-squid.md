# Rock Squid

![[rock-squid.png|481]]

**Summary**: Colossal carnivorous ambush predator native to [[tutin|Tutin]], 20 to 40 metres long with specimens reported at up to 100 metres, which mimics a boulder while motionless and strikes at prey that comes close.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-27

---

## Biology & Behavior

The rock squid is an elongated creature. Its tentacles extend from around a lamprey-like mouth, and it has four black eyes, each roughly two feet in diameter.

It is a carnivore and an ambush hunter. When completely still, the creature's camouflage makes it appear as a large boulder, and it will wait patiently in that posture for prey to approach before striking.

## Habitat & Range

The rock squid is native to [[tutin|Tutin]] (1624), one of only two breathable worlds in the subsector. Tutin has been administered by the [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] government as a restricted nature reserve since the [[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]] of 1077–1083.

## Human Interaction

The rock squid is dangerous to humans. Its size and its habit of lying in wait while disguised as terrain make it a hazard to anyone moving through its territory on foot or by vehicle.

- Typical adults measure 20 to 40 metres in length.
- Specimens of up to 100 metres have been reported, though such reports are unconfirmed.

## Hunting Permits

Visitors to [[tutin|Tutin]] can apply for permits to hunt rock squid near the Sundance colony; applications can take a day or two to clear (Session 82).
