# Solon

**Summary**: Elite cybernetic surgeon in the Icebox on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] who fitted [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]] with an illegal black box.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Solon** is an elite, high-end cybernetic surgeon operating a fortified private medical clinic in [[eternity-station#Key Sectors & Black Markets|The Icebox]] on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] (Session 41).

## Standoff with BIAS CCR & Black Box Installation
On Day 119-1105, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] approached Solon's clinic following a referral from street technician [[ricardo|Ricardo]] (Session 41). Solon initially dismissed the operatives, demanding a massive upfront financial deposit. Refusing to leave, [[isambard|Isambard]] forced entry past the clinic door, prompting an armed standoff.

Faced with the team's combat readiness, Solon capitulated and revealed that he had performed an illegal surgical procedure on gambler [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]], installing an illicit **"black box" neural module** (Session 41). The device provided Sargon with total photographic recall of everything observed at the card tables, giving him an unfair mathematical advantage. The operatives copied Solon's confidential medical records and procedure logs before disabling the clinic alarms and departing (Session 41).
