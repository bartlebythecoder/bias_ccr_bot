# K.C. Station

**Summary**: Research and industrial outpost on the frozen moon Mirassol, organized into the Redshed, Workshop, and Science Building.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Layout & Facilities

K.C. Station is a proprietary corporate research and industrial outpost operated by [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] (Session 9), established on the frigid moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]] in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system (Sessions 1–5). Because of the sub-zero temperatures and thin, low-oxygen atmosphere, station operations are strictly partitioned into three pressurized indoor complexes, connected by transit tunnels:

1. **The Redshed**: The primary habitat hub containing living quarters, common dining, recreation halls, and the station medical infirmary. Operative [[beck|Beck]] was embedded here as medical personnel under Chief Medical Officer Dr. [[elena-dumont|Elena Dumont]] and Base Manager [[tricia-schelling|Tricia Schelling]] (Sessions 1–6).
2. **The Workshop**: Mechanical, plumbing, and electronic engineering bay dedicated to machinery maintenance and vehicle servicing. Operative [[isambard|Isambard]] was stationed here alongside mechanic [[liam|Liam]] (Sessions 1–5).
3. **The Science Building**: High-security research facility housing specialized laboratories and computational equipment. Operative [[asher|Asher]] worked here as a chemical researcher (Sessions 1–5).

## Field Support Division

Exterior expeditions, environmental surveys, and surface maintenance are managed by Field Support. Operative [[sarik|Sarik]] worked as a laborer in this division under supervisor [[sophia|Sophia]] (Sessions 1–5).

## Espionage & Incidents

In 1105, a four-person covert team hired by [[cooper|Cooper]] infiltrated the station to retrieve telemetry from a nearby downed battle cruiser (Sessions 1–5). The deployment was disrupted by an external [[mursa|Mursa]] attack, the illicit exploration of the [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]] by supervisor [[sophia|Sophia]] and mechanic [[liam|Liam]] under coercion by [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]], resulting in Liam's death, Sophia's disappearance, and an imminent investigation by corporate security (Sessions 1–5).

## Spider Incursions & Total Evacuation

In Day 44-1105, following the deployment of corporate security investigators, K.C. Station suffered catastrophic nightly swarms of indigenous giant spiders ([[mirassol-spider|Mirassol Spiders]]) (Session 17). After scientist [[george-castle|George Castle]] was attacked and rescued by maintenance plumber [[mancuso|Mancuso]], facility management ordered an emergency, permanent evacuation, leaving K.C. Station completely abandoned (Session 17).

## Infiltration by The Doves

Debriefs with LSP Corporate Security on Day 93-1105 revealed that Base Scientist Dr. Elena Dumont was secretly an embedded spy for [[the-doves|The Doves]], conducting unauthorized precursor archaeology reconnaissance before fleeing into hiding on [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] (Session 28).
