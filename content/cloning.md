# Cloning and Memory Units

**Summary**: Biological replication technologies in Sector Solo-6, spanning frowned-upon natural cloning, dangerous fast-cloning, and fatal experimental memory transfer units.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Cloning Technologies

- **Traditional Cloning**: Viable across high-tech worlds, though socially frowned upon. Clones are gestated and develop through standard maturation, but consistently experience shorter lifespans and elevated susceptibility to degenerative diseases.
- **Fast Cloning**: Theoretical rapid growth producing a fully formed adult without infancy or childhood.
  - Requires Tech Level A infrastructure minimum and vast capital expenditure.
  - Products suffer severe biological breakdown within one year, spending their brief lives plagued by physical infirmity and violent psychosis.

## Superconducting Memory Units (SMU)

Superconducting Memory Units represent an experimental technology designed to record, store, and digitally transfer memories, acquired skills, and human personalities between individuals or clones. Every recorded attempt has ended in catastrophe, with both donors and recipients dying or descending into irreversible insanity shortly after the neural procedure.

## Alternatives

Due to the extreme ethical, biological, and technical failure rates of cloning and AI robotics, corporations like [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] have turned to biological uplifting of simians as obedient organic labor ([[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]]).

## Romentino Mining Clone Allegations

In 1105, [[real-red-hand|Real Red Hand]] labor leader [[tres-capulet|Tres Capulet]] alleged that planetary mining conglomerates on [[romentino|Romentino]] are illegally cultivating fast-clones or biological duplicates of deceased miners to maintain an indentured, non-unionized workforce (Session 14). Capulet supported his claim by presenting an active prospector who is an exact biological match for a friend confirmed dead two years earlier (Session 14).

- **Morella Station Clones**: Infiltration of [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on Day 47-1105 confirmed an operational clone program replacing dead miners, evidenced by prospector [[danny-conti|Danny Conti]] (who appeared on the station's official dead employee list) and a clone concealed in the machinery room of mechanic [[karl-jurgens|Karl Jurgens]] (Session 17).

- **The Erebus Power Breakthrough**: In Day 47-1105, geneticist [[dr-eva-smart|Dr. Eva Smart]] at [[morella-station|Morella Station]] claimed to have successfully solved the terminal problem of fast-clone psychosis, enabling the rapid cultivation of adult clones without psychological collapse, bankrolled by [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]] (Session 18).
