# The Cube

**Summary**: Anti-AI virus created by Blavatsky to destroy Monarch, hidden on Level 4 of Metropolis Station.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**The Cube** is a specialized cyber-warfare software virus and anti-AI weapon engineered specifically to attack and dismantle the rogue artificial intelligence [[monarch|Monarch]] inside [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]].

## Origins & Location
- **Development by Blavatsky**: The virus was originally programmed by the scientist [[blavatsky|Blavatsky]] during an earlier research or military expedition into Metropolis.
- **Current Custody on Level 4**: According to naval resistance leader [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]], The Cube remains stored on **Level 4** of Metropolis in connection with an individual named [[damien|Damien]].
- **Operational Objective**: In Session 68, the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] strike team prioritized securing The Cube from Level 4 as their foundational weapon before confronting Monarch's mainframe core on Level 5/6.
