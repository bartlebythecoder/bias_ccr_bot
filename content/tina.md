# Tina

**Summary**: Street-level Starlight dealer in Startown Liberty coerced by Joey into smuggling narcotics to Nesvorny; confidante and ally of Asher.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Street Background & Identity

Tina (also encountered in street transactions under the name [[lisa|Lisa]]) is an independent dealer trafficking the illicit hallucinogen [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] across [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Sessions 10, 13). Born into severe local poverty, she viewed herself as trapped in Startown's criminal ecosystem (Session 13).

## Connection with Asher

On Day 43-1105, Tina met with operative [[asher|Asher]] of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] (Session 13). Recognizing Asher's intellect and hearing his personal history escaping severe poverty on [[pharr|Pharr]] to become a chemist, Tina developed deep trust in him and shared critical intelligence (Session 13):
- Identified [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]] as a habitual Starlight customer crushed by Cr 500,000 in debts to mob boss [[eminshi-moth|Eminshi Moth]] (Session 13).
- Disclosed that the [[real-red-hand|Real Red Hand]] was infiltrating mining labor negotiations on Romentino (Session 13).

## The Nesvorny Smuggling Coercion

Tina disclosed that rogue Red Hand soldier [[joey|Joey]] had threatened her life to force her to act as an off-world drug mule, smuggling Starlight to [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] (Session 13). Terrified of execution by [[eminshi-moth|Eminshi Moth]] for violating the syndicate export ban, and equally terrified of long-term Imperial Navy imprisonment, she accepted Asher's promise of protection (Session 13).

## The Aborted Smuggling Run & Rescue

On Day 044-1105, Tina was forced into a vacc suit by [[joey|Joey]] and his brother [[bobby|Bobby]] with packages of [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] taped to her torso, forced to trek across the freezing, corrosive exterior of [[romentino|Romentino]] to breach the starport (Session 16). Having never worn a vacc suit, Tina panicked and collapsed in the wilderness (Session 16). Operative [[sarik|Sarik]] dispatched Bobby with a blade to abort the run, and the team carried Tina through an abandoned airlock into safety, stripping off the narcotics and sheltering her in their Noble Quarter apartment (Session 16).

## Condo Raid and Departure for Csabai

On Day 47-1105, while recovering in BIAS CCR's Noble Quarter condo, Tina was swept up in a police raid conducted by [[the-banks|The Banks]] following [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]' corporate espionage complaints (Session 19). She was interrogated alongside [[sky|Sky]] and [[sydney|Sydney]] before planetary authorities released them (Session 19). Guided by Sky, Tina was escorted to [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s luxury yacht berthed at the starport, departing safely into orbit bound for [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 19).

## Romance with Asher and Crew Friction

During the Jump voyage aboard [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s yacht to [[csabai|Csabai]], Tina engaged in a romantic relationship with [[asher|Asher]] (Session 20). Her extensive interactions with the ship's married male engineer simultaneously sparked jealousy and marital tension with the vessel's female pilot (Session 20).

## Arrival on Csabai and Cryptic Departure Note

On Day 56-1105, upon arriving at the Csabai starport, Tina and [[sky|Sky]] introduced a potential client seeking cyber-surveillance against [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] (Session 21). Immediately following the interaction, Tina vanished into the starport crowds, leaving a warning note for [[asher|Asher]] cautioning the operatives that Sky hid a secret inside her pink valise, that the prospective patron was unhinged, and that Lord Baltimore was far more dangerous than he appeared (Session 21).

## Aiding Sarik with Interrogation Drugs and Irklan Contacts

On Day 92-1105, Tina rendezvoused with [[sarik|Sarik]] on [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] (Session 26). She provided technical advisement on pharmaceutical interrogation compounds and collaborated with Sarik to identify an underworld go-between responsible for brokering [[irklan|Irklan]] assassination contracts on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 26).

## Unmasking the Irklan Broker

On Day 92-1105, Tina utilized her streetwise connections on [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] to identify [[reshka|Reshka]], an ethnic-[[irklan|Irklan]] starship mechanic who brokered outside contracts, enabling [[sarik|Sarik]] to purchase intelligence identifying the Psalmist client behind the assassin (Session 27).
