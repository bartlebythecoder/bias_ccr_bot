# Mosh

**Summary**: Volatile [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] resident linked to [[katarin|Katarin]] who became a prime suspect in [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]]'s murder.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Mosh** is an aggressive resident on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] associated with [[katarin|Katarin]] (Session 41).

## Confrontation in Section 2
On Day 119-1105, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] arrived outside Katarin's apartment in Section 2, overhearing a heated argument between Katarin and Mosh inside (Session 41). When [[asher|Asher]] stepped toward the entrance, Mosh barged out and confronted the operatives with open hostility before abruptly departing down the corridor (Session 41).

## Suspect in Sargon's Murder & Guilt Confirmed
Following the violent death of [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]] later that day, Mosh emerged as a prime murder suspect due to his volatile temper and relationship with Katarin (Session 42). Atmospheric telemetry recovered from station oxygen tracking logs ("O2 stick logs") verified that Mosh and Katarin were present in the immediate hallway outside Sargon's apartment during the exact window of the murder (Session 42).

Mosh's culpability was conclusively proven when [[asher|Asher]] successfully decrypted Sargon's neural "black box" memory module (Session 43). The decrypted sensory logs captured Mosh physically confronting Sargon in his quarters and brutally beating him to death following a violent struggle (Session 43).
