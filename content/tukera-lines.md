# Tukera Lines

**Summary**: An Imperial Megacorporation dominating interstellar passenger shipping, freight transport, and subsector commerce.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Tukera Lines is an Imperial Megacorporation holding extensive transport, shipping, and security operations throughout the Imperium. It operates a massive fleet of passenger liners and commercial freighters running along the established Imperial xboat communication lanes, and enjoys a virtual monopoly over interstellar passenger transit across multiple subsectors.

- **Noble Shareholders**: [[house-liashay|House Liashay]] (18%), [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]] (8%), [[house-tauribi|House Tauribi]] (8%), and [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]] (1%) ([[house-liashay|House Liashay]]; [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]]; [[house-tauribi|House Tauribi]]; [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]).

## Operations in Subsector J

The corporation maintains an entrenched presence on the subsector capital of [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] (1226). The Subsector President of Tukera Lines is married to [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]], the world's noble ruler ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).

Despite this strong political connection, Tukera Lines suffered an unexpected commercial setback in 170-1105 when the Kidderminster Navy awarded its 'Senior Operations Initiative' defense contract to rival megacorp [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], causing Tukera stock on the Arico exchange to drop 5.5 points to 97.5 ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
