# Csabai Starport Alpha

**Summary**: Primary commercial starport terminal on Csabai, where fired palace security chief Morgan was tracked down by Isambard.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview & Location

Csabai Starport Alpha is a bustling high-tech transportation terminal serving orbital transit and interplanetary commerce on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Sessions 22–23). Located adjacent to major urban administrative sectors and transit corridors, Starport Alpha handles both commercial passenger liners and private luxury vessels (Sessions 22–23).

## The Morgan Interrogation

On Day 57-1105, following the theft of the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] from [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]], disgraced former head of security [[morgan|Morgan]] retreated to Csabai Starport Alpha after being summarily fired by butler [[sterling|Sterling]] (Sessions 22–23). Operative [[isambard|Isambard]] tracked Morgan to the starport terminal, successfully interrogating him and extracting the crucial surveillance insight regarding automated camera shut-off timestamps that ultimately enabled [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] to solve the theft (Sessions 22–23).

---

## Berth 17 and Henry's Tavern Incidents

On Day 89-1105, Starport Alpha became the site of a lethal intelligence struggle: broker [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] was assassinated outside [[henrys-tavern|Henry's Tavern]], and an armed confrontation took place at Berth 17 where [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] eliminated an [[irklan|Irklan]] assassin following an [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] precognitive warning (Session 24).

## The Murder Inquiries on Cal's Dream and Theos Bar

On Days 90–91-1105, Starport Alpha's constabulary under [[lt-tony-mcintyre|Lt. Tony McIntyre]] sealed Berth 17 as a triple-homicide crime scene aboard ***[[cals-dream|Cal's Dream]]*** (Session 25). Concurrently, [[theos-bar|Theos Bar]] became the site of cultural negotiations between [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[irklan|Irklan]] go-between [[inkulan|Inkulan]] (Session 25).
