# Thomas

**Summary**: Articulate uplifted simian freed from [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]]'s [[suliman|Suliman]] labs who chose to stay with BIAS CCR.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Thomas** is an articulate uplifted simian engineered by the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation on [[suliman|Suliman]] under [[project-chimera|Project Chimera]] who joined [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] following the core laboratories breakout (Session 40).

## Liberation and Choosing to Stay
On Day 117-1105, Thomas was among the twelve captive uplifted simians liberated from the holding pens of the Suliman core laboratories by BIAS CCR. Fleeing aboard a stolen launch into the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]], the operatives arrived at [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] (Session 40). When an underground contact took custody of ten primates to guide them to safety, Thomas and [[isaiah|Isaiah]] chose to remain with the BIAS CCR team.

## Operations on Eternity Station
On Day 118-1105, Thomas accompanied the operatives through **The Icebox** to the **Stellar Burn** entertainment complex (Session 40). During the Cr 10,000 high-stakes poker game aimed at exposing suspected [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff]] card cheat [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]], Thomas stood watch at a distance alongside [[asher|Asher]], monitoring the room dynamics and providing security while [[isambard|Isambard]] and Isaiah engaged Sargon at the table (Session 40).

## Raid on Jollynn Estate & Eliminating Richard (Sessions 56–57)
On the night of Day 133-1105, Thomas formed part of the tactical assault squad storming Marie Orslo Jollynn's mansion (Sessions 56–57). Inside, Thomas killed an interior guard who had wounded Sky. Later, when chief bodyguard Richard ([[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]]) entered the foyer, Thomas grappled Richard's gun arm while Sky drove a knife into his neck, silently incapacitating him before he was terminated. Thomas also assisted in snapping the necks of two unconscious balcony guards and concealing their bodies (Sessions 56–57).

## Formal Retainer Contract & Project Metropolis Mission (Session 58)
During the seven-week sabbatical on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], Thomas and Isaiah finalized permanent employment contracts with [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]], guaranteeing them a fixed base salary and hazard pay for dangerous missions (Session 58). Thomas deployed aboard the refitted starship on its 3.5-day transit to infiltrate [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 58).
