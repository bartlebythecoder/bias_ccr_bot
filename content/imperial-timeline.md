# Imperial History Timeline

**Summary**: Chronological history of Sector Solo-6, tracking emigration waves, tech level milestones, the establishment of subsector duchies, and major interplanetary wars.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Chronological Eras of Expansion

- **< 0001 — Emigration Wave 0**: Early human colonization utilizing proto-jump hyperdrives and Non-Atmospheric Flying and Landing (NAFAL) sleeper ships. Survival habitats established; peak Tech Level 9.
- **0001 — Wave 1**: Introduction of safe Jump-1 interstellar travel and true gravitics technology; peak Tech Level A.
- **0245 — Wave 2**: The Federation era; integration of safe Jump-2 networks.
- **0800 — Wave 3**: Third Imperium expansion; establishment of reliable safe Jump-3 communications and commerce.
- **1011 — Imperial Subsector Boundaries**: Official demarcation of Sector Solo-6 subsector borders and establishment of the sixteen [[noble-houses|Subsector Duchies]]; peak Tech Level D.

## Major Regional Wars and Crises

- **1069–1070 — [[hessian-revolt|Hessian Revolt]]**: Secessionist uprising of House Gaval, culminating in the decisive 1064 [[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]] fleet clash ([[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]]).
- **1077–1084 — [[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]]**: Kidderminster military campaign crushing the Order of the Psalms, marked by the 1082 Varos Offensive; peak Tech Level E ([[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]]).
- **1089–1090 — [[sanlucar-civil-war|Sanlucar Civil War]]**: Destructive near-vacuum war on Sanlucar de Barram resulting in the annihilation of the Aridush Khanate ([[sanlucar-civil-war|Sanlucar Civil War]]).
- **1105 — [[sanlucar-de-barram|The Sanlucar Crisis]]**: Revolutionary overthrow of the Valentian Republic by the Aridush Freedom Fighters; peak Tech Level F+.
