# House Alkalikoi

**Summary**: The Imperial Royal House, holding the throne of the Third Imperium, with substantial equity across the major megacorporations.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-26

---

## Imperial Throne

House Alkalikoi sits on the Imperial throne, representing the royal crown of the Third Imperium ([[noble-houses|Noble Houses]]). Alongside [[house-aledon|House Aledon]] and [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]], it forms the Three Great Houses that dominate Imperial politics and commerce.

## Megacorporate Holdings

- **[[susag|SuSAG]]**: 22% equity ([[susag|SuSAG]]).
- **[[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]**: 8% equity ([[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]).
- **[[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]]**: 8% equity ([[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]]).
