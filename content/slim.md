# Slim

**Summary**: Regular at the [[desperado-karaoke-bar|Desperado Karaoke Bar]] in [[hampton|Hampton]] who warned [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] about Redshirt spies and gave [[asher|Asher]] directions to [[deborah|Deborah]]'s home.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

---

## Warnings about Spies

Slim is a man the crew met at the [[desperado-karaoke-bar|Desperado Karaoke Bar]] in [[hampton|Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]]. On Day 230-1105 he told [[sarik|Sarik]] that Redshirt spies were listening everywhere in town, some of them in the spa (Session 82). Later that evening, with uniformed Redshirt Navy among the patrons, he warned the crew again that spies were everywhere (Session 83).

## The Poker Game (Day 231-1105)

At the poker game [[isambard|Isambard]] set up at the Desperado, Slim confirmed what the crew had heard about [[deborah|Deborah]]: she cleans wealthy ranches, lives north of town and usually visits him when she comes in for supplies. He gave [[asher|Asher]] a contact number and directions to her place, and agreed the crew could mention his name if they meant to hire her (Session 83).
