# Arico Banks

**Summary**: The three banking houses that control the finances of the Arico El Nuevo system, all headquartered in the Humboldt District of Freeport.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-26

---

## Overview

All three major banks controlling the finances of [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] are headquartered in the Humboldt District on [[freeport|Freeport]], the system's capital Torus station.

## The Three Banks

- **Tagarro Trust**: A wholly owned subsidiary of megacorporation [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]].
- **Montanes Financial**: A wholly owned financial subsidiary of genetics corporation [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]].
- **[[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]]**: An independent, publicly traded institution, though the majority of its shares were held by its namesake, Marie [[orslo-jollynn|Orslo Jollynn]].
