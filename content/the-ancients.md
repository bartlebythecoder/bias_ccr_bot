# The Ancients

**Summary**: Enigmatic precursor species inhabiting Sector Solo-6 over 300,000 years ago whose cataclysmic wars created the La Libertad asteroid belt; artifacts remain strictly regulated by the IIS.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Precursor History & Ruins

The term "Ancients" denotes an unknown number of non-human, hyper-advanced alien civilizations that dominated Sector Solo-6 more than **300,000 years ago**:
- **The La Libertad Cataclysm**: Astronomical surveys indicate that the [[battle-of-liberty|La Libertad (0926) Asteroid Belt]] was shattered into being during an apocalyptic prehistoric naval war between conflicting Ancient fleets.
- **Archaeological Excavations**: In the Kidderminster subsector, Ancient ruins are being excavated under high secrecy by classified Imperial Intelligence Service (IIS) archaeological teams, while black-market treasure hunters illegally plunder unrecorded sites.

- **The Mirassol Pyramid**: A subterranean precursor pyramid situated beneath the permafrost of [[mirassol|Mirassol]] in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system, which became the target of black-market plundering by [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]] in 1105 (Sessions 1–5).

## Prohibited Artifacts & Synthetic Risks

Imperial law strictly mandates that any discovery of Ancient sites or artifacts must be reported immediately to the IIS:
- Illicit experiments combining Ancient alien devices with human computer or neural architectures are suspected of causing the rogue-AI disaster at Wu-Ketai's [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] ([[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]).
