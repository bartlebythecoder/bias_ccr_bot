# Tora Takanabe

**Summary**: Cold, calculating President of [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]] on [[freeport|Freeport]], once kidnapped by [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Tora Takanabe** is the President of [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]] on [[freeport|Freeport]], serving as the primary operational executive under CEO and majority owner [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn]] (Session 55; [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]]).

## Profile & Reputation

Within Arico financial circles, Takanabe is regarded as a cold, unemotional, and calculating corporate executive. While popular with institutional investors and shareholders for his fiscal rigor, he is widely disliked by junior bank staff and union dockworkers.

## Abduction & Escape from Hunter's Heroes

In late Day 133-1105, rival mercenary outfit [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]]—seeking leverage against the [[the-stratemeyers|Stratemeyer Syndicate]]—kidnapped Takanabe and held him at an undisclosed holding site on Freeport (Session 55). 

During the charity gala at the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]], Hunter's Heroes received an emergency alert. Overhearing their panicked argument, [[sarik|Sarik]] learned that Takanabe had managed to break free and escape from his captors, throwing Hunter's Heroes' operations into disarray and escalating the crisis surrounding Jollynn Trust (Session 55).

## Role as Syndicate Conduit & Conscience Informant ("The President")

Following the execution of Marie Orslo Jollynn, decrypted files from her personal mobile phone exposed Takanabe's central role in the [[the-stratemeyers|Stratemeyer]] conspiracy (Sessions 56–57):
- **Syndicate Relay**: Operating under the alias **"The President,"** Takanabe had functioned as Jollynn's principal executive go-between, relaying operational orders and laundering corporate funds directly to field manager [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]].
- **Crisis of Conscience**: Stricken by conscience regarding the syndicate's escalating bloodshed and planned assassinations, Takanabe had turned whistleblower, anonymously tipping off planetary police about impending violence before being targeted by Hunter's Heroes and fleeing into hiding (Sessions 56–57).
