# Marius Hope

**Summary**: Chief of Security at [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] responsible for internal surveillance and facility enforcement.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Security Command

Marius Hope is the Head of Security at [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on [[romentino|Romentino]], commanding the station's armed guard force and monitoring internal electronic surveillance systems (Session 17). 

On Day 47-1105, Hope escorted Rig Manager [[gunther-charm|Gunther Charm]] to receive the undercover [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] operatives (Session 17). Security forces placed electronic listening bugs within the operatives' assigned quarters, which were promptly detected and neutralized by operative [[sarik|Sarik]] using an electronic bug sweeper (Session 17).
