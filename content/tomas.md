# Tomas

**Summary**: Articulate uplifted simian freed from [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]]'s [[suliman|Suliman]] labs who chose to stay with [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] alongside [[isaiah|Isaiah]].

**Last updated**: 2026-10-27

---

**Tomas** is an articulate uplifted simian engineered by the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation on [[suliman|Suliman]] under [[project-chimera|Project Chimera]] who joined [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] following the core laboratories breakout (Session 40).

## Liberation and Choosing to Stay
On Day 117-1105, Tomas was among the twelve captive uplifted simians liberated from the holding pens of the Suliman core laboratories by BIAS CCR. Fleeing aboard a stolen launch into the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]], the operatives arrived at [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] (Session 40). When an underground contact took custody of ten primates to guide them to safety, Tomas and [[isaiah|Isaiah]] chose to remain with the BIAS CCR team.

## Operations on Eternity Station
On Day 118-1105, Tomas accompanied the operatives through **The Icebox** to the **Stellar Burn** entertainment complex (Session 40). During the Cr 10,000 high-stakes poker game aimed at exposing suspected [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff]] card cheat [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]], Tomas stood watch at a distance alongside [[asher|Asher]], monitoring the room dynamics and providing security while [[isambard|Isambard]] and Isaiah engaged Sargon at the table (Session 40).

## Raid on Jollynn Estate & Eliminating Richard (Sessions 56–57)
On the night of Day 133-1105, Tomas formed part of the tactical assault squad storming Marie Orslo Jollynn's mansion (Sessions 56–57). Inside, Tomas killed an interior guard who had wounded Sky. Later, when chief bodyguard Richard ([[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]]) entered the foyer, Tomas grappled Richard's gun arm while Sky drove a knife into his neck, silently incapacitating him before he was terminated. Tomas also assisted in snapping the necks of two unconscious balcony guards and concealing their bodies (Sessions 56–57).

## Formal Retainer Contract & Project Metropolis Mission (Session 58)
During the seven-week sabbatical on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], Tomas and Isaiah finalized permanent employment contracts with [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]], guaranteeing them a fixed base salary and hazard pay for dangerous missions (Session 58). Tomas deployed aboard the refitted starship on its 3.5-day transit to infiltrate [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 58).

## Crew Training & The Cove (Session 80)
After the fall of Metropolis, [[isambard|Isambard]] suggested training Tomas, Isaiah and [[kira|Kira]] to help crew a larger ship, and taught the simians blackjack during the flight across the Arico belts (Session 80). Tomas accompanied the crew into [[the-cove|The Cove]], where [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] questioned the simians over dinner (Session 80).

## The Tutin Rescue Mission (Sessions 81–82)
Tomas joined the team for the covert rescue of [[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]] on [[tutin|Tutin]], assigned to the northern approach route to [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] (Session 81). He had no weapons or armor of his own, so the crew equipped him for the expedition (Session 82). Isaiah told Isambard that he and Tomas had undergone additional experiments on Suliman (Session 82).

On the elevator descent to [[hampton|Hampton]] on Day 230-1105, Tomas grunted when the artist Archie asked about the simians, and did not become angry when [[penelope|Penelope]] touched him. In Hampton the simians attracted attention and stayed close to the crew (Session 82).
