# Blue Parrot

**Summary**: Seedy tavern located in the GAS town industrial district of Startown Liberty on Romentino, site of Harry Proud's final meeting before his murder.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Establishment

The Blue Parrot is a tavern and drinking establishment situated in the GAS town district of [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 10). Serving refinery workers, transport pilots, and local underworld figures, it caters to a rough clientele immersed in the planet's thriving narcotics and contraband culture (Session 10).

## The Proud Murder Investigation

On Day 38-1105, shuttle pilot [[harry-proud|Harry Proud]] visited the Blue Parrot carrying the stolen [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] and Cr 20,000 in stolen cash (Session 10). Proud met with his addict son [[screetch|Screetch]], but when an argument erupted outside the establishment over drug money, Screetch stabbed Proud to death on the pavement a block away (Session 10). The bartender subsequently provided critical intelligence to the investigating crew regarding Screetch's intoxication on [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] (Session 10).
