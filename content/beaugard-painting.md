# Beaugard Painting

**Summary**: Masterpiece historic painting on loan from Kidderminster to Viscount Rittenhouse, stolen from the Csabai Palace gallery and recovered by BIAS CCR.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## History & Significance

The **Beaugard painting** (also referenced as the *Beaugart*) is a priceless, celebrated work of art originating from [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] (Sessions 22–23). In early 1105, the artwork was transported to [[csabai|Csabai]] by curator and art expert [[chandler|Chandler]] on formal diplomatic loan to [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]], scheduled for a high-profile ceremonial unveiling at a grand palace ball (Sessions 22–23).

## The Theft & Recovery

On Day 57-1105, while being guarded in the palace gallery under the personal vigil of [[lord-julian|Lord Julian]], the painting was stolen in an inside operation (Sessions 22–23). Julian's socialite companion, [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]], drugged his coffee to induce momentary unconsciousness, detached the painting, and carried it away (Sessions 22–23).

Retained by Viscount Rittenhouse, operatives from [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] tracked Vivienne's unrecorded movements, distracted her via a social sting led by [[sarik|Sarik]], and searched her private chambers (Sessions 22–23). Operative [[asher|Asher]], acting on a tactical tip from [[beck|Beck]], found the masterpiece stashed behind another painting hung on the wall of Vivienne's room (Sessions 22–23). The recovered painting was returned to the Viscount in time for the 4:00 PM gala (Sessions 22–23).
