# Sundance

**Summary**: Worker colony on [[tutin|Tutin]] where [[sarik|Sarik]]'s family lives; [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s cover story on Tutin is a hunting and work trip to Sundance.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

---

## Overview

Sundance is one of the small worker colonies on [[tutin|Tutin]], alongside Moonlight, Eden and Elysium. [[sarik|Sarik]]'s family lives there (Session 82). The country around it is heavily wooded (Session 83).

## Hunting

Visitors can apply for permits to hunt [[rock-squid|rock squid]] near Sundance; applications can take a day or two to clear (Session 82).

## BIAS CCR's Cover Story

The crew's cover on Tutin is that they are going to work in Sundance and will hunt before they start (Session 82). At the [[admiral-grace-inn|Admiral Grace Inn]], [[beck|Beck]] told the innkeeper they were visiting Sundance for hunting and outdoor work and expected a local contact to meet them (Session 83).
