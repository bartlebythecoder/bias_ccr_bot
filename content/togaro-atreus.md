# Togaro Atreus

**Summary**: Senior Wu-Ketai executive in the Arico system whom BIAS CCR considered as a possible ally.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Togaro Atreus** is a senior corporate executive within the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai Corporation]] operating in the Arico system (Session 53).

## Activities & Role

* **Corporate Standing**: On Day 132-1105, during their interrogation of researcher [[winslow-leach|Winslow Leach]] on [[freeport|Freeport]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] questioned Leach regarding connections to Atreus. Leach acknowledged knowing her prominent corporate reputation but denied direct personal dealings with her (Session 53).
* **Prospective Alliance**: Following revelations that [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]] and the [[the-stratemeyers|Stratemeyer Syndicate]] were entangled in ancient technology and Project Metropolis, BIAS CCR considered reaching out directly to Togaro Atreus as an executive who shared an interest in neutralizing the Stratemeyers' illegal operations (Session 53).
