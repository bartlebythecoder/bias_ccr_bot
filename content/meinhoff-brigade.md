# Meinhoff Brigade

**Summary**: Ruthless organized crime syndicate commanding [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] under [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]], trafficking Sycorax narcotics across the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico]] belt.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Governance of Eternity Station

The Meinhoff Brigade is the de facto criminal authority controlling [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] in the lawless [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] of the Arico belt ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]).
- **Leadership**: Commanded by a shadowy warlord styled as a Demon Prince, known as **Yandee** ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]).
- **Enforcement**: Internal security, protection rackets, and contract enforcement are executed by a heavily armed mercenary outfit, the **Tempest Company** ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]).

## Interstellar Narcotics Pipeline

The Brigade is the primary producer and distributor of **Sycorax**, a potent illicit narcotic:
- Sycorax is smuggled inward from the Dark Crescent to the capital on [[freeport|Freeport]], where it is distributed across the Jonestown entertainment districts exclusively by the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]].
- The Brigade maintains an iron grip over station commerce, extracting tribute through "The Court" and condemning indebted belters to the low-oxygen squalor of Doptown ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]).
