# SuSAG

**Summary**: Imperial Megacorporation specializing in chemicals, geneering, pharmaceuticals, and anagathics ([[deleon|DeLeon]]), protected by TL 12–14 paramilitary security forces.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Corporate Structure & Governance

SuSAG, LIC (Schunamann und Sohn AG, LIC) is an ancient megacorporation dominating chemical engineering, pharmaceutical synthesis, genetics, and biological longevity research across Charted Space.
- **Major Stockholders**: [[house-alkalikoi|House Alkalikoi]] (22%), [[house-aledon|House Aledon]] (5%), [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]] (4%), [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]] (2%).

## Security & Biological Controversies

Due to historical accidents and the notorious reputation of its chemical/biological warfare division, SuSAG is deeply distrusted on many worlds:
- All hazardous research labs and experimental manufacturing stations are placed on uninhabited, remote planetoids.
- To combat persistent industrial espionage and sabotage, SuSAG deploys a private paramilitary security army equipped to advanced Tech Level 12–14 standards.

## Products and Subsidiaries

- **Anagathics**: The primary manufacturer of longevity drugs in the Imperium, including the controversial new life-extension compound [[deleon|DeLeon]] ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).
- **Shipping Dominance**: SuSAG holds majority ownership and operational control of [[oberlindes-lines|Oberlindes Lines]], using its commercial freighters to move pharmaceuticals across the subsector ([[oberlindes-lines|Oberlindes Lines]]).
