# Mirassol Spider

**Summary**: Giant predatory arachnid native to the frozen moon Mirassol in the Romentino system, whose swarms forced the total evacuation of K.C. Station in 1105.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-26

---

![[mirassol_spider.jpg]]

## Biology

The Mirassol spider is an indigenous arachnid lifeform adapted to the extreme cryogenic environment of [[mirassol|Mirassol]] in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system (1428). Alongside the [[mursa|Mursa]], it is one of the moon's dangerous native predators.

## The K.C. Station Evacuation (Day 44-1105)

Around Day 44-1105, nightly swarms of giant spiders repeatedly breached the perimeter of [[kc-station|K.C. Station]], forcing the permanent abandonment of the entire corporate facility (Session 17):
- Scientist [[george-castle|George Castle]] survived a spider attack during the evacuation, saved by maintenance plumber [[mancuso|Mancuso]] (Session 17).
- Castle later confirmed to [[beck|Beck]] that her acquaintance Matteo had been killed by the spiders on Mirassol (Session 37).
