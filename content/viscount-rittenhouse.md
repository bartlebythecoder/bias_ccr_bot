# Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai

**Summary**: Aristocratic ruler of [[csabai|Csabai]], member of [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]] and cousin to [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]], navigating complex alliances with [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Ruling Aristocrat of Csabai

Viscount Rittenhouse is the primary Imperial nobleman governing [[csabai|Csabai]] (1626). His aristocratic heraldic crest prominently features the indigenous **csabaipede**, a venomous arthropod native to the planet.

## Dynastic and Megacorporate Alignments

- **House Yetrina**: A nobleman of [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]] and third cousin to [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]] of Kidderminster, serving as a vocal public pillar of her regional administration.
- **Hortalez et Cie Connection**: Despite his familial loyalty to Duchess Margaret (who is tied to Tukera Lines), Rittenhouse maintains close commercial ties with megacorporation [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]]—a firm widely regarded as aligned with rival [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]—illustrating Csabai's pragmatic political maneuvering.

## The Hidden Painting Inquiry

On Day 56-1105, following the arrival of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] on Csabai, an operational inquiry was tendered regarding a concealed, valuable painting associated with Viscount Rittenhouse's estate (Session 21).

## Recovering the Beaugard Masterpiece and Clinic Rewards

On Day 57-1105, Viscount Rittenhouse retained [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] through [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] to investigate the theft of the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] from the [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] gallery (Sessions 22–23). Following the operatives' unmasking of [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]] and the safe recovery of the artwork, the Viscount rewarded the team with invitations to the ceremonial ball, formal wear, and one month of access to his private medical augmentation clinic for full physical characteristic upgrades (Sessions 22–23).

## Intervening in the Calmer Singh Murder Scene

On Day 90-1105, Viscount Rittenhouse dispatched diplomatic attaché [[margaux-evans|Margaux Evans]] and royal legal counsel to Berth 17 of [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] following the discovery of three murdered crew members on ***[[cals-dream|Cal's Dream]]*** (Session 25). Through Margaux, the Viscount arranged positive media spin for [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] while advising them to depart the planet soon, and granted [[asher|Asher]] access to the private archives of [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] to investigate the name [[darnes|Darnes]] (Session 25).
