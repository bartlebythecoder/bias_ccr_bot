# Apex

**Summary**: Advanced floating repulsor/grav-city on [[csabai|Csabai]] housing corporate regional headquarters, major research libraries, and administrative centers.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Architecture & Society

Apex is a high-tech repulsor-levitated aerial city situated above the planetary terrain of [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 25). Supported by contra-grav arrays and massive structural engineering, Apex stands as a pinnacle of Csabai's technological affluence, housing elite corporate regional divisions—including [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]—as well as prestigious civic repositories and reference databanks (Session 25).

## Inquiries by BIAS CCR

On Days 90–91-1105, during their investigation into the assassination of [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] and the conspiracy surrounding [[the-doves|The Doves]] (Session 25):
- Operative [[asher|Asher]] traveled to Apex to utilize its public and municipal libraries in an effort to identify the targeted person of interest, [[darnes|Darnes]], while attempting electronic penetrations of LSP networks (Session 25).
- Operative [[beck|Beck]] subsequently traveled from [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] to Apex to conduct field inquiries at the regional corporate offices of [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] (Session 25).

---

## Corporate Security Ambush at LSP Headquarters

On Day 92-1105, the floating city of Apex became a focal point of conflict when operative [[beck|Beck]] was detained in a sealed interrogation room by [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] Corporate Security at their regional headquarters while inquiring into [[darnes|Darnes]] (Session 26). Concurrently, [[asher|Asher]] operated a clandestine cyber warfare listening post from the team's rented Apex apartment, breaching LSP mainframes (Session 26).

## Cyber Operations and Darnes's Residence

On Days 92–93-1105, [[asher|Asher]] established a clandestine cyber operations base in the team's rented Apex apartment, successfully infiltrating LSP networks to discover that [[darnes|Darnes]] maintained an Apex residence and was secretly employed by [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]] (Session 27).
