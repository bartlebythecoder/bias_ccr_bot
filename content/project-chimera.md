# Project Chimera

**Summary**: Classified Wu-Ketai bioengineering program on Suliman tied to its uplifted simian research.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Project Chimera** is a classified, high-priority bioengineering and research program operated by the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation on the asteroid settlement [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 34).

## Discovery
During [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s undercover investigation of the disappearance of [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]], [[asher|Asher]] hacked into the Wu-Ketai corporate network on Suliman. Among several restricted project files—including *Operation Nightingale*, *Gemini Initiative*, *Shadow Walker*, and *Echo Bloom*—the team identified **Project Chimera** as their primary target for deep investigation (Session 34).

## Connections to Uplift Research & Metabolic Reduction
The project appears intricately tied to Wu-Ketai's covert genetic modifications and the mystery uncovered by Lundquist regarding uplifted simians that were far older than corporate records publicly claimed (Session 34).

Further technical telemetry recovered by [[asher|Asher]] during detention on Day 115-1105 revealed that Project Chimera specifically focuses on engineering uplifted simian strains possessing drastically reduced metabolic and energy requirements (Session 35). This metabolic engineering would significantly decrease life-support and caloric costs for industrial extraction workforces, enhancing profitability and viability in deep-space environments.

## Articulate Speech & Laboratory Revolt
On Day 117-1105, BIAS CCR penetrated the animal pen laboratories holding experimental Chimera specimens (Session 39). The operatives discovered that the uplifted simians had developed articulate human speech capabilities. The team negotiated an alliance with the captive apes: BIAS CCR unlocked their holding pens, and the liberated simians staged a chaotic revolt throughout the facility corridors, rounding up research personnel and creating the critical diversion necessary to breach the chrono lab (Session 39).
