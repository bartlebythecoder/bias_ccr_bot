# Fagan

**Summary**: Black market fence on Eternity Station who buys artifacts and salvage from Metropolis Station.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Fagan** is a black market fence and antiquities/salvage broker operating on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] in the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]].

## Commercial Interests
- **Metropolis Artifacts**: According to veteran station shuttle pilot [[arkady|Arkady]], Fagan actively purchases exotic artifacts, unidentified precursor devices, and anomalous salvage retrieved from [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]].
- **Heavy Marbles**: When shown the [[heavy-marbles|heavy marbles]] recovered by the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] crew from Level 2 and the Level 1 cafeteria, Arkady recommended Fagan as an immediate cash buyer on Eternity Station, even though the exact technical or precursor function of the spheres remains uncataloged.
