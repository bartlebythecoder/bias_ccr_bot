# Lt. Tony McIntyre

**Summary**: Starport Alpha police detective on Csabai who led the official investigation into the assassinations of Calmer Singh and his starship crew.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Law Enforcement Career

Lieutenant Tony McIntyre is a seasoned criminal investigator serving with the constabulary of [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 25). Familiar with the underworld factions and cultural dynamics of the planet, McIntyre maintains working ties with cultural informants such as the [[irklan|Irklan]] intermediary [[inkulan|Inkulan]] (Session 25).

## The Calmer Singh Homicide

On Day 90-1105, McIntyre led the homicide inquiry following the fatal envenomed dart strike on [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] outside [[henrys-tavern|Henry's Tavern]] (Session 25). He formally interrogated operatives [[asher|Asher]], [[beck|Beck]], and [[sarik|Sarik]], identifying the strike as an Irklan assassination and expressing deep concern over Singh's missing crew (Session 25).

Later that day, McIntyre secured Berth 17 as an active crime scene after forensic units discovered the murdered bodies of three of Singh's crew hidden aboard ***[[cals-dream|Cal's Dream]]*** (Session 25). McIntyre accepted the slain assassin's kukri blade from Sarik and liaised with [[margaux-evans|Margaux Evans]], the attaché dispatched by [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] (Session 25).
