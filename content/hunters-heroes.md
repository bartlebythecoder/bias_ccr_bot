# Hunter's Heroes

**Summary**: Rival mercenary outfit in the Arico system that competes aggressively with BIAS CCR for contracts and bounties.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**Hunter's Heroes** is an independent mercenary outfit and rival adventuring band operating throughout the Arico system and the Outer Rim. Known for aggressive contract acquisition, they compete directly with other free-agent groups, notably [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]].

---

## Operational History

### The Stratemeyer Bounty Race (Day 120-1105)
On Day 120-1105 on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], Hunter's Heroes accepted an identical high-stakes syndicate contract offered by [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]]: the identification and assassination of the clandestine leader of [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] for a bounty of **MCr 2.0** (Session 44).

Preparing for an immediate launch into the Dark Crescent, Hunter's Heroes seemed poised to outpace BIAS CCR, who had opted to undergo a week-long cybernetic surgical sabbatical in the station's slickbays. However, prior to their departure from the docking bays, [[beck|Beck]] executed a covert counter-surveillance operation, tagging one of the mercenary operatives' shoes and attaching a tracking beacon to their starship hull. This allowed BIAS CCR to monitor their movements and vector while recuperating.

### Counter-Surveillance on Freeport (Day 130-1105)
On Day 130-1105 on [[freeport|Freeport]], Hunter's Heroes attempted their own counter-intelligence operation against BIAS CCR, managing to plant an electronic tracking bug onto [[beck|Beck]] (Session 45). However, Beck quickly detected the surveillance device while at [[club-strata|Club Strata]] in The Lux, discreetly slipping the bug into the pocket of bouncer [[andre|Andre]] to throw off their surveillance tracking.

### The Abduction of David Parker & Townhouse Infiltration (Day 130-1105)
Believing that the murder of [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] designer [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] was the linchpin to finding the Stratemeyer leader, Hunter's Heroes ambushed and kidnapped prime police suspect [[david-parker|David Parker]] from the streets of Freeport, holding him captive at their townhouse safehouse (Session 46). Tracking their operatives back to the residence, [[beck|Beck]] and [[asher|Asher]] negotiated entry. After Hunter's Heroes failed to break Parker during initial interrogations, Beck questioned him and deduced he was innocent of the murder. To protect their operational lead and mislead Hunter's Heroes into guarding an innocent man, Beck falsely confirmed to the rival mercenaries that Parker was indeed the killer (Session 46).

### The Conde Borbon Lead (Day 130/131-1105)
Following the circulation of an anonymous tip asking *"Where is Conde Borbon?"*, Hunter's Heroes messaged BIAS CCR directly, claiming that they had already located the whereabouts of [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]]—the underworld figure connecting the Meinhoff Brigade and the Primero Cartel (Session 47).

### Infiltration of Jonestown (Day 132-1105)
On Day 132-1105, Hunter's Heroes beat BIAS CCR to Jonestown by several hours in their search for Borbon. An operative described as a "big guy in an orange suit" posing as a member of the [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]] breached the Homestead Apartments and interrogated Borbon's close friend [[rico-mathers|Rico Mathers]] regarding Borbon's movements (Session 50).

### The Taunting Notes & Park 32 Cordon (Day 132-1105)
Later that evening, Hunter's Heroes operative [[palmas|Palmas]] raided [[cafe-dimitri|Cafe Dimitri]] shortly before BIAS CCR's arrival, executing the occupants and leaving a taunting handwritten note inside a laptop addressed to Beck (*"MCB - Just think, you could have been here when we were. I hope you are considering my offer. :-P"*). 

Following the money trail to [[omni|Omni]] employee [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]], Hunter's Heroes burgled Pramerski's residence, abducted him, and left a second note on the fridge (*"MCB - I am sorry we are so good. We cannot help it. :-P"*). At 10:00 PM, a six-man Hunter's Heroes squad converged on Park 32, cordoning the area in the shadows, where Palmas confronted Beck hiding under a footbridge (Session 52).

### Park 32 Sweep & The Recruitment Offer (Day 132-1105)
At Park 32, Hunter's Heroes conducted a military-style search of the park grounds searching for the armed man that [[sky|Sky]] had disarmed (Session 53). Under the pedestrian bridge, [[palmas|Palmas]] questioned Beck, expressing serious concern that local police had arrived because only Hunter's Heroes were supposed to have received the transmission. Palmas attempted to probe Beck for details on Starka Pramerski and the Stratemeyers, and formally offered Beck a spot in Hunter's Heroes. Beck rejected the offer, maintaining BIAS CCR's independence and operational edge (Session 53).

### The Mall Crime Scene Summons (Day 133-1105)
On Day 133-1105, [[palmas|Palmas]] contacted Beck directly, directing BIAS CCR to a shopping mall where Starka Pramerski was found dead in a restroom. Palmas vouched for Beck with [[inspector-coker|Inspector Coker]] and the police cordon, giving BIAS CCR access to examine the body and uncover evidence of staged homicide and Stratemeyer internal purge (Session 54). Later that day, BIAS CCR noted that Hunter's Heroes were entirely absent from both the bank tower and Jollynn's residence (Session 54).

### The Stardust Gala & The Takanabe Escape (Day 133-1105)
Later that evening, Palmas and a team of Hunter's Heroes infiltrated the fourth-floor charity gala at the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]]. Their surveillance was abruptly interrupted by an emergency call. Operative [[sarik|Sarik]] trailed them down to their staging headquarters, overhearing a panicked, heated dispute revealing that their high-value captive—[[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]] bank president [[tora-takanabe|Tora Takanabe]]—had broken free and escaped from their custody (Session 55).

### The Mansion Woods Negotiation & 50/50 Bounty Pact (Day 133-1105)
Following BIAS CCR's armed storming of Marie Orslo Jollynn's mansion, Palmas emerged from the perimeter tree line as police sirens drew near. Recognizing that neither mercenary outfit possessed completely airtight proof alone to satisfy [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]], Palmas proposed pooling their investigative dossiers in exchange for a 50/50 bounty split. BIAS CCR accepted the pact, allowing both outfits to successfully claim the MCr 2.0 contract payout from attorney [[ridpath|Ridpath]] (Sessions 56–57).
