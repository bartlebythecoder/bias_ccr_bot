# Irquez

**Summary**: Union dock supervisor at [[orion-docks|Orion Docks]] who took payoffs from [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]] to ease syndicate cargo through customs.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Irquez** is a dockworking supervisor and union official stationed at [[orion-docks|Orion Docks]] on [[freeport|Freeport]] (Session 54).

## Dealings with the Stratemeyers

* **Customs Payoffs**: Irquez served as the corrupt dockside contact for [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]]. The two met frequently for lunch, where Pramerski delivered recurring small cash payments to guarantee union cooperation and smooth customs clearance for syndicate shipments passing through the docks (Session 54).
* **Interrogation by BIAS CCR (Day 133-1105)**: Following the decryption of the Park 32 burner phone and the murder of Starka Pramerski, BIAS CCR tracked Irquez down at Orion Docks. Irquez confirmed that no illicit shipments were currently on-site. When shown a surveillance image of [[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]], Irquez identified him as a man who had accompanied Starka to past meetings. Before departing, [[sarik|Sarik]] warned Irquez that a replacement contact would be arriving soon and instructed him to keep quiet (Session 54).
