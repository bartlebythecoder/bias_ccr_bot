# Alice Wayo

**Summary**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] operative found unconscious in a Level 1 storage room of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], shot by [[sarik|Sarik]] and stripped of her battle dress and weapons.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Alice Wayo** was an operative affiliated with [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] discovered unconscious within [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] on Day 191.

## Encounter & Demise
- **Discovery**: The [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] salvage crew discovered Wayo slumped unconscious but breathing in the corner of a Level 1 storage room beneath a duct-taped eye scanner. She was fully equipped in standard Wu-Ketai battle dress armor.
- **Execution by Sarik**: Without hesitation, [[sarik|Sarik]] shot her in the head to prevent any threat, ambush, or alert to station networks or naval forces.
- **Salvaged Equipment**: The team searched her body, identifying her from personal credentials as Alice Wayo. They confiscated her battle dress armor, submachine gun, combat shotgun, and identity cards. [[asher|Asher]] extracted a biological tissue sample for laboratory genetic sequencing to determine whether she was organic or a synthetic construct.
- **Scanner Attempt**: The crew attempted to use Wayo's eye to activate the storage room's wall scanner, but the scan failed to register or grant access.
