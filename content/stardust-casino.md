# Stardust Casino

**Summary**: Aging casino in North Lux on [[freeport|Freeport]] owned by [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]] and used by underworld brokers.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
The **Stardust Casino** is a prominent gambling resort and commercial establishment situated in the North Lux entertainment sector of [[freeport|Freeport]] (Session 46).

---

## Operational History

### Underworld Information Brokerage (Day 130-1105)
On Day 130-1105, during their investigation into the murder of [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] operatives [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sky|Sky]] visited the Stardust Casino coffee shop (Session 46). The establishment served as the operational hub for information broker [[stadler|Stadler]]. It was inside this coffee shop that Isambard negotiated the purchase of classified surveillance footage proving that prime homicide suspect [[david-parker|David Parker]] had been kidnapped by rival mercenary group [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]].

### Assassination Contract Allegations & Investigation (Day 132-1105)
On Day 132-1105, following an encrypted tip from [[julie|Julie]] claiming that the casino's owner had sought to contract the assassination of [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]], [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] cased the Stardust (Session 50). 

Meeting in a conference room with owner [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]] and head of security [[vince|Vince]], the operatives probed the allegations. Frey confirmed she had been asked to set up a meeting with Borbon in the past, but disclaimed ongoing interest. Vince corroborated that no other outfits had visited and maintained that the casino operated with honesty despite its aging and worn exterior. While waiting in the coffee shop, Beck and Sarik met with [[stadler|Stadler]] to purchase the address of Borbon's friend [[rico-mathers|Rico Mathers]] (Session 50).

### Fourth-Floor High-Society Charity Gala (Day 133-1105)
On the evening of Day 133-1105, the Stardust Casino hosted a high-society charity gala organized by upscale event planner [[javier|Javier]] on its fourth-floor Grand Ballroom and foyer (Session 55).
- **Infiltration by BIAS CCR**: Operatives [[beck|Beck]], [[sarik|Sarik]], [[sky|Sky]], and [[asher|Asher]] breached the venue using paid admission tickets. Sarik scouted the employees-only back corridors, while Sky engaged venue security and Beck monitored VIP arrivals.
- **Convergence of Key Figures**: The event brought together casino owner Ortega Frey, [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]] CEO [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn]], her bodyguard [[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]], and a squad of rival mercenaries from [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] led by [[palmas|Palmas]].
- **Crisis & Flight Plans**: Amidst speeches in the sealed ballroom, Beck intercepted orders for Jollynn's midnight starship departure off Freeport, while Sarik tracked Hunter's Heroes to the lower spiral stairs where they discovered bank president [[tora-takanabe|Tora Takanabe]] had escaped (Session 55).
