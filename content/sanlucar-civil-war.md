# Sanlucar Civil War

**Summary**: Devastating 1089–1090 conflict on [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] that resulted in the annihilation of the Khanate of Aridush and controversial covert intervention by Imperial Marines.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Background and Factions

Following the collapse of a central world authority, [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] (1022) fractured into warring states, dominated by:
- **Republic of Valentia**: The largest, most disciplined technocratic power.
- **Khanate of Aridush**: The second-largest power, fiercely independent.
- **Hegemony of Cygnus**: An allied regional bloc.

## The 1089–1090 Conflict

In 1089, Valentia declared open war on the Khanate. Fought across airless moon settlements, combat was constrained by fragile life-support infrastructure:
- Both sides engaged in brutal urban and dome assaults.
- Catastrophic dome breaches wiped out civilian populations in disputed strikes.
- **Imperial Marine Intervention**: Imperial Marines intervened covertly on behalf of Valentia, seeking rapid stabilization; instead, this deepened the bloodbath and triggered international outrage.

## Fall-Out and Legacy

The Khanate of Aridush was dismantled and annexed by Valentia. A lethal strike on Aridush high command left young [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]] as the sole survivor. This trauma directly spawned the [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]], culminating 15 years later in the 1105 revolution. Marine veterans of the conflict, such as operative [[sarik|Sarik]], later transitioned into civilian or mercenary roles across the subsector (Sessions 1–5).

## Amnesty Settlement (1105)

On Day 44-1105, following Imperial diplomatic recognition of the Commonwealth under President [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]], Weber officially withdrew all legal demands for war crimes prosecutions against Imperial Marine veterans, granting complete legal amnesty to participating veterans such as operative [[sarik|Sarik]] (Session 17).
