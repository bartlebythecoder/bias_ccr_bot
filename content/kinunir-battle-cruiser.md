# Kinunir-Class Battle Cruiser

**Summary**: 1,200-ton Imperial front-line combat vessel class built by [[general-products|General Products]], featuring Jump-4 capabilities, heavy particle weaponry, and a history marred by disappearances and combat losses.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Technical Specifications

The Kinunir class represents a specialized 1,200-ton (16,800 cubic meters) armored battle cruiser designed for patrol, interdiction, and planetary assault operations:
- **Dimensions**: 73.5m length, 52.5m beam, 15.5m hull height, supplemented by an 11m vertical dorsal fin.
- **Performance**: 4G maneuver acceleration via dual Dupree 734 impulse drives; Jump-4 capability powered by a Shva r3-55 jump drive with integral r2-55 backup.
- **Power & Operations**: Two Deltic Mk1827 JK Fusion plants; 200 days standard life support; manned by 10 officers, 35 enlisted crew, and 35 marine assault troops.
- **Armament & Electronics**: Dual ventral missile turrets, port and starboard particle accelerator turrets, dorsal anti-boat laser batteries, ISMM Model/7.3 tactical computer with fiber-optic backup networks.
- **Auxiliary Craft**: Carries one 22.5m ship's pinnace and one 7.5m sub-orbital grav APC.

## Combat Service & Dispositions

While intended as the backbone of subsector naval defense, construction by [[general-products|General Products]] was rushed, leading to mechanical flaws and severe losses ([[general-products|General Products]]):
- **Kinunir (BC-9514)**: Class ship; disappeared without a trace while on routine patrol in mid-1088.
- **Allamu (BC-9516)**: Held the fleet vanguard for four hours at the [[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]] in 1084 under relentless fire before screen failure destroyed the ship with all hands; name memorialized for future Imperial dreadnoughts.
- **Regal Splendor (BC-9518)**: Presented by Empress Iolanthe in 1092 to the Pleasant Hill subsector navy as a diplomatic gift; reconfigured for insurrection suppression.
- **Ninkur Sagga (BC-9521)**: Destroyed with all hands at the [[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]] (1084) following a catastrophic screen failure or missile detonation.
- **Gaesh (BC-9528)**: Failed jump trials; converted in 1098 into an uncrewed orbital prison hulk at Hutchison (1028).
- **Adda Dubsar (BC-9532)**: Cancelled and scrapped in 1089; hull components covertly acquired by [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]].
