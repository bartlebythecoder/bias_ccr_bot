# Real Red Hand

**Summary**: Militant splinter faction of [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]] seeking to break [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]]'s syndicate truce by resuming lucrative kidnappings and illicit Starlight exports.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Formation & Ideology

The "Real Red Hand" is a clandestine, hardline splinter movement that emerged from internal fractures within [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 12). Over a year prior to 1105, off-world crime boss [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]] negotiated a peace pact between the Red Hand and the planetary banking oligarchy, granting the gang control of Startown vice in exchange for ending kidnappings and stopping the off-world export of [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] (Session 12).

Dissident soldiers and lower-level earners, deprived of extortion revenue, rebelled against organizational constraints, adopting the "Real Red Hand" moniker to conduct rogue extortion campaigns—including the high-profile abduction of [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]] for an MCr 1.0 ransom (Sessions 11–12).

## Labor Rights Ideology & Clone Exposures

Under elder leader [[tres-capulet|Tres Capulet]], the Real Red Hand claims the moral and ideological mantle of the original Red Hand, repudiating criminal kidnappings and focusing on militant labor advocacy for Romentino's miners (Session 14). Capulet actively aided [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] by exposing Elin Butler's kidnapping hoax, while soliciting the operatives to investigate what he alleges is an illegal corporate replacement program using [[cloning|clones]] to supplant deceased miners with synthetic, union-busting labor (Session 14).
