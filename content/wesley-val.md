# Wesley Val

**Summary**: Commercial merchant broker on Csabai who liaised with Calmer Singh and met with BIAS CCR at Henry's Tavern.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Commercial Role

Wesley Val is a commercial merchant broker and intermediary operating out of [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 24). He maintains trade and logistical contacts with independent ship captains, cargo haulers, and intelligence brokers, including [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] (Session 24).

## Collaboration with BIAS CCR

On Day 89-1105, following Singh's fatal poisoning outside [[henrys-tavern|Henry's Tavern]], [[beck|Beck]] located Wesley's contact frequency among Singh's personal possessions (Session 24). Contacted by the operatives, Wesley agreed to rendezvous at Henry's Tavern (Session 24). He explained that Singh had discovered intelligence concerning [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] and intended to hire the firm to locate an individual, providing context that spurred the operatives to search Singh's starship at Berth 17 (Session 24).
