# Risiano Tiler

**Summary**: Independent investigative journalist in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico]] system who shares leads with BIAS CCR.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**Risiano Tiler** is an independent investigative journalist operating across the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system, renowned for uncovering corporate malpractice, syndicate corruption, and illegal technological developments (Sessions 47–48).

---

## Operational History

### Meeting with BIAS CCR (Day 130-1105)
On the evening of Day 130-1105, Tiler tracked [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] to their townhouse headquarters on [[freeport|Freeport]], admitted by [[isaiah|Isaiah]] (Session 47). [[asher|Asher]] verified her credentials and press record. Tiler explained she was investigating classified technologies involving consciousness transfer, personality backups, and elite resurrection, noting [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]]'s presence in the system. 

In a reciprocal intelligence exchange, BIAS CCR provided her with information regarding [[morella-station|Morella Station]]'s cloning vats and the youth-siphoning operations at [[suliman|Suliman]] (framing their sources through the Red Hand to maintain plausible deniability), while Tiler agreed to monitor Freeport developments and syndicate ties for the team.

### Tracking the Belladonna & Cartel Connections (Day 131-1105)
Following the telepathic abduction of [[sarik|Sarik]], Tiler contacted [[beck|Beck]] at 2:30 PM with vital intelligence connecting [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]] to the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] via the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]] on Freeport (Session 48). Later that afternoon, Tiler identified and confirmed that an associated starship, the ***[[belladonna|Belladonna]]***, was berthed within Freeport's massive Class A starport complex, alerting the team so they could mount an intercept.
