# Margaux Evans

**Summary**: Diplomatic attaché and high-level fixer for Viscount Rittenhouse who managed the crime scene fallout on Cal's Dream and arranged palace archival access for BIAS CCR.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Service to Viscount Rittenhouse

Margaux Evans is an influential diplomatic attaché, legal liaison, and political fixer serving in the executive administration of [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 25). Known for poise and sharp political instincts, Evans manages delicate public relations, sensitive crimes involving foreign off-worlders, and palace interests (Session 25).

## The Berth 17 Crime Scene

On Day 90-1105, following the discovery of three murdered crew members aboard [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]]'s starship ***[[cals-dream|Cal's Dream]]***, Evans arrived at Berth 17 accompanied by royal legal counsel (Session 25). Intervening in the discussion between [[lt-tony-mcintyre|Lt. Tony McIntyre]] and operatives [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]]:
- She took note of the name [[darnes|Darnes]], which the operatives claimed was whispered by Singh as his dying word (Session 25).
- She pledged to guide media outlets to depict [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] as valiant heroes, while frankly advising the operatives that it would be in their best interest to depart Csabai swiftly (Session 25).
- At Beck's request, Evans authorized special clearance allowing [[asher|Asher]] to conduct research within the restricted private libraries of [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] (Session 25).
