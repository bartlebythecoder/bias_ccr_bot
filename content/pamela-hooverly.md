# Pamela Hooverly

**Summary**: Personal executive assistant to Lord Baltimore aboard his private luxury yacht, harborer of resentment toward Sky.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Service to Lord Baltimore

Pamela Hooverly serves as the confidential executive assistant and secretary to [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]], managing the influential noble's logistical, domestic, and administrative affairs aboard his [[gsbag|GSbAG]] luxury yacht (Session 20).

## Friction with Sky

During the voyage from [[romentino|Romentino]] to [[csabai|Csabai]] in early 1105, Lord Baltimore offered passage to [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]], [[tina|Tina]], and [[sky|Sky]] (Session 20). As Lord Baltimore began spending substantial amounts of personal time in Sky's company, Pamela developed a strong and overt personal animosity toward Sky, viewing her presence and intimacy with Baltimore with deep suspicion and disapproval (Session 20).
