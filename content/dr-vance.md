# Dr. Vance

**Summary**: Wu-Ketai scientist on Suliman who recaptured an escaped simian and walked into BIAS CCR's ambush in the animal pen labs.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Dr. Vance** is a senior research scientist and laboratory official employed by the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation in the core research complex of the asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 39).

## Recapturing the Simian and Subjugation
On Day 117-1105, following the breach of the core laboratories by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]], an uplifted simian escaped from the animal holding pens. Dr. Vance successfully tracked and recaptured the fleeing simian, returning it to the restricted animal pen labs (Session 39).

Upon re-entering the laboratory, Dr. Vance walked directly into an ambush staged by operatives [[sarik|Sarik]], [[isambard|Isambard]], [[asher|Asher]], and [[beck|Beck]], who had just allied with the captive articulate apes. The operatives subdued Dr. Vance, locked her two laboratory technicians into the empty animal holding cells, and strapped Vance onto a medical gurney (Session 39).

## Involuntary Key to the Chrono Lab
As the liberated simians rampaged through the facility corridors creating a massive diversion, BIAS CCR wheeled Dr. Vance through the corridors to the sealed chrono lab. Using Dr. Vance's access keycard and biometric fingerprint, the operatives unlocked the high-security portal, granting them entry to document the operational [[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]] apparatus under [[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]] before fleeing the asteroid (Session 39).
