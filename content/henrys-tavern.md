# Henry's Tavern

**Summary**: Popular drinking establishment and concourse venue in [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]], site of [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]]'s assassination.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Location & Commercial Setting

Henry's Tavern is a well-patronized public house and meeting establishment located along the main transit concourse of [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 24). Frequented by starport workers, itinerant traders, corporate contractors, and information peddlers, the venue offers discrete booths for commercial negotiations (Session 24).

## Incident of Day 89-1105

On Day 89-1105, operatives [[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], and [[sarik|Sarik]] selected Henry's Tavern to meet with prospective client and broker [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] (Session 24). Before entering, Singh was fatally struck down outside the tavern doors by an [[irklan|Irklan]] poisoned dart (Session 24). The operatives subsequently used Henry's Tavern to debrief merchant broker [[wesley-val|Wesley Val]] and later returned there to decrypt Singh's starship files regarding [[the-doves|The Doves]] (Session 24).
