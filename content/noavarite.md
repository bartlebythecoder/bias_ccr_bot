# Noavarite

**Summary**: Vital mineral harvested from the corrosive crust of Romentino, essential for starship jump fuel refining and illicitly used to manufacture Starlight.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Industrial Significance

Noavarite is a rare, high-value mineral extracted almost exclusively on the hostile world of [[romentino|Romentino]] (1428) ([[romentino|Romentino]]). Its primary commercial value stems from its indispensable role in catalytic interstellar fuel refining, ensuring safe hydrogen fuel stabilization for Jump-capable starships ([[romentino|Romentino]]).

## Dual-Use & The Illicit Starlight Trade

Beyond its legitimate aerospace application, refined Noavarite serves as the foundational chemical precursor in the production of **[[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]]** ([[starlight-narcotic|Starlight Narcotic]]):
- When combined with subterranean psychoactive geyser compounds by the Shadow Bazaar, Noavarite creates a powerful, psionically active hallucinogen banned across the Imperium ([[starlight-narcotic|Starlight Narcotic]]).

## Corporate Control and Conflict

- **LSP Dominance**: Megacorporation [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] operates the primary extraction and processing concessions across Romentino ([[romentino|Romentino]]).
- **Red Hand Insurgency**: LSP's aggressive exploitation of Noavarite deposits incited violent anti-mining resistance from [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]], culminating in the fatal 1105 suicide bombing of the Morella mining outpost ([[news-1105-vol-5|News Dispatch — Volume 5]]).
