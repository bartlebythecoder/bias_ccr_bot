# The Rusty Bolt

**Summary**: Cramped Warrens tavern on [[suliman|Suliman]] frequented by laborers, union members and uplifted simians.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**The Rusty Bolt** is a cramped subterranean tavern located in [[the-warrens|The Warrens]] district on the asteroid settlement of [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 34).

## Atmosphere & Significance
Frequented by asteroid laborers, union leaders, and uplifted simians, the establishment serves as an informal hub for working-class conversation away from standard corporate dining halls. Union leader [[carl-parker|Carl Parker]] brought the undercover operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] here upon their entry into the Warrens, where they observed articulate simians performing varied tasks and discussing daily operations (Session 34).
