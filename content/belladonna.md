# Belladonna

**Summary**: Starship berthed at Freeport's Class A starport and linked to underworld figure Conde Borbon.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The ***Belladonna*** is a starship docked at the Class A starport in [[freeport|Freeport]], linked by investigative reporter [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]] to the underworld operative [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]] and his connections between the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]] and the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] (Session 48).

## Operational History

* **Freeport Docking & Underworld Links**: During the investigation into [[sarik|Sarik's]] abduction and the search for [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]], [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]] uncovered intelligence linking Conde Borbon to the *Belladonna*. Tiler confirmed that the vessel was currently berthed at Freeport's vast Class A starport, though locating its specific berth within the sprawling complex required boots-on-the-ground recon (Session 48).
* **BIAS CCR Starport Sweep**: [[isambard|Isambard]] and his team members departed for the Freeport starport complex specifically to track down and survey the *Belladonna* and identify its crew, cargo, and potential role in moving personnel or contraband off-station (Session 48).
* **Shielding and Julie's Boarding**: Upon locating the *Belladonna* at the starport, Isambard discovered that the vessel was heavily shielded and locked down under tight security protocols, making any illicit physical entry impossible without alerting starport authorities and risking immediate arrest (Session 49). Following the release of Sarik, rogue telepath [[julie|Julie]] retreated directly to the starport and boarded the *Belladonna* through a secured gate under Isambard's surveillance (Session 49).
