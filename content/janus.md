# Janus Asteroid Base

**Summary**: Covert research outpost in the Arico belt operated by Carbrooke Minerals under Erebus Power, housing a 40-person team dedicated to classified studies.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Janus is a secluded asteroid installation in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] belt (1324) operated by Carbrooke Minerals, an industrial subsidiary of [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]]. The facility maintains a secretive footing with an elite staff of approximately 40 personnel dedicated to undisclosed scientific research.

## Key Personnel

- **Sergei Menchin**: Operations Manager overseeing logistics and base security.
- **Dr. Callow**: Chief Medical Doctor and research supervisor.

## Operational Intelligence
- **Ancient Precursor Artifact**: Intelligence recovered by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] indicates that an uncataloged precursor artifact of [[the-ancients|The Ancients]] is concealed within the Janus research facility (Session 46).
- **Wu-Ketai & Metropolis Link**: On Day 130-1105, contracts exfiltrated by [[sarik|Sarik]] from the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporate tower on [[freeport|Freeport]] proved active commercial and scientific ties between Wu-Ketai, Carbrooke Minerals, Janus asteroid, and Project [[metropolis-station|Metropolis]] (Session 46).
