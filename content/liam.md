# Liam

**Summary**: Mechanic at K.C. Station on Mirassol and friend of Isambard, who was coerced by Blue Lou Boyle into raiding the Mirassol Pyramid and was found dead.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role at K.C. Station

Liam was a civilian mechanic employed in the Workshop division at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on the frozen moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]] (Sessions 1–5). He worked on machinery and equipment repairs alongside operative [[isambard|Isambard]], who formed a close professional friendship with him during his undercover cover deployment (Sessions 1–5).

## Illicit Expedition & Death

Under pressure and coercion orchestrated by underworld boss [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]], Liam was recruited by Field Support supervisor [[sophia|Sophia]] to assist in breaking into the subterranean [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]] to recover [[the-ancients|Ancient]] relics (Sessions 1–5). The expedition turned fatal; Liam was discovered dead, while Sophia vanished, precipitating the arrival of corporate security investigators (Sessions 1–5).
