# Dark Crescent

**Summary**: The remote, lawless outer region of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico]] asteroid belt, home to [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] and the militarized Stratemeyer Syndicate.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

The Dark Crescent is the sector of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] asteroid belt located at the greatest orbital distance from the system capital on [[freeport|Freeport]]. Because the belt is too vast for local authorities to adequately patrol, secure, or support, the Dark Crescent has evolved into a de facto lawless zone outside official governmental oversight.

## Major Outposts and Factions

- **Eternity Station**: The primary civilian and logistics station in the outer belt, paralyzed by strikes following syndicate hostage killings ([[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).
- **The Stratemeyers**: A loosely allied syndicate of heavily armed, militarized miners that ruthlessly defends claimed asteroid claims and stages aggressive raids against rival mining outfits ([[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]]).
- **Smuggling Corridors**: Routes through the Dark Crescent are used by syndicates such as the Meinhoff Brigade to traffic illicit narcotics like Sycorax inward toward Freeport.
