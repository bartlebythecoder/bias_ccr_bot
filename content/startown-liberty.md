# Startown Liberty

**Summary**: Frontier starport community on [[romentino|Romentino]] (pop ~5,000) divided into green safety zones, GAS town industrial sector, and the Barrikday slums.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Layout & Frontier Society

Startown Liberty is the primary starport settlement on the frigid, corrosive world of [[romentino|Romentino]] (1428), housing roughly 5,000 permanent residents and off-world contract laborers ([[romentino|Romentino]]). Because of widespread frontier lawlessness, off-world travelers are strongly advised to visibly carry firearms and hire local child guides like [[sydney|Sydney]] to navigate designated "green zones" of relative safety (Session 10).

## Key Districts & Locations

- **Starport Administration**: Central commercial terminal handling customs, weapon sales, and passenger transit (Session 10).
- **The Plaza**: Public gathering concourse used for clandestine rendezvous (Session 10).
- **GAS Town**: Heavy industrial district housing refinery personnel and seedy establishments such as the [[blue-parrot|Blue Parrot]] tavern (Session 10).
- **Barrikday**: Impoverished slum district composed of ramshackle shacks, home to narcotics addicts, outcasts, and [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] users such as [[screetch|Screetch]] (Session 10).

## The Espionage Manhunt and The Backways

On Day 47-1105, Startown Liberty became the staging ground for a corporate espionage manhunt when [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] and [[the-banks|The Banks]] broadcasted warrants for BIAS CCR across public screens in [[gas-town|GaS Town]] (Session 19). The operatives bypassed planetary authorities by descending into [[the-backways|The Backways]]—an unmonitored subterranean network beneath Startown inhabited by homeless scavengers and orphaned children living in the refuse processing sector (Session 19).
