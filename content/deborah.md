# Deborah

**Summary**: Woman who cleans wealthy ranches near [[hampton|Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]], said to work at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]]; [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s lead on it. Overdue in town as of Day 231-1105.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

---

## Work & Visits to Town

Deborah, known as Deb, works on [[tutin|Tutin]] outside [[hampton|Hampton]]. On Day 230-1105 the boy [[necktie|Necktie]] told [[isambard|Isambard]] that she works at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] beyond the hills and comes into town once a week (Session 82).

Necktie later added that she usually flies in weekly by air/raft for supplies, tips him, and flies back the same day. She had not been to town for a week and a half (Session 83). According to [[slim|Slim]], she cleans wealthy ranches, lives north of town and usually visits him when she comes in for supplies (Session 83).

## BIAS CCR's Search (Day 231-1105)

- [[beck|Beck]] watched the arriving aircraft on the evening of Day 231; Deborah was not on any of them (Session 83).
- At a poker game in the [[desperado-karaoke-bar|Desperado Karaoke Bar]], Slim gave [[asher|Asher]] her contact number and directions to her place, and said the crew could use his name if they meant to hire her (Session 83).
- With no taxi or rental vehicle in Hampton, Asher, Isambard and [[sky|Sky]] walked out to her place, a trip expected to take three or four hours each way (Session 83).

## Deborah's Place

A long road, apparently the driveway, leads to a ramshackle steel shed, a windmill and an old automated turret, badly corroded and not working. When the crew arrived, an elderly-sounding woman threatened to blow their heads off unless they said who they were and what they wanted. They answered that they had come to see Deb (Session 83).
