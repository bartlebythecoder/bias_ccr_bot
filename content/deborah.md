# Deborah

**Summary**: Former worker at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch near Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]], dismissed by its boss [[duquette|Duquette]] when armed men arrived; told [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] about [[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]] and the ranch.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

## Work & Visits to Town

Deborah, known as Deb, works on [[tutin|Tutin]] outside [[hampton|Hampton]]. On Day 230-1105 the boy [[necktie|Necktie]] told [[isambard|Isambard]] that she works at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] beyond the hills and comes into town once a week (Session 82).

Necktie later added that she usually flies in weekly by air/raft for supplies, tips him, and flies back the same day. She had not been to town for a week and a half (Session 83). According to [[slim|Slim]], she cleans wealthy ranches, lives north of town and usually visits him when she comes in for supplies (Session 83).

## BIAS CCR's Search (Day 231-1105)

- [[beck|Beck]] watched the arriving aircraft on the evening of Day 231; Deborah was not on any of them (Session 83).
- At a poker game in the [[desperado-karaoke-bar|Desperado Karaoke Bar]], Slim gave [[asher|Asher]] her contact number and directions to her place, and said the crew could use his name if they meant to hire her (Session 83).
- With no taxi or rental vehicle in Hampton, Asher, Isambard and [[sky|Sky]] walked out to her place, a trip expected to take three or four hours each way (Session 83).

## Deborah's Place

A long road, apparently the driveway, leads to a ramshackle steel shed, a windmill and an old automated turret, badly corroded and not working. When the crew arrived, an elderly-sounding woman threatened to blow their heads off unless they said who they were and what they wanted. They answered that they had come to see Deb (Session 83).

The woman was Deborah's mother, Sybil, who challenged them over a speaker and threatened them with a shotgun, but let them in once they offered to pay for information. The common room inside is clean and comfortable. Sybil's husband, Gerald, sits connected to old breathing machinery; she introduced him as cranky. [[sky|Sky]] recognised the machine as an ancient iron-lung-like device keeping his body breathing, and [[isambard|Isambard]]'s mirror showed no breath: Gerald is a corpse. Sybil welcomed an offer to have [[beck|Beck]] examine him, and Isambard assured her the doctor would come (Session 84).

Pictures of [[duchess-margaret|the Duchess]] hang around the room; [[asher|Asher]] noticed them after finding her image inside his teacup. Why they are there was not established (Session 84).

## Interview (Day 231-1105)

[[isambard|Isambard]] said he represented a wealthy prospective buyer of the ranch and paid Sybil Cr 500 to bring Deborah in and answer questions. Deborah, frail and visibly frightened, told the crew (Session 84):

- **Her dismissal:** the boss, [[duquette|Duquette]], dismissed her when eight armed men arrived at the ranch. Their arrival was recent, which is why she stopped coming to town.
- **[[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]]:** a scholar from [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] working on a book, with an office and guest suite on the ground floor.
- **The armed men:** they carry rifles and radios and keep their weapons with them. She once saw one of them recover from a cut or bite within an hour. They had been able to leave the ranch, and made trips to [[hampton|Hampton]].
- **Duquette:** he handles outside communications and wanted six people kept in the building. She believes he is an android because of the equipment attached to his head, used for communications and calculations.
- **The site:** the ranch lies in a valley below a mountainside. From her description the crew identified a forested western slope from which it could be watched.

## The Bargain with Sybil

Sybil agreed to keep the conversation private if the crew helped Gerald. Isambard implied that their employer was connected to the Duchess, and before leaving told Sybil that the Duchess remembered her. The crew left Sybil expecting Beck to come within days (Session 84).
