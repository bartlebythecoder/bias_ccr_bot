# The Blue Moose

**Summary**: Warrens bar on [[suliman|Suliman]] where [[hector|Hector]] Nilasen was last seen before he vanished.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**The Blue Moose** is a popular subterranean bar and drinking hole located in [[the-warrens|The Warrens]] on the asteroid settlement of [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 36).

## Disappearance of Hector Nilasen
The venue gained acute investigative importance when security surveillance logs audited by [[asher|Asher]] and [[beck|Beck]] revealed that corporate security guard [[hector|Hector Nilasen]] was last seen entering the establishment before vanishing into a camera blind spot (Sessions 35–36).

## Infiltration Staging Ground
On Day 116-1105, [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] entered The Blue Moose to inspect the blind spot. While [[isambard|Isambard]] engaged in poker with local card player [[splits|Splits]] and [[sky|Sky]] and Beck distracted off-duty asteroid laborers, Asher and [[sarik|Sarik]] investigated an unmarked door in the unmonitored alcove (Session 36). The door led through a back restroom and utility closet concealing the clandestine security entrance into Wu-Ketai's central core laboratories.
