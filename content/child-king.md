# Child King

**Summary**: Cruel, delusional android ruler of Level 2 of Metropolis Station who believes it is a human child and holds court over The Chosen and The Fallen.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The **Child King** is an eccentric, tyrannical android ruler who presides over Level 2 of **[[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]**.

*(Session 62)*

---

## Overview & Psychology
The Child King inhabits an adult-sized android chassis, yet suffers from a deep, unshakable cognitive delusion that it is a living human child and sovereign monarch. Described by the scavenger **[[magnus|Magnus]]** as exceptionally cruel, volatile, and impulsive, the Child King harbors an intense fixation on outside intruders who enter Level 2.

---

## Domain & Subjects
The Child King holds dominion over the bizarre android society populating Level 2, which is polarized into two broad factions:
- **[[the-chosen|The Chosen]]**
- **[[the-fallen|The Fallen]]**

The King subjects visitors to erratic, dangerous interrogations before granting permits or access conduits through Level 2. He rigidly enforces station taboos, including an immediate death penalty for any entity that speaks the name of **[[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]]**.

---

## Encounters
- **Interrogation of Magnus**: Prior to Session 62, Magnus and his scavenger crew were cornered and interrogated by the Child King. The King eventually granted them passage through a concealed ventilation duct that bypassed security into **Level 3**.
- **Target of Infiltration**: In Session 62, the crew of the *BIAS CCR* identified navigating the Child King's court as an unavoidable prerequisite for exploring Level 2 and reaching **[[the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]**.
- **Audience at the Court of the Chosen King (Session 70)**: On Day 192+, following a firefight against security bots and a tour through the Bot's Playground guided by [[ella|Ella]], the *BIAS CCR* team entered the **Court of the Chosen King** under the guise of newly born explorers to obtain navigation permits and locate [[damien|Damien]].
- **Audience, EMP Regicide & Imprisonment (Session 71)**: The King sat upon a bone throne crafted from hollow android skeletons, masked by breathing tubing that artificially modulated his voice. When questioned about [[damien|Damien]], the King fled to an adjoining room, returned demanding flattery and gifts, and was incapacitated when [[sarik|Sarik]] presented an EMP grenade as a tribute and detonated it at his feet. The crew subdued his guards and locked the King and [[ella|Ella]] in the galley. Terminal logs in his private quarters revealed [[monarch|Monarch]] had explicitly ordered the King to block and deny passage to the crew.

## Relationships
- **[[ella|Ella]]**: Humanoid android who guided BIAS CCR into the court; locked with him in the galley.
- **[[the-chosen|The Chosen]] & [[the-fallen|The Fallen]]**: Droid subjects under the King's erratic rule.
- **[[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]]**: The taboo entity whose very mention prompts execution.
- **[[magnus|Magnus]]**: Survivor of a previous interrogation.
- **[[monarch|Monarch]]**: The station AI presiding over Metropolis who issued secret directives to the King.
