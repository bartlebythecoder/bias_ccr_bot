# Jollynn Trust

**Summary**: Overview of the leadership, corporate structure, and recent financial dealings of the Jollynn Trust on Freeport.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Content Summary

- **Institution Profile**: One of the most prominent independent financial institutions in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system, based on [[freeport|Freeport]].
- **Leadership**:
  - **CEO & Majority Shareholder**: Marie Orslo Jollynn, a mini-celebrity on Freeport, board member of the Freeport Housing Charity, and well-loved public figure.
  - **President**: Tora Takanabe, described as an unemotional businessman who is popular with shareholders but disliked by staff.
- **Recent Activities**:
  - Major donation to the Freeport Housing Charity.
  - Signed exclusive partnership to serve as official bank of the Starport Workers Union.
  - Bailed out three prominent Arico casino companies via loans and equity restructuring: Landmark Casino Gaming, Lydia Casinos, and Stardust Casino.

## Operational Incidents

* **BIAS CCR Surveillance & Security Link (Day 133-1105)**: Investigating casino bailouts following the murder of [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] scouted the Jollynn Trust headquarters tower and CEO [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn's]] residence (Session 54). Operatives spotted [[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]] acting as Jollynn's armed protection detail, directly linking the bank to syndicate hit squads. BIAS CCR prepared an infiltration of Jollynn's evening speaking engagement to clone her personal phone (Session 54).
