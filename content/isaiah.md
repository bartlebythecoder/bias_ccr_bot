# Isaiah

**Summary**: Articulate uplifted simian freed from [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]]'s [[suliman|Suliman]] labs who chose to stay with BIAS CCR.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-27

---

**Isaiah** is an articulate uplifted simian genetically engineered by the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation on [[suliman|Suliman]] under [[project-chimera|Project Chimera]] who joined [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] following the core laboratories breakout (Session 40).

## Liberation and Choosing to Stay
On Day 117-1105, Isaiah was among the twelve captive uplifted simians liberated from the animal holding pens by BIAS CCR during their infiltration of the Suliman research facility. After escaping the asteroid aboard a stolen corporate launch, the operatives docked at [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] in the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] (Session 40). While ten of the liberated simians were transferred to an underground primate rescue network, Isaiah and fellow uplift [[tomas|Tomas]] chose to remain alongside BIAS CCR.

## High-Stakes Operations on Eternity Station
On Day 118-1105, Isaiah accompanied [[isambard|Isambard]] into the **Stellar Burn** entertainment complex to infiltrate a high-stakes poker game against [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]] (Session 40). Acting as Isambard's personal valet and attendant under his "Humphrey DuPont" persona, Isaiah's keen behavioral observations were instrumental in identifying Sargon's subtle physical tell—momentarily looking away before calling bets—which led to Isambard accusing Sargon of using an illicit neural cheating implant (Session 40).

## Reception of Risiano Tiler & Strategic Debrief on Freeport
On Day 130-1105 at the team's Freeport lodging, Isaiah acted as attendant admitting investigative journalist [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]] into their quarters (Session 47). Following the rescue of [[sarik|Sarik]] from [[julie|Julie's]] apartment on Day 131, Isaiah functioned as an operational intelligence analyst, preparing and delivering a comprehensive strategic briefing for BIAS CCR that synthesized their discoveries regarding the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], [[ling-standard-products|LSP's]] influence across the sector, and the spreading anti-psionic riots (Session 49).

## Procuring the Jollynn Estate Schematics (Day 133-1105)
During the charity gala at the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]] on Day 133-1105, as BIAS CCR uncovered [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn's]] imminent midnight starship flight off Freeport, Isaiah acquired and delivered the complete architectural floor plan and perimeter layout of Jollynn's private mansion (Session 55). This intelligence provided the team with an alternative vector to intercept Jollynn at her residence before she could reach the starport.

## Raid on the Jollynn Estate (Sessions 56–57)
During the mansion assault on the night of Day 133-1105, Isaiah joined the tactical breach team (Sessions 56–57). Inside the residence, Isaiah tackled and subdued screaming housemaid [[isabel|Isabel]], preventing her from sounding an alarm while the team engaged interior security guards.

## Formal Retainer Contract & Project Metropolis Mission (Session 58)
During the seven-week sabbatical on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], Isaiah and Tomas finalized formal employment contracts with [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]], guaranteeing them a fixed base salary plus hazard pay for high-risk missions (Session 58). Isaiah deployed aboard the refitted starship on its 3.5-day transit to infiltrate [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 58).

## Shipboard Security & Monitoring Asher (Session 62)
Stationed aboard the *BIAS CCR* starship during operations at Metropolis (Session 62):
- **Radio Comms**: Maintained radio communications with the boarding party inside *The Bell* and Metropolis.
- **Asher Surveillance**: Confided in by [[isambard|Isambard]] regarding the threat of [[monarch|Monarch]] neurological duplication and brain-scanning, Isaiah was tasked alongside [[sky|Sky]] to quietly scrutinize [[asher|Asher]] for any unusual or compromised behaviors.

## Mapping Friends & Enemies (Session 80)

During the two-day flight to [[the-cove|The Cove]], Isaiah asked the crew to sort people and factions by their relationship with BIAS CCR, so that connections could be mapped rather than each encounter treated in isolation. He linked [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] and [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]] as rival megacorporations controlling the lower and upper circuits, with [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]] tied to Ling and [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] to Hortalez. He placed the Psalmists and the psionicists in opposition, associating [[george-castle|George Castle]] with the Psalmists and [[leluz|Leluz]] with the psionicists, while admitting the map did not account for everyone (Session 80). See [[ledger|the Ledger]] for the resulting classification.

## The Tutin Rescue Mission (Sessions 81–82)

Isaiah joined the team for the covert rescue of [[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]] on [[tutin|Tutin]], taking the northern route to [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] (Session 81). He had no weapons or armor of his own; the crew equipped him and assigned him heavy weapons (Session 82).

During the voyage, Isaiah told [[isambard|Isambard]] that he wants to find other unusually intelligent uplifted simians and establish a place for them to live. He knows others like him existed and believes more remain; he and [[tomas|Tomas]] underwent additional experiments on [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 82). On the elevator down to [[hampton|Hampton]], Isaiah grunted at Isambard to make him change the subject when Isambard told an artist a story about buying the simians cheaply (Session 82).
