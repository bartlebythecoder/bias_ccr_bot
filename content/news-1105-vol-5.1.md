# News Dispatch — Volume 5.1

**Summary**: Dispatches spanning days 070 to 092 of 1105 focused entirely on Marcolandia, covering a major refinery monopoly deal between Youst/Foust Refining and Hexham Mining, emergency penal labor recruitment with Ouro Correction, and a systemic shortage of contra-grav surface lifters impacting independent commerce.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Dispatches and Timeline

### 070-1105: Youst / Foust Refinery Scores Sole-Provider Deal with Hexham Mining
- **Location**: [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] (1425)
- **Actors**: [[youst-refining|Youst Refining]], [[hexham-mining|Hexham Mining]]
- Youst Refining Inc (also referred to as Foust Refinery) entered into a comprehensive long-term agreement with Hexham Mining Inc, securing sole refining rights for all Hexham mining output.
- The deal resolves Hexham's chronic processing logistics bottlenecks and is projected to inject millions in revenue into the refinery in its first year.

### 077-1105: Labor Scramble and Penal Work Contracts
- **Location**: [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] (1425)
- **Actors**: [[youst-refining|Youst Refining]], [[ouro-correction-department|Ouro Correction Department]]
- Facing an urgent mandate to triple its operational workforce following the Hexham contract, the refinery scrambled for personnel across all roles (engineers, technicians, janitors, cooks).
- To meet staffing quotas quickly, the refinery entered into a prison labor agreement with the [[ouro-correction-department|Ouro Correction Department]].

### 092-1105: Contra-Grav Surface Lifter Shortage
- **Location**: [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] (1425)
- A critical shortage of contra-grav surface lifters severely disrupted local business and planetary logistics on Marcolandia.
- Independent businesses reported vehicle and transport lines being completely monopolized by exclusive contracts across concentrated industrial operations.
