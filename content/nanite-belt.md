# Nanite Belt

**Summary**: Technological belt containing nanites that lets the guards at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch near Hampton]] regenerate; [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] took one from the dead guard [[rajiv|Rajiv]].

**Last updated**: 2026-11-16

---

## Regenerating Guards

Before the crew reached the ranch, [[deborah|Deborah]] described seeing one of its armed men recover from a cut or bite within an hour, for reasons she could not explain (Session 84).

On Day 236-1105 the captured guard [[rene-desjardins|René Desjardins]] explained that the guards' regeneration came from technological belts containing "some nanite shit". [[rajiv|Rajiv]] wore one; René said he did not (Session 85).

The crew took the threat seriously: [[asher|Asher]] warned that Rajiv could regenerate, and [[sarik|Sarik]] decapitated him after killing him (Session 85).

## The Crew's Belt

BIAS CCR recovered Rajiv's belt at the ambush site west of the ranch (Session 85). How it works, and whether it can be used by the crew, is not yet known.
