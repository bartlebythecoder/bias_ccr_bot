# René Desjardins

**Summary**: Hired guard at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch near Hampton]] captured by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] in an ambush on Day 236-1105; kept alive to answer [[duquette|Duquette]]'s radio check-ins.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-23

---

## Guard at the Ranch

René was one of the guards hired to hold [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] on [[tutin|Tutin]]. He and [[rajiv|Rajiv]] patrolled the terrain around the house, wearing cloth armor beneath their clothes (Session 85).

## Capture (Day 236-1105)

The crew ambushed René and Rajiv as they set out on patrol, west of the ranch. [[tomas|Tomas]] restrained René and kept him from shouting while the others dealt with Rajiv, who was killed (Session 85).

## What He Told the Crew

- [[duquette|Duquette]] stays mostly in the master bedroom. **Alfred**, second-in-command, runs the control center in the great room; **Genrich**, third-in-command, relieves him there. The two share an upstairs room and handle the motion sensors (Session 85).
- On a warning, the guards take cover, then put on combat armor; armored guards go outside while the others come in to suit up (Session 85).
- The generator is in the basement, probably reached from the utility room (Session 85).
- The guards were hired for a month to frighten away anyone approaching, and nobody is allowed on or off the ranch. He did not know who [[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]] was or why he mattered (Session 85).
- The guards' regeneration comes from technological belts containing nanites (see [[nanite-belt|Nanite Belt]]). Rajiv wore one; René said he did not (Session 85).

## Captive

René volunteered to answer Control's radio check-ins, which he said were requests for the patrol to report. [[sarik|Sarik]] argued he should be killed; [[asher|Asher]] wanted him alive to work the radio; [[isambard|Isambard]] said he should be restrained and kept out of any fight. The group agreed to keep him alive for now (Session 85).

Sarik kept René's identification and a photograph of Jennifer, threatening her if René betrayed them. René swore allegiance against Duquette and offered to shoot him (Session 85).

Isambard noticed a patch on René reading "I am not a robot". René denied being a robot and challenged the suggestion (Session 85).

At the end of Day 236 René was still held at the ambush site, as the captured radio carried Control's call for the patrol to check in (Session 85).

## Working the Radio (Session 86)

- **Interrogation:** The crew questioned René about [[rajiv|Rajiv]]'s belt — whether it detected life signs or could be monitored at the ranch. He said he could imitate Rajiv's voice if allowed to speak freely, and that the two of them usually spent hours on patrol. [[beck|Beck]] assessed him over the walkie-talkie and judged him already frightened (Session 86).
- **Untied for the checks:** [[isambard|Isambard]] warned René to use only approved words on the radio and threatened him if he said anything else. The crew agreed to restrain him afterwards rather than leave someone behind to guard him; [[asher|Asher]] argued against killing him yet, in case he knew something useful. René stayed untied for the checks and gave the cover story about a [[rock-squid|rock squid]], but the calls grew more suspicious (Session 86).
- **Duquette's belt:** René said Duquette was the only other person at the ranch with a [[nanite-belt|nanite belt]] (Session 86).
