# Rimward Circuit Collective

**Summary**: A regional interstellar coalition or organization providing economic coordination and humanitarian aid in the Rimward systems.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

The Rimward Circuit Collective is an alliance or cooperative organization operating in the Rimward portions of the subsector. The government of [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] is an active participant or administrative hub within the collective ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## Humanitarian Actions

In 025-1105, the Collective authorized and dispatched an emergency relief package valued at MCr 45.0 to [[pharr|Pharr]] to mitigate an ongoing humanitarian crisis ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).
