# Bobby

**Summary**: Underworld smuggler and brother of Red Hand soldier Joey, killed by Sarik during a hazardous surface trek on Romentino.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role in the Smuggling Operation

Bobby was the brother and trusted accomplice of [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]] soldier [[joey|Joey]] in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 16). On Day 044-1105, Bobby was assigned by Joey to guide drug mule [[tina|Tina]]—who had illicit [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] taped to her torso—across the hazardous, corrosive surface of Romentino to slip into the starport through an abandoned exterior airlock (Session 16).

## Death in the Toxic Waste

Escorting Tina alongside undercover operatives [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[asher|Asher]], Bobby attempted to rush the 30-minute crossing before the Frontier Liner's 4:00 PM launch to [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] (Session 16). When Tina panicked and collapsed in her vacc suit, threatening total mission failure, Sarik created a tactical distraction and executed Bobby with a combat blade out on the frozen plains to free Tina from the operation (Session 16).
