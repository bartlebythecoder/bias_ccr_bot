# News Dispatch — Volume 10

**Summary**: Dispatches from 212-1105: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] CEO [[david-ives|David Ives]] faces two inquiries into [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]]'s Suliman exposé, the [[orslo-jollynn|Jollynn]] murder stalls, and [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]] wins the Subsector Navy's banking.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-13

---

## Dispatches and Timeline

### 212-1105: Jollynn Murder Investigation Stalls
- **Location**: [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] (1324)
- **Actors**: [[orslo-jollynn|Orslo Jollynn]]
- Authorities are no closer to an arrest. The killers are believed to be professionals hired by a rival drug cartel, and to have left the planet.

### 212-1105: Wu-Ketai Under Investigation
- **Location**: [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] (1324)
- **Actors**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]], [[david-ives|David Ives]], [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]], [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]]
- Wu-Ketai President [[david-ives|David Ives]] is under pressure from two separate investigations, one by the Subsector authorities and one by the Imperial authorities, prompted by journalist [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]]'s report on illegal experiments at the company's flagship [[suliman|Suliman Asteroid]].
- The investigations have weighed on the share prices of Wu-Ketai and of its largest shareholder, [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]].

### 212-1105: Megacorporation News Summary
- **Actors**: [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]]
- **Arico**: Hortalez et Cie denies any knowledge of Wu-Ketai's alleged illegal experiments on aging. Rival Tukera Lines has bought an undisclosed number of shares from Hortalez; some reports say the purchase followed pressure from the Imperial governor's office.
- **Marcolandia**: In a surprise move, Ling Standard Products bought enough shares to take a controlling interest in mining and refinery operations in the [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] system, finally winning control of a number of its refineries.
- **Kidderminster**: Hortalez et Cie's banking line became the official financial provider for the Subsector Navy, following its naval contract win earlier in the year. The Navy's 'redshirts' are leaving Tukera Lines.
- **Market Trading Since Last Summary**:
  - Hortalez et Cie: Down 3 to 183
  - Ling Standard Products: Up 3.5 to 145
  - Tukera Lines: Down 0.5 to 97

### 212-1105: Psalmist Watch
- **Actors**: [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]
- **Arico**: A [[freeport|Freeport Station]] Psalmist leader accused of financial irregularities has damaged Psalmist influence on Arico, though their popularity on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] appears unaffected. Estimated influence: was 5/6, now 4/6.
- **Kidderminster**: An official Psalmist Center has opened on [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]], a sign of the movement's growing acceptance that would have seemed impossible a year ago. Lobbying and advertising in the subsector capital have softened the public mood. Estimated influence: was 2/6, now 3/6.
- **Nesvorny and Sanlucar**: Formal Psalmist organizations have started on [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] and [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar]]. Estimated influence: was 1/6, now 2/6.
