# Pil

**Summary**: Local black marketeer and fixer operating out of Errol's cafe in Startown Liberty on Romentino who supplied illicit munitions to the crew.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Black Market Brokerage

Pil is a seasoned fixer and contraband broker operating out of Errol's cafe, an establishment frequented by both off-world travelers and locals in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 11). Capable of procuring controlled technologies and black-market ordnance, Pil operates under the protection of informal starport networks (Session 11).

## Transactions with the Crew

On Day 39-1105, child guide [[sydney|Sydney]] introduced the [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] operatives to Pil (Session 11):
- Welcomed the crew with drinks of "Red Shot," a potent local moonshine (Session 11).
- Contracted with [[isambard|Isambard]] to supply an illicit cache of assorted combat grenades (Session 11).
- Guided the team through the Shanty handmade goods market, where [[sarik|Sarik]] acquired a concealed walking stick rifle (Session 11).
