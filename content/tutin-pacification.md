# Tutin Pacification

**Summary**: Prolonged 1077–1083 counter-insurgency on Tutin where [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] military forces crushed the militant Order of the Psalms, laying the groundwork for the modern 134 Psalms movement.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

---

## Outbreak of Hostilities (1077)

In 1077, disputed election results on the agricultural/frontier world of [[tutin|Tutin]] (1624, UWP C764265-6) saw a militant religious sect, *The Order of the Psalms*, claim sovereign authority. Alarmed by the prospect of an aggressive theocracy, the subsector government at [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] deployed an expeditionary army to forcefully depose the Order.

## The Five-Year Insurgency

The war devolved into grueling guerrilla combat across rugged terrain:
- **Varos Offensive (1082)**: The Order launched a massive surprise offensive, inflicting heavy casualties on Kidderminster forces and winning a major propaganda triumph.
- **Final Defeat (1083)**: Kidderminster committed overwhelming mechanized reinforcements and high-tech weaponry, systematically eliminating the Order's leadership by late 1083.

## Long-Term Repercussions

- **Rise of the 134 Psalms**: While the political-militant Order was exterminated, its non-political theological wing—the **134 Psalms**—dissociated from the insurgency, spreading peacefully across the subsector and evolving into the modern [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement.
- **The Dawn Seekers**: The Psalmist people and families left over from the war remain on Tutin as the [[dawn-seekers|Dawn Seekers]], nomads who live off the land and mostly stay in hiding.
- **Devastation of Tutin**: The planet's infrastructure collapsed. In 1105, Tutin remains a closely monitored protectorate under direct Kidderminster administration, its full-time population reduced to fewer than 1,000 residents subject to strict residency vetting.

## Veterans & Memorials (Session 83)

- [[beck|Beck]] served seven years in the pacification, including the Varos counteroffensive. Her rivalry with Lazlo Kovacs, whom she believes to be [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]], began here (Session 83).
- A bronze statue of Lars Tanaka, a hero of a battle near [[hampton|Hampton]] during the pacification, stands near the town center (Session 83).
