# Morgan

**Summary**: Former head of security at Csabai Palace who was fired following the Beaugard theft, whose surveillance insight helped BIAS CCR solve the case.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Service & Termination

Morgan served as the chief of protective security at [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]], managing automated sensor arrays, access perimeters, and guards for [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] (Sessions 22–23). On Day 57-1105, when the historic [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] vanished from the family gallery, head butler [[sterling|Sterling]] summarily dismissed Morgan from service, holding him responsible for the breach (Sessions 22–23).

## Surveillance Breakthrough

Following his termination, Morgan relocated to [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] (Sessions 22–23). He was located by operative [[isambard|Isambard]], who questioned him regarding the palace's monitoring systems (Sessions 22–23). Morgan provided the crucial forensic insight that cracked the case: although the palace's surveillance video feeds automatically switched off in the presence of noble family members, the system timestamps recording when the cameras powered off and on remained intact, allowing investigators to track movements and detours through unmonitored hallways (Sessions 22–23).
