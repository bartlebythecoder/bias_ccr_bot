# Cutter

**Summary**: Second in command to [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]] in the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]], residing at [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] in the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]].

**Last updated**: 2026-10-20

---

## Position in the Meinhoff Brigade

Cutter serves as second in command to [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]], the criminal overlord of the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]). In that role he stands directly beneath Yandee in the Brigade's hierarchy, above the syndicate's enforcers, brokers, and station-level operators.

## Residence at Eternity Station

Cutter resides at [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], the quasi-legal habitat in the lawless [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] belt that the Brigade governs in all but name ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]). The station is where Yandee holds court and where the Brigade's enforcement arm, the **Tempest Company**, patrols the decks.
