# Hal the Grocer

**Summary**: Imposing Startown merchant, protector of Sydney Antonelli, and connector for The Red Hand who revealed Blue Lou Boyle's syndicate truce.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role & Demeanor

Hal the Grocer is a colossal, physically imposing shopkeeper operating in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 12). Despite his intimidating stature, Hal serves as a protective guardian for young street guide [[sydney|Sydney Antonelli]] and functions as an established "connector" and intelligence conduit for [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]] (Session 12).

## Underworld Revelations

Introduced to [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] by fixer [[pil|Pil]] on Day 42-1105, Hal provided pivotal intelligence regarding Startown's criminal balance of power (Session 12):
- Revealed that off-world Demon Prince [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]] negotiated a landmark peace between The Red Hand and Romentino's financial oligarchy, granting the gang a monopoly on vice while prohibiting kidnappings and the export of [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] (Session 12).
- Disclosed that the ban on kidnappings strained rank-and-file soldiers such as his associate [[joey|Joey]], contributing to the emergence of the [[real-red-hand|Real Red Hand]] splinter movement (Session 12).
- Offered to arrange an introductory meeting between the crew and Joey (Session 12).
