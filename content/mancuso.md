# Mancuso

**Summary**: Maintenance plumber at K.C. Station on Mirassol who saved scientist George Castle from a giant spider attack during the station evacuation.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Heroism on Mirassol

Mancuso was a civilian maintenance plumber employed in the Workshop division of [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]] (Session 17). During the nocturnal swarms of [[mirassol-spider|Mirassol Spiders]] that forced the total evacuation of the research post in Day 44-1105, Mancuso intervened to save the life of scientist [[george-castle|George Castle]] during a violent arachnid attack (Session 17).
