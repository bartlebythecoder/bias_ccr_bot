# Agnes Tuttle

**Summary**: Head housekeeper of Csabai Palace who oversees domestic maintenance, room servicing, and household staff for Viscount Rittenhouse.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role & Household Oversight

Mrs. Agnes Tuttle is the lead housekeeper at [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]], managing domestic cleaning, chamber servicing, and guest suite maintenance across the sprawling administrative estate on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Sessions 22–23).

Following the theft of the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] on Day 57-1105, Mrs. Tuttle was listed among the household staff initially questioned by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] regarding access to the gallery corridors and noble chambers before the painting was recovered in [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]]'s suite (Sessions 22–23).

---

## Treachery and The Doves Infiltration

On Day 91-1105, Mrs. Tuttle was revealed to be a secret agent embedded within [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] on behalf of the extremist Psalmist cult, [[the-doves|The Doves]] (Session 26). Operating with palace footmen under her control, Tuttle orchestrated an ambush to paralyze [[asher|Asher]] with neurotoxin in the palace library, seize the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (referred to in her orders as the "snow globe"), and incinerate his paralyzed body down a garbage chute (Session 26). The assassination plot was foiled only when Asher experienced a precognitive prophetic vision from the artifact and fled the palace (Session 26).
