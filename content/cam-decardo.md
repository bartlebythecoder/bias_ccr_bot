# Cam Decardo

**Summary**: Anti-psionic activist on [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] who warned BIAS CCR about the telepath [[julie|Julie]] and urged them to wear psionic defense helmets.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Cam Decardo** (also referenced as *Caminente Decardo*) is an operative and anti-psionic activist on [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] who contacted [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] regarding psychic threats and telepathic tracking (Session 48; [[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).

## Activities & Role

* **Warning on Julie & Telepaths**: During the search for [[sarik|Sarik]] following his abduction, Cam Decardo contacted [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] (speaking with [[beck|Beck]] at [[asher|Asher's]] communications hub). Decardo reported that his network and associates were actively gathering leads on the rogue telepath [[julie|Julie]], and heavily urged the crew to equip psionic defense helmets to protect against telepathic assault and psionic Suggestion (Session 48).
* **Cairo District Background**: Planetary news dispatches also linked an activist by the name of Caminente Decardo to anti-psionic civil tensions in the Cairo district of Arico El Nuevo around day 130-1105 ([[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).
* **Leverage Against Julie**: During the apartment standoff, [[beck|Beck]] utilized Cam Decardo's anti-psionic network and aggressive posture as major psychological leverage against [[julie|Julie]], threatening to expose her psychic identity and starship berth to Decardo and port authorities if she refused to negotiate in good faith (Session 49).
* **Death in Cairo District Riots**: On the morning of Day 132-1105, news broadcasts circulating across Freeport confirmed that three vigilantes had been killed during anti-psionic mob violence in the Cairo district, identifying Cam Decardo among the dead (Session 50; [[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).
