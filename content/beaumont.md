# Beaumont

**Summary**: System 1422 in Solo Sector Subsector J, an authoritarian world ruled by [[supreme-leader-volkov|Supreme Leader Volkov]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Beaumont (1422) carries the UWP designation B6A07AA-9 ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]). The world is governed under the autocratic regime of [[supreme-leader-volkov|Supreme Leader Volkov]], titled "Leader for Life."

## External Politics

Beaumont maintains a suspicious, hardline posture toward regional rebellions and anti-authoritarian uprisings. In 035-1105, Beaumont's leadership formally urged the Imperium to keep the newly established [[commonwealth-states-of-sanlucar|Commonwealth States of Sanlucar]] under tight military surveillance ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]).
