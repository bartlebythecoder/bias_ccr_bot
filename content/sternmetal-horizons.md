# Sternmetal Horizons

**Summary**: Imperial megacorporation dominating interplanetary mining, city-scale power generation equipment, starship reactors, and automated food synthesis technology.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Power and Mining Monopoly

Sternmetal Horizons, LIC is a foundational industrial megacorporation operating extensively across Sector Solo-6:
- **Energy Infrastructure**: The leading manufacturer of power generation systems across the Imperium, building reactor systems for starships, air/ground combat vehicles, metropolitan power grids, and deep-space orbital installations.
- **Resource Extraction**: Extensive automated deep-core planetary mining networks.
- **Food Synthesis**: The single largest manufacturer of automated food synthesis equipment in the Third Imperium, supplying vital nutritional technology to vacuum worlds and orbital stations.
