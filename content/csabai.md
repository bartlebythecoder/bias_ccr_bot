# Csabai

**Summary**: Populous world 1626 in Solo Sector Subsector J, ruled by Viscount Rittenhouse, marked by extreme stratification, corporate IT contract battles, and the arrest of Psalmist leader Darlene Suitor.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview and Feudal Rule

Csabai (1626) is a high-tech, populous world carrying the UWP profile A985898-A ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]).
- **Feudal Administration**: Governed by **[[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]]** of [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]], whose family crest displays the native *csabaipede* ([[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]]).
- **Diplomatic Realignment**: While Rittenhouse is a loyal cousin to [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]] of Kidderminster, he maintains profitable commercial alliances with [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], which displaced [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] in 1105 to claim Csabai's planetary IT infrastructure contract ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]; [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]]).

## Urban Sectors and Social Conditions

Csabai is divided into distinct, rigidly segregated urban environments:
- **Apex**: Enclave of the ultra-wealthy, hosting scientific conventions, black-market longevity therapies, and elite private medical galas ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]).
- **Nexus**: Sprawling residential maze experiencing population pressure, severe housing deficits, and street warfare ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]).
- **Transit**: Starport and logistics center under intense customs interdiction against smuggling cartels ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]).
- **Stratus**: Bohemian art quarter subject to art forgery scandals ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]).

## Crime and Religious Scandals (1105)

- **Psalmist Discreditation**: In 098-1105, security forces smashed a major criminal syndicate, arresting [[darlene-suitor|Darlene Suitor]], head of the Csabai Psalmist Center ([[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]), on charges of narcotics trafficking, racketeering, and murder, forcing the local church into hiding ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]; [[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
- **Cultural Tour**: Pop icon [[sabrina|Sabrina]] performed across the planet under Duchess Margaret's Grand Tour initiative ([[news-1105-vol-5|News Dispatch — Volume 5]]).

- **BIAS CCR Evacuation Destination**: In early 1105, [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] offered his private luxury star yacht to transport [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] from Romentino directly to Csabai to escape corporate and syndicate pursuit (Session 18).

## Destination of BIAS CCR

On Day 47-1105, facing planetary arrest warrants on Romentino, BIAS CCR evacuated aboard [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s luxury yacht with the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], Tina, and their corporate treasury, setting course for Csabai (Session 19).

## Intelligence from Isidora and Portents of Peril

During BIAS CCR's Jump transit to Csabai aboard [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s yacht, steward [[isidora|Isidora]] briefed the team on local societal and aristocratic customs (Session 20). The team's impending arrival was overshadowed by an ominous psionic premonition experienced by [[sky|Sky]] through the flashing [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], warning that extreme danger awaited them on the world (Session 20).

## Arrival of BIAS CCR and Public Celebrity

On Day 56-1105, [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] arrived on Csabai aboard [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s yacht following a 43-hour sub-light in-system burn (Session 21). Baltimore's media promotion resulted in a swarm of reporters and enthusiastic crowds at the starport tarmac (Session 21). Immediately upon arrival, the team was offered three separate operational contracts: locating a hidden painting belonging to [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]], managing a covert corporate-underworld data transfer, or stealing a proprietary therapeutic pharmaceutical to heal the chronic afflictions of [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] (Session 21).

## The Beaugard Palace Crisis and Augmentation Sabbatical

On Day 57-1105, the capital of Csabai became the center of an art heist scandal when the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] vanished from the [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] gallery (Sessions 22–23). Solved by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] via electronic surveillance analysis and security contacts at [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]], the resolution earned the team a one-month sabbatical in Viscount Rittenhouse's high-tech physical augmentation clinic (Sessions 22–23).

## The Doves Extremist Threat and Irklan Strikes

On Day 89-1105, following the team's release from the Rittenhouse Clinic, Csabai became the battleground for religious and martial intrigue when broker [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] was murdered at [[henrys-tavern|Henry's Tavern]] in [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] (Session 24). BIAS CCR uncovered a radical Psalmist terror cell known as [[the-doves|The Doves]], who plotted the death of [[darnes|Darnes]] and targeted the team for their precursor artifact (Session 24).

## The Cal's Dream Homicides and the Floating City of Apex

On Days 90–91-1105, the capital district was shaken by the discovery of three slain crew members hidden aboard ***[[cals-dream|Cal's Dream]]*** at Starport Alpha, investigated by [[lt-tony-mcintyre|Lt. Tony McIntyre]] and royal attaché [[margaux-evans|Margaux Evans]] (Session 25). Inquiries led operatives into the floating repulsor-city of [[apex|Apex]] to access high-tech municipal archives and the regional headquarters of [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] (Session 25).

## Basin City and the Psalmist Center Infiltration

On Days 92–93-1105, operations expanded into the terrestrial metropolis of [[basin-city|Basin City]], where the [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] was identified as the hub of a young Psalmist extremist who contracted an [[irklan|Irklan]] hit through mechanic [[reshka|Reshka]] (Session 27). Simultaneously, cyber intrusions in [[apex|Apex]] unmasked corporate ties linking the "Witchfinder General" directly to [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]] (Session 27).
