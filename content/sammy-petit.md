# Sammy Petit

**Summary**: Hot-tempered martial arts instructor and proprietor of Sammy's Dojo in Basin City on Csabai, defeated in a ring match by Isambard.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Personality & Dojo Management

Sammy Petit is a short-tempered, physical martial artist and owner of [[sammys-dojo|Sammy's Dojo]], a combat sports gym located in [[basin-city|Basin City]] on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 29). Harboring deep xenophobic contempt for off-worlders, Petit was a personal trainer and acquaintance of Psalmist courier [[jacob-godard|Jacob Godard]] (Session 29).

## The Challenge Match

On Day 94-1105, when [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] questioned Petit regarding Godard's flight, Petit challenged the team to an unarmed wager bout in the ring: a victory for the operatives would earn Cr 1,000 and Godard's location, while defeat would cost them Cr 5,000 (Session 29). 

Operative [[isambard|Isambard]] accepted the bout (Session 29). Although Petit initially slammed Isambard to the ground, Isambard quickly countered with expert joint manipulation and locked Petit into a submission chokehold (Session 29). Subdued and gasping for breath, Petit surrendered the Cr 1,000 and confessed that Godard had gone to [[freeport-university|Freeport University]] to meet [[chloe-vinit|Chloe Vinit]] (Session 29).
