# BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution

**Summary**: Legally registered corporate consulting and private security firm established on [[romentino|Romentino]] by [[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[sarik|Sarik]] as an operational cover.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Establishment & Incorporation

BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution (BIAS CCR) was formally chartered on Day 39-1105 in the commercial district of [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] by operative [[beck|Beck]] (Session 11). The acronym derives from the names of the founding partners:
- **[[beck|Beck]]**: Executive administration, psychological profiling, and negotiation.
- **[[isambard|Isambard]]**: Field logistics, mechanics, and security operations.
- **[[asher|Asher]]**: Scientific diagnostics, chemical research, and cyber-warfare.
- **[[sarik|Sarik]]**: Physical enforcement, tactical reconnaissance, and heavy weapons.

## Corporate Purpose & Operations

Chartered ostensibly as a high-end corporate dispute resolution, risk assessment, and crisis consulting agency, the corporate identity provides legitimate legal cover for the crew's covert intelligence, artifact recovery, and mercenary contracting operations across Sector Solo-6 (Session 11).

## Major Case Milestones

On Day 044-1105, BIAS CCR secured its first major commercial fee of **MCr 1.0** from client [[christine|Christine Butler]] following their successful unmasking and resolution of the staged kidnapping of [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]] (Session 15).

## Arrival on Csabai and Three Contracts

On Day 56-1105, BIAS CCR arrived on Csabai to a media spectacle orchestrated by [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] (Session 21). With [[tina|Tina]] departing after leaving an ominous warning note, the firm was presented with three potential operations: assisting with a hidden painting belonging to [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]], managing a covert corporate-underworld data transfer, or executing a corporate raid to acquire an experimental therapeutic drug capable of healing the persistent medical impairments of [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] (Session 21).

## The Beaugard Recovery and Augmentation Sabbatical

On Day 57-1105, BIAS CCR was retained by [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] through [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] to investigate the theft of the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] within [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] (Sessions 22–23). Unmasking the inside conspiracy orchestrated by [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]] and [[lord-edgar|Lord Edgar]], the operatives recovered the artwork in time for the grand gala (Sessions 22–23). In addition to gala tickets and wardrobe, the firm was granted one month in the Viscount's elite augmentation clinic, where all four principals completed physical characteristic enhancements (Sessions 22–23).

## Target of The Doves and Irklan Ambush

On Day 89-1105, following the completion of their clinic augmentations and [[isambard|Isambard]]'s temporary Scout mobilization, BIAS CCR was targeted on Csabai (Session 24). After investigating the poisoned dart murder of broker [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]], [[asher|Asher]] received a precognitive vision from the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] that allowed the operatives to execute an [[irklan|Irklan]] assassin in a counter-ambush at Berth 17 (Session 24). Intelligence recovered from Singh's ship exposed [[the-doves|The Doves]]—a militant Psalmist cell actively planning to kill the team and seize the Orb (Session 24).

## Investigation of the Cal's Dream Murders and The Doves

On Days 90–91-1105 on Csabai, BIAS CCR assisted police detective [[lt-tony-mcintyre|Lt. Tony McIntyre]] after three slain crew members were found aboard ***[[cals-dream|Cal's Dream]]*** (Session 25). The team liaised with royal attaché [[margaux-evans|Margaux Evans]], who advised them to leave Csabai soon, while [[sarik|Sarik]] obtained [[csabaipede|Csabaipede]] antidote from [[irklan|Irklan]] liaison [[inkulan|Inkulan]] and [[asher|Asher]] scoured [[apex|Apex]] and [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] for [[darnes|Darnes]] (Session 25).

## Tracing the Assassin to Basin City and Unmasking Erebus Power

On Days 92–93-1105, BIAS CCR paid Cr 75,000 to starport mechanic [[reshka|Reshka]] to secure a photograph of the Psalmist client who hired the [[irklan|Irklan]] assassin, prompting [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] to infiltrate the [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] in [[basin-city|Basin City]] (Session 27). Concurrently, [[asher|Asher]] breached LSP mainframes, discovering that the "Witchfinder General" [[darnes|Darnes]] was a full-time operative of [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]], connecting the Csabai intrigue directly back to the Romentino cloning conspiracy (Session 27).

## Undercover Operation on Suliman
On Day 115-1105, BIAS CCR mounted a covert penetration of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] mining asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] disguised as a workers' rights lobby group on a fact-finding mission (Session 34). Operating under fabricated identities crafted by [[asher|Asher]], the operatives cleared security interrogations, linked up with union leader [[carl-parker|Carl Parker]] in [[the-warrens|The Warrens]], and debriefed corporate security guard [[hector|Hector Nilasen]] at [[derry|Derry]] regarding the disappearance of [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]]. Asher breached Wu-Ketai's internal servers, uncovering classified corporate genetic projects, principally [[project-chimera|Project Chimera]] (Session 34).

Following an electrified-knuckle brawl in the Warrens on the night of Day 115, the operatives were detained overnight by [[anderton|Officer Anderton]]. On Day 116, hiring local guide [[orla|Orla]] and consulting brokers [[cardo|Cardo]] and [[romero|Romero]], the firm discovered that three prominent figures had vanished: geneticist Richard Lundquist, security guard Hector Nilasen, and Chief of Corporate Security [[marcus-fortune|Marcus Fortune]]. Hacking internal surveillance feeds, the team tracked all three to the unmonitored core laboratories and discovered a secret hospital bed being transported through Fortune's private suite (Session 35).

## Infiltration of the Core Laboratories
Later on Day 116, the firm tracked Hector's disappearance to a surveillance blind spot inside [[the-blue-moose|The Blue Moose]] tavern (Session 36). With [[sky|Sky]] and [[beck|Beck]] distracting off-duty workers, Asher and Sarik identified a concealed utility entrance behind the restroom. Beck stole an executive scientist pass on the upper levels, enabling Isambard to unlock the security passage. Disguised in janitorial uniforms and lab coats, BIAS CCR penetrated the active laboratories—where they were immediately greeted by their former [[mirassol|Mirassol]] colleague, [[george-castle|Dr. George Castle]] (Session 36).

## Uncovering Project Echo Bloom
Rendezvousing with Castle at Red's Restaurant in Delta Green on Day 117, BIAS CCR learned of his secret mission from the [[children-of-the-psalms|Psalmist Movement]] to investigate canine uplift research (Session 37). Following a lead to technician [[david-orana|David Orana]], Beck and Asher investigated the Suliman Care Facility, discovering that missing researcher [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]] and security guard [[hector|Hector Nilasen]] had been subjected to [[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]] under **[[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]]**, forcibly stealing their lifespan and leaving them aged into decrepit senility (Session 37).

## Infiltration of the Yellow Labs & New Bounties
After reporting Richard's fate to [[joanna-lundquist|Joanna Lundquist]], BIAS CCR received their Cr 100,000 fee and accepted new contracts: Cr 100,000 for Richard's verified DNA and Cr 1,000,000 (MCr 1.0) for concrete proof of the Chrono-Energetic Siphoning machine (Session 38). Rearming with crop dusters and a TL-12 stunner from [[orla|Orla]], the team neutralized the Green Area security office, while Asher processed Lundquist's DNA. Moving deeper into the Yellow Labs under leadership intel on [[dr-young|Dr. Young]] and [[dr-harknett|Dr. Harknett]], Sarik slipped into the sub-level maintenance tunnels while Asher and Beck entered the medical sector—just as a panicked uplifted simian escaped toward them (Session 38).

## Simian Revolt, Chrono Lab Breach, and Asteroid Escape
Evading the running simian, Beck and Asher linked back up with Sarik and Isambard in the red section animal holding pens after Sarik shorted the electronic lock with a crop duster (Session 39). Discovering that the uplifted simians possessed articulate speech, BIAS CCR struck an alliance, liberating the apes in exchange for a synchronized corridor revolt rounding up laboratory staff. Subduing returning researcher [[dr-vance|Dr. Vance]], the operatives wheeled her on a gurney and used her biometric credentials to breach the chrono lab. The team captured complete video documentation of the operational [[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]] apparatus, radioed [[sky|Sky]] to extract informant [[romero|Romero]] to [[freeport|Freeport]], and escaped Suliman aboard an emergency launch craft into deep space (Session 39).

## Operations on Eternity Station
On Day 118-1105, BIAS CCR steered their stolen corporate launch to the uncertified habitat [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] to avoid corporate patrols at Freeport (Session 40). After transferring ten liberated simians to an underground network, two articulate primates—[[isaiah|Isaiah]] and [[thomas|Thomas]]—remained with the team. Countering suspicions from dockmaster [[loshi|Loshi]] and broker [[angus|Angus]], the team adopted the cover identity [[petow|PETOW]] (*People for the Ethical Treatment of Uplifted Workers*). Guided by [[dana-westby|Dana Westby]] through The Icebox, the operatives met Teamster leader [[reidmar|Reidmar]] at Stellar Burn. To secure a vital pass extension while awaiting extraction by [[sky|Sky]], BIAS CCR funded a Cr 10,000 poker buy-in, enabling Isambard, Isaiah, and Sarik to expose [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff]] card cheat [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]] (Session 40).

## Exposing the Black Box & Sargon's Murder
On Day 119-1105, as news broke of Reidmar's freighters being captured by [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]], BIAS CCR pursued Sargon's cybernetic secrets (Session 41). Paying informant [[roxy|Roxy]] for Sargon's girlfriend [[katarin|Katarin]]'s address, the team parried hostile resident [[mosh|Mosh]] and negotiated for intel pointing to street surgeon [[ricardo|Ricardo]] in The Icebox. Escalating to elite cyberneticist [[solon|Solon]], Isambard, Asher, and Sarik forced entry and held the clinic at gunpoint, coercing Solon into confessing that he had installed a total-recall "black box" cognitive module in Sargon. After downloading Solon's records, the team presented the forensic proof to Reidmar—who summoned them back mere hours later with the breaking news that Sargon had been murdered (Session 41).

## Sargon Murder Investigation & Tempest Mercenary Ambush
Retained by Reidmar to clear the Teamsters and investigate the homicide, BIAS CCR questioned Sargon's sociopathic bodyguard [[ripley|Ripley]] and examined Sargon's ransacked quarters, discovering signs of violent blunt-force struggle, recent intimacy, and an intact black box implant (Session 42). Questioning Katarin revealed a bloodied knife and directed the team to Bunk 43 to confront [[sargons-wife|Sargon's Wife]]. While station oxygen stick logs placed Katarin and [[mosh|Mosh]] at the murder scene, four heavily armed enforcers from the Tempest Mercenary Company ambushed the operatives, placing them under arrest for their armed assault on cyberneticist [[solon|Solon]] (Session 42).

## Disabling Tempest Enforcers & Audience with Demon Prince Yandee
Beck neutralized the arresting Tempest squad using rapid sedatives, allowing BIAS CCR to take refuge in Reidmar's safehouse (Session 43). Asher decrypted Sargon's black box module, uncovering sensory records proving Mosh killed Sargon. Reidmar escorted the crew before [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]], who granted BIAS CCR a syndicate pardon for the Solon clinic raid in exchange for damages and an apology. Furthermore, Yandee commissioned the operatives with two MCr 2.0 contracts: to assassinate the leader of [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] and recapture Reidmar's hijacked fleet (Session 43).

## Stratemeyer Bounty Selection, Cybernetic Sabbatical & Hunter's Heroes Rivalry
On Day 120-1105, BIAS CCR evaluated their mission options: assassinating the clandestine leader of [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] (MCr 2.0), recapturing Reidmar's hijacked fleet (MCr 2.0), or investigating a local station comms failure (Cr 100–250) (Session 44). Selecting the Stratemeyer leader contract as the least time-sensitive strategic operation, the operatives scheduled major cybernetic augmentations at The Chopshop and committed to a week-long recovery sabbatical in the healing slickbays. Upon learning that rival mercenary outfit [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] had accepted the same Stratemeyer assassination bounty and were leaving immediately, [[beck|Beck]] tagged one of their operatives and their starship hull with tracking bugs to monitor the competition while BIAS CCR healed (Session 44).

## Payout Release, Dark Web Bounty, and the Soto Case on Freeport
On Day 127-1105, emerging from the healing slickbays, BIAS CCR received word that **Cr 900,000** in frozen funds from the [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]] ransom had been released by [[ling-standard-products|LSP Financial]] into Kidderminster accounts (Session 45). Concurrently, a **Cr 1,000,000** dark web bounty on [[sarik|Sarik]] was uncovered originating from Maseru, alongside a cryptic clue: *"Louise Soto did not have to die."* On Day 130-1105, the team deployed to [[freeport|Freeport]], liaising with [[ridpath|Attorney Ridpath]] and interviewing [[inspector-coker|Inspector Coker]] regarding the assassination of [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]]. While police focused on her violent ex-boyfriend [[david-parker|David Parker]], BIAS CCR deduced that the use of a banned ballistic firearm in The Lux pointed toward a premeditated syndicate execution linked to [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]]. Meanwhile, Beck identified an electronic tracker planted on her by [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] and reverse-pickpocketed it onto Club Strata bouncer [[andre|Andre]] (Session 45).

## Multi-Pronged Investigation & The Mysterious Townhouse Visitor
On the evening of Day 130-1105, following contact from Soto's fearful friend Julie, BIAS CCR executed a three-pronged operation (Session 46):
1. **Stadler Deal**: Isambard and Sky bought footage at the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]] revealing Parker had been violently kidnapped by an armed strike team.
2. **Hunter's Heroes Standoff**: Beck and Asher infiltrated Hunter's Heroes' townhouse safehouse, discovering the rivals held Parker captive; Beck interrogated Parker, proved his innocence, and deceptively affirmed his guilt to mislead the rivals.
3. **Wu-Ketai Tower Infiltration**: Sarik breached the corporate tower via a tavern fire distraction, discovering contracts relating to Project [[metropolis-station|Metropolis]], Carbrooke Minerals, and an Ancient precursor artifact on the [[janus|Janus]] asteroid.
Upon returning to their Freeport headquarters, the team was confronted by an unexpected woman sitting comfortably with [[isaiah|Isaiah]] and [[thomas|Thomas]], greeting them with *"Hello, you must be BIAS CCR."* (Session 46).

## Risiano Tiler Interview, Julie's Ambush & Sarik's Abduction
The mysterious visitor was revealed as independent journalist [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]], who sought information on BIAS CCR's activities at [[suliman|Suliman]] while probing illegal elite personality backup and consciousness transfer tech (Session 47). The crew exchanged sanitized second-hand accounts of Suliman vitality extraction and [[morella-station|Morella Station]] cloning in return for future Syndicate intelligence.

On Day 131-1105, a woman claiming to be [[julie|Julie]] arrived anxious and claiming to be pursued. Escorted alone by [[sarik|Sarik]] to retrieve medication, she ambushed him with psionic *Suggestion*, incapacitating and abducting him (Session 47). Realizing she was an imposter telepathic assassin seeking the Cr 1M bounty on Sarik, BIAS CCR acquired anti-psionic defense helmets, initiated trace protocols via Asher, and commissioned broker [[stadler|Stadler]] to circulate her photo at the starports (Session 47). Concurrently, an anonymous tip asked *"Where is Conde Borbon?"*, which rival mercenaries [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] claimed to know (Session 47).

## Acoustic Triangulation, the Belladonna, and Julie's Ransom Deal
Formulating a novel tracking method, [[beck|Beck]] and [[asher|Asher]] boosted Sarik's earbud receiver while [[sky|Sky]] set off an air horn at transit stations across the Freeport LRT network (Session 48). Vetted intel from Risiano Tiler tied target [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]] to the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] via the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]] and placed his starship, the ***[[belladonna|Belladonna]]***, at Freeport's Class A starport. Isambard deployed to the starport to locate the vessel while Asher's acoustic telemetry successfully isolated Sarik's location to an LRT station building (Session 48). 

Around 6:00 PM, Sarik was sedated and crated. Beck and Sky staked out the building while Asher and [[ridpath|Mr. Ridpath]] engaged Julie in a three-way call at 7:00 PM. Threatening Sarik's life, Julie offered to release him unharmed if BIAS CCR completed an undisclosed mission on her behalf—an agreement Ridpath accepted while the crew closed in on the building (Session 48).

## The Condo Standoff, Sarik's Rescue & Syndicate Contract Restructuring
On the evening of Day 131-1105, Asher breached the condo tower's surveillance feeds, guiding Beck and Sky to Julie's 8th-floor apartment (Session 49). Beck bypassed the door lock, entering to find Julie wielding a dead-man trigger. Beck defused the lethal impasse through strategic counter-threats and dealmaking:
- Threatening to leak Julie's starship coordinates and telepathic identity to anti-psionic agitator [[cam-decardo|Cam Decardo]] and starport security.
- Offering Julie a **one-sixth share** (approx. Cr 333,333) of [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee's]] MCr 2.0 [[the-stratemeyers|Stratemeyer]] assassination contract, backed by a Cr 100,000 escrow if the target was not eliminated in two months.
- Julie surrendered Sarik, renounced the Cr 1M bounty, and departed peacefully under Asher and Isambard's surveillance, boarding the shielded ***[[belladonna|Belladonna]]*** at the starport. All future communications with Julie were routed through [[ridpath|Attorney Ridpath]] (Session 49).

Beck resuscitated Sarik with commercial fast-drug antidote, while simian analyst [[isaiah|Isaiah]] presented an intelligence debrief linking the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], [[ling-standard-products|LSP]], and the escalating anti-telepath riots (Session 49).

## Stardust Investigation, Jonestown Lead & The True Target Breakthrough
On Day 132-1105, while Isambard rested post-surgery and news feeds confirmed the death of anti-psionic activist [[cam-decardo|Cam Decardo]], an encrypted tip from [[julie|Julie]] claimed the owner of the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]] sought a hit on [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]] (Session 50). 

Beck and Sarik investigated the Stardust Casino in North Lux, interviewing owner [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]] and security chief [[vince|Vince]], before purchasing a lead on Borbon's best friend from broker [[stadler|Stadler]]. In the Homestead Apartments of Jonestown, they interrogated [[rico-mathers|Rico Mathers]], learning that Borbon was an independent dealer and that rival mercenary squad [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] (a "big guy in an orange suit") had questioned Rico earlier that morning (Session 50).

Back at the comms hub, Asher cracked the case wide open: analysis of surveillance footage outside [[club-strata|Club Strata]] proved that Conde Borbon was standing directly in front of [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] when she was shot, establishing that Borbon—not Soto—was the assassin's intended target (Session 50).

## Frey Surveillance, Rico Rapport & The Cafe Dimitri Raid
Later on Day 132-1105, operations progressed across two fronts (Session 51):
1. **Frey Surveillance**: [[sky|Sky]] mapped Ortega Frey's daily grav car movements across North Lux, observing her single bodyguard protocols and solitary retail visits.
2. **Jonestown & Cafe Dimitri**: In the Homestead Apartments, Beck spent hours bonding with Rico Mathers over video games, discovering Borbon went off-grid without a comms phone and learning of [[cafe-dimitri|Cafe Dimitri]], a fortified clubhouse for the rising [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]] linked to operative [[montanez|Montañez]]. Journalist [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]] corroborated the clubhouse's status.

At 7:30 PM, Beck set up street overwatch while Sarik infiltrated Cafe Dimitri, discovering a horrific triple execution scene (a man bound to a chair in pooled blood and two gunshot corpses). Asher verified that planetary police bands were silent, while Beck's scanner detected hostile electronic counter-surveillance. When two figures equipped with psionic shield headsets approached the building, Beck and Sarik laid down a foam fire extinguisher screen, successfully escaping into the Jonestown alleys with an unpowered laptop and recovered syndicate paperwork (Session 51).

## The Palmas Notes, Omni Money Laundering & Park 32 Standoff
Late on Day 132-1105, BIAS CCR faced an aggressive escalation from rival mercenaries [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] (Session 52):
- **Palmas's Taunt**: Inside the seized laptop, Beck found a handwritten note from Hunter's Heroes operative [[palmas|Palmas]] addressed to "MCB" (Michelle Cora Beck), confirming Hunter's Heroes had raided Cafe Dimitri first.
- **Weapons Deal**: Sky secured emergency black-market assault pistols and cloth armor from [[stadler|Stadler]] at the Stardust Casino for Cr 14,000.
- **Omni & Starka Pramerski**: At Halfway Cafe, Sarik and Beck decrypted the laptop to discover the Vipers laundered narcotics proceeds through [[omni|Omni]], whose sole employee was [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]]. Rushing to Pramerski's home, the crew found it ransacked with a second note from Palmas on the fridge (*"MCB - I am sorry we are so good. We cannot help it. :-P"*).
- **Park 32 Convergence**: Acting on a Tipster alert (*"Save Starka Pramerski. Park 32. Hurry."*), Beck triggered a police diversion via an emergency call. At 10:00 PM, Sky intercepted a fleeing suited suspect's comms device, while six Hunter's Heroes operatives cordoned the park in the shadows, leaving Beck pinned beneath a footbridge as Palmas called out her name (Session 52).

## Park 32 Resolution, Winslow Leach Interrogation & Project Metropolis

Around 10:15 PM on Day 132-1105, BIAS CCR neutralized the immediate threat at Park 32 and uncovered a massive web connecting the underworld to ancient precursor archaeological sites (Session 53):
- **Palmas Standoff & Recruitment Offer**: Under the pedestrian bridge, [[palmas|Palmas]] questioned Beck about the police arrival, worried their communications were compromised. Palmas probed Beck about Starka Pramerski and the Stratemeyers and extended a recruitment offer for Beck to join [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]]. Beck flatly rejected the offer.
- **Burner Phone Decryption**: Sky disarmed the fleeing gunman on the park perimeter, securing his firearm and burner handset. [[asher|Asher]] decrypted the device, finding orders to meet Starka Pramerski at Park 32 at 10:00 PM, contacts at Orion Docks, and surveillance logs tracking university researcher [[winslow-leach|Winslow Leach]].
- **The Leach Interrogation**: BIAS CCR tracked Leach to a flat in the University district shared with seven students. Beck interrogated Leach while Asher cloned his personal mobile phone.
- **Ortega Frey & Project Metropolis**: Panicked by the interrogation, Leach dialed a private line belonging directly to [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]]. Asher's forensic clone revealed Frey had covertly funded Leach's research into an Ancient precursor site where [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] was built, and Leach had transmitted the site's coordinates and architectural maps directly to Frey.
- **Corporate Alliances**: With the links between the Stardust Casino, the Stratemeyers, and ancient precursor technology laid bare, the crew evaluated potential corporate counter-allies, identifying high-level [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] executive [[togaro-atreus|Togaro Atreus]] (Session 53).

## The Mall Restroom Murder, Orion Docks Payoffs & Jollynn Trust Surveillance

On Day 133-1105, BIAS CCR followed the syndicate trail to the highest tiers of Freeport high finance (Session 54):
- **The Staged Overdose Crime Scene**: [[palmas|Palmas]] contacted Beck, summoning the crew to a Freeport shopping mall. Vouched for by Palmas at the police cordon, Beck examined the dead body of [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]] in a public restroom. Beck refuted [[inspector-coker|Inspector Coker's]] overdose theory by discovering neck bruising from manual strangulation, concluding Pramerski was murdered within the last twelve hours and collecting physical evidence.
- **CCTV Video Extraction**: Asher cracked municipal and mall surveillance feeds, locating video footage of [[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]] following Pramerski into the restroom and exiting moments later to fake discovering the corpse and notify security.
- **Orion Docks Union Interrogation**: Investigating the contact from the seized burner phone, the crew questioned union dock supervisor [[irquez|Irquez]] at [[orion-docks|Orion Docks]]. Irquez confirmed regular cash payoffs from Pramerski to guarantee union cooperation for customs clearance, and identified The Man With The Gun as Pramerski's companion. Sarik warned Irquez to stay silent.
- **Jollynn Trust & The Hitman's Identity**: Tracing financial bailouts of the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]], the team targeted [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]] and CEO [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn]]. While Sarik scouted Jollynn's gated residence and Sky watched the bank tower, Sky spotted The Man With The Gun acting openly as Jollynn's personal protection detail, which Asher corroborated with public media photos.
- **The Speaking Gala Infiltration Plan**: Beck swept the team for bugs and bought event tickets for Jollynn's evening public speech, preparing a plan to observe security and snatch her purse to access and clone her personal smartphone (Session 54).

## Stardust Gala Infiltration, The Takanabe Escape & Midnight Flight Plans

On the evening of Day 133-1105, BIAS CCR infiltrated the fourth-floor charity gala at the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]] (Session 55):
- **Covert Infiltration & Reconnaissance**: Sky distracted venue guards with casual conversation, allowing Sarik to slip into an employees-only corridor to scout event logistics and hear of event planner [[javier|Javier]]. In the Grand Ballroom, Asher watched the emergency exit, Sky covered the room from a rear table, and Beck monitored the VIP area.
- **Eavesdropping on the Elite**: Using her neural comms implant, Beck eavesdropped on private conversations between casino proprietor [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]] and [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn]], who was closely guarded by [[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]] and police.
- **The Tora Takanabe Breakthrough**: When an emergency dispatch drew [[palmas|Palmas]] and his [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] squad away, Sarik shadowed them to the lower spiral stairs. Sarik overheard their panicked dispute, learning that [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]] bank president [[tora-takanabe|Tora Takanabe]] had escaped their custody.
- **The Midnight Flight & The Mansion Target**: Beck intercepted a call from Jollynn's executive assistant ordering a starship prepared for an immediate midnight departure off Freeport. Recognizing the assistant's data tablet held the flight manifests, the team devised a ruse using Javier's name to lure the assistant backstage. Concurrently, simian ally [[isaiah|Isaiah]] delivered architectural schematics of Jollynn's gated mansion, providing an alternate interception route before Jollynn reaches the starport (Session 55).

## The Jollynn Estate Assault, Decapitation & Stratemeyer Bounty Closure (Session 56–57)

On the night of Day 133-1105, BIAS CCR executed a decisive military strike against the leadership of [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] (Sessions 56–57):
- **Storming the Mansion**: As Jollynn wrapped up her speech, Sky, Sarik, Asher, Isaiah, and Thomas breached the gated estate. Inside, Sky and Thomas neutralized an interior guard, while Isaiah restrained housemaid [[isabel|Isabel]]. Outside, Sarik engaged and incapacitated two balcony guards in close-quarters combat.
- **Ambush & Execution of Jollynn**: When a security hovercart arrived carrying Jollynn, her assistant, and chief bodyguard [[the-man-with-the-gun|Richard]], the crew struck. Thomas and Sky incapacitated Richard inside the foyer. Sarik killed the hovercart driver through the windshield, Beck and Sarik returned fire on the assistant, eliminating her, and Sarik executed Marie Orslo Jollynn directly within the vehicle.
- **Safe Heist & Tunnel Extraction**: Guided by servant intelligence from Isabel, Beck discovered a secret library room, recovered an Advanced Combat Rifle (ACR), and blasted open the hidden safe to seize **Cr 65,000**. The operatives exfiltrated through a subterranean escape tunnel into a public park, paying Isabel Cr 10,000 for her silence.
- **Bounty Settlement & The Informant Unmasked**: Meeting through attorney [[ridpath|Ridpath]], the crew agreed to a 50/50 evidence pool with [[palmas|Palmas]] of [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]], securing a net payout of **Cr 800,000** from [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]]. Decrypted records revealed that escaped bank president [[tora-takanabe|Tora Takanabe]] was the informant "The President." Flush with funds, BIAS CCR prepared to depart Freeport for [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] to pursue [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] and the [[janus|Janus Asteroid]] (Sessions 56–57).

- **Neutralizing Stranded Psalmists**: Moving forward, Isambard cleared the Seminar Room with fragmentation grenades, eliminating an armed Psalmist operative and uncovering Wu-Ketai presentation files denying station shutdown plans. In the Inspection Booth, Sarik sniped a wounded Psalmist with his gauss rifle, seizing the hostile's radio.
- **Discovery of the Nuclear Warhead**: Cutting through the final reinforced vault door, BIAS CCR discovered the station's massive **[[nuclear-warhead|Nuclear Warhead]]**, with a cryptic human woman seated atop it reciting poetry about the "Silicone Monarch."

## The Warhead Standoff, The Ghost on the Bomb & The 4.5-Hour Countdown (Session 78)
On Day 208 inside the zero-gravity warhead chamber (Session 78):
- **Encounter with the Ghost**: The woman perched on the nuclear warhead was discovered to be an agitated holographic station ghost of a deceased Wu-Ketai crew member. The [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] pulsed with intense energy in resonance with her emotional instability.
- **De-escalation & Arming**: Beck calmed the ghost by falsely agreeing to detonate the bomb; the ghost provided access instructions to Asher. As security bots breached the vault, Sarik and Isambard held the perimeter with EMP grenades and gauss rifle fire. When the ghost demanded instant explosion, Isambard disrupted her projection with an EMP grenade.
- **Psychic Invasions & The Diary Clue**: Reforming, the ghost struck Asher with a traumatic psychic memory surge, revealing she hid a personal **Diary** in the Disassembly Area. Seizing the terminal, Beck manually armed the warhead, triggering station-wide alarms.
- **The Psychology Book Gambit**: When the ghost threatened immediate apocalypse, Isambard pacified her by presenting Beck's water-damaged paperback (*Modern Theories on Psychological Profiling*) as her diary. When Sarik attempted to calm her, he suffered another traumatic memory attack before Isambard banished her with a second EMP.
- **Programming the 4.5-Hour Delay**: Under cover of simulated console alarms and continuous bot suppression, Asher hacked the detonation firmware, locking in a **four-and-a-half-hour (4.5-hour) countdown** before calculating extraction routes back to Micro-Hangar C-118.

## The Great Escape, The Renzo Psychic Trauma, Conway's Shakedown & The Fall of Metropolis (Session 79)
On Day 208, during the final four hours before nuclear detonation (Session 79):
- **Rocket Booby Traps & Ghost Ambush**: Retreating from the nuclear vault, Asher constructed two proximity-triggered booby traps using Sarik's rockets at the War Studies and Quarantine choke points. En route, the ghost [[renzo|Renzo]] ambushed the squad with a devastating psychic assault, permanently damaging Asher's mind (blunting analytical intellect while instilling an acute empathy for synthetics). Isambard dispersed Renzo with an EMP grenade.
- **Duchess Extraction & Micro-Hangar Breach**: Retrieving the suspended [[exact-replica-of-the-duchess|Exact Replica of Duchess Margaret]], the crew reached Micro-Hangar C-118, where Sarik used the cutting laser for 30 minutes to slice through sealed blast doors. Boarding the orbital shuttle bus, Isambard piloted them out into space just as Asher's rocket traps detonated inside the station at 17:00 hours.
- **Parley with Officer Conway**: Rendezvousing with *The Raconteur* at the Loading Dock, the crew faced a hostile confrontation with corrupt security chief [[conway|Officer Conway]]. Beck negotiated safe passage by offering a 50% split on low-tier salvage while concealing the high-value Precursor relics. When Conway attempted to seize [[kira|Kira]], Isambard fiercely defended her status as protected crew. BIAS CCR deliberately kept Conway in the dark about the armed nuclear warhead.
- **Silent Escape & The Flash**: Setting sublight thrusters to silent running and cutting transponders, *The Raconteur* slipped into the uncharted Kidderminster asteroid belt toward safehouse coordinates provided by [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]].
- **The Annihilation of Metropolis**: At 20:30 hours on Day 208, the nuclear warhead detonated. The resulting catastrophic thermonuclear fireball vaporized [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], the primary core of [[monarch|Monarch]], the scavenger habitat ***[[the-bell|The Bell]]*** (killing Arkady and all remaining inhabitants), and the blockading [[the-doves|Psalmist]] warship. At midnight, the Duchess's Redshirt fleet arrived only to find glowing radioactive debris.
