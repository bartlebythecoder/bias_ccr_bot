# GSbAG

**Summary**: Exclusive, high-cost starship construction megacorporation contracted to build personal yachts for the Imperial family and frontline Imperial Navy capital warships.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Prestige Starship Engineering

GSbAG (colloquially termed "Gas-bag") differs from other megacorporate giants by strictly limiting its portfolio to starship design and aerospace manufacturing. Operating since antiquity, the corporation maintains a reputation for peerless engineering excellence:
- **Imperial Patronage**: One of only two aerospace firms entrusted with constructing custom personal yachts and royal vessels for the Imperial family.
- **Naval Contracts**: Major prime contractor providing high-spec capital combat starships for the Imperial Navy.
- **Premium Pricing**: Products command exorbitant price points across the galaxy, but are universally recognized as unmatched in reliability and performance.
