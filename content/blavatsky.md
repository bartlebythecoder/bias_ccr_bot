# Blavatsky

**Summary**: Scientist who created The Cube, the anti-Monarch virus, and was the sole survivor of the team sent to deploy it inside Metropolis Station.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Blavatsky** was a research scientist who operated within [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] and developed the primary cyber-warfare countermeasure against [[monarch|Monarch]].

## The Anti-Monarch Virus
- **Creation of The Cube**: Blavatsky successfully coded and synthesized **[[the-cube|The Cube]]**, a sophisticated software virus designed to purge and dismantle the neural core of the rogue AI Monarch.
- **Tragic Expedition**: According to [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]], Blavatsky led a specialized team into the depths of Metropolis to deploy the weapon; however, the entire team was killed or assimilated, with Blavatsky emerging from the station as the sole survivor.
- **Legacy**: The software payload was left secured within the station on **Level 4** under the custody of [[damien|Damien]].
