# General Products

**Summary**: Heavy industrial megacorporation manufacturing machinery and starships, plagued by disasters in its Kinunir battle cruiser construction contracts.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Industrial Manufacturing

General Products, LIC is a primary manufacturing megacorporation operating across Imperial space. Known for inexpensive, adequate-quality products, it dominates commercial transport hulls, industrial machines, and planetary utility vehicles.

## The Kinunir Cruiser Scandal

General Products was the primary prime contractor for the 1,200-ton [[kinunir-battle-cruiser|Kinunir-class battle cruiser]] ([[kinunir-battle-cruiser|Kinunir-Class Battle Cruiser]]). Rushed production and defective construction led to chronic naval disasters:
- The class ship *Kinunir (BC-9514)* mysteriously vanished without a trace on patrol in 1088 ([[kinunir-battle-cruiser|Kinunir-Class Battle Cruiser]]).
- Both *Allamu* and *Ninkur Sagga* suffered fatal screen/hull failures at the [[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]] ([[kinunir-battle-cruiser|Kinunir-Class Battle Cruiser]]).
- Naval authorities cancelled further procurement, converting surviving hulls like *Gaesh* into orbital prison hulks or scrapping them on the slipways ([[kinunir-battle-cruiser|Kinunir-Class Battle Cruiser]]).
