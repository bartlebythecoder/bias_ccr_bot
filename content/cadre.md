# Cadre

**Summary**: Club Strata bartender and friend of Louise Soto who had warned her about her violent ex-boyfriend David Parker.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**Cadre** is a bartender working at [[club-strata|Club Strata]], located in The Lux within the Humboldt District on [[freeport|Freeport]] (Session 45).

---

## Operational History
On Day 130-1105, during [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s homicide inquiries into the death of [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] designer [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]], operatives [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] questioned Cadre inside Club Strata (Session 45). Cadre confirmed she was a personal acquaintance of Louise Soto and revealed that she had repeatedly cautioned Louise regarding the dangerous temper of her abusive ex-boyfriend, [[david-parker|David Parker]].
