# Duchess Margaret

**Summary**: Ruling noblewoman of [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] and head of [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]], allied with [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]], and patron of the subsector-wide Grand Tour cultural initiative.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Noble Titles & Family Lineage

Duchess Margaret is the supreme aristocrat of the [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] subsector (Subsector J) and the reigning head of **[[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]]**, one of the sixteen Imperial Noble Houses ([[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]]; [[noble-houses|Noble Houses]]).
- **Cadet Alliances**: Her extended family includes third cousin [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]], ruler of [[csabai|Csabai]] ([[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]]).
- **Marriage & Megacorporate Ties**: Married to the Subsector President of megacorporation [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]], cementing a close political and logistical axis between House Yetrina and the shipping giant ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]; [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]]).

## Cultural & Economic Initiatives (1105)

On 049-1105, Duchess Margaret launched the Subsector Cultural Exchange Initiative centered around a regional "Grand Tour" ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]):
- Features artistic showcases, concerts by pop sensation [[sabrina|Sabrina]], and aristocratic trade galas across Kidderminster, Csabai, and Arico El Nuevo ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]; [[news-1105-vol-5|News Dispatch — Volume 5]]; [[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).

## Political and Commercial Setbacks

- **Anti-Psalmist Stance**: Firmly opposes the expansion of [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]], maintaining vigilance against theocratic incursions sponsored by [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]] ([[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]]).
- **Navy Contract Shock**: In 170-1105, despite Margaret's high-level family ties to Tukera Lines, the Kidderminster Navy unexpectedly awarded its flagship 'Senior Operations Initiative' defense contract to rival [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]] ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).

## Redshirt Navy Fleet Deployment & Monarch's Synthetic Duplicate (Sessions 77–79)

In Session 77 (Session 77):
- **Redshirt Navy Fleet Deployment**: Duchess Margaret dispatched an armed combat fleet from the Redshirt Navy toward [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]], scheduled to intercept and arrive at the station by the end of Day 208-1105, precipitating an emergency incursion by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]].
- **Synthetic Duplicate Uncovered**: Deep inside the Infiltrator-Android Area adjacent to Micro-Hangar C-118, BIAS CCR discovered the **[[exact-replica-of-the-duchess|Exact Replica of the Duchess]]**—an inert, full-scale synthetic android husk manufactured by [[monarch|Monarch]]. Pre-programmed scout ships bound for [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] indicated Monarch planned to imprint Margaret's cognitive persona into the construct to seize control of House Yetrina and the Kidderminster subsector.

In Session 79 (Session 79):
- **Evacuation of the Duplicate**: BIAS CCR retrieved the inert synthetic duplicate and successfully evacuated it aboard *The Raconteur* into the asteroid belt.
- **Fleet Arrives at Nuclear Ruins**: When the Redshirt Navy fleet arrived at Metropolis around midnight on Day 208, they found only expanding radioactive wreckage and thermal radiation, as the station had been completely obliterated by the nuclear warhead two hours prior.
