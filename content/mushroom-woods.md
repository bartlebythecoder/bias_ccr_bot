# Mushroom Woods

**Summary**: Subterranean fungal forest and cavern wilderness on [[csabai|Csabai]] where fugitive Psalmist courier [[jacob-godard|Jacob Godard]] was apprehended.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Wilderness Geography

The Mushroom Woods is an extensive rural wilderness on [[csabai|Csabai]] characterized by giant endemic fungal flora, dense spore-canopies, and rugged subterranean cave formations (Session 29). The secluded, maze-like nature of the caverns makes the region a favored retreat for fugitives seeking to evade planetary satellite sweeps (Session 29).

## The Apprehension of Jacob Godard

On Day 94-1105, armed with location history from Godard's phone and guided by [[sarik|Sarik]]'s tracking skills, operatives from [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] hunted fugitive courier [[jacob-godard|Jacob Godard]] into the caverns of the Mushroom Woods (Session 29). The team flushed Godard from his cave, seized the stolen Psalmist data wafer, and sanitized references to the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] before planetary police transports arrived to take custody of the prisoner (Session 29).
