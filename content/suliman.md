# Suliman

**Summary**: Wu-Ketai's mining and research asteroid in the Arico system, home to about 70,000 people and its uplift laboratories.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Suliman is a resource-rich mining and research asteroid located in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system, operated under the jurisdiction of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation (Session 33). The asteroid settlement supports a population of approximately seventy thousand residents (Session 34).

## Colony Profile
- **Purpose**: Wu-Ketai's primary scientific research and industrial centre for genetic engineering and uplifted simian development.
- **Conditions**: Draconian working conditions, heavy surveillance of communications, and restrictions on personal freedom have made the colony a powder keg of labour resentment.
- **Key Personnel (per colony dossier)**:
  - [[dr-harknett|Dr. Demetri Harknett]]: Lead research geneticist.
  - [[marcus-fortune|Marcus Fortune]]: Colony Administrator and ruthless corporate enforcer.
  - [[carl-parker|Carl Parker]]: Union leader advocating for labour rights.
  - [[hector|Hector Nilasen]]: Corporate security officer.

## Corporate Facility, Districts & Labor Unrest
- **[[the-warrens|The Warrens]]**: The sprawling subterranean tunnel network where the majority of the population resides. Amenities are minimal, and labor forces consist of both human workers and articulate uplifted simians performing industrial duties (Session 34). Notable local spots include [[the-rusty-bolt|The Rusty Bolt]] tavern.
- **[[derry|Derry]]**: A commercial sector and sushi bar frequented by off-duty corporate personnel, including security officer [[hector|Hector]] (Session 34).
- **Electronic Surveillance**: Wu-Ketai maintains pervasive electronic tracking throughout Suliman's residential tunnels, backed by rapid-response armed security personnel (Session 34).
- **Labor Tension**: Union leader [[carl-parker|Carl Parker]] represents the laborers amidst mounting resentment fueled by heightened production quotas and the rumored cancellation of a worker care center (Session 34).
- **Apex Zenith**: An exclusive, high-security upper sector accessible through corporate security checkpoints or high-fee brokers such as [[cardo|Cardo]] (Session 35).
- **Core Laboratories**: Highly restricted central research laboratories where [[project-chimera|Project Chimera]] and [[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]] operate. The complex is divided into color-coded clearance zones:
  - **Green Area**: Preliminary administrative and research concourse overseen by [[dr-kamron|Dr. Kamron]], containing general offices and preliminary testing bays (Session 38).
  - **Yellow Labs**: High-security experimental sector led by [[dr-young|Dr. Young]] and nominal director [[dr-harknett|Dr. Harknett]], featuring advanced medical theaters and subterranean service tunnels (Session 38).
  - **Red Section & Animal Pens**: Restricted bioengineering containment area housing experimental uplifted simians, accessible via locked security blast doors from the service tunnels (Session 39).
  - **Chrono Lab**: Ultra-restricted inner sanctum where the [[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]] apparatus of [[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]] is housed, guarded by biometric and supervisor-level clearances like those of [[dr-vance|Dr. Vance]] (Session 39).
  - **Surveillance Blind Spot**: The core labs possess zero internal surveillance camera monitoring, creating an absolute informational blackout (Session 35).
- **The Triple Disappearances**: Beyond geneticist [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]], both corporate security officer [[hector|Hector Nilasen]] and Chief of Corporate Security [[marcus-fortune|Marcus Fortune]] vanished in rapid succession under suspicious circumstances, with sightings converging on the core laboratories and Fortune's private suite (Sessions 35, 37).
- **Emergency Escape Launch Bays**: Subterranean launch tubes linked to service tunnels allowing high-velocity evacuation vessels to launch directly into deep space (Session 39).
