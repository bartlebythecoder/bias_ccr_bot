# David Ives

**Summary**: President and CEO of [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]], former [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]] executive, facing two inquiries in 1105 into illegal experiments at [[suliman|Suliman]].

**Last updated**: 2026-10-13

---

The President and CEO of [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] Industries is **David Ives**.

Before taking leadership at Wu-Ketai in 1099, he served as Vice President of [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]]'s [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] division. He is a childhood friend of [[duke-dulinor|Duke Dulinor]] of [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]] and a prominent supporter of the [[children-of-the-psalms|Children of the Psalms]] religious movement.

## Suliman Investigations (1105)

By 212-1105, Ives was under pressure from two separate investigations, one by the Subsector authorities and one by the Imperial authorities, opened after journalist [[risiano-tiler|Risiano Tiler]] reported on illegal experiments at Wu-Ketai's flagship [[suliman|Suliman Asteroid]]. The investigations depressed the share prices of Wu-Ketai and of its largest shareholder, Hortalez et Cie ([[news-1105-vol-10|News Dispatch — Volume 10]]).
