# Chandler

**Summary**: Art historian and courier who transported the Beaugard painting on loan from [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] to [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role & Art Expertise

Mr. Chandler is a respected art appraiser, historian, and courier originating from [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] (Sessions 22–23). In early 1105, Chandler escorted the priceless [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] on interstellar transit from Kidderminster to [[csabai|Csabai]], facilitating its temporary cultural exhibition at [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] for [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] (Sessions 22–23).

Following the sudden theft of the artwork from the gallery, Chandler was among the principal individuals interviewed by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] before the internal conspiracy involving [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]] was exposed (Sessions 22–23).
