# The Bell

**Summary**: Arkady's thruster habitat moored beside Metropolis Station, a base for scavenger-divers.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

***The Bell*** is a detached thruster habitat and salvage craft moored in close orbital proximity to the derelict space station [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 59).

## Overview & Function
Owned and operated by veteran scavenger-diver [[arkady|Arkady]], *The Bell* functions as an independent, mobile base of operations situated on the fringe of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] restricted interdiction zone. It provides living quarters, environmental support, and docking capabilities for divers braving the hazardous interior of Metropolis (Session 59).

## Scavenger Community & Facilities (Session 62)
Beyond Arkady, *The Bell* hosts a small, hardened enclave of surviving scavenger-divers (Session 62):
- **Residents**: Includes **[[magnus|Magnus]]** (survivor of the *Saltpeter* waiting for [[lars|Lars]]), **[[ghost-eater|Ghost Eater]]** (a heavily cybernetic diver reconstructed on Eternity Station), and **[[noriko|Noriko]]** (a retired diver whose crew perished a year prior).
- **Amenities & Storage**: Features zero-g sleeping bunks, an orbital galley where Arkady prepares zero-g sandwiches, and an armored personal vault/safe where Arkady safeguards visitors' recovered artifacts (including those salvaged by Magnus).
- **Intelligence Hub**: In Session 62, *The Bell* served as the staging debriefing station where the *BIAS CCR* crew interviewed Ghost Eater, Noriko, and Magnus, uncovering critical lore regarding [[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]], [[the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]], and the [[child-king|Child King]] of Level 2.

## Destruction in Nuclear Blast (Session 79)

At 20:30 hours on Day 208-1105, the strategic [[nuclear-warhead|Nuclear Warhead]] armed by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] detonated inside [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Session 79). Because *The Bell* remained moored in immediate orbital proximity to the station, the habitat craft was caught in the epicenter of the cataclysmic thermonuclear blast and completely obliterated, likely killing Arkady and any remaining divers aboard.
