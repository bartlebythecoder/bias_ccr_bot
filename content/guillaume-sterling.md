# Guillaume Sterling

**Summary**: Militant operative of The Doves on Csabai who contracted the Irklan assassin to eliminate Calmer Singh and BIAS CCR, critically wounded in a shotgun duel with Sarik.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Allegiance & The Doves

Guillaume Sterling is a militant operative in his early twenties affiliated with [[the-doves|The Doves]], the radical anti-psionic Psalmist terror cell operating out of [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] and [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 28). Sharing kinship with Csabai Palace head butler [[sterling|Sterling]], Guillaume operated out of the restricted executive administration tier of the [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] in [[basin-city|Basin City]] under the protection of center director [[darlene-suitor|Darlene Suitor]] (Session 28).

## Contracting the Irklan & The Gunfight

- **The Assassination Contract**: On Day 92-1105, Guillaume met with starport cutout [[reshka|Reshka]] on [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]], paying a substantial fee to contract an [[irklan|Irklan]] assassin to execute information broker [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] and liquidate [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] to suppress their knowledge of [[the-doves|The Doves]] and seize the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (Sessions 27–28).
- **The Gunfight at the Psalmist Center**: On Day 93-1105, operative [[sarik|Sarik]] breached the upper floor of the Psalmist Center (Session 28). Guillaume engaged Sarik in a point-blank shotgun duel inside a fortified chamber; while Sarik's ballistic cloth armor absorbed the brunt of the blast, the unarmored Guillaume was shot down and critically wounded as law enforcement units arrived to secure the premises (Session 28).
