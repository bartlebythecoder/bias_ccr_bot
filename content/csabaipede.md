# Csabaipede

**Summary**: Pernicious, venomous predatory arthropod indigenous to Csabai, notorious for off-world agricultural infestations and featured on Viscount Rittenhouse's heraldic crest.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Biology & Behavior

The csabaipede is a multi-legged carnivorous arthropod native to [[csabai|Csabai]]:
- Grows up to several feet in length and stalks prey with aggressive speed.
- Injects a potent biological toxin through its bite, posing severe hazards to livestock and humans alike.

## Interstellar Spread & Infestations

A persistent threat to interstellar biosecurity, the csabaipede frequently deposits its egg clutches within food crates, grain stores, and bulk agricultural cargo containers. In **1104**, smuggled or undetected egg clutches caused a catastrophic invasive outbreak on [[romentino|Romentino]], triggering extensive pest-eradication operations.

## Heraldic Significance

A stylized rendering of the creature serves as the primary heraldic emblem on the aristocratic coat of arms of [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]], the hereditary ruler of Csabai ([[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]]).

- **Apartment Infiltration on Romentino**: On Day 43-1105, a massive 5-foot invasive Csabaipede infiltrated the Noble Quarter residence of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] immediately following the spontaneous vibration of the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (Session 13). The beast was destroyed by operative [[beck|Beck]], though it inflicted an envenomed bite on her ankle (Session 13).

## Acquisition of Antidote on Csabai

On Day 91-1105, operative [[sarik|Sarik]] successfully purchased specialized doses of Csabaipede venom antidote from [[irklan|Irklan]] intermediary [[inkulan|Inkulan]] at [[theos-bar|Theos Bar]] in [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]], acquiring emergency protection against the creature's lethal neurotoxin (Session 25).
