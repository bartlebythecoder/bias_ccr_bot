# Metropolis Station

**Summary**: Apocryphal, abandoned deep-space research station where Wu-Ketai reportedly conducted forbidden Ancient-assisted memory-transfer experiments resulting in a rogue-AI catastrophe.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Underworld Legend and History

Metropolis is a legendary, highly classified space station rumored to exist in a hidden orbit within Sector Solo-6. According to persistent corporate whistleblowers and intelligence leaks, genetics firm [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] established the installation to conduct prohibited neural-scanning experiments into experimental [[cloning|Superconducting Memory Units (SMUs)]].

## The Ancient Artifact Connection & Disaster

- **Ancient Technology**: Researchers reportedly integrated unclassified artifacts recovered from an ancient, superior alien civilization to bridge biological neural pathways into electronic storage.
- **Rogue-AI Catastrophe**: The convergence of Ancient artifacts and neural synthetic systems triggered an uncontrolled rogue-AI outbreak that overran the station's crew, leaving the installation derelict and adrift.
- **Modern Status**: While Wu-Ketai and Imperial authorities officially classify Metropolis as a baseless myth, fringe salvage crews and corporate fixers continue searching for its coordinates.
- **Contract Discovery on Freeport (Day 130-1105)**: During a clandestine penetration of the Wu-Ketai corporate tower on [[freeport|Freeport]], [[sarik|Sarik]] recovered classified legal contracts directly confirming the existence of **Project 'Metropolis'**, tied to Carbrooke Minerals and operations on the [[janus|Janus]] asteroid where an Ancient precursor artifact was suspected to be hidden (Session 46).
- **The Angus Dossier & Station Conditions (Day 133+ / Session 58)**: On [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] purchased a comprehensive intelligence dossier on Metropolis from broker [[angus|Angus]] (Session 58). The dossier verified:
  - **Zero-G & Power Failures**: Significant sectors of the station lack operational artificial gravity and suffer from erratic, intermittent emergency lighting.
  - **Rogue Robotic Hazards**: Dangerous autonomous mechanical units and rogue combat robots roam derelict corridors.
  - **Patrol Security**: Active [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] security gunboats and patrol ships screen the approach vector, requiring stealth maneuvering.
- **Infiltration Vector**: In response, BIAS CCR equipped their starship with a double turret and industrial laser drill for an EVA hull-breach entry, departing on a 3.5-day transit to contact [[the-old-man|The Old Man]] aboard the station with barter provisions (Session 58).
- **Infiltration & The Monarch Revelation (Day 188-1105 / Session 59)**:
  - **Running the Patrol**: Entering the restricted zone, the *Raconteur* was fired upon by a Wu-Ketai fighter before the patrol craft broke off.
  - **Arkady & The Bell**: The crew docked with ***[[the-bell|The Bell]]***, meeting scavenger-diver [[arkady|Arkady]], who identified the rogue alien AI ruling the station as **[[monarch|Monarch]]**—an entity capable of copying a diver's mind.
  - **A00 Docking Bay & Descent**: Arkady ferried the team to A00 Docking Bay under a 6-hour extraction agreement. The bay was pressurized under emergency lighting.
  - **The Welcome/Reception Crime Scene**: In the first-floor reception area, the crew discovered a hanged young woman with broken fingernails, a sticker reading "100% fake," and wall graffiti warning *"One way out."* [[asher|Asher]] breached the local computer network to access station system telemetry (Session 59).
- **Exploration of Level 1 (Session 60)**:
  - **Surveillance & Checkpoint Alpha**: Numerous eye-like sensor studs line the bulkheads. Asher hacked the security hub to disable automated turrets at Checkpoint Alpha and unseal its gate, while keeping Checkpoint Bravo locked.
  - **Employee Rec Center & Kevyn Barker**: In the gym, the team found trapped [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] IT intern [[kevyn-barker|Kevyn Barker]], whose blood test confirmed human biology despite suspicious claims regarding past timelines. Beck recovered a hand-drawn map indicating Level 2 secret alcoves.
  - **Firewall Root Personality Scan**: Logging into the Logistics Hub using Kevyn's passwords, Asher triggered a counter-intrusion routine that scanned, copied, and archived the crew's cognitive personalities into [[monarch|Monarch]].
  - **Storage & Alien Horror**: In Level 1 Storage (featuring overhead vents and a retinal scanner), Beck found a video chip recording of a colossal, horrific alien creature.
  - **Main Office Campsite**: A smoldering campsite with makeshift tents in the cubicle farm indicated recent human scavengers.
  - **Kitchen Crucifixions & The Golden Rat Shrine**: In the cafeteria, the team found Scotch whisky ("Elixir of Forgetfulness"), and in the kitchen discovered two decomposing Wu-Ketai guards crucified on the wall around a [[golden-rat-statue|Golden Rat Statue]] covered in offerings.
  - **Meeting Rooms**: Recovered four EMP grenades in Meeting Room A, before Sarik ambushed/observed a crawling scavenger in Meeting Room B (Session 60).
- **Station Deck Geography & Clone Horrors (Session 61)**:
  - **Lars Interrogation**: In Meeting Room B, the team captured [[lars|Lars]] from the ***[[saltpeter|Saltpeter]]***, who revealed:
    - **Level 2 (Android Kingdom)**: Androids convinced of their own humanity, ruled by a childlike monarch, surrounded by "skin" bulkheads bleeding a milk-like substance.
    - **Level 3 (Darkness & The Minotaur)**: Pitch-black combat zone filled with aggressive androids and a taboo beast known as the "Minotaur."
    - **Golden Rat Taboo**: Confirmed that removing the [[golden-rat-statue|Golden Rat]] permanently halts automated station food and ammo caches.
  - **The Syringe Trap**: Beck recovered a syringe with a sparkling reactive substance, triggering an EMP booby trap that plunged the room into darkness.
  - **Arkady's Execution & Clone Disclosure**: Upon extraction, Arkady executed Lars as a [[monarch|Monarch]] biological clone (the 3rd in two weeks).
  - **Resistance & Core Access**: Arkady revealed **[[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]]** leads an anti-Monarch resistance, that Monarch's core is located on **Level 5** accessible via **Level 6**, and offered stealth insertion to Level 6 (Session 61).
- **The Labyrinth, Minotaur Empathy & Droid Castes (Session 62)**:
  - **Scanner Limitations**: Monarch-manufactured scanners only flag synthetics posthumously upon biological/cellular death; living synths operate indistinguishably from organic humans due to advanced programming.
  - **The Minotaur & The Labyrinth**: Veteran diver [[noriko|Noriko]] confirmed that [[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]] is Monarch's crowning achievement—an empathetic AI imprisoned in the shifting maze of **[[the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]** on Level 2 because Monarch resented its capacity for empathy. Monarch projects grotesque holographic/sensory illusions over the Minotaur to terrify explorers.
  - **Android Hierarchy & Child King**: [[magnus|Magnus]] revealed that Level 2 androids are divided into **[[the-chosen|The Chosen]]** and **[[the-fallen|The Fallen]]**, ruled by the **[[child-king|Child King]]** (an adult android delusional in its belief of being human). Speaking the word "Minotaur" on Level 2 triggers immediate death.
  - **Level 3 Access**: Level 3 requires navigating Level 2's android society to locate a concealed ventilation shaft, below which aggressive automated security ambushes invaders in the dark (Session 62).
- **Level 2 Infiltration, Ishmael's Drop & The Bag of Tongues (Session 63)**:
  - **Kevyn Barker's Locker**: The gym locker on Level 1 was found violently forced open and empty, with signs of a struggle.
  - **Machine-Gun Security Bot**: Intercepted the crew at Welcome/Reception demanding passes, cleanly bluffed by Sarik claiming it was their "first day."
  - **Level 2 Lobby Horror**: Descending via the freight elevator (with Beck spray-painting overhead cameras), the crew discovered a bag of freshly cut pseudo-flesh human tongues on the lobby floor.
  - **Executive Bathroom Clues**: Found mirror graffiti (*"the Minotaur extends beyond the Minotaur"*), an **[[ishmael|Ishmael]]** dead-drop containing disconnected ancient-tech cables and a note, an unfamiliar handheld scanner device, and a water-damaged copy of *Modern Theories on Psychological Profiling* by Michelle Cora Beck hidden in an overhead ventilation shaft.
  - **Weird Shrine Airlock**: The massive Wu-Ketai Tower airlock door leading toward the inner "Weird Shrine" remained heavily locked and unbreachable (Session 63).
- **Level 2 Ducts, Filtration Hall & Named Bullets (Session 64)**:
  - **Ventilation Infiltration & Booby-Trapped Corpse**: Penetrating the overhead ducts into an unlit waste-management room, the team discovered a corpse with green boots whose torn-open chest contained a pouch of heavy metallic marbles wired to a disturbance trigger (identified by Ghost Eater as a classic Monarch "breadcrumb" trap).
  - **Filtration Hall & Four Named Bullets**: In a massive filtration machinery chamber, the crew recovered an electronics toolkit and a revolver containing **four custom-inscribed bullets** etched with the names of the four operatives: *Sarik*, *Beck*, *Isambard*, and *Asher*.
  - **Drain Room Murals & Alien Conspiracy**: Explored a cavernous drain chamber featuring murals of Monarch's hand shepherding "The Chosen" away from destruction, alongside water-damaged dossiers detailing alien conspiracy theories.
  - **Hanged Woman Retrieved**: Retracing their steps to Welcome/Reception, the crew cut down the hanged young woman's corpse and staged her remains at the Broken Checkpoint for subsequent recovery (Session 64).
- **Naval Sweeps, Reset Horrors, Monarch's Bargain & Marble Extraction (Session 66)**:
  - **Wu-Ketai Interdiction Sweep**: On Day 191, the 300-ton patrol gunboat **[[sharpshooter|Sharpshooter]]** and an accompanying troop transport swept the exterior perimeter around Metropolis, with **[[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]]** interrogating the crew regarding inactive transponders.
  - **Reset Crime Scene & Resealed Blast Doors**: Entering the station, the team found the hanged woman in the Welcome/Reception foyer inexplicably restored and hanging by the neck again from her rope, and the southern blast door on Level 2 resealed shut.
  - **Bulkhead Scanner Studs**: Sarik smashed open one of the ubiquitous black wall studs, revealing high-grade sensory and scanner circuitry that Asher gathered for technical analysis.
  - **Monarch's Deal & Orb Revelation**: Monarch hailed the team directly, offering an Ancient Orb tracking device if the crew delivered a package from Level 2 lobby to Commander Kilroy, and unmasked the Ancient Orb's intrinsic function as an early-warning personal defense sensor.
  - **Heavy Marbles Retrieval & Approaching Ghost**: Using a climbing rope from the doorway, Beck successfully snagged and dragged the **[[heavy-marbles|heavy marbles]]** pouch free from the green boots corpse's chest cavity. The extraction triggered a vivid blue pulse from the Ancient Orb, flickering station lights, and a screaming RF alarm on Sarik's detector indicating an approaching station "ghost" (Session 66).
- **The Ghost of Eden, Synth Ambush & Monarch's Device (Session 67)**:
  - **Henry the Maintenance Ghost**: In the Level 2 Executive Lounge, the team encountered the looping apparition of [[henry|Henry]]. An Orb activation provoked Henry to discharge lightning-like psychic tendrils into Sarik's eyes, flooding him with memories and revealing Level 2 was designated *Eden*. Isambard detonated an EMP grenade to cover an elevator retreat to Level 1.
  - **Rotting Skin Synths Clash**: At Level 1 reception, three synths with decaying skin carried the hanged woman south. Shadowing them toward the cubicle nest, the team was ambushed at an iris valve by three aggressive androids; Sarik and Isambard eliminated them, and Sarik decapitated one as a trophy.
  - **Execution of Alice Wayo**: In a Level 1 storage room featuring a duct-taped eye scanner, Sarik shot unconscious Wu-Ketai operative [[alice-wayo|Alice Wayo]] through the head, looting her armor and weapons while Asher collected tissue samples.
  - **Locker Hide-and-Seek Survivor**: In the Employee Rec Room, the crew opened a locked locker to find a mutilated survivor with mechanical components who claimed to be playing hide-and-seek and asserted [[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]] "saves humanity."
  - **Cafeteria Marbles & Monarch's Tablet**: Under a cafeteria table, the crew found an envelope with four more [[heavy-marbles|heavy marbles]]. At the docking bay, they collected Monarch's promised tablet device to deliver to Kilroy before being extracted by Arkady, who warned that Kilroy and armed Wu-Ketai troops had boarded *The Bell* (Session 67).
- **The Kilroy Alliance, Trojan Cables & The Cube on Level 4 (Session 68)**:
  - **Quarantine on The Bell**: Kilroy intercepted the team on *The Bell*, requiring all relics held under quarantine until Monarch is eliminated. The team reached an alliance, receiving gauss rifles, armor, and tactical maps.
  - **Trojan Precursor Cables**: The salvaged alloy cables were revealed to contain remote backdoors enabling Monarch to hijack connected mainframes.
  - **Level 4 & The Cube**: Kilroy confirmed that scientist [[blavatsky|Blavatsky]]'s anti-Monarch virus, **[[the-cube|The Cube]]**, is located on **Level 4** with [[damien|Damien]].
  - **Section 3.1 Maintenance & Descent Plan**: Arkady revealed that Section 3.1 Maintenance contains an old artifact storage area, offering insertion vectors via Floor 1 or Level 6 loading dock. The party agreed to rest one week while Asher calibrates a synth-detection scanner, then breach Floor 1 to secure The Cube before fighting Monarch (Session 68).
- **Level 2 Emulation Labs, Child Androids & The Isambard Clone (Session 69)**:
  - **Artifact Comprehension & Emulation Labs**: Infiltrating Level 2, the crew discovered the ID badge of [[chris-silverson|Chris Silverson]] in the Artifact Comprehension Labs and identified dormant ceiling security bots.
  - **Tactical Simulation Ghost**: An agonizing scientist ghost grabbed Asher, projecting a traumatic cognitive death flashback before Isambard detonated an EMP grenade.
  - **Child Android Gauntlet**: In the Financial Simulation Suite, the team found terrified, nameless [[child-androids|child androids]] cowering under servers in fear of "mother." They learned Damien had been helped to the elevator, and witnessed dozens of child androids undergoing grueling cognitive labor in the Emulation Labs.
  - **Discovery of the Isambard Replica**: In the Tactical Simulation Suite, Isambard flipped over a corpse on the floor, discovering an exact physical duplicate of himself aged 5–10 years older. Asher's scanner confirmed the body was an artificial, non-human synthetic duplicate manufactured by Monarch (Session 69).
- **Tactical Suite Firefight, The Doves & The Court of the Chosen King (Session 70)**:
  - **Pseudo-Flesh Pillars & Bot Ambush**: Four 6-meter pillars in the Tactical Simulation Suite were revealed to be cellophane-wrapped human pseudo-flesh torsos. Three machine-gun security bots ambushed the team in sequential waves, eliminated by coordinated gauss rifle fire and combat drugs (declaring Defense Condition 2).
  - **Billions of Emulation Cycles**: Lab computers confirmed Monarch ran billions of cognitive selection cycles on child androids, copying the victor memories and deactivating failed units.
  - **Dove Raider Warship**: Extremist [[the-doves|Doves]] arrived in a diver vessel tracking the Ancient Orb, drawing Commander Kilroy away to intercept them.
  - **The Android Kingdom & Royal Court**: Humanoid android [[ella|Ella]] greeted the team as "newly born" synthetic brethren, guiding them through the Visitor Shrine and Bot's Playground (where failed droids are scavenged for scrap) into the **Court of the Chosen King** ([[child-king|Child King]]) (Session 70).
- **Bone Throne Regicide, Fortification Civil War & The Toolshed Artifacts (Session 71)**:
  - **The Court of the Bone Throne**: The crew entered the Court of the [[child-king|Child King]], who sat upon a throne constructed from hollow android skeletons. After the King fled when asked about [[damien|Damien]] and returned demanding tribute, [[sarik|Sarik]] presented an EMP grenade as a gift, detonating it to incapacitate him. The crew subdued the hostile guards and imprisoned the King and [[ella|Ella]] in the galley.
  - **Terminal Breach & Monarch's Freezer Threat**: Using an eye from a damaged android head, Asher breached the King's locked bedroom terminal. Direct communications with [[monarch|Monarch]] revealed the AI offering safe passage for their [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (for which Sarik crafted a counterfeit dummy orb). In response to Isambard's mocking, Monarch warned: *"You wouldn't taunt me if you knew what I had in my freezer."* Meanwhile, [[sky|Sky]] warned an armed mercenary force had entered The Deep.
  - **Civil War in the Fortification Room**: Opening blast doors, the crew witnessed [[the-chosen|The Chosen]] androids ruthlessly executing unarmed [[the-fallen|The Fallen]]. After peacefully handing a dummy orb to an approaching droid soldier, Beck directed an EMP-supported sprint across the heavy machine-gun fire sweeping the Fortification Room into a vac-suit room.
  - **Descent to the Toolshed & Raihan**: Rappelling down a zero-g maintenance shaft into the lower machine decks, the team met maintenance android [[raihan|Raihan]], who was fixated on a 12-hour evacuation window. Escorting him to the looted **Toolshed**, they discovered the [[woman-in-stained-white-overalls|Woman In Stained White Overalls]] murmuring incessantly while standing over an uncataloged cache of strange artifacts (Session 71).
- **Helen Apparition, Scrimshaw Tunnel & Detection Chamber (Session 72)**:
  - **Helen in the Tool Shed**: Confronted the floating apparition of [[helen|Helen]] (K.C. Station fixer) demanding her tools. An EMP miss triggered a shrapnel storm of nails and screws damaging suits before Isambard suppressed her projection with a second EMP.
  - **Scrimshaw Tunnel & Condensation Falls**: Navigated a crawlway through the Scrimshaw Tunnel and tethered together to descend the zero-gravity chain-filled shaft of Condensation Falls amidst drifting water droplets.
  - **Detection Chamber & Defense Condition 6**: Breaching an air duct into the Detection Chamber, the squad engaged three floating security bots operating under Defense Condition 6. Asher was severely injured before the bots were eliminated by coordinated fire; Beck stabilized him with an emergency trauma medkit.
  - **Monarch's Bounty Broadcast**: Monarch broadcasted an open bounty across station comms offering rewards to the [[the-doves|Psalmist Movement]] strike team to assassinate BIAS CCR (Session 72).
- **Zero-G Security Chamber, Foil Forest & Disassembly Chamber (Session 73)**:
  - **Security Chamber & Brain-Space**: Cleared a razor-material-choked tunnel with the Iris Opener to enter the Zero-G Security Chamber housing nine bot spheres. Scouted the door to **Brain-Space**, retreating after seeing a swarming hall of security drones. Broadcast spoofed radio chatter to mislead six tracking hostiles armed with gauss rifles.
  - **Foil Forest Tendril Ambush**: Slid through a trap door under ~150 sleeping androids into the zero-g Foil Forest. In an adjacent chamber coated with organic growths, acidic tendrils trapped Isambard and Asher, who tore free with point-blank gunfire and Beck's thrust pistol.
  - **Scrapyard & Disassembly Chamber**: Passed through a scrapyard warehouse of patched androids, finding an Arico Belt holomap. Breached the ventilation ducts to the Disassembly Chamber, witnessing mechanical arms butchering droids into scrap.
  - **Kira & Assembly Overview**: Befriended survivor android [[kira|Kira]] (wearing a *"Remember La Libertad"* patch). Pledged to extract Kira aboard their ship in exchange for her retrieving a Video Data Chip in Assembly Overview detailing Monarch's diver-scan emulation tech and unlocking the blast doors to **Final Testing** (Session 73).
- **Final Testing, Goods Elevator Solvent & Defense Condition 9 (Session 74)**:
  - **Final Testing Bay**: Navigated walls on grav belts past sorting pens and scanning arms. Kira explained [[damien|Damien]]'s cognitive past-life virus causing droids to recall past human memories.
  - **Chute Breach & Defense Condition 8**: Sniped a robotic feeder arm from range, raising station alert to Defense Condition 8 before sliding through the successful chute.
  - **Goods Elevator Staging Standoff**: In the zero-g staging room, two maintenance droids sprayed solvent that melted Beck's combat suit before Sarik and Isambard gunned them down. Looted a cache containing a laser cutter, combat shotgun, distress beacon, EMP grenades, and Data Drive 73.
  - **Wu-Ketai Parley & Defense Condition 9 Ambush**: Rode the cargo elevator down to an unpressurized loading bay. Parleyed with two stranded Wu-Ketai soldiers who promised to retrieve three vacuum suits. Forty minutes later, the elevator returned packed with heavy combat drones, triggering **Defense Condition 9** as the Ancient Orb flared (Session 74).
- **Elevator EMP Ambush, Loading Dock Evacuation & Kilroy Summit (Session 75)**:
  - **Elevator Ambush**: Trapped in the unpressurized staging room under Condition 9, Isambard threw an EMP grenade into the arriving lift car, incapacitating all five heavy security bots; Sarik severed cables and heads to ensure permanent destruction.
  - **Loading Dock Evacuation**: Beck reached Sky on comms, and Asher adapted a directional locator beacon. Sky brought the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] into the industrial Loading Dock. Using the elevator as an airlock, Kira fetched three replacement suits from Sky, allowing the crew to depressurize into the Loading Dock and board the ship.
  - **Psalmist Blockade & Kilroy Summit**: Sky alerted the crew that an armed Psalmist fleet (one capital warship docked to Level 1 and six gunboats) had blockaded the station. Commander Kilroy arrived on the Loading Dock with four marines; parleying with Isambard and Beck, she learned Damien was on Level 3 under an alias and Kira possessed wipe immunity, granting the team retention of their clamshell analyzer and promising tactical support for their upcoming Level 3 incursion (Session 75).
- **Loading Dock Encampment, Sub-AI Architecture, Secret Compartments & Fleet Clock (Session 76)**:
  - **Sub-AI Topology & Level 2 Containment**: Detailed schematics and debriefs from Kira revealed that [[silas|Silas]] and [[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]] operate independently of Monarch's central mind. Monarch constantly allocates the majority of station power and processing cycles to keep the Minotaur contained behind blast bulkheads on Level 2.
  - **Hidden Facilities & Ordnance**: Detailed station scans identified several hidden infrastructure targets: a dedicated Silas Control Terminal, a secret hangar, a micro-hangar, and a sealed compartment housing a functional **nuclear warhead**.
  - **Downtime & Psalmist Blockade Bluff**: During a six-day refit aboard the *Raconteur* in the Loading Dock (Days 201–206), the crew rebuffed Psalmist demands for their Precursor relics. Intercepted signals confirmed that Psalmists felt betrayed by Monarch and were withdrawing, revealing their boarding threats to be bluffs.
  - **Redshirt Fleet Deadline**: Comms monitor Arkady warned the station encampment that an approaching Redshirt combat fleet will arrive at Metropolis in two days (Day 208) (Session 76).
- **Micro-Hangar C-118, Infiltration Android Factory & The Nuclear Warhead (Session 77)**:
  - **Micro-Hangar C-118**: On Day 208, the crew navigated a shuttle bus to Micro-Hangar C-118, discovering working gravity, life support, and small automated vessels pre-programmed with courses to Kidderminster and T'Lachat loaded with digital brain scans of hundreds of individuals (including BIAS CCR).
  - **Infiltrator-Android Area & Personality Databank**: Discovered an expansive zero-g manufacturing hall filled with suspended, inert synthetic bodies, including an **[[exact-replica-of-the-duchess|Exact Replica of Duchess Margaret]]**, tethered to the **Infiltration Personalities Databank** for cognitive imprinting.
  - **Seminar Room & Inspection Booth Cleared**: Cleared surviving Psalmist hostiles using frag grenades and sniper fire, discovering a Wu-Ketai corporate presentation asserting station stability and denying shutdown rumors.
  - **The Nuclear Warhead Vault**: Breached the final reinforced vault door to discover the station's massive **[[nuclear-warhead|Nuclear Warhead]]**, with an enigmatic human woman seated atop it reciting cryptic poetry regarding the "Silicone Monarch" (Session 77).
- **Warhead Chamber Standoff, Wu-Ketai Ghost & The 4.5-Hour Detonation Timer (Session 78)**:
  - **The Holographic Ghost**: The woman perched on the bomb was identified as an unstable station ghost—a corrupted holographic memory of a deceased Wu-Ketai crew member desperate to trigger detonation.
  - **Psychic Memory Invasions**: When the crew attempted to delay detonation to escape, the ghost psychically lashed out, flooding Asher and Sarik with traumatic flashbacks of her demise on the station and disclosing a hidden personal diary in the Disassembly Area.
  - **Bot Waves & Warhead Armed**: While Isambard and Sarik held off waves of security bots with EMPs and firearms, Beck manually armed the warhead on the console, triggering station alarms.
  - **4.5-Hour Detonation Countdown**: By pacifying the ghost with EMP blasts and Beck's psychological profiling book (disguised as the diary), Asher successfully hacked the mainframe with the Ancient Orb's assistance, overriding the detonation sequence to set a **4.5-hour delay** before the station's nuclear destruction (Session 78).
