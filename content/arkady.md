# Arkady

**Summary**: Elderly scavenger-diver living aboard [[the-bell|The Bell]] outside [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], the contact known in briefings as [[the-old-man|The Old Man]] and BIAS CCR's guide to the station's dangers.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Arkady** is an elderly scavenger-diver who resides in a detached thruster habitat known as ***[[the-bell|The Bell]]*** moored outside the derelict orbital installation of [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 59). He is the resident contact previously referred to in mission briefings as **[[the-old-man|The Old Man]]**.

## Habitat and Knowledge of Metropolis
Arkady lives a solitary existence on the perimeter of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] restricted zone, surviving by diving into the derelict station for salvage. He possesses extensive technical and operational familiarity with the station's multi-level structure, its security systems, and its automated robotic threats (Session 59).

## The Warning of Monarch
When [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] arrived at *The Bell* on Day 188-1105, Arkady provided vital tactical orientation regarding the station:
- **Ancient Rogue AI**: He warned the operatives that Project Metropolis is ruled by an ancient alien rogue artificial intelligence named **[[monarch|Monarch]]**.
- **Mind Copying**: Arkady revealed that Monarch has the terrifying capability to scan, penetrate, and *copy a diver's mind*.
- **Tactical Advice**: He strictly cautioned the crew never to split their party, warning that divers who separate or become isolated are relentlessly picked off, manipulated, or replicated by the AI and its mechanical servants (Session 59).

## Ferrying BIAS CCR & Extraction Protocol
Arkady agreed to ferry BIAS CCR in his vessel to an unsealed entry point at **A00 Docking Bay** in exchange for provisions and salvage considerations. However, he imposed strict, uncompromising operational boundaries:
- **No Distress Responses**: He explicitly stated he would refuse to answer any emergency distress calls or SOS transmissions from inside the station.
- **Six-Hour Window**: He established a precise six-hour pickup window, promising to return to A00 Docking Bay exactly six hours after insertion (Session 59).

## The Execution of Lars & The Monarch Clone Rule (Session 61)
When Arkady returned to A00 Docking Bay to extract the crew, he discovered stranded scavenger [[lars|Lars]] accompanying them (Session 61). Arkady immediately shot Lars twice in the face and vented his body into space. He explained his ironclad survival rule: *any diver returning alone is a biological clone created by Monarch*. Arkady revealed this was the third "Lars" he had executed in two weeks.

## Commander Kilroy & Level 6 Insertion Offer
Arkady explained the broader internal conflict regarding Monarch, revealing that **[[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]]** led a faction dedicated to destroying the AI. He informed the crew that Monarch's core is located on **Level 5**, accessible via **Level 6**, and offered to ferry BIAS CCR directly to Level 6 for a stealthy approach (Session 61).

## Station Safe & Hospitality Aboard The Bell (Session 62)
During the debriefing return to *The Bell* in Session 62 (Session 62):
- **Artifact Custody**: Arkady clarified that he maintains no uncatalogued artifacts loose in the habitat; instead, he locks recovered artifacts belonging to visitors in his personal vault for security (including relics recovered by **[[magnus|Magnus]]**).
- **Managing Diver Politics**: Arkady alerted the crew that Magnus was desperately seeking news of [[lars|Lars]], while prudently avoiding raising the subject of Monarch with **[[noriko|Noriko]]** or **[[ghost-eater|Ghost Eater]]** due to their volatile histories with the AI.
- **Zero-G Galley**: Arkady prepared zero-gravity sandwiches for the gathered scavengers in *The Bell*'s galley while facilitating intelligence exchanges.

## Diver Consultations & Secrecy Warning (Session 65)
In a private interview on Day 190 with **[[asher|Asher]]** in his quarters (Session 65):
- **Magnus's "Bends"**: Arkady explained that Magnus was incapacitated by psychological trauma ("The Bends") from diving into Metropolis, urging the crew to transport him far away from the station when they leave.
- **Civilization Warning**: Strictly cautioned Asher never to speak of Metropolis or reveal station artifacts in civilized space, warning of severe corporate elimination.
- **Ghost Eater's Exile**: Disclosed that Ghost Eater has lived on *The Bell* for one to two years and is physically unable to leave.
- **Group Diving Protocol**: Reiterated that diving teams must always exit the station together to prevent Monarch from picking off or duplicating solitary divers.
