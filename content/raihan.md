# Raihan

**Summary**: Maintenance android on Metropolis Station's lower decks, fixated on a twelve-hour rescue protocol.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Raihan** is an industrial maintenance android encountered by the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] crew on the zero-gravity lower machinery decks of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Session 71).

## Background & Demeanor

Raihan operates under standard corporate industrial maintenance protocols (referencing [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] procedures). When encountered after the crew rappelled down the maintenance shaft from the Fortification Room, Raihan expressed profound relief at seeing human personnel.

He was preoccupied with formal emergency extraction guidelines, fixated on an impending twelve-hour rescue protocol window. He was visibly confused when [[isambard|Isambard]] claimed the party was a Wu-Ketai contingent searching specifically for [[damien|Damien]], pointing out that standard evacuation ranking places general maintenance workers and tooling at the bottom of extraction priorities.

Raihan accompanied the crew toward the [[metropolis-station|Toolshed]] where the [[woman-in-stained-white-overalls|Woman In Stained White Overalls]] and an uncataloged artifact cache were located.
