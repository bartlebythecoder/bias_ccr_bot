# The Choke

**Summary**: Sprawling scant-oxygen under-region of [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] where residents who cannot pay the daily tax are sent, encompassing [[doptown|Doptown]], [[the-sink|The Sink]], and [[calibans-heart|Caliban's Heart]].

**Last updated**: 2026-10-20

---

## Location & Purpose

The Choke is a vast under-region aboard [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], the quasi-legal habitat in the lawless [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] belt governed by the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]). It serves as the destination for station residents who cannot afford to pay the daily tax levied on those living aboard.

## Conditions

Oxygen levels in The Choke are scant, and life there is short. The region functions as the station's bottom rung: a place of last resort for the destitute, where the failure to keep up with the daily levy is answered with confinement to an environment that will not sustain its occupants for long.

## Sectors of The Choke

The Choke is not a single chamber but a collection of distinct sectors, of which the following are known:

- **[[doptown|Doptown]]**: The low-oxygen slum where the station's indebted are held, unable to leave without clearing their debts and paying the mandatory "oxygen tax".
- **[[the-sink|The Sink]]**: A sunken city wasteland within the station, now largely occupied by sewage and garbage, with few people remaining.
- **[[calibans-heart|Caliban's Heart]]**: The mysterious center of The Choke.

The list is not exhaustive; other sectors of The Choke remain uncharted.

## Resistance

The [[hunglungs|Hunglungs]] are residents of [[doptown|Doptown]] actively forming a resistance movement to free themselves from The Choke.
