# Pastor

**Summary**: Laborer in [[suliman|Suliman]]'s core laboratories who told [[isambard|Isambard]] about the lab's management under [[dr-young|Dr. Young]] and [[dr-harknett|Dr. Harknett]].

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

**Pastor** is an industrial laborer and support technician employed in the core laboratory facility of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation on the asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 38).

## Disclosing Lab Politics
On Day 117-1105, operative [[isambard|Isambard]] engaged Pastor in casual dialogue in the main corridor of the Green Area (Session 38). Pastor provided invaluable internal intelligence regarding management dynamics, explaining that a difficult and demanding supervisor named [[dr-young|Dr. Young]] had recently taken charge, answering to the elderly, benevolent head director, [[dr-harknett|Dr. Harknett]] (Session 38).
