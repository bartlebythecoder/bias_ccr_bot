# News Dispatch — Volume 2

**Summary**: Dispatches covering late 1104 into early 1105, including SuSAG's anagathic drug DeLeon, Pleasant Hill ceding governance to the Children of the Psalms with Duke Dulinor's backing, and stalled diplomatic talks between Kidderminster and Sanlucar over war crime trials.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Dispatches and Timeline

### 304-1104: SuSAG Announces DeLeon
- **Location**: Vale do Paraiso (1903)
- **Actors**: [[susag|SuSAG]]
- Megacorporation SuSAG announced the development of [[deleon|DeLeon]], a new anagathic drug formulated to extend human lifespans.

### 353-1104: Children of the Psalms Assume Control of Pleasant Hill
- **Location**: [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]] (1218)
- **Actors**: [[children-of-the-psalms|Children of the Psalms]], [[duke-dulinor|Duke Dulinor]], [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]
- Pleasant Hill, the capital system of the Pleasant Hill Subsector, formally ceded governing authority to the religious organization Children of the Psalms.
- The transition took place with the explicit, full endorsement and support of Duke Dulinor.

### 030-1105: Diplomatic Impasse Between Kidderminster and Sanlucar
- **Location**: [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] (1226) / [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] (1022)
- **Actors**: [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]], [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]], Imperial Marines
- Diplomatic negotiations between the Kidderminster planetary government and the newly established provisional government of Sanlucar de Barram have completely stalled.
- The deadlock is caused by rebel leader Giuliana Weber's demand that Imperial Marines accused of killing her family during the Sanlucar Civil War be extradited and prosecuted for war crimes.
