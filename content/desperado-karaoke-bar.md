# Desperado Karaoke Bar

**Summary**: Karaoke bar in [[hampton|Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]], managed by [[francis|Francis Wahlgren]], where [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] gathered local gossip about [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] on Days 230–231-1105.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

---

## Overview

The Desperado Karaoke Bar is a bar in [[hampton|Hampton]], the elevator town on [[tutin|Tutin]]. It is managed by [[francis|Francis Wahlgren]], and the ten-year-old [[necktie|Necktie]] works there.

## BIAS CCR's Visit (Day 230-1105)

After riding the elevator down to Hampton, BIAS CCR spent time at the Desperado asking around about the ranch, the town and the surrounding country (Session 82):

- A man named [[slim|Slim]] told [[sarik|Sarik]] that Redshirt spies were listening everywhere in town, some of them in the spa.
- Francis warned the crew that [[jiro|Jiro]] was a bad gambler who would ask them for money.
- Necktie told [[isambard|Isambard]] that a woman named [[deborah|Deborah]] works at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] beyond the hills and comes into town once a week, before Francis called him back to work.
- [[isaiah|Isaiah]] and [[tomas|Tomas]] attracted attention and stayed close to the crew.

## The Karaoke Night (Day 230-1105)

[[jiro|Jiro]] brought the crew to the Desperado that evening and offered to host them. About twenty-five people were there, including uniformed Redshirt Navy, and [[slim|Slim]] warned that spies were everywhere (Session 83).

- A local man named Saul confronted the crew over the simians, saying they were taking local jobs and that they and their employers were not welcome. [[sarik|Sarik]] called the simians their porters, and lovers, and offered drinks and paid work to anyone willing to guide the expedition. The argument faded (Session 83).
- At [[necktie|Necktie]]'s suggestion, [[isambard|Isambard]] sang "Duchess Is a Princess". The patrons stood and joined in, Sarik and [[asher|Asher]] sang, and [[beck|Beck]] danced. Several patrons recorded Isambard (Session 83).
- Isambard, Sarik and [[sky|Sky]] stayed on after the others left for the [[admiral-grace-inn|Admiral Grace Inn]]. Necktie offered himself as someone who could find out things or get them done, and told Isambard more about [[deborah|Deborah]] (Session 83).

## The Poker Game (Day 231-1105)

The next evening Isambard and Asher returned. Isambard organized a poker game, put Cr 5,000 on the table and played badly on purpose to keep people talking. He lost the Cr 5,000, but Slim gave Asher Deborah's contact number and directions to her home (Session 83).
