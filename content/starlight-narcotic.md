# Starlight Narcotic

**Summary**: Potent, highly addictive hallucinogen manufactured on Romentino combining Noavarite with psychoactive geyser compounds to induce temporary telepathic faculties.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Chemical Composition & Synthesis

Starlight is an illicit, planet-specific narcotic synthesized exclusively on the corrosive world of [[romentino|Romentino]]:
- **Primary Precursor**: Refined [[noavarite|Noavarite]] mineral harvested from the planetary crust.
- **Secondary Component**: Naturally occurring psychoactive biochemical compounds gathered from deep subterranean volcanic geysers.

## Physiological & Psionic Effects

- **Telepathic Awakening**: Beyond producing intense sensory hallucinations and severe chemical dependency, Starlight temporarily lowers neural barriers, awakening latent [[psionics|psionic/telepathic abilities]] in users.
- **Imperial Interdiction**: Because of its dangerous psionic side-effects and high addiction rate, Starlight is subject to an absolute interstellar prohibition by the Imperium.

## Underworld Distribution: The Shadow Bazaar

Manufacture is concealed in hidden subterranean refineries and distributed across regional black markets by the **Shadow Bazaar** syndicate, challenging the rule of "The Banks" and fueling esoteric religious rituals and illegal cybernetic integrations via the Cybertech Guild.

## Street Distribution & Precursor Interaction

In [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]], Starlight is peddled across impoverished districts like Barrikday by local street dealers such as [[lisa|Lisa]] (Session 10). Severe addiction is prevalent among marginalized youths, including [[screetch|Screetch]], whose narcotic intoxication triggered a fatal altercation with his father [[harry-proud|Harry Proud]] (Session 10).

Notably, an individual saturated with Starlight was able to interact directly with the precursor [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], causing it to glow purple and manifest precognitive visions of the future (Session 10).

## Aborted Nesvorny Smuggling Run

On Day 044-1105, an illicit shipment of Starlight was taped to the torso of street dealer [[tina|Tina]] to smuggle aboard the Frontier Liner to [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] (Session 16). The operation was aborted when escort [[bobby|Bobby]] was killed by [[sarik|Sarik]], and the narcotics packages were stripped and discarded inside an exterior starport maintenance airlock (Session 16).
