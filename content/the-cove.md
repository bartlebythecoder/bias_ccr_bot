# The Cove

**Summary**: [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s private landing dock inside a large asteroid in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico]] belts, where [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] sold him their artifacts after the fall of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis]].

**Last updated**: 2026-10-27

---

## Location & Layout

The Cove is a pressurized landing dock built inside a large asteroid in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico]] belts. It serves as a base for [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] and has its own flight control, which challenges ships that approach without an active transponder (Session 80).

Visitors pass an armed checkpoint and must surrender their weapons before going further inside. Baltimore's assistant, [[pamela-hooverly|Pamela Hooverly]], meets guests at the dock (Session 80).

## BIAS CCR's Visit (Days 209–211)

After escaping the destruction of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] crossed the Arico belts for about two days to reach Baltimore's coordinates. The ship's route around the sun and through the asteroids hid it from the Red Fleet approaching from [[freeport|Freeport]] (Session 80).

When flight control challenged the cold-transponder approach, [[sky|Sky]] switched the transponder on, identified the ship, and was cleared into the Cove. The crew arrived with [[tomas|Tomas]], [[isaiah|Isaiah]] and [[kira|Kira]]. [[asher|Asher]] carried the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] inside while the smaller artifacts stayed locked aboard (Session 80).

Over dinner at the Cove, Baltimore revealed his work for Redshirt naval intelligence, bought the crew's artifacts and hired them for the Tutin hostage rescue (Session 80). See [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] for details.
