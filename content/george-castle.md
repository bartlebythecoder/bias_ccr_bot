# George Castle

**Summary**: Research scientist formerly stationed at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]] who survived giant spider attacks and departed to join the Psalmist faith.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Mirassol Incursions & Evacuation

Dr. George Castle was a civilian research scientist employed at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on the frozen moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]] (Session 17). On Day 44-1105 in Starport Administration on [[romentino|Romentino]], Castle encountered operative [[asher|Asher]] and reported that following the arrival of corporate security investigators, nocturnal swarms of indigenous giant spiders ([[mirassol-spider|Mirassol Spiders]]) repeatedly invaded K.C. Station (Session 17). Castle was attacked by a giant spider and saved by station plumber [[mancuso|Mancuso]] before base management fully evacuated and abandoned the outpost (Session 17).

Shaken by the trauma, Castle underwent a spiritual crisis and departed Romentino bound for [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]] to dedicate his life to the [[children-of-the-psalms|Psalmist Movement]] (Session 17).

## Reappearance in Suliman Core Laboratories
On Day 116-1105, when [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] covertly infiltrated the restricted [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] core laboratories on [[suliman|Suliman]], [[asher|Asher]] breached an inner lab door disguised in janitorial garb (Session 36). Standing unexpectedly on the opposite side of the doorway was Dr. George Castle, who immediately recognized the operative and exclaimed: *"Asher, is that you?"*

## Delta Green Rendezvous & The Psalmist Deal
Castle met BIAS CCR at Red's Restaurant in Delta Green at 3 AM on Day 117-1105 to explain his circumstances (Session 37):
- **Mirassol Truths**: Castle confirmed that Beck's acquaintance Matteo had been killed by giant spiders on Mirassol. He also revealed that his own work on Mirassol was dedicated to **Project Phoenix**, attempting to enhance [[black-globe-generator|Black Globe force screens]] using genetic adaptations found in octopus DNA.
- **The Canine Uplift Deal**: Castle revealed that his current two-month training contract with Wu-Ketai was brokered by the [[children-of-the-psalms|Psalmist Movement]]. In exchange for investigating a secretive Wu-Ketai project aimed at the **uplifting of canines / dogs**, the Psalmists promised Castle asylum and permanent resettlement on [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]].
- **Lead to David Orana**: While unfamiliar with [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]], Castle connected the team to colleague [[david-orana|David Orana]], which ultimately led the operatives to uncover [[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]] at the Care Facility.
