# Theos Bar

**Summary**: Popular tavern and meeting place in Csabai Starport Alpha frequented by starport fixers, travelers, and Irklan cultural go-between Inkulan.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Setting & Clientele

Theos Bar (also referenced as *Theos*) is an established drinking house and social venue situated within [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 25). Known for hosting a diverse cross-section of off-world traders, local security personnel, and planetary intermediaries, the bar is notable as the primary public salon of [[inkulan|Inkulan]], the cultural liaison representing the [[irklan|Irklan]] sect (Session 25).

## Inquiries by Sarik

On Days 90 and 91-1105, following the assassination of [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]], operative [[sarik|Sarik]] visited Theos Bar to consult with Inkulan (Session 25). Over shared spirits, Sarik utilized the venue to negotiate the purchase of [[csabaipede|Csabaipede]] venom antidote and glean critical insights into Irklan martial contracts and their ruthless policy regarding the elimination of witnesses (Session 25).
