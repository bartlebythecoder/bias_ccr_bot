# Ridpath

**Summary**: Freeport attorney who acts as Demon Prince Yandee's legal counsel and BIAS CCR's only channel to her.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**Ridpath** is an attorney on [[freeport|Freeport]] who serves as the personal legal counsel and operational cut-out for [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]], the criminal overlord of the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] (Session 45).

---

## Operational History

### Freeport Legal Conduit for BIAS CCR (Day 130-1105)
Following [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s emergence from surgical convalescence and arrival on Freeport, [[sky|Sky]] arranged an introductory meeting with Ridpath (Session 45). 

Ridpath established strict operational communication protocols:
- Operatives were strictly forbidden from contacting Yandee or Eternity Station directly while operating on Freeport; all communications had to pass exclusively through Ridpath.
- He briefed the crew on the murder of 24-year-old [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] graphic designer [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]], which appeared connected to the syndicate network.
- Ridpath leveraged his connections to secure an afternoon interview for BIAS CCR with [[inspector-coker|Inspector Coker]], the lead police investigator on the case, and assisted the team in petitioning for official access to the coroner's post-mortem ballistics report.

### Ransom Negotiations with Julie (Day 131-1105)
At 7:00 PM on Day 131-1105, following the abduction and sedation of [[sarik|Sarik]] by the rogue telepathic assassin [[julie|Julie]], Ridpath called [[asher|Asher]] and facilitated an encrypted three-way conference call between himself, BIAS CCR, and Julie (Session 48). During the call, Julie reminded them of the Cr 1,000,000 bounty on Sarik's life but offered to release him unharmed without ransom if the crew carried out a specific task for her. Acting as the corporate and legal broker, Ridpath accepted Julie's terms on behalf of the syndicate and BIAS CCR while the crew actively triangulated Sarik's physical holding location (Session 48).

### Formalizing the Julie Settlement (Day 131-1105)
Following Beck's confrontation in Julie's condo apartment, Julie stipulated that Ridpath legally formalize the arrangement: assigning her a one-sixth share of the MCr 2.0 [[the-stratemeyers|Stratemeyer]] assassination contract backed by a Cr 100,000 escrow (Session 49). Both BIAS CCR and Julie agreed that all future operational communications between the two parties would be routed exclusively through Ridpath to maintain security and insulation (Session 49).

### Stratemeyer Bounty Verification & Settlement (Day 133-1105)
Following the assassination of [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn]], BIAS CCR transmitted data recovered from Jollynn's unlocked mobile phone to Ridpath to claim the MCr 2.0 bounty (Sessions 56–57). Ridpath initially cautioned that the phone evidence alone was circumstantial and that [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]] might withhold or reduce the payout without conclusive operational proof. When BIAS CCR partnered with [[palmas|Palmas]] of [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] to combine evidence dossiers in a 50/50 split, Ridpath certified the claim, facilitating an **800,000 credit** net disbursement to BIAS CCR after syndicate fees (Sessions 56–57).
