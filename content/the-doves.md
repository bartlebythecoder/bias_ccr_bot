# The Doves

**Summary**: Clandestine, militant Psalmist extremist cell operating on [[csabai|Csabai]], plotting the assassination of [[darnes|Darnes]] and targeting BIAS CCR to seize the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Ideology & Operations

The Doves are an underground extremist offshoot of the broader [[psalm-134|Psalmist faith]] operating on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 24). Unlike mainstream ecclesiastical institutions like the [[children-of-the-psalms|Children of the Psalms]], the Doves operate as a covert paramilitary terror cell dedicated to violent ideological purges and the acquisition of esoteric precursor artifacts (Session 24).

## Conspiracies on Csabai

The existence of the Doves was uncovered on Day 89-1105 by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] through decrypted intelligence logs recovered from the starship of slain information broker [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] at [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] (Session 24). Singh's files revealed two active Dove operations:
1. **The Assassination of Darnes**: An imminent lethal plot targeting an individual identified as [[darnes|Darnes]] on Csabai (Session 24).
2. **The Hunt for the Ancient Orb**: The Doves acquired intelligence confirming that [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] possesses the genuine precursor [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], authorizing an operational directive to liquidate the operatives and confiscate the artifact for the cult (Session 24).

---

## Palace Infiltration via Agnes Tuttle

On Day 91-1105, The Doves' operational reach was demonstrated when lead housekeeper [[agnes-tuttle|Agnes Tuttle]] was revealed to be a covert cult asset inside [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] (Session 26). Tuttle commanded palace footmen to paralyze operative [[asher|Asher]] and incinerate him to recover the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], confirming the cult's relentless drive to liquidate BIAS CCR (Session 26).

## Ties to the Csabai Psalmist Center

On Day 92-1105, intelligence purchased from starport cutout [[reshka|Reshka]] tied the assassin contract on [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]] and BIAS CCR to a young man in his 20s based out of the [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] in [[basin-city|Basin City]], confirming that the extremist cell operated from within the formal regional Psalmist apparatus on Csabai (Session 27).

## Kidderminster Roots, Electronic Bugging, and Dumont's Flight

Intelligence recovered on Day 93-1105 revealed that The Doves are rooted in [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]], combining fanatical hatred of [[psionics|Psionics]] with precursor relic plunder (Session 28). The sect successfully bugged BIAS CCR's Romentino apartment to track the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], embedded Dr. Elena Dumont as a deep-cover operative at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on Mirassol, and directed operative [[guillaume-sterling|Guillaume Sterling]] out of the [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] before his cell was dismantled in a shootout with [[sarik|Sarik]] (Session 28).

## Pursuit to Metropolis Station (Sessions 70–72)

On Day 192+, a diver vessel manned by an armed detachment of extremist Doves arrived in-system at [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]], tracking the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] (Session 70). Their inbound vector forced [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]] to scramble her gunboat to intercept the Dove raiders in the station perimeter.

## Fleet Blockade & Metropolis Rift (Sessions 75–76)

In Session 75, an imposing Psalmist battle fleet arrived at [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], docking a capital warship directly to **Level 1** supported by an outer cordon of six armed gunboats actively sweeping the station perimeter to hunt BIAS CCR and seize the Ancient Orb (Session 75).

In Session 76 (Days 202–205):
- **Extortion Demand**: A Psalmist negotiator offered safe passage to the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] in exchange for all precursor relics; Isambard rejected the offer, mocking the cult with false claims that the Orb was left on Mirassol or given to the Child King.
- **Castle's Warning**: Former K.C. Station contact [[george-castle|George Castle]] warned Asher that the Psalmists were mobilizing to storm the ship and plundering relics from the station.
- **Monarch Treachery & Retreat**: Beck intercepted encrypted traffic between Monarch and the Psalmist leadership, revealing the cultists felt deceived and betrayed by Monarch and were retreating back to their capital vessel, exposing their imminent boarding threats as a desperate bluff (Session 76).

## Station Stragglers & Tactical Elimination (Session 77)

In Session 77 (Session 77):
- **Abandoned Stragglers**: While the primary Psalmist fleet detachment retreated to their capital warship, armed Zealot stragglers remained trapped or stranded inside the interior decks of Metropolis Station.
- **Seminar Room Fragmentation Ambush**: In the Seminar Room, an armed Psalmist in a combat suit attempting to transmit distress signals back to their ship was eliminated when [[isambard|Isambard]] detonated two frag grenades inside the chamber.
- **Inspection Booth Elimination**: In the zero-gravity Inspection Booth, a wounded Psalmist taking cover near the quarantine airlock was spotted by Beck's drone and neutralized by [[sarik|Sarik]]'s sniper fire; the squad recovered the soldier's radio to intercept remaining Psalmist communications.

## Fleet Interdiction & Nuclear Annihilation (Session 79)

In Session 79 (Session 79):
- **Blockade Interdiction**: The Psalmist fleet continued to hold an outer blockade around Metropolis Station as BIAS CCR completed their warhead arming run.
- **The Conway Deal**: Officer [[conway|Officer Conway]] agreed to use Wu-Ketai naval assets to hold off the Psalmist warships in exchange for half of BIAS CCR's recovered artifacts, allowing the *Raconteur* to slip past.
- **Annihilation in Nuclear Detonation**: When the strategic [[nuclear-warhead|Nuclear Warhead]] detonated at 20:30 hours on Day 208, the docked and blockading Psalmist warship and its escort craft were engulfed in the nuclear blast radius and completely obliterated.
