# Tres Capulet

**Summary**: Veteran labor activist and leader of the Real Red Hand on Romentino who unmasked Elin Butler's kidnapping hoax and exposed the mining clone conspiracy.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Leadership of the Real Red Hand

Tres Capulet (pronounced *Trez*) is an elder labor organizer and the ideological leader of the [[real-red-hand|Real Red Hand]] in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 14). Unlike the mainstream syndicate faction that compromised with the planetary oligarchy under [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]]'s truce, Capulet's faction remains committed to the original founding purpose of [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]]: militant advocacy for miners' safety, wages, and workers' rights (Session 14).

## Resolving the Butler Hoax

On Day 44-1105, when [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[beck|Beck]] arrived at his safehouse, Capulet hosted them for coffee and shared his investigative findings (Session 14):
- Disproved the accusation that his faction kidnapped [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]], proving Elin had staged his own abduction to extort money from his sister [[christine|Christine Butler]] (Session 14).
- Provided the party with Elin's secret luxury apartment address (Session 14).

## The Clone Replacement Allegation

Capulet presented a mining prospector hostage whom he identified as an exact biological double of a close friend who died two years prior (Session 14). Capulet asserted that Romentino mining corporations are operating illegal [[cloning|Cloning and Memory Units]] facilities to generate compliant replacement laborers, accusing mainstream Red Hand leadership of actively colluding with the corporations in exchange for territorial dominance in Startown (Session 14). Capulet solicited [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] to investigate the mining corporations in exchange for intelligence (Session 14).

## Custody of Dr. Smart & Starship Brokering

On Day 47-1105, Capulet took formal custody of the captive geneticist [[dr-eva-smart|Dr. Eva Smart]] from [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] and fulfilled his commitment to the crew by connecting them with [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]], who provided immediate starship passage to [[csabai|Csabai]] aboard his private yacht (Session 18).
