# Club Strata

**Summary**: Nightclub in The Lux on [[freeport|Freeport]] where [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] was shot dead while waiting in line.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**Club Strata** is a premier nightclub situated in **The Lux** entertainment sector of the Humboldt District on [[freeport|Freeport]] (Session 45).

---

## Operational History

### The Louise Soto Shooting (Day 113-1105)
On Day 113-1105, 24-year-old [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] graphic designer [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] was assassinated by a masked gunman armed with an illegal projectile firearm while waiting in line outside Club Strata (Session 45; [[news-1105-vol-7|News Dispatch — Volume 7]]).

### BIAS CCR Inquiries & Surveillance Gaps (Day 130-1105)
On Day 130-1105, operatives [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] inspected Club Strata and the surrounding concourse (Session 45):
- **Surveillance Blind Spots**: The operatives noted that the civic and private surveillance arrays around the entrance possessed significant blind spots, which the killer exploited.
- **Bouncer & Bartender Testimonies**: The bouncer, [[andre|Andre]], discussed the district's gambling circuits, while bartender [[cadre|Cadre]] corroborated knowing Soto and having warned her against her abusive ex-partner [[david-parker|David Parker]].
- **Hunter's Heroes Counter-Operation**: While at the venue, [[beck|Beck]] detected an electronic tracker secretly placed on her clothing by rival outfit [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]]; Beck subtly reverse-planted the bug onto bouncer Andre.
