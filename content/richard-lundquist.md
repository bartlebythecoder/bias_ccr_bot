# Richard Lundquist

**Summary**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] researcher who vanished on [[suliman|Suliman]] and was later found artificially aged in the Care Facility.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Richard Lundquist is an employee of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation stationed on the mining asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system (Session 33).

## Disappearance & Covert Research
In late 1105, Richard vanished under suspicious circumstances while working on [[suliman|Suliman]]. Believing that Wu-Ketai was actively concealing the truth behind his disappearance, his wife [[joanna-lundquist|Joanna Lundquist]] contracted [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] to uncover his fate and extract him if alive (Session 33).

According to his close friend, security guard [[hector|Hector Nilasen]], Richard was an inquisitive researcher who had gathered disturbing evidence regarding uplifted simians that were far older than officially admitted by Wu-Ketai management (Session 34). Hector confirmed that standard corporate security was not involved in his vanishing, indicating Richard likely uncovered illicit research inside the central facilities before being eliminated or detained by higher authorities (Session 34).

## Discovery in the Care Facility & Fatal Cardiac Arrest
On Day 117-1105, acting on intelligence from technician [[david-orana|David Orana]], [[beck|Beck]] and [[asher|Asher]] visited the Suliman Care Facility (Session 37). There they found that Richard had been subjected to [[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]] under [[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]], forcibly draining his youth and transforming him into a decrepit geriatric known to hospital staff as "Dickie Lundquist". Recognizing Beck, the withered researcher called out *"Richard Lundquist, Echo Bloom"* before collapsing into fatal cardiac arrest (Session 37).
