# Palmas

**Summary**: Hunter's Heroes operative on Freeport who taunts Beck and competes with BIAS CCR.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Palmas** is a field operative and prominent member of rival mercenary outfit [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]], operating on [[freeport|Freeport]] in direct competition with [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] (Session 52).

## Activities & Rivalry with Beck

* **The Cafe Dimitri Taunt (Day 132-1105)**: When BIAS CCR raided the [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]] clubhouse at [[cafe-dimitri|Cafe Dimitri]], [[beck|Beck]] uncovered a physical handwritten note left inside the recovered laptop addressed directly to her: *"MCB - Just think, you could have been here when we were. I hope you are considering my offer. :-P"*, revealing Hunter's Heroes had beaten them to the clubhouse and executed the occupants (Session 52).
* **The Pramerski Burglary**: After BIAS CCR traced laundered funds to [[omni|Omni]] employee [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]], they arrived at his ransacked home to find a second note left on the refrigerator from Palmas: *"MCB - I am sorry we are so good. We cannot help it. :-P"* (Session 52).
* **Park 32 Standoff & Recruitment Offer**: Around 10:00 PM on Day 132, as Hunter's Heroes conducted a military-style sweep of Park 32 searching for the disarmed gunman, Palmas confronted Beck beneath the pedestrian bridge (Session 53). Expressing alarm that local police arrived on scene—suspecting a comms breach since only Hunter's Heroes were meant to receive the dispatch—Palmas questioned Beck about Pramerski and the Stratemeyers, and formally offered Beck a position in Hunter's Heroes. Beck flatly declined the offer (Session 53).
* **Freeport Mall Crime Scene (Day 133-1105)**: On Day 133, Palmas contacted Beck directly, directing BIAS CCR to a shopping mall restroom where Starka Pramerski had been found dead. Palmas vouched for Beck with [[inspector-coker|Inspector Coker]] and the police, granting Beck access to inspect the corpse. Palmas speculated that Pramerski's elimination was part of an internal leadership cleanup within [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] (Session 54).
* **Stardust Gala & The Takanabe Crisis (Day 133-1105)**: Palmas led a detachment of Hunter's Heroes into the charity gala at the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]]. Their surveillance was cut short by an urgent comms dispatch informing them that bank president [[tora-takanabe|Tora Takanabe]] had escaped their custody, forcing Palmas and his team to hastily abandon the gala floor (Session 55).
* **Mansion Woods Compromise & Bounty Split (Day 133-1105)**: Following BIAS CCR's raid on Jollynn's estate and the execution of Jollynn, Palmas stepped out from the surrounding woods. Holding evidence of his own, Palmas offered to pool their proof of Jollynn's syndicate operations in exchange for splitting the MCr 2.0 [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]] bounty 50/50. BIAS CCR accepted the deal, allowing both outfits to cash in through attorney [[ridpath|Ridpath]] (Sessions 56–57).
