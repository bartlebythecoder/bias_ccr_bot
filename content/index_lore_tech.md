## Ancient Relics & Archaeological Mysteries

- [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]]: Luminous azure crystal sphere recovered from the Mirassol Pyramid, genuine precursor relic of The Ancients.
- [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]]: Buried subterranean Ancient precursor structure on Mirassol targeted for artifact plunder.
- [[the-ancients|The Ancients]]: Hyper-advanced precursor civilizations that fought cataclysmic fleet wars in Solo-6 over 300,000 years ago.
- [[golden-rat-statue|Golden Rat Statue]]: Gilded rodent idol and shrine surrounded by offerings discovered on Metropolis Level 1.
- [[heavy-marbles|Heavy Marbles]]: Pouch of metallic spheres wired to a chest tripwire inside a corpse on Metropolis Level 2.

## Artificial Intelligence & Synthetic Beings

- [[monarch|Monarch]]: Ancient alien rogue AI controlling Project Metropolis with the capability to copy living minds.
- [[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]]: Empathetic AI created by Monarch, imprisoned in The Labyrinth and cloaked under horrifying illusions.
- [[silas|Silas]]: Autonomous station sub-AI functioning inside Metropolis Station independently of Monarch.
- [[alexandria-ai|Alexandria AI]]: Advanced supercomputer knowledge repository and Imperial-compliant AI advisor.
- [[ai-spider|AI Spider]]: Highly illegal, valuable autonomous robotic device recovered from a hidden cache in the Backways of Romentino.
- [[the-chosen|The Chosen]]: Privileged, armed android caste serving the Child King and Monarch within Metropolis Level 2.
- [[the-fallen|The Fallen]]: Disenfranchised, unarmed android caste subjugated and culled across Metropolis Level 2.
- [[exact-replica-of-the-duchess|Exact Replica of the Duchess]]: Inert synthetic android duplicate of Duchess Margaret discovered inside Metropolis Station.
- [[isambard-replica|Isambard Replica]]: Deceased non-human synthetic duplicate of Isambard discovered in the Tactical Simulation Suite.
- [[the-cube|The Cube]]: Specialized cyber-warfare virus and anti-AI weapon engineered by Blavatsky to dismantle Monarch.

## Science, Genetics & Technology

- [[black-globe-generator|Black Globe Generator]]: Impenetrable energy-absorbing force screen prone to catastrophic internal detonation on overload.
- [[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]]: Theoretical bio-mechanical transfer of biological lifespan and youth between organisms.
- [[cloning|Cloning and Memory Units]]: Traditional cloning methods, lethal fast-cloning breakdown, and failed SMU personality transfer experiments.
- [[deleon|DeLeon]]: Proprietary anagathic life-extension drug developed by SuSAG, banned across worlds and rumored used by the Emperor.
- [[noavarite|Noavarite]]: Rare catalytic fuel-refining mineral mined under extreme conditions on Romentino by LSP; precursor to Starlight.
- [[pressure-crystals|Pressure Crystals]]: Gravitationally formed crystalline matrices harvested from Nazca, essential for contra-grav drives.
- [[project-chimera|Project Chimera]]: Classified Wu-Ketai corporate genetic engineering and uplift project on Suliman.
- [[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]]: Classified Wu-Ketai lifespan siphoning project on Suliman utilizing Chrono-Energetic Siphoning.
- [[nuclear-warhead|Nuclear Warhead]]: Strategic nuclear device discovered in a secure vault within Metropolis Station with an enigmatic woman atop it.
- [[kinunir-battle-cruiser|Kinunir-Class Battle Cruiser]]: 1,200-ton Imperial combat warship built by General Products, plagued by structural defects and disappearances.

## Psionics & Substances

- [[psionics|Psionics]]: Mental faculties allowing telepathy and energy manipulation, subject to Imperial suppression and violent pogroms.
- [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight Narcotic]]: Illicit hallucinogen synthesized on Romentino from Noavarite, temporarily inducing telepathic faculties.

## Historical Events & Crises

- [[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]]: Decisive 1064 fleet engagement that stopped House Gaval; La Libertad remains an interdicted graveyard.
- [[hessian-revolt|Hessian Revolt]]: 11th-Century secessionist rebellion launched by House Gaval against the Kidderminster subsector.
- [[imperial-timeline|Imperial History Timeline]]: Master chronological history of Sector Solo-6 from Emigration Wave 0 through the 1105 crises.
- [[ruby-refinery-heist|Ruby Refinery Heist]]: Armed mercenary theft of rare super-metal containers from Marcolandia in 1104.
- [[sanlucar-civil-war|Sanlucar Civil War]]: Devastating 1089–1090 conflict on Sanlucar de Barram that abolished the Khanate of Aridush.
- [[sanlucar-crisis|Sanlucar Crisis]]: 1105 revolution on Sanlucar de Barram that toppled the Valentian Republic and brought Giuliana Weber to power.
- [[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]]: Brutal 1077–1083 counter-insurgency on Tutin that crushed the militant Order of the Psalms.

## Faith & Culture

- [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]: Theological and political movement originating from the 1083 Tutin Pacification, now active subsector-wide.
- [[the-134-psalms|The 134 Psalms]]: Sacred 134-verse poetic and moral canon forming the doctrinal foundation of the Psalmist faith.
- [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]]: Historic masterpiece on loan from Kidderminster to Viscount Rittenhouse, stolen from Csabai Palace and recovered by BIAS CCR.

## Indigenous Fauna & Ecology

- [[csabaipede|Csabaipede]]: Venomous multi-legged arthropod on Csabai, cargo infestation vector and Rittenhouse heraldic crest.
- [[mursa|Mursa]]: Giant apex predators native to Mirassol (Romentino), nitrogen-insulated fur, liquid methane metabolism.
- [[mirassol-spider|Mirassol Spider]]: Visual biological record for the indigenous arachnid of Mirassol in the Romentino system.
