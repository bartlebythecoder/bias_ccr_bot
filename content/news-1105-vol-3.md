# News Dispatch — Volume 3

**Summary**: Dispatches covering day 034 to 044 of 1105, featuring the diplomatic resolution on Sanlucar with [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] recognizing the Commonwealth States under President [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]], and the Imperial Navy lifting travel restrictions in [[romentino|Romentino]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Dispatches and Timeline

### 034-1105: Diplomatic Breakthrough on Sanlucar
- **Location**: [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] (1226) / [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] (1022)
- **Actors**: [[commonwealth-states-of-sanlucar|Commonwealth States of Sanlucar]], [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]], [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]], [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]], Imperial Navy
- Kidderminster formally recognized the Aridush-led [[commonwealth-states-of-sanlucar|Commonwealth States of Sanlucar]] as the legitimate government of Sanlucar, decisively ending the crisis.
- **Settlement Terms**:
  - The Aridush coalition gains control over Sanlucar's primary infrastructure and gas mining operations.
  - Former Valentian administration retains limited civilian operations in designated outposts under Imperial Subsector Naval protection.
  - In a gesture of reconciliation, President Giuliana Weber withdrew demands for war crimes trials against Imperial officers and ordered the release of several Psalmist-affiliated political detainees.
  - Imperial Navy upgraded Sanlucar's travel advisory to Green status, with commercial shipping returning to pre-crisis levels.

### 044-1105: Romentino Travel Restrictions Lifted
- **Location**: [[romentino|Romentino]] (1428)
- **Actors**: [[commodore-koroleva|Commodore Koroleva]], Imperial Navy
- Romentino Imperial Commanding Officer Commodore Koroleva announced the complete lifting of travel restrictions, restoring Green travel status to the system.
- All interstellar traffic (including Free Traders and independent vessels) may operate freely, while standard Imperial security protocols—particularly regarding AI and robotics controls—remain in effect.
