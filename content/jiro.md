## Gambling Debts

Jiro is a resident of [[hampton|Hampton]]. [[francis|Francis]] warned [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] that Jiro was a bad gambler who would ask for money (Session 82).

On Day 228-1105, Jiro asked [[asher|Asher]] for a Cr 5,000 loan, promising to repay Cr 10,000 after a poker game. Asher refused, and Jiro went on looking for money elsewhere (Session 82).
