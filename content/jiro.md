# Jiro

**Summary**: Gambler in [[hampton|Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]] who asked [[asher|Asher]] for a Cr 5,000 poker loan and was refused; later hosted the crew at the [[desperado-karaoke-bar|Desperado]] and took them to the [[admiral-grace-inn|Admiral Grace Inn]].

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

---

## Gambling Debts

Jiro is a resident of [[hampton|Hampton]]. [[francis|Francis]] warned [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] that Jiro was a bad gambler who would ask for money (Session 82).

On Day 230-1105, Jiro asked [[asher|Asher]] for a Cr 5,000 loan, promising to repay Cr 10,000 after a poker game. Asher refused, and Jiro went on looking for money elsewhere (Session 82).

## Host in Hampton (Session 83)

That evening Jiro brought the crew to the [[desperado-karaoke-bar|Desperado Karaoke Bar]] and offered to host them. He later took [[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isaiah|Isaiah]] and [[tomas|Tomas]] to the [[admiral-grace-inn|Admiral Grace Inn]], where they took rooms (Session 83).
