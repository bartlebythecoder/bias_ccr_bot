# Ancient Orb

**Summary**: Precursor artifact recovered from the Mirassol Pyramid; a crystalline sphere emitting a luminous azure glow, initially mistaken for the source of regional psionic blasts.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Physical Description & Discovery

The Ancient Orb is a hyper-advanced alien artifact discovered circa Day 34-1105 within an interior vault of the subterranean [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]] by operative [[asher|Asher]] (Session 7). It takes the form of a translucent crystal ball that radiates a steady, vibrant blue luminosity (Session 7).

## Psionic Field Anomaly

Upon discovery, field sensors recorded massive psionic interference coinciding with proximity to the orb, prompting the recovery team ([[asher|Asher]], [[beck|Beck]], [[isambard|Isambard]], [[sarik|Sarik]], and [[sky|Sky]]) to assume the orb was actively generating the destructive psionic shockwaves radiating across the moon (Session 7).

However, after securing the orb in a wooden crate and departing aboard an air-raft flyer, telemetry demonstrated that ambient psionic readings dissipated with distance from the pyramid, proving the orb was not the generator of the pulses, but rather a passive or resonant component of the larger precursor complex (Session 7).

## Strategic & Commercial Value

Recognized as an authentic relic of [[the-ancients|The Ancients]], the orb possesses immense commercial value on the Imperial black market—sought by figures like [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]]—and represents high-risk contraband strictly prohibited by the Imperial Intelligence Service (Session 7; [[the-ancients|The Ancients]]).

## Archival Research

Following extraction from Mirassol, archival analysis of Imperial academic and archaeological databases conducted by [[asher|Asher]] revealed zero cataloged entries, historical mentions, or functional equivalents to the Ancient Orb, underscoring its unprecedented nature (Session 8).

## Romentino Infiltration

On Day 37-1105, as [[ling-standard-products|LSP]] security forces surrounded the arrival landing pad on [[romentino|Romentino]], the orb was covertly smuggled past the checkpoint by flyer pilot [[sky|Sky]], who slipped into the settlement while her four comrades acted as decoy stowaways to withstand corporate interrogation (Session 9).

## Chromatic Shift & Precognitive Properties

On Day 38-1105 in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]], when brought into proximity with an individual heavily intoxicated on [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] ([[screetch|Screetch]]), the Ancient Orb underwent a remarkable metamorphosis: its luminescence shifted from steady azure blue to a vibrant **purple** (Session 10). In this purple state, the orb functioned as a precognitive catalyst, projecting vivid and accurate visions of immediate future timelines into the mind of the user—including a fatal armed confrontation that was only averted when operative [[sarik|Sarik]] consciously altered his actions (Session 10).

## Scientific Precedent: Dr. Pearson's Research

On Day 39-1105, queries by [[asher|Asher]] uncovered academic publications by [[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]] at [[nesvorny-university|Nesvorny University]], which explicitly document pre-human cultures on [[romentino|Romentino]] and their creation of "Ancient Orbs," verifying the artifact's historical context and providing a direct lead to Nesvorny (Session 11).

## Spontaneous Activation & Biological Aggression

On Day 43-1105, while stored in the crew's Noble Quarter apartment, the Ancient Orb spontaneously began vibrating violently and emitting pulses of light (Session 13). Moments later, a lethal 5-foot [[csabaipede|Csabaipede]] broke into the residence and attacked [[beck|Beck]] and [[asher|Asher]], suggesting the orb's energetic emissions may act as an aggressive biological beacon or resonant attractor for dangerous predatory fauna (Session 13).

## Retrieval from the Condo and Departure for Csabai

On Day 47-1105, while BIAS CCR was hunted across Startown Liberty, the Ancient Orb remained hidden in their Noble Quarter apartment during a police raid by [[the-banks|The Banks]] (Session 19). Following her release from detention, [[sky|Sky]] recovered the Orb from the quarters on orders from [[sarik|Sarik]] and transported it safely to [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s luxury yacht, where it accompanied the team on their flight to [[csabai|Csabai]] (Session 19).

## Flashing and Precognitive Warning in Jump Space

During the Jump transit from Romentino to [[csabai|Csabai]] aboard [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s yacht, the Ancient Orb unexpectedly began flashing with radiant light (Session 20). When [[sky|Sky]] held the pulsating sphere, it imparted an acute precognitive premonition of grave danger, prompting her to warn the operatives that deadly peril awaited their arrival at Csabai (Session 20).

## Precognitive Life-Saving Warning at Berth 17

On Day 89-1105 on Csabai, as Asher carried the Ancient Orb out of Calmer Singh's starship at Berth 17, the artifact vibrated furiously and glowed with intense energy (Session 24). Touching it, Asher received an immediate precognitive vision of himself and Beck being ambushed and slain by an [[irklan|Irklan]] assassin (Session 24). The forewarning allowed Beck and Sarik to set a lethal counter-ambush, killing the assassin and preserving their lives (Session 24). Subsequent intelligence revealed that a radical Psalmist cult called [[the-doves|The Doves]] was actively hunting BIAS CCR to seize the Orb (Session 24).

## The Palace Library Premonition and Decoy Snow Globes

On Day 91-1105 in the Csabai Palace library, the Ancient Orb vibrated violently and granted [[asher|Asher]] a terrifying vision of [[agnes-tuttle|Agnes Tuttle]] paralyzing him and dumping him down an incinerator chute to steal the relic (Session 26). Acting on the vision, Asher escaped immediate death (Session 26). Concurrently, [[sarik|Sarik]] acquired novelty souvenir snow globes and backpacks on [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]] to serve as decoy artifacts against superficial visual inspections (Session 26).

## The Suicide-Grenade Premonition in Apex

On Day 93-1105, the Ancient Orb vibrated in the team's Apex apartment, granting [[asher|Asher]] a precognitive vision of an attractive female assassin knocking on the door and detonating a metallic grenade in his face (Session 28). Acting on the vision, [[sky|Sky]] barricaded the door and secured Asher, forcing the assassin to flee before [[beck|Beck]] arrived (Session 28). Additionally, intelligence from LSP Corporate Security revealed that [[the-doves|The Doves]] had discovered the Orb by bugging BIAS CCR's leased luxury apartment on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 28).

## Deployment to Metropolis & The Labyrinth (Session 62–63)

Prioritizing their ongoing search for Precursor artifacts and operational survival inside **[[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]**, the crew of the *BIAS CCR* decided to bring the Ancient Orb with them on future incursions into the station, specifically during their planned dive into **[[the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]** on Level 2 (Session 62).

On Day 189-1105 (Session 63), **[[asher|Asher]]** carried the Ancient Orb secured inside his backpack during the team's re-entry into Metropolis and their descent via freight elevator into the Level 2 Lobby (Session 63). Asher monitored the sphere throughout their exploration of the Level 2 Lobby and Executive Bathroom, confirming that the Precursor artifact remained quiescent and inert throughout the incursion.

## True Function Revealed & Marble Retrieval Flashes (Session 66)

On Day 191 (Session 66), two major revelations and events occurred regarding the Ancient Orb (Session 66):
- **True Functional Nature**: The rogue AI [[monarch|Monarch]] directly addressed the team and explained the Ancient Orb's intrinsic design: it is not a destructive explosive or energy weapon, but an early warning security and precognitive defense sensor engineered to detect imminent personal violence and threat intent toward its carrier.
- **Psalmist Hunting**: In-system comms from [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] warned the crew that [[psalm-134|Psalmists]] and religious fanatics are actively hunting [[sarik|Sarik]] across the system specifically to capture the Ancient Orb.
- **Blue Strobing Activation**: When [[beck|Beck]] pulled the [[heavy-marbles|heavy marbles]] pouch from the chest cavity of the green-booted corpse in the Level 2 restroom, the Ancient Orb abruptly flared with brilliant blue pulses, station emergency lights flickered, and [[sarik|Sarik]]'s radio scanner shrieked an alert for an incoming station "ghost" (Session 66).

## Executive Lounge Activation & Henry's Surge (Session 67)

In the Level 2 Executive Lounge, after remaining dormant beside the retrieved heavy marbles in the bathroom, [[asher|Asher]]'s Ancient Orb flared with spontaneous energy when [[sarik|Sarik]] questioned the maintenance ghost [[henry|Henry]] (Session 67). The Orb's energetic flare provoked an immediate violent reaction from the apparition, which shrieked and shot lightning-like memory tendrils from its eyes into Sarik's eyes.

## Ghost Resonance in the Warhead Chamber & Terminal Guidance (Session 78)

In Session 78 (Session 78):
- **Emotional Resonance with Ghost**: When the squad breached the zero-gravity warhead chamber, the Ancient Orb reacted intensely to the escalating emotional distress of the holographic station ghost perched atop the [[nuclear-warhead|Nuclear Warhead]], glowing and pulsing as her agitation mounted.
- **Terminal Navigation Assistance**: As [[asher|Asher]] interfaced with the warhead's control mainframe to hack and modify the detonation countdown, the Ancient Orb provided passive sensory guidance and focus, aiding Asher in successfully overriding station protocols to set the **4.5-hour detonation timer** amidst incoming security bot waves.
