# Children of the Psalms

**Summary**: The militant/political governing wing of the Psalmist movement that assumed sovereign authority over [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]] on 353-1104 with Imperial ducal sponsorship.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

The Children of the Psalms is the organized political governing arm of the broader [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]; [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]). While mainstream Psalmists emphasize decentralized, peaceful contemplation of [[the-134-psalms|The 134 Psalms]], the Children operate as a disciplined faction seeking institutional governmental power ([[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]).

## Political Ascendancy on Pleasant Hill

On **353-1104**, the established government of [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]] (1218), capital world of the Pleasant Hill Subsector, formally abdicated and transferred sovereign administrative authority to the Children of the Psalms ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]; [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]]).
- This transition was officially sanctioned and patronized by [[duke-dulinor|Duke Dulinor]], establishing an Imperial-backed theocratic protectorate in the subsector capital ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).

## Interplanetary Polarities

The Children's elevation to state power has exacerbated regional ideological tensions:
- On resource and mining worlds like [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]], working-class resentment against theocratic patronage fueled the overthrow of the Psalmist-aligned [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]] ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).
- Corporate entities such as [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] actively suppress Psalmist activities on industrial worlds to prevent similar political incursions ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
