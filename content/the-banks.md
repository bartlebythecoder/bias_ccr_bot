# The Banks

**Summary**: Ruling financial oligopoly of Romentino that exercises de facto governing authority, controls planetary security, and issues law enforcement warrants.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Governance & Authority

On the corrosive and cold mining world of [[romentino|Romentino]], executive and judicial governance is held by a consortium of financial syndicates known colloquially as **The Banks** ([[romentino|Romentino]]). Operating as an oligarchic corporatocracy, The Banks oversee civic administration, contract law enforcement, planetary security constabularies, and starport customs alongside [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] ([[romentino|Romentino]]).

While The Banks hold civil authority across [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] and resource installations such as [[morella-station|Morella Station]], their extraterritorial jurisdiction does not extend to sovereign Imperial Navy installations or extraterritorial private starships docked at the starport (Session 19).

## Actions Involving BIAS CCR

- **Morella Station Financial Auditing**: On Days 44–47-1105, operatives from [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]]—[[sarik|Sarik]], [[beck|Beck]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[asher|Asher]]—infiltrated Morella Station under forged credentials as bank auditors dispatched to review discrepancies in mining quotas and equipment depreciation (Session 17).
- **Espionage Arrest Warrants and Raids**: On Day 47-1105, following the breach of Morella Station and the exposure of the illegal fast-cloning laboratories of [[dr-eva-smart|Dr. Eva Smart]], LSP executives pressured The Banks into issuing planetary arrest warrants against BIAS CCR for corporate espionage (Session 19). The Banks' security forces:
  - Broadcasted the operatives' faces across public holoscreens in [[gas-town|GaS Town]] (Session 19).
  - Raided the team's Noble Quarter apartment, taking [[sky|Sky]], [[tina|Tina]], and urchin guide [[sydney|Sydney]] into custody for interrogation before releasing them (Session 19).
  - Petitioned Imperial Navy [[commodore-koroleva|Commodore Koroleva]] to issue an Imperial arrest warrant, a request Koroleva refused (Session 19).
