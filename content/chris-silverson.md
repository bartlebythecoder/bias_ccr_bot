# Chris Silverson

**Summary**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] scientist from the Human Emulation Labs on [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], known to the crew only from an ID badge [[sarik|Sarik]] found.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Dr. Chris Silverson** was a research scientist employed by [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] within the Human Emulation Labs on Level 2 of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]].

## Artifact Comprehension & Emulation Research
- **Station Role**: An employee ID badge bearing Dr. Silverson's name and credentials was discovered by [[sarik|Sarik]] on an office desk in the Artifact Comprehension Labs overlooking a chamber of automated robotic arms.
- **Connection to Emulation Experiments**: Dr. Silverson was assigned to the *Human Emulation Labs*, the facility responsible for the synthesis, training, and cognitive stress-testing of [[child-androids|child androids]] and synthetic human copies under [[monarch|Monarch]].
