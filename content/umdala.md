# Umdala

**Summary**: Supreme overall crime lord and executive head of The Red Hand on Romentino, superior to captain Eminshi Moth.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Underworld Hierarchy

The Umdala serves as the supreme commander and titular head of [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]], the dominant criminal syndicate controlling [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] and the illicit trade of [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 15). Sitting above regional captains such as [[eminshi-moth|Eminshi Moth]], the Umdala exercises ultimate authority over syndicate arbitration, territorial allocations, and enforcement of the peace pact brokered with [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]] (Sessions 12, 15).

## Leverage Point for BIAS CCR

On Day 044-1105, rogue syndicate soldier [[joey|Joey]] offered to broker a high-level summit between [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] and the Umdala to appeal against Eminshi Moth's personal vendetta regarding the disappearance of [[sophia|Sophia Baker]], providing the operatives a prospective diplomatic lifeline within the syndicate (Session 15).
