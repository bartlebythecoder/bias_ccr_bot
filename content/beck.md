# Beck

**Summary**: High-society psychological profiler from Kidderminster who serves as medical specialist, interrogator, and social engineer for the crew.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Background & Demographics

Beck originates from [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] (1227), the high-technology subsector capital of the Sanlucar subsector (Sessions 1–5). Coming from an elite social stratum and high-tech world, she enjoys advanced Imperial medical and cellular rejuvenation benefits: although chronologically 55 years old, she physically appears to be 30 (Sessions 1–5).

Following the loss of her regular professional position, Beck entered corporate espionage alongside [[asher|Asher]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[sarik|Sarik]] (Sessions 1–5).

## Operational Role

Beck specializes in psychological profiling, behavior reading, and subtle interrogation:
- **Cover Identity**: Posed as an incoming medical officer at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]] (Sessions 1–5).
- **Station Deployment**: Stationed in the Redshed, managing the medical bay and interacting with personnel in common recreational areas (Sessions 1–5).
- **Techniques**: Utilized psychological manipulation, emotional profiling, and genuine rapport-building to extract intelligence regarding station activities and personnel movements (Sessions 1–5).

- **Manipulation of Dr. Dumont**: Circa Day 32-1105, Beck expertly manipulated station Chief Medical Officer [[elena-dumont|Elena Dumont]], appealing to her career ambition and anxiety over corporate reputation to secure official secret authorization for a nighttime search party, providing cover to enter the [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]] (Session 6).
- **Cognitive Risk Assessment**: Calculated psychological decay rates under the pyramid's psionic emissions, determining the crew had only a 60–120 minute window before lapsing into violent insanity (Session 6).

- **Neurological Diagnosis & Contagion Ruse**: On Day 35-1105, Beck analyzed team blood and imaging diagnostics, discovering that psionic exposure had left [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] with latent neural instability carrying a 1-in-36 daily chance of temporary insanity (Session 8). To bypass incoming corporate investigators, she convinced Base Manager [[tricia-schelling|Tricia Schelling]] and Dr. [[elena-dumont|Elena Dumont]] that deceased mechanic [[liam|Liam]]'s body was a bio-hazard requiring immediate evacuation, securing early shuttle launch to [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 8).

- **LSP Detention & Interrogation**: On Day 37-1105 upon landing on [[romentino|Romentino]], surrendered as a decoy "stowaway" while [[sky|Sky]] smuggled the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] past corporate security. Endured isolated interrogation by [[ling-standard-products|LSP]] corporate security under an electronic comms blackout before securing release onto Romentino (Session 9).

- **Startown Liberty Investigation**: On Day 38-1105, retained 11-year-old street guide [[sydney|Sydney]] to navigate Startown Liberty, bought weapons to maintain safety, discovered [[harry-proud|Harry Proud]]'s bag theft, and successfully cornered [[screetch|Screetch]] in Barrikday to recover the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (Session 10).

- **BIAS CCR Incorporation & High-Society Diplomacy**: On Day 39-1105, Beck filed corporate paperwork on [[romentino|Romentino]] to establish [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] as the crew's legal enterprise (Session 11). On Day 40-1105, she led the team at [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s soiree, conversing with Imperial Navy Commodore [[commodore-koroleva|Commodore Koroleva]] and facing down [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] President [[marion-rampagne|Marion Rampagne]] (Session 11).

- **Combat with Csabaipede**: On Day 43-1105, defended [[asher|Asher]] when a 5-foot [[csabaipede|Csabaipede]] attacked their apartment following the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]]'s activation, killing the creature with two pistol shots while taking a bite wound to her ankle (Session 13).

- **Tres Capulet Negotiation**: On Day 44-1105, met with Real Red Hand leader [[tres-capulet|Tres Capulet]], learning of Elin Butler's self-kidnapping con and inspecting evidence of corporate mining [[cloning|Cloning and Memory Units]] (Session 14).

- **MCr 1.0 Recovery Settlement**: On Day 044-1105, diplomatically engaged [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] and [[christine|Christine Butler]] to secure payment of a one-million-credit fee for the resolution of Elin's staged kidnapping (Session 15).

- **Naval Intelligence Tip-Off**: On Day 044-1105, delivered a confidential intelligence briefing to Imperial Navy Commodore [[commodore-koroleva|Commodore Koroleva]] regarding Joey's illegal Starlight smuggling scheme, attempting to orchestrate naval interception at the starport (Session 16).

- **Heroic Trauma Surgery & Lab Infiltration**: Intercepted the berserk Sarik at Morella Station, tranquilizing him while taking a blade wound, stabilized Dr. Smart's life, tranquilized security guards, and photographed the hidden Erebus Power cloning laboratory (Session 18).

## Infiltration of Suliman as Emily Cora
On Day 115-1105, Beck joined [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s covert infiltration of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] mining asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] under the cover identity of **Emily Cora** (Session 34). Following overnight detention on Day 115, Beck attempted an electronic intrusion into Wu-Ketai's project networks. On Day 116, Officer Anya questioned Beck and Asher regarding their clandestine contact with [[hector|Hector Nilasen]], revealing that Hector had vanished (Session 35). Collaborating with Asher and informant [[romero|Romero]], Beck successfully breached the station's surveillance system, tracing the last sightings of Hector, missing scientist [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]], and absent security chief [[marcus-fortune|Marcus Fortune]] to the blind spot of the core laboratories (Session 35).

Later on Day 116 at [[the-blue-moose|The Blue Moose]], Beck collaborated with [[sky|Sky]] to ply two laborers with drinks, creating the distraction that allowed Asher and Sarik to inspect the secret utility entrance (Session 36). Beck then ascended to the academic and administrative sectors alone, expertly pickpocketing an executive-level keycard from a Wu-Ketai scientist. Returning to the bar, she provided the pass that unlocked the utility closet security door, enabling BIAS CCR to disguise themselves and enter the core laboratories (Session 36).

Inside the labs, Beck learned from [[george-castle|Dr. George Castle]] that her friend Matteo had been killed by spiders on Mirassol (Session 37). Following leads from technician [[david-orana|David Orana]] on Day 117, Beck and Asher investigated the Suliman Care Facility. Attempting to interview patient [[shailok|Shailok]], Beck witnessed an aged patient call out "Richard Lundquist, Echo Bloom" before dying of cardiac arrest, learning from worker [[elara|Elara]] that both Lundquist and Hector Nilasen had been admitted as decrepit elders. Collaborating with Asher, Beck hacked Wu-Ketai servers to uncover [[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]], exposing the corporate technology of [[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]] (Session 37).

Preparing for a full penetration of the core facilities on Day 117, Beck and Isambard secured five laboratory passes, including a supervisor card (Session 38). Equipping herself with a black-market TL-12 stunner from [[orla|Orla]] and extracting lab schematics, Beck executed a tactical takedown of the Green Area security post: feigning a malfunction with her keycard, she stunned the security officer when he opened the door, bound him, and seized a backup stunner. Beck then entered the restricted Yellow Labs with Asher, inspecting an advanced medical wing just before a panicked simian broke loose (Session 38).

When the simian bolted past, Beck ducked into an empty medical lab to avoid detection (Session 39). Rejoining Sarik and Isambard in the animal pen labs, she assisted in subduing senior researcher [[dr-vance|Dr. Vance]] and confining the lab technicians into holding pens. Staging Vance on a gurney, Beck and the team wheeled her through corridor chaos caused by the liberated uplifted apes to breach the chrono lab. Beck helped capture video evidence of the siphoning machine, after which the team fled through the service tunnels and launched an emergency escape craft into deep space (Session 39).
