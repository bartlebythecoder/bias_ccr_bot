# Kidderminster

**Summary**: Major system 1226 in Solo Sector Subsector J, historical naval anchor during the [[hessian-revolt|Hessian Revolt]], and administrator of Tutin following the 1077–1083 Pacification.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Kidderminster (1226) carries the UWP profile A7B49AA-B and serves as the economic and financial capital of the subsector, housing the subsector's primary stock exchange ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]; [[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).

## Military and Strategic History

- **The Battle of Liberty (1064)**: During the [[hessian-revolt|Hessian Revolt]], the Kidderminster subsector navy formed the core of the Imperial fleet that checked House Gaval at La Libertad, preventing the strategic collapse of the subsector ([[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]]).
- **The Tutin Pacification (1077–1083)**: Kidderminster launched a decisive expeditionary campaign to crush the theocratic Order of the Psalms on [[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]] ([[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]]). Since 1083, Tutin has remained under strict Kidderminster administration with heavy residency restrictions ([[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]]).

## Diplomatic Role in the Sanlucar Crisis

In 1105, Kidderminster mediated the resolution of the Sanlucar conflict:
- Initial talks stalled over [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]]'s demands for Imperial Marine war crime tribunals ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).
- On 034-1105, Kidderminster extended official diplomatic recognition to the [[commonwealth-states-of-sanlucar|Commonwealth States of Sanlucar]], securing amnesty for Psalmist prisoners and reopening commerce ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).

## Cultural and Economic Leadership

- **Grand Tour**: In 049-1105, [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]] inaugurated the Cultural Exchange Initiative, launching a subsector tour featuring pop icon [[sabrina|Sabrina]] ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]).
- **Financial Markets**: The Kidderminster Stock Exchange drove massive trading in Sanlucar **pressure crystals** and tracked shifting corporate fortunes between [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], and [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]] ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]; [[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).

## Notable Individuals

- **[[beck|Beck]]**: High-status psychological profiler and medical specialist who benefits from advanced cellular rejuvenation before turning to freelance corporate espionage (Sessions 1–5).
