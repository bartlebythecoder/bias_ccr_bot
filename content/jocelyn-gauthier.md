# Jocelyn Gauthier

**Summary**: Former Csabai servant who eloped with Jean Luc Cartier aboard the Raconteur.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Jocelyn Gauthier is a former servant on [[csabai|Csabai]] who eloped with young nobleman [[jean-luc-cartier|Jean Luc Cartier]], nephew of [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse]] (Session 30).

## Flight to Marcolandia
Facing intense hostility from the Cartier family due to their class differences, Jocelyn and Jean Luc booked high-passage aboard the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] to start a new life on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]]. Their background was deduced by [[beck|Beck]] prior to jump insertion (Session 30).
