# Jin

**Summary**: Head of Corporate Security for [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] who interrogated the crew following their arrival from [[mirassol|Mirassol]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role & Interrogation Operations

Jin serves as the Chief of Corporate Security for [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] (LSP) on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 11). On Day 37-1105, Jin commanded the landing pad security squad that interdicted the medical shuttle arriving from [[kc-station|K.C. Station]], placing the port under communications jamming and conducting separate interrogations of [[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[sarik|Sarik]] (Sessions 9, 11).

## Baltimore House Encounter

On Day 40-1105, Jin attended Lord Baltimore's soiree escorting LSP President [[marion-rampagne|Marion Rampagne]] (Session 11). Jin was visibly stunned and disgruntled to find her recent interrogation detainees attending an elite noble gala dressed in formal aristocratic attire (Session 11).
