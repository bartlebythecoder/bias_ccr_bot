# Amelise Dubois

**Summary**: Master palace chef at [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] who oversees state banquets and culinary operations for Viscount Rittenhouse.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Culinary Role

Madame Amelise Dubois serves as the executive head chef at [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]], directing kitchen staff, food preparation, and state banquet menus for [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] and his household on [[csabai|Csabai]] (Sessions 22–23).

On Day 57-1105, Madame Dubois was preparing the banquet menus for the scheduled 4:00 PM grand ball when the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] vanished from the palace gallery, placing her among the internal household staff briefly interviewed by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] (Sessions 22–23).
