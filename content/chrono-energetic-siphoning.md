# Chrono-Energetic Siphoning

**Summary**: Theoretical bio-mechanical process to drain the biological lifespan of one sentient being and transfer it to another, representing an extreme frontier of life extension.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Concept and Theoretical Physics

Chrono-Energetic Siphoning is a hypothetical procedure posited in fringe bio-physical theory:
- Posits that biological vitality and cellular time-potential can be energeticized and transferred from a donor organism to a recipient.
- A theoretical machine would artificially age and drain the donor's future lifespan to confer physical youth and longevity to the recipient.

## Scientific Context & Operational Reality in Sector Solo-6

While long considered purely hypothetical among academic institutions, investigations by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] on Day 117-1105 uncovered that the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation had successfully industrialized a working Chrono-Energetic Siphoning apparatus on the asteroid settlement [[suliman|Suliman]] under the classified title **[[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]]** (Session 37).

### Operational Victims
Internal corporate records and Care Facility admissions confirmed the device was actively used to drain decades of biological lifespan from unwilling donor personnel:
- Geneticist [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]] was drained into an elderly, dying patient ("Dickie Lundquist") who suffered fatal cardiac arrest (Session 37).
- Security officer [[hector|Hector Nilasen]] was similarly drained of vitality and institutionalized as a senile geriatric patient (Session 37).
- Chief of Corporate Security [[marcus-fortune|Marcus Fortune]] was strongly suspected of utilizing the apparatus or receiving harvested youth via the secret hospital bed seen escorted through his private quarters (Sessions 35, 37).

### Chrono Lab Breach & Video Evidence
On Day 117-1105, [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] breached the central chrono lab facility on Suliman using the biometric authentication of captured supervisor [[dr-vance|Dr. Vance]] amid a diversionary simian revolt (Session 39). The operatives confronted two hiding research scientists and recorded extensive video telemetry directly documenting the operational machinery and architectural layout of the siphoning complex, securing incontrovertible physical evidence for client [[joanna-lundquist|Joanna Lundquist]] before escaping into deep space (Session 39).
