# Lady Eleanor Finch

**Summary**: Daughter of Viscount Rittenhouse, who expressed grave concern for her brother Lord Julian during the Beaugard painting theft investigation.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Lineage & Role

Lady Eleanor Finch is the daughter of [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] and sister to [[lord-julian|Lord Julian]] within the ruling dynasty of [[csabai|Csabai]] (Sessions 22–23).

## The Beaugard Incident

During the crisis surrounding the theft of the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] from [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] on Day 57-1105, Lady Finch was deeply distressed regarding her brother Julian's plight (Sessions 22–23). Aware of the harsh standards of their father, she feared that the apparent failure of Julian's guard vigil would permanently rupture his standing with the Viscount (Sessions 22–23). She was among the inner household figures questioned by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] before the true culprit, [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]], was unmasked (Sessions 22–23).
