# Hortalez et Cie

**Summary**: Ancient Imperial Megacorporation founded in 730, dominating interstellar banking, planetary government lending, mercenary unit bonding, and insurance across Solo-6.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Corporate History & Ownership

Hortalez et Cie, LIC was established in **730** by Lucienne Hortalez, capitalized from aristocratic family fortunes. It is one of the premier financial megacorporations in Charted Space ([[megacorporations|Megacorporations]]).
- **Major Stockholders**: [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]] (19%), [[house-alkalikoi|House Alkalikoi]] (8%), [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]] (2%).

## Core Business Operations

- **Sovereign Finance**: Direct long-term loans to planetary governments, underwriting planetary mega-projects, and acting as the premier interstellar insurance underwriter.
- **Mercenary Bonding**: Serves as the primary mercenary unit bonding house and clearinghouse, underwriting private military contracts across the subsector.
- **Arico Banking**: Operates **Tagarro Trust** in the Humboldt District on [[freeport|Freeport]] ([[arico-banks|Arico Banks]]).

## Spheres of Influence

The Kidderminster Subsector headquarters are located on [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]]. Hortalez exercises dominant economic influence across:
- [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]], [[beaumont|Beaumont]], [[maseru|Maseru]], [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]], [[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]], Calpulalpan, and Murnau.

## Subsector J Contract Expansions (1105)

- **Csabai**: Won the comprehensive planetary government IT contract in 1105, displacing rival [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] after 12 years ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
- **Kidderminster**: Awarded the 'Senior Operations Initiative' defense contract with the Kidderminster Navy, beating out [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]] ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
- **Marcolandia**: Attempted a buyout of the Foust Refinery and gas giant mining rights, but faced a defensive block by LSP ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
- **Regional Dominance Assessed by BIAS CCR (Session 58)**: During operational planning on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] identified Hortalez et Cie as the dominant corporate and financial hegemon in the subsector, factoring their ubiquitous influence into corporate risk assessments for the upcoming Project Metropolis infiltration (Session 58).
