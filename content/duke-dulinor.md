# Duke Dulinor

**Summary**: Senior Imperial nobleman, Duke of Ilelish and head of Great [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]], wielding controlling equity in [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], sponsoring [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]], and leading the anti-psionic campaign.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Dynastic Stature

Duke Dulinor is the head of **[[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]**, one of the Three Great Houses of the Imperium (alongside House Alkalikoi and House Aledon) ([[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]; [[noble-houses|Noble Houses]]). The dynasty holds hereditary feudal stewardship over the Pleasant Hill subsector ([[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]).

## Megacorporate Power

Through House Ilethian, Dulinor controls a commanding 19% equity stake in [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], giving the house massive leverage over planetary loans, mercenary bonding, and government contracts throughout Charted Space ([[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]).

## Political & Religious Patronage

- **Sponsorship of the Psalms**: In late 1104 (353-1104), Duke Dulinor provided full ducal backing to the theocratic transfer of power on [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]], installing the [[children-of-the-psalms|Children of the Psalms]] as sovereign administrators ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).
- **Anti-Psionic Crusade**: Dulinor is a vocal, militant opponent of [[psionics|Psionics]], utilizing his seat in the Imperial Moot to push for an absolute interstellar prohibition on psionic research and practices ([[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]).
- **Great House Rivalry**: Actively counters the expansionist maneuvers of [[house-aledon|House Aledon]] across bordering subsectors ([[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]).
