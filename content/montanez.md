# Montañez

**Summary**: Zero-G Vipers gang member tied to the Cafe Dimitri clubhouse in Jonestown on Freeport.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Montañez** is an underworld figure and member of the [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]] operating in Jonestown on [[freeport|Freeport]], specifically associated with the gang clubhouse at [[cafe-dimitri|Cafe Dimitri]] (Session 51).

## Role & Activities

* **Cafe Dimitri Nexus**: During intelligence debriefings with [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]], informant [[rico-mathers|Rico Mathers]] identified Montañez as a prominent Viper operative tied to Cafe Dimitri, identifying the venue as an exclusive hub for the syndicate's local enforcement (Session 51).
