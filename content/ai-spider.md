# AI Spider

**Summary**: Highly illegal, powerful, and valuable autonomous cybernetic/robotic device recovered from a hidden subterranean cache in the Backways of Romentino.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Technical Nature & Discovery

An **AI Spider** is a compact, autonomous technological device characterized by high black-market value, advanced technological sophistication, and strict illegality across Imperial and corporate jurisdictions (Session 19).

On Day 47-1105, during [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]]'s clandestine transit through [[the-backways|The Backways]] beneath [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]], street urchin guide [[marco|Marco]] discovered a secret survival stash hidden along a disused subterranean conduit (Session 19). Alongside emergency food rations and potable water, Marco unearthed an intact AI spider, noting that the device was extraordinarily valuable, powerful, and banned by planetary authorities (Session 19).

Recognizing Marco's loyalty in guiding them through an armed ambush, the operatives—[[sarik|Sarik]], [[beck|Beck]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[asher|Asher]]—permitted the youth to keep the device as salvage (Session 19).
