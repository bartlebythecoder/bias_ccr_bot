# Mursa

**Summary**: Towering apex predators native to [[mirassol|Mirassol]] in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system, insulated by nitrogen-hollow fur, feeding on liquid methane plants, and actively hunting humans.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Biology & Adaptations

The Mursa are colossal, carnivorous behemoths inhabiting the sub-zero tundras and methane ice plains of Mirassol in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system (1428):
- **Cryo-Insulation**: Thick, dense coats formed of hollow hairs filled with nitrogen gas, providing total protection against extreme cryogenic environments.
- **Liquid Methane Diet**: Possess specialized internal biochemistries adapted to metabolize complex liquid methane derived from native tundra flora, supplemented by hunting large herbivores.
- **Sensory Tracking**: Acute olfactory organs enable Mursa to pinpoint and dig out prey buried meters beneath solid methane ice.

## Human Interaction & Danger

Mursa represent an extreme threat to human colonization:
- They actively stalk and kill humans traversing wilderness regions.
- Exhibiting an attraction to manufactured metals and artificial structures, Mursa frequently infiltrate and lair inside abandoned planetary mining bases, research stations, and vehicle scrapyards.

- In 1105, a wild Mursa stalked and attacked an exterior expedition from [[kc-station|K.C. Station]], engaging the covert operations crew including [[sarik|Sarik]] before being driven off (Sessions 1–5).

## Conservation Status

Recognized as a keystone ecological species, wild populations are monitored by planetary conservation authorities to protect the beasts from off-world big-game poachers.
