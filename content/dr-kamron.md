# Dr. Kamron

**Summary**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] scientist and administrator in the Green Area of [[suliman|Suliman]]'s core laboratories whom [[sarik|Sarik]] spoke with while casing the facility.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Dr. Kamron** is a corporate scientist and administrative officer stationed in the preliminary Green Area of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] core laboratories on the asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 38).

## Encounter with BIAS CCR
On Day 117-1105, during [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s undercover penetration of the core laboratories, operative [[sarik|Sarik]] spoke with Dr. Kamron in an administrative office while casing the facility and locating the locked security operations center (Session 38).
