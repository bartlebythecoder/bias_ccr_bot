# House Yetrina

**Summary**: Ruling noble house of the [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] subsector headed by [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]], allied with [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]], and opposed to the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Subsector Governance

House Yetrina is the sovereign noble house of the **Kidderminster subsector** (Subsector J), anchored at the capital system of [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]]. The dynasty is headed by **[[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]]** of Kidderminster.

## Corporate Equity & Alliances

- **[[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]] Alliance**: House Yetrina owns an 8% equity stake in Tukera Lines, reinforced by Duchess Margaret's marriage to Tukera Subsector President Count Aaron ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
- **Diversified Megacorporate Stakes**: Holds 2% equity each in [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], and [[susag|SuSAG]].

## Political and Regional Stance

- **Anti-Psalmist Doctrine**: House Yetrina is a vocal opponent of the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement, standing in opposition to the religious transformations sponsored by House Ilethian on Pleasant Hill.
- **Dynastic Branches**: Counts [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] of [[csabai|Csabai]] among its prominent cadet branches ([[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]]).
