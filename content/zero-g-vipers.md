# Zero-G Vipers

**Summary**: Violent street gang operating in the industrial and lower docking levels of Jonestown on Freeport, led by Serpent Rix in opposition to the Primero Cartel.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

The Zero-G Vipers are an aggressive, street-level criminal gang operating within the decaying industrial sectors and secondary docking ports of Jonestown on [[freeport|Freeport]]. Commanded by **Serpent Rix**, a volatile and combative street warlord, the gang prioritizes physical intimidation, street respect, and aggressive expansion over formal corporate partnerships.

## Modus Operandi & Turf Warfare

- **Cut-Rate Narcotics**: Sells cheap, locally cut street narcotics and hijacked narcotics shipments to undercut the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]].
- **Street Crime**: Actively involved in muggings, cargo heists, starport brawling, and extortion.
- **Information Brokering**: Gathers and sells operational intelligence regarding Primero Cartel smuggling runs and starport movements to the highest bidder.
- **Conflict with Conde Borbon & Cafe Dimitri**: In late 1105, the Vipers aggressively expanded into Jonestown, establishing a fortified clubhouse at [[cafe-dimitri|Cafe Dimitri]] linked to operative [[montanez|Montañez]] (Session 51). Independent dealer [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]] believed the Vipers targeted him for assassination because he refused to purchase his narcotic distribution stock from them. On Day 132-1105, Cafe Dimitri was the site of a brutal triple execution discovered by BIAS CCR (Session 51).
