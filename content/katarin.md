# Katarin

**Summary**: Girlfriend of [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]] on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] who sold BIAS CCR information about him.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Katarin** is a resident of Section 2 on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] and the girlfriend of high-stakes gambler [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]] (Session 41).

## Encounter with BIAS CCR
On Day 119-1105, operatives [[isambard|Isambard]], [[asher|Asher]], and [[sarik|Sarik]] tracked Katarin to her apartment in Section 2 after purchasing a Cr 50 lead from [[roxy|Roxy]] (Session 41). Outside her door, the operatives overheard a loud argument between Katarin and a man named [[mosh|Mosh]]. After Mosh stormed off, Katarin answered the door armed and defensive.

Refusing Asher's initial offer of Cr 1,000 and off-station passage, Katarin demanded Cr 20,000 for her information. Isambard successfully negotiated terms, after which Katarin disclosed that Sargon had frequently visited the **Slick Base** clinic within [[eternity-station#Key Sectors & Black Markets|The Icebox]], directing the operatives to contact a street surgeon named [[ricardo|Ricardo]] (Session 41).

## Murder Interrogation & The Secret Wife
Following Sargon's murder later that day, BIAS CCR questioned Katarin again (Session 42). Displaying a bloodied knife, Katarin claimed she could have killed him in her own style, but denied committing the murder. She disclosed that [[sargons-wife|Sargon's Wife]] was residing on the station in Bunk 43. However, subsequent oxygen stick logs obtained by [[reidmar|Reidmar]] placed Katarin and [[mosh|Mosh]] directly in Sargon's corridor during the timeframe of the homicide (Session 42).
