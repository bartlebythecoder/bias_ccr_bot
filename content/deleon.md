# DeLeon

**Summary**: Proprietary anagathic life-extension drug developed by [[susag|SuSAG]], banned across multiple Imperial worlds amidst rumors of Imperial consumption.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Development and Chemical Profile

DeLeon is an advanced, proprietary anagathic compound engineered by megacorporation [[susag|SuSAG]] to retard biological aging ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]). Announced in late 1104 from Vale do Paraiso (1903), the drug represents SuSAG's flagship longevity product ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).

## Interstellar Bans and Imperial Rumors

- **Planetary Prohibitions**: A substantial number of worlds across the Imperium have banned the importation, sale, and use of DeLeon, citing unknown long-term cellular side-effects or ethical objections.
- **Imperial Palace Rumors**: Persistent rumors circulating through aristocratic circles allege that the Emperor himself takes DeLeon, granting the drug illicit prestige despite planetary bans.
