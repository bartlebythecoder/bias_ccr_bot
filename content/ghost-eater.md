# Ghost Eater

**Summary**: Heavily cybernetic scavenger-diver aboard [[the-bell|The Bell]] who dives into [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], which the divers call The Deep.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Ghost Eater** is an enigmatic, heavily cybernetic scavenger and diver residing aboard **[[the-bell|The Bell]]**.

*(Session 62)*

---

## Background
Ghost Eater survived a catastrophic industrial mining disaster earlier in his career, after which he underwent extensive cybernetic reconstruction on Eternity Station, situated in the **[[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]]**.

Following his cybernetic transformation, Ghost Eater turned to diving into **[[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]** (commonly referred to by the divers as "The Deep"). He considers the station an awe-inspiring wonder of architecture and technology, though far too expansive and labyrinthine for any single explorer to fully comprehend or chart.

---

## Dealings with the BIAS CCR Crew
In Session 62, while sharing zero-g rations prepared by **[[arkady|Arkady]]** in the galley, Ghost Eater engaged in conversation with **[[beck|Beck]]**:
- He offered to trade a profound, high-level secret of "The Deep" in exchange for the crew capturing a "ghost" from the station.
- When questioned regarding the mysterious monstrosity stalking the lower levels, Ghost Eater directed Beck to speak directly with **[[noriko|Noriko]]** to learn the truth about **[[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]]**.
- Arkady noted that Ghost Eater possesses considerable knowledge regarding the station AI **[[monarch|Monarch]]**, but generally remains elusive and reticent to discuss it openly.

---

## Warning on Monarch's "Breadcrumbs" (Session 64)
In Session 64 (Session 64), the crew consulted Ghost Eater regarding the booby-trapped corpse wearing green boots found in the Level 2 waste room:
- Ghost Eater explained that Monarch deliberately places enticing "breadcrumbs," items, and staged cadavers across the station to bait and exterminate inquisitive explorers.
- He praised the crew's discipline in refusing to disturb the heavy marbles chest trigger.
- He noted he had not ventured to Level 2 recently and had not seen that specific body.

## Zero-G Beers & The Mystery of True Ghosts (Session 65)
In Session 65 (Session 65):
- **Permanent Confinement**: Arkady revealed to Asher that Ghost Eater has lived on *The Bell* for one to two years and is physically incapable of departing.
- **Dialogue with Sarik**: Sarik approached Ghost Eater in *The Bell*'s common area, offering two cans of beer. Ghost Eater accepted, remarking on the experience of drinking in microgravity. When Sarik mentioned the crew might have encountered a ghost on Level 2, Ghost Eater cryptically replied that true ghosts were yet to be seen, reassuring Sarik that he would unmistakably recognize a ghost when he truly saw one.
- **BIAS CCR Assessment**: The crew evaluated Ghost Eater as holding deeper station knowledge than he admitted, with Asher considering hiring him for future dives.

## Defining Station Ghosts & Survival Rules (Session 66)
In Session 66 (Session 66), Ghost Eater elaborated in detail to [[sarik|Sarik]] regarding the true nature of the "ghosts" stalking [[metropolis-station|Metropolis]]:
- **Living Corrupted Memories**: He explained that station ghosts are not supernatural specters or mere digital recordings, but *living, corrupted memories* given physical/energetic form by the station's systems.
- **Precursor Static**: Ghosts are preceded by distinctive RF static, electromagnetic field interference, and spatial distortions.
- **Survival Rules**: Ghost Eater warned the crew never to play audio cassette tapes discovered in the corridors and to constantly watch ventilation shafts, as ghosts often manifest or traverse through ducting.
