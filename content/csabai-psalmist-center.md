# Csabai Psalmist Center

**Summary**: Prominent regional headquarters, temple, and administrative complex of the Psalmist faith on [[csabai|Csabai]], located in [[basin-city|Basin City]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Complex Layout & Function

The Csabai Psalmist Center is the primary theological and administrative installation of the [[psalm-134|Psalmist movement]] on [[csabai|Csabai]], located within [[basin-city|Basin City]] (Session 27). 

The facility is divided into distinct functional tiers:
- **Ground Floor**: Publicly accessible area containing quiet meditation chambers, a central worship chapel, administrative reception desks, communal break rooms, and hospitality coffee bars (Session 27). Visitors are provided with complimentary scripture books and guided orientation tours (Session 27).
- **Upper Administrative Floors**: Strictly restricted to authorized personnel via secured stairwells and private elevators, housing senior clergy offices, communications arrays, and executive directors (Session 27).

## Infiltration by BIAS CCR

On Day 93-1105, operatives [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] infiltrated the Center seeking to locate a 20-something Psalmist who had commissioned an [[irklan|Irklan]] hit against the team (Session 27). Guided on a public tour by receptionist Amelise, the operatives determined that their target operated out of the restricted upper administration floors (Session 27). Posing as wealthy off-world donors, Isambard secured an immediate audience with the center's executive director (Session 27).

---

## The Shootout on the Upper Level

On Day 93-1105, the restricted administrative tier of the Center was stormed by [[sarik|Sarik]], who incapacitated two armed guards and engaged [[the-doves|The Doves]] operative [[guillaume-sterling|Guillaume Sterling]] in a point-blank shotgun exchange, resulting in Sterling's defeat and a police raid (Session 28).
