# House Ilethian

**Summary**: Imperial Great House governing [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]] subsector under [[duke-dulinor|Duke Dulinor]], owning 19% of [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], sponsoring [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]], and demanding a total Imperial ban on psionics.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview and Domains

House Ilethian is one of the Three Great Houses of the Imperium, holding hereditary feudal rule over the **Pleasant Hill subsector**. Commanded by **[[duke-dulinor|Duke Dulinor]]**, the house's seat is the subsector capital of [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]] ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).

## Financial Stakes & Research Outposts

- **[[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]]**: Holds an enormous 19% controlling stake in the premier banking and mercenary bonding megacorporation ([[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]]).
- **Other Megacorporate Stakes**: Owns 4% of [[susag|SuSAG]] and 1% of [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]].
- **Colonial Research**: Directly funds the [[incubu|Incubu Asteroid Colony]] asteroid research colony in the Arico belt, conducting experimental genetics amidst Psalmist settlers ([[incubu|Incubu Asteroid Colony]]).

## Religious Patronage and Anti-Psionic Crusade

- **Psalm 134 Alliance**: Ilethian is the primary noble patron of the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] faith; Duke Dulinor directly endorsed the [[children-of-the-psalms|Children of the Psalms]] taking sovereign governmental authority over Pleasant Hill in 1104 ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).
- **Anti-Psionic Militancy**: Vocal, hardline opponent of [[psionics|Psionics]], aggressively lobbying the Imperial Moot to institute an absolute interstellar ban on psionic study and practice ([[psionics|Psionics]]).
- **Rivalry with House Aledon**: Continual strategic opposition to [[house-aledon|House Aledon]].
