# Darlene Suitor

**Summary**: Former head of the Psalmist Center on Csabai who was arrested in 1105 for leading an organized crime and illegal drug ring.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Darlene Suitor was a prominent religious figure on [[csabai|Csabai]] (1626), serving as the head of the local Psalmist Center aligned with the broader [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).

## Criminal Indictment

In 098-1105, Csabai law enforcement arrested Suitor and two confederates, dismantling an organized crime syndicate. Suitor was charged with murder, extortion, and overseeing an illegal narcotics distribution operation ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]). 

Following the arrest, the Psalmist Center issued an immediate public disavowal, condemning Suitor's illicit actions and asserting that the organization had no institutional involvement in her criminal enterprise ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).

## Confrontation with BIAS CCR

On Day 93-1105, Suitor was confronted at the [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] by [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] regarding a photograph of [[guillaume-sterling|Guillaume Sterling]] (Session 28). Suitor attempted to dismiss Sterling as an expelled addict, but summoned police when Sarik bypassed security and stormed the restricted upper administrative level, leading to a violent shotgun shootout with Sterling (Session 28).
