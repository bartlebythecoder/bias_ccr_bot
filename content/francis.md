# Francis

## In Hampton

Francis lives and works in [[hampton|Hampton]], the elevator town on [[tutin|Tutin]]. On Day 228-1105, after BIAS CCR arrived in town, Francis warned the crew that [[jiro|Jiro]] was a bad gambler who would ask them for money (Session 82).

Francis also called the ten-year-old [[necktie|Necktie]] back to work after the boy told [[isambard|Isambard]] about Debra, a woman who works at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] (Session 82).
