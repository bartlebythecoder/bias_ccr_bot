# Francis Wahlgren

**Summary**: Manager of the [[desperado-karaoke-bar|Desperado Karaoke Bar]] in [[hampton|Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]], who employs the boy [[necktie|Necktie]] and warned [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] about [[jiro|Jiro]].

## In Hampton

Francis Wahlgren manages the [[desperado-karaoke-bar|Desperado Karaoke Bar]] in [[hampton|Hampton]], the elevator town on [[tutin|Tutin]]. On Day 228-1105, after BIAS CCR arrived in town, Francis warned the crew that [[jiro|Jiro]] was a bad gambler who would ask them for money (Session 82).

Francis also called the ten-year-old [[necktie|Necktie]] back to work after the boy told [[isambard|Isambard]] about Debra, a woman who works at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch]] (Session 82).
