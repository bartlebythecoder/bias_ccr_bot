# Psionics

**Summary**: Mental powers allowing sensory enhancement, telepathic communication, and energy manipulation; subject to historical Imperial suppressions, violent mob pogroms, and noble resistance.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Capabilities & Theory

Psionics refers to the theoretical and practical application of disciplined mental faculties to interact directly with matter and energy without mechanical mediation:
- **Telepathy & Sensory Enhancement**: Non-physical transmission of thoughts, feelings, and perceptual scanning across distances.
- **Telekinesis & Energy Manipulation**: Physical manipulation of environmental energy and material structures through mental force.

## The Psionic Suppressions and Social Hostility

While the Third Imperium lacks a single, uniform statute outlawing psionic potential, intense societal paranoia in the previous century triggered the **Psionic Suppressions**:
- Suspected practitioners, students, and research institutes were systematically persecuted, legally disenfranchised, or driven into exile.
- Fear of mind-reading and mental manipulation generated extreme public hysteria, frequently culminating in summary executions and mob murders.

## Contemporary Polarization (1105)

Over the past decade, a growing reformist movement has advocated for objective scientific study and legal tolerance of psionics, creating sharp ideological divisions across worlds:
- **Noble Opposition**: Imperial noble house [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]] stands as one of the most intransigent and militant opponents of psionic research and legalization in the region.
- **Arico Pogroms**: In 1105, anti-telepath hysteria flared in the Cairo district of [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]], where vigilantes lynched accused telepaths Dario Caminante and a mother and child, forcing the declaration of **Martial Law** ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]; [[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).

## Precursor Psionic Energy Fields

Ancient architectural sites, such as the [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]], demonstrate persistent non-biological psionic emissions capable of radiating across surrounding terrain (Session 6). Extended unshielded exposure to these rhythmic psionic shockwaves induces severe neural disorientation and acute violent psychosis in sentient minds, though the emissions can be detected and tracked via field-modified radiation sensors (Session 6).
