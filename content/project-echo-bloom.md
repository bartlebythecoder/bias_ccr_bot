# Project Echo Bloom

**Summary**: Secret [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] program on [[suliman|Suliman]] that siphons lifespan from victims, leaving them artificially aged.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Project Echo Bloom** is an ultra-classified bioengineering initiative developed by the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation on the mining and research asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] (Sessions 34, 37).

## Technological Mechanism: Chrono-Energetic Siphoning
While initially logged merely as an encrypted project title alongside *Project Chimera*, investigations by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] on Day 117-1105 revealed the true nature of the program (Session 37). Technical files breached by [[asher|Asher]] and [[beck|Beck]] defined the project's operational mechanism as **[[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]]**:
- **Lifespan Extraction**: The process forcibly siphons biological longevity and cellular vitality from donor organisms.
- **Lifespan Transfer**: Stolen vital chronological years are transferred directly into a recipient subject, restoring physical youth and arresting or reversing natural aging.

## Human Experimentation & Victims on Suliman
Project Echo Bloom was covertly deployed against station personnel who probed too deeply into corporate affairs or were selected as involuntary donors:
- **[[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]]**: Siphoned into an elderly, decrepit man ("Dickie Lundquist") admitted to the Suliman Care Facility, suffering fatal cardiac arrest on Day 117 after uttering the project's name (Session 37).
- **[[hector|Hector Nilasen]]**: Siphoned into an elderly senile patient following his interaction with BIAS CCR (Session 37).
- **[[shailok|Shailok]]**: An elderly patient in the Care Facility frequently visited by Lundquist, suspected of being an earlier siphoning test subject.
- **[[marcus-fortune|Marcus Fortune]]**: Chief of Corporate Security, whose mysterious movement toward the core labs and the secret medical bed seen in his suite strongly implicated him as either a primary recipient or an architect of the siphoning operation (Sessions 35, 37).

## Compromise and Exposure
On Day 117-1105, the central chrono laboratory facility housing the Echo Bloom apparatus was breached by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] (Session 39). Leveraging the biometric credentials of captured researcher [[dr-vance|Dr. Vance]] amidst an uplifted simian revolt, the operatives confronted two sheltering scientists, downloaded project telemetry, and recorded definitive video footage of the operational siphoning machinery before escaping the asteroid (Session 39).
