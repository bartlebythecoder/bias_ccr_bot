# Lisa

**Summary**: Street-level Starlight dealer operating in Startown Liberty on Romentino who identified Screetch for the crew.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Narcotics Distribution

Lisa (also operating under or associated with [[tina|Tina]]) is an underworld street dealer (Sessions 10, 13) trafficking [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] across the frontier districts of [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 10). Because Starlight induces temporary telepathic properties and vivid hallucinations, its distribution is closely monitored by local syndicates (Session 10; [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight Narcotic]]).

## Interrogation by the Crew

On Day 38-1105, following the stabbing death of [[harry-proud|Harry Proud]], young street guide [[sydney|Sydney]] brought the operatives to Lisa (Session 10). In exchange for a Cr 100 bribe, Lisa matched the bartender's description of a young man in a blue shirt to Proud's addict son, [[screetch|Screetch]], and directed the team to his hideout in the Barrikday slum district (Session 10).
