# Sargon's Wife

**Summary**: [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]]'s estranged wife, found by BIAS CCR in Bunk 43 on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] during the murder investigation.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Sargon's Wife** is the legal spouse of gangster and gambler [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]] who resided covertly in Bunk 43 on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] (Session 42).

## Encounter with BIAS CCR at Bunk 43
On Day 119-1105, during [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s investigation into Sargon's murder, mistress [[katarin|Katarin]] revealed that Sargon's estranged wife had arrived on station (Session 42). The operatives tracked her to Bunk 43.

Presenting herself as an aggrieved woman in deep distress, she confirmed their stormy relationship, marked by persistent debts and sudden reconciliations. She admitted to having visited Sargon's apartment for a passionate encounter shortly before his death. Her timeline was initially backed by docking clerk [[loshi|Loshi]], complicating the team's forensic reconstruction of the crime scene (Session 42).
