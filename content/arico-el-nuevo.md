# Arico El Nuevo

**Summary**: Wealthy high-tech asteroid-belt world (1324) governed as a feudal technocracy, with its capital at the [[freeport|Freeport]] Torus and [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]]'s operations on [[suliman|Suliman]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Arico El Nuevo (commonly referred to as **Arico**) is an inhabited world located along the interstellar routes of the Spinward Marches (Sessions 31–32).

## System Profile
- **Classification**: World 1324 on the Kidderminster subsector's Main Circuit. Technically an asteroid belt with numerous populated asteroid habitats rather than a single planet.
- **Infrastructure & Economy**: Class A starport and Tech Level C (capable of building Jump-3 vessels and planetwide weather control). Governed as a feudal technocracy with millions of inhabitants, it has the highest per capita income and Gross World Product in the subsector.
- **Capital**: The [[freeport|Freeport]] Torus station.
- **Corporate Presence**: Subsector headquarters of Imperial megacorporation [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], and base of genetics corporation [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]], which achieved uplifted simian breeding in 1105.
- **Social Tension**: Despite its cosmopolitan diversity, the system has seen an alarming rise in violent anti-telepath pogroms.

## Major Locations
- [[freeport|Freeport]]: Capital Torus habitat.
- [[suliman|Suliman]]: Wu-Ketai genetics asteroid.
- [[janus|Janus Asteroid Base]]: Erebus Power / Carbrooke Minerals research base.
- [[incubu|Incubu Asteroid Colony]]: House Ilethian-sponsored research asteroid.
- [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]]: Lawless outer fringe of the belt, home to [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]].

## Destination for Psionic Healing
Following their victory at the [[foust-refinery|Foust Refinery]] on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]], the crew of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] set course aboard the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] for Arico El Nuevo in the hope of finding medical and psychiatric treatment for the lingering psionic madness affecting both [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] (Sessions 31–32).

## System Features & The Freeport Hub
The system hosts [[freeport|Freeport]], a 20km Stanford Torus station home to over a million residents, as well as the corporate mining asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]], operated by [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] (Session 33).
