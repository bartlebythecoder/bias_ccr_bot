# Isambard Replica

**Summary**: Dead synthetic clone of Isambard, some years older than him, found in the Tactical Simulation Suite on Level 2 of Metropolis Station.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The **Isambard Replica** was a deceased synthetic clone of operative [[isambard|Isambard]] discovered within the Tactical Simulation Suite on Level 2 of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]].

## Discovery & Physical Evidence
- **Location**: Discovered lying face-down on the floor of the darkened Tactical Simulation Suite on Metropolis Level 2 beneath overhead pedestals and fluttering fabric.
- **Physical Appearance**: When flipped over by Isambard using his rifle, the body was revealed to be a perfect physiological duplicate of Isambard, exhibiting identical facial features, build, and structure, but aged approximately 5 to 10 years older than the living operative.
- **Synthetic Verification**: [[asher|Asher]] scanned the corpse using his newly calibrated post-mortem biological scanner, confirming unequivocally that the body was constructed from non-human synthetic biology.
- **Significance**: The discovery provided physical confirmation of [[monarch|Monarch]]'s ability to fabricate mature, synthetic biological duplicates of station intruders from neural personality scans taken in earlier incursions.
