# Orslo Jollynn

**Summary**: Influential banker, philanthropist, and CEO of Jollynn Trust on Arico El Nuevo who was murdered in 1105 and exposed as the secret mastermind of the Stratemeyer Syndicate.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Public Profile and Banking Empire

Marie Orslo Jollynn was one of the most prominent business figures in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system, presiding as CEO and majority shareholder of **Jollynn Trust** in the Humboldt District on [[freeport|Freeport]] ([[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]]; [[arico-banks|Arico Banks]]).
- Celebrated as a mini-celebrity on Freeport and a major civic figure through her role on the board of the Freeport Housing Charity ([[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]]).
- Brokered high-profile institutional partnerships, making Jollynn Trust the official bank of the Starport Workers Union and refinancing major casino enterprises including Landmark Gaming, Lydia Casinos, and Stardust Casino ([[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]]).

## BIAS CCR Investigation & Surveillance (Day 133-1105)

Following the murder of [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] identified Jollynn Trust's financial bailouts of Ortega Frey's [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]] (Session 54).
- **Executive Security Detail**: When [[beck|Beck]] attempted to book an appointment with Jollynn, her schedule was completely full. The team surveilled her gated residential community and corporate bank tower.
- **The Bodyguard Connection**: Operative [[sky|Sky]] spotted [[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]]—the hitman who strangled Starka and shadowed Winslow Leach—serving as Jollynn's armed escort. Cyber-searches by [[asher|Asher]] retrieved public photos confirming the hitman was part of Jollynn's standard protection detail.
- **The Speaking Gala Target**: Discovering Jollynn was slated to speak at a public evening gala, BIAS CCR purchased tickets, aiming to infiltrate the event, observe security, and execute a distraction purse-snatch to seize and clone her personal smartphone (Session 54).
- **Charity Gala at the Stardust (Session 55)**: Attending the gala flanked by [[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]] and her executive assistant, Jollynn mingled with casino owner [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]] while guarded by local police (Session 55).
- **Midnight Starship Departure**: Intercepted comms by [[beck|Beck]] revealed Jollynn's assistant arranging an immediate starship departure for that very night. This prompted BIAS CCR to target the assistant's itinerary tablet and obtain architectural floor plans of Jollynn's gated mansion from [[isaiah|Isaiah]] to prepare for an interception before she could reach the starport (Session 55).

## Assassination and Syndicate Unmasking (Day 133/134-1105)

On the night of Day 133-1105, following the Stardust charity gala, Jollynn returned to her gated residential estate via security hovercart accompanied by her executive assistant and head bodyguard [[the-man-with-the-gun|Richard]] (Sessions 56–57). 
- **The Mansion Ambush**: [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] infiltrated the property and neutralized the residence guards. When Richard entered the foyer, he was neutralized and killed.
- **Hovercart Firefight & Execution**: As the hovercart attempted to pull away, [[sarik|Sarik]] shot and killed the driver through the windshield. [[beck|Beck]] and Sarik engaged the armed assistant in a fierce exchange, eliminating her. Sarik then confronted Marie Orslo Jollynn inside the disabled vehicle, delivering a chilling final message before executing her with close-range gunshots.
- **Data Recovery & Safe Loot**: The operatives seized Jollynn's unlocked mobile phone, cracked her concealed library safe with an ACR for Cr 65,000, and escaped through an underground tunnel.
- **Syndicate Overlord Confirmed**: Data transmitted to attorney [[ridpath|Mr. Ridpath]] corroborated that Jollynn had operated as the supreme commander of [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]], utilizing her bank president [[tora-takanabe|Tora Takanabe]] as the conduit to pass directives and funds to field commanders like [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]] (Sessions 56–57; [[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).

## Political Repercussions

Her murder and criminal exposure dealt a shattering blow to Arico financial circles and fueled the anti-establishment rhetoric of the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] populist movement across the belt ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
