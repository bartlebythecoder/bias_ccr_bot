# Winslow Leach

**Summary**: [[freeport|Freeport]] professor of Ancient archaeology, secretly funded by [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]], who helped uncover Project Metropolis.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Winslow Leach** is a university professor and academic researcher on [[freeport|Freeport]] specializing in Ancient precursor archaeology and lost technological installations (Session 53).

## Research & Project Metropolis

* **Covert Funding by Ortega Frey**: On Day 132-1105, digital forensics conducted by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] on a gunman's seized burner phone revealed that Leach was under hostile surveillance. When operatives visited Leach's apartment—where he worked alongside seven students—Leach admitted that [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]], the owner of the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]], was covertly bankrolling his university research initiative (Session 53).
* **Uncovering Project Metropolis**: After allowing [[asher|Asher]] to clone his personal phone, decrypted data confirmed that Leach believed he had pinpointed the exact Ancient precursor planetary site upon which the classified [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] was built, and had transmitted these survey coordinates directly to Ortega Frey (Session 53).
