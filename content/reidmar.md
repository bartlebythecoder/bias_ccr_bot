# Reidmar

**Summary**: A leader on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] who instituted a total general strike in response to hostage killings by [[the-stratemeyers|the Stratemeyers]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Reidmar is a prominent civic and labor leader on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], serving as the head of Teamsters Local 315F with administrative authority over station pass extensions and worker governance (Session 40; [[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).

## Encounter with BIAS CCR & The Sargon Bounty
On Day 118-1105, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] met Reidmar at the **Stellar Burn** entertainment complex seeking an extension of their 24-hour station pass while awaiting extraction by [[sky|Sky]] (Session 40). Concerned that the team's arrival in a stolen [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] launch would draw corporate retribution, Reidmar leveraged their predicament: he offered to extend their passes in exchange for BIAS CCR exposing [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]], a high-stakes gambler linked to the rival [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] suspected of using illicit cheats to drain the union's games (Session 40).

## Fleet Hijacking & Sargon's Murder
On Day 119-1105, Reidmar's merchant freighter fleet was captured in the Dark Crescent by [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyer Syndicate]], prompting Reidmar to demand armed escort and intervention from the station's mercenary Tempest Company (Session 41). Later that day, BIAS CCR presented forensic files extracted from cyber-surgeon [[solon|Solon]], proving Sargon utilized an illegal "black box" neural recall implant. Reidmar pledged to confront the Meinhoff leadership and ban the technology. Merely two hours later, however, Reidmar urgently summoned the operatives back to announce that Sargon had been **murdered** (Session 41).

## Safehouse Shelter & Audience with Yandee
Following BIAS CCR's escape from the Tempest Company, Reidmar hid the operatives in a secure safehouse for several hours while [[asher|Asher]] decrypted Sargon's neural black box (Session 43). Using the recovered visual logs proving [[mosh|Mosh]] killed Sargon, Reidmar escorted the crew into the executive court of [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee]], successfully securing a full syndicate pardon for the operatives (Session 43).

## 1105 Crisis and General Strike
Later on Day 131-1105, in retaliation against the murder of hostages by [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]], Reidmar mobilized the workforce and declared an indefinite general strike, halting all starship traffic and port logistics across Eternity Station ([[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).
