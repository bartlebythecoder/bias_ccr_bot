# Vivienne LaCroix

**Summary**: High-society socialite and girlfriend of [[lord-julian|Lord Julian]] who orchestrated the theft of the Beaugard painting inside [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] by drugging Julian's coffee.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Social Standing & Intrigue

Vivienne LaCroix is a prominent party girl and high-society socialite on [[csabai|Csabai]], intimately involved with [[lord-julian|Lord Julian]], the son of [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] (Sessions 22–23).

## The Beaugard Theft

On Day 57-1105, Vivienne participated in a conspiracy to steal the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] from the [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] gallery (Sessions 22–23):
- Forensic tracking of automated camera logs conducted by [[asher|Asher]] and [[sky|Sky]] revealed that Vivienne spent ten minutes conferring in the private chambers of [[lord-edgar|Lord Edgar]] immediately before entering the gallery (Sessions 22–23).
- Entering the gallery, she provided Lord Julian with coffee containing a fast-acting sedative (Sessions 22–23).
- Once Julian collapsed, she unhooked the masterpiece, stashed it, and administered a chemical stimulant to awaken Julian, masking the period of unconsciousness (Sessions 22–23).
- She concealed the stolen masterpiece behind another wall painting inside her private palace guest quarters (Sessions 22–23).

Her scheme unraveled when [[sarik|Sarik]] charmed her out of her room with an invitation to drink vintage wine, allowing [[asher|Asher]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] to search her suite and recover the painting (Sessions 22–23).
