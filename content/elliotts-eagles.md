# Elliott's Eagles

**Summary**: Five-man mercenary fire team hired by BIAS CCR to guard the Foust Refinery warehouse on Marcolandia.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Elliott's Eagles is a private five-man mercenary fire team operating in the Spinward Marches (Session 30).

## Contract at Foust Refinery
Upon arriving on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]], the crew of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] utilized a Cr 50,000 operational expense account provided by [[kino-yuba-foust|Kino Yuba Foust]] to hire Elliott's Eagles as supplemental muscle and tactical support to safeguard the [[foust-refinery|Foust Refinery]] in Hollow against an expected rare metals heist (Session 30).

### Warehouse Defense Deployment
Recognizing the rare metals warehouse as the vital objective, [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] garrisoned Elliott's Eagles inside the warehouse 24/7. Their permanent presence secured the stockpiles against any potential breakthrough by [[larry-mckarney|Larry McKarney]]'s strike team, securing the full contract payout (Sessions 31–32).
