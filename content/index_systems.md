## Star Systems & Worlds

- [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]]: Wealthy high-tech asteroid belt system (1324), home to Freeport, genetics hubs, and deep social divides.
- [[beaumont|Beaumont]]: Authoritarian system (1422) ruled by Supreme Leader Volkov.
- [[csabai|Csabai]]: High-population world (1626) ruled by Viscount Rittenhouse, home to the Irklan, IT contract battles, and Sabrina's tour.
- [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]]: System (1226) financial center, naval anchor during Hessian Revolt, and administrative ruler of Tutin.
- [[lachat|Lachat]]: Low-population world (1030), base for Erebus Power's Alexandria project.
- [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]]: Icy moon of Vilnohirsk (1425, pop 700,000) balkanized into Ouro and Paraty, refining super-metals.
- [[maseru|Maseru]]: System (1224) known for prison moons and penal colonies facing severe overcrowding.
- [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]]: Industrial world (1229), home to Nesvorny University and subsector division HQ of Ling Standard Products.
- [[pharr|Pharr]]: System (1130) undergoing a major humanitarian crisis in 1105.
- [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]]: Subsector capital (1218) governed by Children of the Psalms.
- [[romentino|Romentino]]: Frigid corrosive world (1428) ruled by "The Banks," centered on Noavarite mining and Red Hand terror.
- [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]]: Arid gas-mining moon system (1022) site of the Commonwealth government, pressure crystal trade, and equipment shortages.
- [[mirassol|Mirassol]]: Frigid, low-oxygen moon in Romentino system hosting K.C. Station, Mursa ecology, and Ancient pyramid.

## Habitats, Stations & Major Facilities

- [[freeport|Freeport]]: Capital 20km Torus orbital station of Arico (pop 1,045,200), containing Humboldt, Cairo, and Jonestown.
- [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]]: Lawless outer perimeter of the Arico belt sheltering Eternity Station and syndicate raiders.
- [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]: Interstellar habitat in the Dark Crescent ruled by Meinhoff Brigade, site of Doptown and general strike.
- [[incubu|Incubu Asteroid Colony]]: Asteroid research colony in the Arico belt funded by House Ilethian, site of Psalmist genetic friction.
- [[janus|Janus Asteroid Base]]: Secretive 40-person asteroid research outpost in the Arico belt operated by Carbrooke Minerals.
- [[suliman|Suliman]]: Massive Wu-Ketai corporate genetics asteroid (pop 70,000) facing severe labor unrest.
- [[kc-station|K.C. Station]]: Modular research post on Mirassol (Redshed, Workshop, Science Building) target of espionage mission.
- [[morella-station|Morella Station]]: Subterranean mining rig and settlement on Romentino, site of illicit clone replacement labor.
- [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]: Rumored abandoned Wu-Ketai research station that suffered a rogue-AI catastrophe.
- [[foust-refinery|Foust Refinery]]: Rare metals processing facility in Hollow, Marcolandia.
- [[ruby-refinery|Ruby Refinery]]: Industrial refining facility on Marcolandia owned by Rainford Ruby.
- [[freeport-university|Freeport University]]: Academic institution in Freeport attended by Chloe Vinit.

## Settlements, Urban Sectors & Establishments

### Csabai
- [[apex|Apex]]: High-tech floating repulsor/grav-city on Csabai housing corporate headquarters and municipal databanks.
- [[basin-city|Basin City]]: Major surface metropolis on Csabai, site of the Csabai Psalmist Center.
- [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]]: Grand seat of government and ancestral estate of Viscount Rittenhouse on Csabai.
- [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]]: Regional headquarters and temple complex of the Psalmist movement in Basin City on Csabai.
- [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]]: Primary commercial starport terminal and transportation hub on Csabai.
- [[sammys-dojo|Sammy's Dojo]]: Martial arts gym in Apex utilized as a Dove training and staging facility.
- [[mushroom-woods|Mushroom Woods]]: Wilderness area outside Apex housing Jacob Godard's safehouse.
- [[henrys-tavern|Henry's Tavern]]: Popular drinking establishment in Csabai Starport Alpha, site of Calmer Singh's assassination.
- [[theos-bar|Theos Bar]]: Popular drinking establishment in Csabai Starport Alpha frequented by Irklan liaison Inkulan.

### Romentino & Startown Liberty
- [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]]: Frontier starport community on Romentino (pop ~5,000) divided into green zones, GAS town, and Barrikday.
- [[the-backways|The Backways]]: Subterranean labyrinth of abandoned maintenance passages, rusty waterways, and refuse slums beneath Startown Liberty.
- [[gas-town|GaS Town]]: Industrial and vice district in Startown Liberty on Romentino, known for illicit trade and public holoscreens.
- [[blue-parrot|Blue Parrot]]: Tavern in the GAS town industrial district of Startown Liberty on Romentino.
- [[ricci-plaza|Ricci Plaza]]: Civilized public commercial square and cafe concourse in Startown Liberty on Romentino.
- [[rickeys-casino|Rickeys Casino]]: Gaming house in the Entertainment district of Startown Liberty on Romentino.
- [[rose-and-crown|Rose and Crown]]: Public house in Startown Liberty on Romentino used as the Butler ransom drop site.

### Freeport & Suliman
- [[the-warrens|The Warrens]]: Sprawling subterranean residential and industrial tunnel network on Suliman.
- [[the-rusty-bolt|The Rusty Bolt]]: Cramped tavern in The Warrens on Suliman frequented by laborers and uplifted simians.
- [[the-blue-moose|The Blue Moose]]: Subterranean tavern in The Warrens on Suliman concealing the core lab entrance.
- [[derry|Derry]]: Commercial sector and sushi bar on Suliman where BIAS CCR met officer Hector.
- [[club-strata|Club Strata]]: Nightclub in The Lux entertainment district on Freeport, site of the Louise Soto homicide.
- [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]]: Entertainment and gaming establishment in North Lux on Freeport, operational base of broker Stadler.
- [[cafe-dimitri|Cafe Dimitri]]: Zero-G Vipers clubhouse and site of triple execution in Jonestown on Freeport.
- [[orion-docks|Orion Docks]]: Commercial freight and cargo shipping facility on Freeport with union customs payoffs.

### Project Metropolis Sub-Sectors
- [[the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]: Shifting, organic maze sector on Metropolis Level 2 housing the Minotaur.

## Starships & Spacecraft

- [[raconteur|Raconteur]]: Scout/Courier vessel on loan to Isambard from the Imperial Scout Service.
- [[cals-dream|Cal's Dream]]: Starship belonging to broker Calmer Singh at Berth 17 of Starport Alpha where his murdered crew was discovered.
- [[belladonna|Belladonna]]: Starship berthed at Freeport's Class A starport linked to Conde Borbon and Primero Cartel.
- [[sharpshooter|Sharpshooter]]: 300-ton Wu-Ketai naval patrol gunboat conducting interdiction sweeps around Project Metropolis.
- [[the-bell|The Bell]]: Detached thruster habitat and dive craft outside Project Metropolis operated by Arkady.
- [[saltpeter|Saltpeter]]: Scavenger starship whose crew operated at Project Metropolis and abandoned Lars.
