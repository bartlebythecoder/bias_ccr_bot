# Harvon Sargon

**Summary**: Meinhoff-linked high-stakes gambler on Eternity Station whose winning streak and later murder BIAS CCR investigated.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Harvon Sargon** is a high-stakes gambler, underworld figure, and suspected card cheat associated with the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] (Session 40).

## Persona and Appearance
Characterized by his slicked-back hair, stylish attire, and flamboyant confidence, Sargon frequents the high-roller tables at the **Stellar Burn** entertainment complex escorted by personal bodyguards (Session 40).

## The Stellar Burn Poker Match
Targeted by Teamster leader [[reidmar|Reidmar]] as a Meinhoff-backed cheat draining the union's games, Sargon faced [[isambard|Isambard]] (operating under his aristocratic persona "Humphrey DuPont") in a Cr 10,000 buy-in poker showdown on Day 118-1105 (Session 40).

During the extended game, observers [[sarik|Sarik]] and uplifted simian [[isaiah|Isaiah]] identified Sargon's behavioral tell: momentarily breaking eye contact and glancing away before making pivotal betting decisions. After scooping a massive pot and taunting Isambard, Sargon was publicly accused by Isambard of using an internal neural implant to cheat. Though Sargon vocally denied the charge, his momentary hesitation and physical reaction confirmed to Isambard that he was lying (Session 40).

## The Black Box Module & Murder
On Day 119-1105, BIAS CCR tracked Sargon's girlfriend [[katarin|Katarin]] in Section 2, leading them through street cyberneticist [[ricardo|Ricardo]] to elite practitioner [[solon|Solon]] in The Icebox (Session 41). Under armed duress, Solon confessed that he had surgically installed an illicit **"black box" neural module** into Sargon, granting him complete photographic recall of all cards and gestures at the tables (Session 41).

BIAS CCR copied the surgical files and handed the evidence over to Teamster head [[reidmar|Reidmar]]. However, merely hours after Reidmar prepared to confront the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] leadership, Harvon Sargon was found **murdered** aboard the station (Session 41).
