# Paolo

**Summary**: Young orphan street urchin in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] living in the subterranean refuse processing district who inquired after [[sydney|Sydney]] Antonelli.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Background & Encounter

Paolo is an orphaned child living within the squalid refuse processing sector beneath [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 19). The refuse processing tunnels serve as an informal encampment and home for the city's abandoned and orphaned street children, including [[sydney|Sydney]] and [[marco|Marco]] (Session 19).

On Day 47-1105, as [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] emerged from [[the-backways|The Backways]] following a brutal skirmish with scavenger mobs, they encountered Paolo in the refuse processing sector (Session 19). Paolo approached the operatives and asked when Sydney was returning home (Session 19). This inquiry directly revealed to [[sarik|Sarik]], [[beck|Beck]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[asher|Asher]] that their resourceful eleven-year-old guide, Sydney, was not merely an independent street fixer, but an orphan living in these toxic subterranean slums (Session 19).
