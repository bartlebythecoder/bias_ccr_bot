# Aronis

**Summary**: Armoured defender at [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the ranch near Hampton]] who took sustained rifle fire from [[beck|Beck]] and [[sky|Sky]] during [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s assault on Day 236-1105, and appeared dead after [[sarik|Sarik]] reported him down.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-23

---

## The Assault (Day 236-1105)

Aronis was among the defenders gathered on the terrace with their rifles when the crew attacked the ranch (Session 86).

[[beck|Beck]] opened the fight with an armor-piercing round that badly wounded him without dropping him. [[sky|Sky]] fired on him and Hedwig with her Gauss rifle, and after the breaching charge opened the wall of [[the-ranch-near-hampton|the Den]], Beck and Sky fired on him again (Session 86).

Sarik moved to a clearer firing position and reported that Aronis was down. Beck fired once more, after which Aronis appeared dead; the crew did not confirm the kill (Session 86).
