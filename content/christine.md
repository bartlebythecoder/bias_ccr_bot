# Christine

**Summary**: Aristocratic woman on [[romentino|Romentino]] whose younger brother [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]] was abducted by a militant cell of [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]], offering a million-credit rescue contract.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## The Kidnapping Crisis

Christine Butler is a high-status young woman (Session 12) associated with aristocratic and business circles on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 11). In early 1105, her brother was kidnapped by a violent militant cell of [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]], the anti-mining extremist movement operating on Romentino (Session 11).

## Extortion & The Million-Credit Bounty

Despite Christine complying with initial extortion demands and paying a substantial ransom, the insurgent captors refused to release her brother, demanding an additional MCr 1.0 (Session 11). At a private dinner at Baltimore House on Day 40-1105, [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] introduced her situation to the [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] operatives, suggesting Christine would gladly pay the MCr 1.0 fee directly to the party if they conducted a rescue operation to bring her brother back safely (Session 11).

## Resolution of Extortion

On Day 44-1105, the abduction was exposed as an extortion scheme orchestrated by her brother [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]] himself to extract wealth from Christine under false pretenses (Session 14).

## MCr 1.0 Payment to BIAS CCR

On Day 044-1105, prior to her departure for the planetary capital of Rosselini, Christine met with [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] and [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] (Session 15). Following Baltimore's recommendation, Christine paid the operatives their agreed MCr 1.0 fee in gratitude for safely securing her brother (Session 15).
