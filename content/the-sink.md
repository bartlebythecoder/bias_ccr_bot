# The Sink

**Summary**: Sunken city wasteland within [[the-choke|The Choke]] on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], now largely occupied by sewage and garbage and home to few people.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-20

---

## Location & Purpose

The Sink is a sunken city wasteland aboard [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], the quasi-legal habitat in the lawless [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] belt governed by the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]). It forms part of [[the-choke|The Choke]], the wider scant-oxygen under-region where residents who cannot pay the daily tax are sent.

## Conditions

The Sink is now largely occupied by sewage and garbage. Few people remain in its flooded, sunken streets.
