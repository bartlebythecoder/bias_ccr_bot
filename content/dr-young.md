# Dr. Young

**Summary**: Harsh [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] lab director on [[suliman|Suliman]] who runs the restricted Yellow Laboratories and appears to hold the real power over [[dr-harknett|Dr. Harknett]].

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

**Dr. Young** is a senior research scientist and laboratory director for the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation on the mining and research asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 38).

## Operational Control of Core Labs
Described by facility personnel like [[pastor|Pastor]] as a demanding, uncompromising, and harsh supervisor, Dr. Young recently assumed direct day-to-day authority over operations within the restricted Yellow Laboratories (Session 38). Although technically subordinate to the overall laboratory director, [[dr-harknett|Dr. Harknett]], staff suspect Young exercises dominant influence over facility decisions and the secretive bioengineering experiments conducted in the core.
