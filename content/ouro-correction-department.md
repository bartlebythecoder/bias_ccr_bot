# Ouro Correction Department

**Summary**: A correctional agency supplying penal labor to industrial corporations, notably Youst Refining on Marcolandia.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

The Ouro Correction Department is a regional or local penal administrative agency. In 077-1105, the department entered into a substantial prison work contract with [[youst-refining|Youst Refining]] on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]], supplying convict work crews to address the refinery's urgent labor deficit ([[news-1105-vol-5.1|News Dispatch — Volume 5.1]]).
