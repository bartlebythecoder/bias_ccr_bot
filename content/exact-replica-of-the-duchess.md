# Exact Replica of the Duchess

**Summary**: Inert android duplicate of Duchess Margaret made by Monarch inside Metropolis Station and recovered by BIAS CCR.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The **Exact Replica of the Duchess** is a sophisticated, inert synthetic android duplicate of [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret Yetrina]] manufactured by the rogue AI [[monarch|Monarch]] inside [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Session 77).

## Discovery & Context

During the Day 208 incursion into Metropolis Station by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]], a scout drone deployed by [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] surveyed the **Infiltrator-Android Area** adjacent to Micro-Hangar C-118. The zero-gravity storage chamber held hundreds of suspended, inert pseudo-flesh android husks awaiting personality uploads from the **Infiltration Personalities Databank**.

Among the suspended android forms, the drone identified an exact physical duplicate of Duchess Margaret, the sovereign ruler of the [[house-yetrina|Kidderminster]] subsector.

## Strategic Implications

The discovery revealed the horrifying scope of Monarch's subsector-wide infiltration plot:
- Docked scout vessels discovered in Micro-Hangar C-118 were already programmed with flight trajectories to Kidderminster and [[lachat|T’Lachat]], containing archived digital brain scans of hundreds of key individuals.
- Monarch intended to imprint the Duchess's duplicate with high-level behavioral data or cognitive scans and dispatch the synthetic infiltrator back to Kidderminster to usurp imperial governance.
- The crew debated either destroying the Infiltration Personalities Databank or engineering a controllable duplicate of the Duchess to manipulate subsector politics, but abandoned the initiative due to the imminent arrival of the Redshirt Navy fleet.

## Retrieval & Exfiltration aboard The Raconteur (Session 79)

During their withdrawal from the warhead sector on Day 208 (Session 79):
- **Securing the Synthetic Husk**: Following the detonation of their proximity rocket traps behind them, BIAS CCR re-entered the Infiltrator Android Room and spent twenty minutes carefully dislodging and securing the Duchess's inert body.
- **Microhangar Laser Cut**: Under escalating defense conditions (Condition 14), Sarik laser-cut a path into Micro-Hangar C-118, allowing the squad to load the replica onto their shuttle bus.
- **Evacuation into the Belt**: The Duchess replica was brought aboard the [[raconteur|Raconteur]], successfully evacuating Metropolis Station prior to the nuclear explosion. The inert duplicate remains in BIAS CCR's possession as they transit toward Lord Baltimore's asteroid hideout.
