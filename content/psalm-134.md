# Psalm 134

**Summary**: A pervasive religious and socio-political movement centered on 134 sacred verses, characterized by decentralized study groups, ducal political backing, and sharp working-class resistance.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Historical Origins: The Tutin Pacification (1077–1083)

The roots of the modern Psalm 134 movement stem from the [[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]] ([[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]]):
- In 1077, a militant theological faction known as *The Order of the Psalms* claimed power on [[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]] (1624), triggering military intervention by [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] ([[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]]).
- Following the eradication of the militant Order in late 1083, a non-political, theological wing—the **134 Psalms**—dissociated from the violent insurgency ([[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]]).
- In the ensuing decades, this theology expanded peacefully across Subsector J, transforming into an influential cultural and political current ([[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]]).

## Core Philosophy and Organization

The movement is founded on [[the-134-psalms|The 134 Psalms]], an ancient poetic canon teaching non-violence, inner enlightenment, resilience, and harmony with the universe ([[the-134-psalms|The 134 Psalms]]).
- **Decentralized Network**: Officially operates without a central governing body, presenting itself as an autonomous federation of independent study groups and congregations.
- **Shadow Direction**: Sociological and security observers suspect clandestine leadership structures coordinate high-level political appointments and wealth transfers across subsectors.

## Political Factions and Regional Friction

The movement has evolved into a deeply polarizing interstellar force:
- **Pleasant Hill**: Its political splinter group, the [[children-of-the-psalms|Children of the Psalms]], assumed sovereign administrative authority over the subsector capital of [[pleasant-hill|Pleasant Hill]] on 353-1104 with direct backing from [[duke-dulinor|Duke Dulinor]] ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).
- **Sanlucar Revolution**: Adoption of Psalmist doctrine by the ruling [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]] alienated the working-class belters and miners, helping ignite the 1105 revolution led by the [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]] ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).
- **Arico El Nuevo Polarization**: Arico serves as the informal subsector hub ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]). Radical street preachers stoked anti-psionic mob violence in the Cairo district, prompting [[psionics|martial law intervention]] ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]; [[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]). Following the murder of banker [[orslo-jollynn|Orslo Jollynn]], Psalmists launched aggressive anti-wealth proselytizing across Belter habitats ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
- **Csabai Scandal**: Local leader [[darlene-suitor|Darlene Suitor]] was arrested in 1105 for narcotics trafficking and murder, forcing the local church into a defensive retreat ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]; [[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
- **Marcolandia Corporate Suppression**: [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] systematically banned known Psalmists from corporate employment and forced center closures ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
