# Andy

**Summary**: Real Red Hand operative on Romentino who escorted BIAS CCR into Morella Station under cover as bank compliance auditors.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role in the Real Red Hand

Andy is a field soldier belonging to the [[real-red-hand|Real Red Hand]] labor movement headed by [[tres-capulet|Tres Capulet]] in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 17). 

On Day 47-1105, Andy accompanied operatives [[sarik|Sarik]], [[beck|Beck]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[asher|Asher]] to [[morella-station|Morella Station]], leveraging his regional knowledge and authoritative demeanor to successfully introduce the operatives to Rig Manager [[gunther-charm|Gunther Charm]] as financial and operational auditors representing "The Banks" (Session 17).
