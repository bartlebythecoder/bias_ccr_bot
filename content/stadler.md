# Stadler

**Summary**: Information broker at the Stardust Casino coffee shop who sold BIAS CCR footage of David Parker's abduction.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**Stadler** is an underworld information broker operating out of the Stardust Casino coffee shop in the North Lux entertainment sector of [[freeport|Freeport]] (Session 46).

---

## Operational History

### Sale of the David Parker Abduction Footage (Day 130-1105)
On Day 130-1105, acting on intelligence supplied by Club Strata bouncer [[andre|Andre]] and bartender [[cadre|Cadre]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] operatives [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sky|Sky]] approached Stadler at his usual booth in the Stardust Casino coffee shop (Session 46).

Stadler possessed high-value surveillance footage documenting the fate of [[david-parker|David Parker]], the estranged ex-boyfriend of murdered [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] designer [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]]. Following negotiations, Isambard purchased the video file from Stadler. The footage revealed that Parker had not simply fled from the police, but had been ambushed and abducted off the street by a covert tactical squad—later identified as rival mercenary group [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]].

### Contracted to Track Julie (Day 131-1105)
Following [[sarik|Sarik's]] psionic abduction, [[isambard|Isambard]] re-engaged Stadler to track down the rogue telepath [[julie|Julie]] (Session 47). The crew evaluated whether to coerce Stadler or enter a pay-on-delivery agreement, opting for the latter. Stadler pushed Julie's image across his starport and transit informant network to flag her if she attempted to flee Freeport with Sarik (Sessions 47–48). By 3:30 PM, Stadler updated Isambard that Julie had been deliberately keeping an extremely low profile across local sectors (Session 48).

### Sale of the Rico Mathers Lead (Day 132-1105)
On Day 132-1105, Stadler met with [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] at the Stardust Casino coffee shop (Session 50). Stadler shared intelligence on [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]] breaking away from the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]] to become an independent dealer. Following negotiations, Stadler accepted a down payment to reveal the identity of Borbon's best friend, [[rico-mathers|Rico Mathers]], located in Jonestown's Homestead Apartments. Stadler also offered to supply the team with black-market firearms at inflated prices (Session 50).

### Emergency Arms Procurement Deal (Day 132-1105)
Later that evening, [[sky|Sky]] returned to the Stardust Casino to purchase immediate tactical weaponry from Stadler (Session 52). Sky committed Cr 14,000 to clear an outstanding Cr 10,000 balance and cover expedited delivery fees. By 9:00 PM, Stadler delivered two black-market assault pistols and a tailored suit of ballistic cloth armor to the crew before their raid on Starka Pramerski's residence (Session 52).
