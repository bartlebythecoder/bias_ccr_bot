# Erebus Power

**Summary**: Energy and technological corporation based on Lachat, developer of the Alexandria AI advisory system, led by Byron Falcon Power with backing from Sabrina and LSP.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview and Ambition

Erebus Power is a fast-growing technology and energy corporation headquartered on [[lachat|Lachat]] in the Kidderminster subsector. The company is led by CEO and principal shareholder **Byron Falcon Power**, a Lachat native who has publicly pledged to transform Erebus into a recognized Imperial Megacorporation by **1112**.

## Shareholders and Research

- **High-Profile Backing**: Equity holders include Brett McRae (wealthy Kidderminster investor), cultural pop superstar [[sabrina|Sabrina]], and Imperial megacorporation [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] (holding a 10% stake).
- **Secretive R&D**: Renowned for covert laboratory complexes, including the [[janus|Janus Asteroid Base]] asteroid research station in the Arico belt operated via its subsidiary Carbrooke Minerals ([[janus|Janus Asteroid Base]]).

## The Alexandria AI Project

In 018-1105, Erebus Power announced [[alexandria-ai|Alexandria AI]], an advanced data repository and artificial intelligence advisor strictly designed within Imperial AI compliance limits (advisory-only, no executive operational control) ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]; [[alexandria-ai|Alexandria AI]]).

## Outsourced Operations at Morella & Fast-Cloning

In 1105, investigations revealed that Erebus Power secretly operates [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] under an outsourcing contract from [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] (Session 18). Behind an infirmary vault, Erebus Power funded an illicit genetic laboratory headed by [[dr-eva-smart|Dr. Eva Smart]], developing high-speed human cloning and claiming to have eliminated the neurological breakdown and madness historically associated with fast-clones (Session 18).

## Subcontracting the "Witchfinder General" to LSP

On Day 93-1105 on Csabai, cyber forensics conducted by [[asher|Asher]] uncovered that [[darnes|Darnes]], the feared "Witchfinder General" conducting internal purges within [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] in [[apex|Apex]], was secretly a full-time operative of Erebus Power on corporate loan (Session 27). This revealed a continuing covert footprint of Erebus Power operations following the loss of their cloning facilities at [[morella-station|Morella Station]] (Session 27).
