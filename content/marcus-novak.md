# Marcus Novak

**Summary**: [[freeport|Freeport]] researcher offering Cr 50,000 per escaped uplifted simian returned to him.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

Marcus Novak is a researcher operating out of a scientific facility on [[freeport|Freeport]] in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system (Session 33).

## Uplifted Simian Bounty
Novak conducted bio-research utilizing uplifted simians purchased from the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation. Following an escape of several violent simian subjects, Novak offered a bounty of Cr 50,000 for each returned specimen (Session 33).
