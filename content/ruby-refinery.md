# Ruby Refinery

**Summary**: [[rainford-ruby|Rainford Ruby]]'s metals refinery on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]], whose earlier robbery started the feud with [[kino-yuba-foust|Kino Yuba Foust]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The Ruby Refinery is a major metals refining plant located on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]], owned and operated by [[rainford-ruby|Rainford Ruby]] (Sessions 31–32).

## Operational History
One year prior to Day 104, Year 1105, the facility was raided and plundered by mercenaries covertly commissioned by [[kino-yuba-foust|Kino Yuba Foust]]. This robbery triggered the retaliatory industrial war between the cousins culminating in the battle at the [[foust-refinery|Foust Refinery]] (Sessions 31–32).
