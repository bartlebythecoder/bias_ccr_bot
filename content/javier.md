# Javier

**Summary**: Event coordinator at the Stardust Casino whose name BIAS CCR used in a ruse during the charity gala.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Javier** is an upscale event coordinator and private party planner operating out of the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]] in North Lux on [[freeport|Freeport]] (Session 55).

## Role in the Charity Gala

On Day 133-1105, Javier coordinated the high-profile fourth-floor charity gala hosted at the Stardust Casino, which brought together high-society benefactors, casino owner [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]], and [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]] CEO [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn]] (Session 55).

While conducting covert reconnaissance of the casino's backstage employee corridors, [[sarik|Sarik]] overheard staff members discussing Javier's logistical authority. BIAS CCR subsequently formulated a ruse using Javier's name to lure Jollynn's executive assistant away from security and snatch her flight itinerary tablet (Session 55).
