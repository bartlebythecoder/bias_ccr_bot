# The Old Man

**Summary**: The contact inside [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] named in BIAS CCR's dossier, later identified as the diver [[arkady|Arkady]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**The Old Man** is an enigmatic resident and potential technical or intelligence contact living inside the derelict orbital installation known as [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 58).

## Operational Target for BIAS CCR
Following the purchase of the "Metropolis" dossier from information broker [[angus|Angus]] on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] identified The Old Man as their primary contact objective inside the station (Session 58). 

Setting out on a three-and-a-half-day transit while preparing to slip past [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] security gunboats, the operatives assembled high-grade rations and luxury provisions to barter in exchange for The Old Man's technical expertise, local orientation, or confidential intelligence regarding the station and its rogue robotic and precursor technologies (Session 58).

## Identification as Arkady
Upon arriving in the vicinity of Project Metropolis on Day 188-1105, the crew established contact with veteran scavenger-diver [[arkady|Arkady]], who resides aboard ***[[the-bell|The Bell]]*** outside the station (Session 59). Arkady fulfilled the role of The Old Man, orienting the team to station layouts, warning them of the rogue AI [[monarch|Monarch]], and ferrying them to A00 Docking Bay under a six-hour dive protocol (Session 59).
