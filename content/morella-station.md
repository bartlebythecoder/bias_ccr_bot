# Morella Station

**Summary**: Subterranean industrial mining settlement on Romentino operated under Rig Manager Gunther Charm; site of an illicit corporate cloning program replacing deceased miners.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Industrial Profile & Infrastructure

Morella Station (often referred to simply as Morella) is a dedicated mineral extraction settlement and industrial rig established on the frigid, corrosive surface of [[romentino|Romentino]] (1428) (Session 17; [[romentino|Romentino]]). Housing roughly 500 permanent extraction personnel, heavy drilling machinery, and chemical refining vaults, the facility operates under the executive authority of Rig Manager [[gunther-charm|Gunther Charm]] and Security Chief [[marius-hope|Marius Hope]] (Session 17; [[romentino|Romentino]]).

## The 1105 Clone Replacement Conspiracy

On Day 47-1105, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] infiltrated Morella Station posing as compliance auditors from "The Banks" on behalf of labor organizer [[tres-capulet|Tres Capulet]] (Session 17). Investigations revealed that corporate interests were secretly maintaining an illegal [[cloning|Cloning and Memory Units]] program to replace deceased mining laborers with biological duplicates—evidenced by the presence of prospector [[danny-conti|Danny Conti]] (who appeared on the facility's official deceased personnel roster) and a hidden clone harbored by mechanic [[karl-jurgens|Karl Jurgens]] (Session 17). The cloning operation appears centered behind a reinforced secret vault door in the station infirmary managed by [[dr-eva-smart|Dr. Eva Smart]] (Session 17).

## Outsourcing & Fast-Cloning Raid

While ostensibly an asset of [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], Morella Station was revealed to be an outsourced operational facility managed directly by [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]] (Session 18). On Day 47-1105, a firefight erupted in the infirmary when Security Chief [[marius-hope|Marius Hope]] attempted to execute visiting auditors under a false "terrorist attack" alert, culminating in the capture of [[dr-eva-smart|Dr. Eva Smart]] and extraction of evidence from the hidden fast-cloning laboratory (Session 18).
