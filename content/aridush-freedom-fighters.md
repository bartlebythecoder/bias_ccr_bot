# Aridush Freedom Fighters

**Summary**: A revolutionary rebel movement on the moon Sanlucar, founded to overthrow the Valentian Republic and resist Psalm 134 influence.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

The Aridush Freedom Fighters are an insurgent and political movement native to the arid gas-mining moon [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]]. Formed in the aftermath of the Sanlucar Civil War and the annexation of the Khanate of Aridush by the [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]], the group champions local self-determination, workers' rights, and an end to resource exploitation ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## Leadership and Ideology

The group is commanded by [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]], daughter of a high-ranking official of the former Khanate of Aridush. Its popular platform draws heavily from the working-class resentment towards the Valentian regime and the conservative religious/social tenets of the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## 1105 Overthrow of Sanlucar

Following years of asymmetrical guerrilla tactics, sabotage, and hit-and-run attacks on Valentian infrastructure, the group launched a surprise offensive in early 1105. By 003-1105, Valentian military forces had formally surrendered, ceding administrative control of the moon to an Aridush provisional government ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]). 

The movement currently faces the critical challenge of converting a fragile gas-mining extractive economy into an equitable system while bracing against potential external intervention from Imperial or neighboring powers ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).
