# Carl Parker

**Summary**: Union leader on [[suliman|Suliman]] who helped BIAS CCR pose as a workers' rights delegation to search for [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Carl Parker is a labor union leader stationed on the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] mining asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system (Session 33).

## Undercover Alliance with BIAS CCR
A friend and ally of [[joanna-lundquist|Joanna Lundquist]], Parker coordinated covertly with [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] to help investigate the disappearance of [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]]. Parker assisted the crew in concocting a cover story as a workers' rights delegation to bypass corporate security on Suliman (Session 33).

## Infiltration of The Warrens
Following the team's arrival and interrogation on Day 115-1105, Parker met the operatives and guided them through [[the-warrens|The Warrens]] (Session 34). At [[the-rusty-bolt|The Rusty Bolt]] tavern, he outlined the living conditions and strict electronic tracking enforced by Wu-Ketai security. Parker informed them that Richard was well regarded among laborers and had uncovered something significant prior to his disappearance. He directed BIAS CCR to contact corporate security officer [[hector|Hector Nilasen]], Richard's personal friend, at the [[derry|Derry]] sushi bar after 6 PM (Session 34).

On the morning of Day 116-1105, following the team's release from overnight security detention, Parker sent local guide and fixer [[orla|Orla]] to assist BIAS CCR in navigating the Warrens, securing discreet supplies, and tracking further leads on the disappearances (Session 35).
