# Karl Jurgens

**Summary**: Industrial mechanic at [[morella-station|Morella Station]] who harbored a secret cloned prospector inside his machinery room.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role at Morella Station

Karl Jurgens is a civilian maintenance mechanic employed in the lower machinery and engineering sections of [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 17). 

On Day 47-1105, during an inspection by undercover operative [[isambard|Isambard]], Jurgens quietly broke protocol to disclose that an illegal cloned prospector was actively hiding inside his private quarters down within the mechanical works, providing direct eyewitness confirmation of the facility's clandestine [[cloning|Cloning and Memory Units]] operations (Session 17).
