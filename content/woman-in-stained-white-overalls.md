# Woman In Stained White Overalls

**Summary**: Mysterious muttering figure guarding a cache of artifacts in the looted Tool Shed of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The **Woman In Stained White Overalls** is an enigmatic human or android figure discovered within the looted **Toolshed** on the lower machinery deck of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Session 71).

## Encounter

While investigating the lower levels alongside [[raihan|Raihan]], the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] crew discovered the Toolshed, which appeared thoroughly ransacked. Standing alone in the center of the workshop, this woman was murmuring incoherently to herself. 

Directly behind her position, [[asher|Asher]] observed a cache containing multiple uncataloged, mysterious artifacts stored in the workshop, indicating she was either guarding or bound to the relics.
