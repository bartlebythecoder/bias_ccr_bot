# The Ranch near Hampton

**Summary**: Heavily defended luxury wilderness retreat on [[tutin|Tutin]], beyond the hills from [[hampton|Hampton]], where [[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]] is held hostage; target of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s covert Redshirt rescue mission.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-27

---

## Overview

The ranch is a wealthy person's luxury retreat in the mountains of [[tutin|Tutin]], not a working livestock operation. [[dr-pearson|Dr. Pearson]] has been held there as a hostage for more than a year. [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] hired BIAS CCR to extract him alive (Sessions 80–81).

The ranch has no driveway and no road connection; like most travel on Tutin, access is by air. Few of [[hampton|Hampton]]'s residents are likely to visit or supply it (Session 81). A local boy, [[necktie|Necktie]], told [[isambard|Isambard]] that a woman named Debra works at the ranch "beyond the hills" and comes into town once a week (Session 82).

## Layout

The single main building has (Session 81):

- a master suite, a bonus room and four bedrooms with double beds;
- numerous windows, with a stream and trees outside the living-room windows;
- climbable walls and a flat roof;
- two garages holding two vehicles each, and an autocourt.

Cover lies about a hundred yards from the building, and the surrounding mountains offer concealed observation points (Session 81).

## Defenders & Security

The residents are few, armed and dangerous, and wear combat armor. Few if any are conventional patrol guards, and there is no barracks, but their exact number is unknown. They may also have friends in Hampton (Session 81).

Security can detect powered equipment, including grav belts and powered armor, from kilometers away. The ranch runs at three readiness levels (Session 81):

1. Ordinary activity under automated security.
2. Armed residents taking up positions.
3. Defenders in powered armor with fusion guns.

Being spotted or causing trouble can raise the readiness level. There are no authorities close to the ranch (Session 81).

## The Kill Switch

The local Redshirt Intelligence contact identified the ranch's security frequency and hid a portable, illegal kill switch near the start of the approach. Carried to the ranch and activated, it cuts camera feeds and proximity-alarm signals to the central console. It does not stop the cameras themselves or affect ordinary communications. Activating it immediately raises the ranch from its lowest readiness level to the next (Session 81).

## Approach Routes

Three routes to the ranch were identified (Session 81):

| Route | Time | Risks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Southern** | About five days at a leisurely pace | Easiest going, but the greatest chance of patrols or being seen. |
| **Northern** | Faster than the southern route | Rugged and less likely to be patrolled, but risks injury. |
| **Direct (eastern)** | Two or three days | Needs climbing gear; cannot be seen from the ranch; greatest risk of bodily injury. Unpowered gliders are feasible for the descent. |

## BIAS CCR's Plan

The crew settled on the northern route for [[isambard|Isambard]], [[asher|Asher]], [[sarik|Sarik]], [[isaiah|Isaiah]] and [[tomas|Tomas]], while [[beck|Beck]] takes the direct climbing route alone. Beck expects to arrive about a day early, scout the ranch and report by communicator (Session 81). Reconnaissance priorities are (Session 81):

- Dr. Pearson's quarters, so he can be secured first.
- The defenders' weapons and armor, so they can be kept from their equipment before an open fight.
- The vehicles.

No extraction has been arranged. A ship can be hired through a network to collect the team after a radio call. The ranch's own vehicles could be stolen for the return, but local law enforcement would treat the team as criminals (Session 81).
