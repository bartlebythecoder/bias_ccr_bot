# Commodore Koroleva

**Summary**: Imperial Navy officer serving as Commanding Officer for the [[romentino|Romentino]] system.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Commodore Koroleva is the Imperial Commanding Officer stationed at [[romentino|Romentino]] (1428). 

## Operations at Romentino

On 044-1105, Commodore Koroleva officially announced the end of the multi-week shipping blockade/restriction around Romentino, restoring the system's travel advisory to Green status ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]). Koroleva confirmed that while free commercial movement has resumed, strict Imperial security protocols focusing on AI and robotics compliance remain actively enforced ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).

## Social Diplomatic Engagement

On Day 40-1105, Commodore Koroleva attended an exclusive reception at Baltimore House in the Noble Quarter of Startown Liberty, conversing with [[beck|Beck]] of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] amid escalating tensions involving [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] and insurgent violence (Session 11).

## Starlight Smuggling Briefing

On Day 044-1105, Commodore Koroleva received a direct intelligence report from operative [[beck|Beck]] alerting naval authorities to an imminent off-world narcotics shipment orchestrated by Red Hand soldier [[joey|Joey]] aboard the Frontier Liner to Nesvorny (Session 16).

## Shielding BIAS CCR from Imperial Arrest

On Day 47-1105, following the covert raid on Morella Station, planetary authorities representing [[the-banks|The Banks]] formally petitioned Commodore Koroleva to issue an Imperial arrest warrant against [[beck|Beck]] and BIAS CCR on charges of corporate espionage (Session 19). Koroleva refused the warrant request, but personally contacted Beck to warn him that corporate pressure was intensifying and that she would be unable to shield the operatives if they remained on Romentino (Session 19).
