# Officer Conway

**Summary**: Corrupt Wu-Ketai naval officer outside Metropolis Station who took over the perimeter when Kilroy went silent and bargained with BIAS CCR over salvage.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Officer Conway** is a naval security officer serving with the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai Corporation]] interdiction detachment stationed outside [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Sessions 66, 76).

## Background & Reputation

Conway operates as an officer under [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]]. According to shuttle diver [[arkady|Arkady]], Conway maintains a notoriously corrupt reputation, being amenable to cash bribes and salvage cuts in sharp contrast to Kilroy's rigid enforcement of artifact seizures.

When Kilroy disappeared from station comms, Conway assumed operational command of the local perimeter. Conway bluntly declared to BIAS CCR that his official orders were to liquidate unauthorized scavengers and [[the-doves|Psalmist]] raiders alike. However, he was eager to cut private side-deals and accept bribes in exchange for escort or look-the-other-way protection.

## Artifact Division & Safe Passage Deal (Session 79)

On Day 208, with one hour remaining on the station's [[nuclear-warhead|Nuclear Warhead]] countdown, [[isambard|Isambard]] re-opened communications with Officer Conway to negotiate safe transit through the blockading [[the-doves|Psalmist]] fleet (Session 79):
- **The 50% Artifact Demands**: Conway agreed to protect the *Raconteur* from the Psalmists in exchange for 50% of the artifacts recovered by BIAS CCR from Metropolis.
- **Alternating Selection & Kira Excluded**: Meeting aboard the vessel, the crew laid out items including an orb and pseudo-flesh compounds. When Conway's marines discovered [[kira|Kira]], Isambard successfully argued she was an active crew member rather than salvage. Conway accepted the alternate artifact draft and concluded the deal.
- **Unwarned Fate**: The crew deliberately withheld any warning regarding the nuclear warhead. Conway and his detachment were caught in the massive nuclear fireball that annihilated Metropolis Station, The Bell, and the Psalmist fleet at 20:30 hours.
