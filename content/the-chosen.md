# The Chosen

**Summary**: Armed android caste serving [[child-king|the Child King]] and [[monarch|Monarch]] on Level 2 of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**The Chosen** are the privileged, armed android caste serving under the [[child-king|Child King]] and the AI [[monarch|Monarch]] within Level 2 of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Sessions 70–71).

## Role & Organization

Bearing physical resemblances to models like [[ella|Ella]], members of The Chosen are equipped with stunners, firearms, and enforcement gear. They form the honor guard around the Child King's bone throne.

Following the destabilization of the Court, The Chosen entered an active, violent civil war against the subjugated underclass, [[the-fallen|The Fallen]], conducting systematic executions across sections such as the Fortification Room.
