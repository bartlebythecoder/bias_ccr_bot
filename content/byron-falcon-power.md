# Sir Byron Falcon Power

**Summary**: Chairperson of [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]], who spearheaded the launch of the Alexandria supercomputer advisory project.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Sir Byron Falcon Power is an influential industrialist and the chairperson of [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]]. In 018-1105 on [[lachat|Lachat]], Power publicly unveiled [[alexandria-ai|Alexandria AI]], positioning the system as a revolutionary knowledge repository ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## Regulatory Posture

Recognizing the Imperial restrictions and public wariness surrounding artificial intelligence, Power has explicitly defended Alexandria's architecture, stating that because the system acts strictly as an analytical advisor without execution authorization, it remains fully compliant with Imperial AI safety standards ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).
