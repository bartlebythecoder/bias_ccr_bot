# Cindy

**Summary**: Young girl who greeted Asher and Sarik and offered directions in the Green Area of Suliman's core laboratories.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Cindy** is a young girl encountered within the main hall of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] core laboratories facility on the asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 38).

## Encounter in the Green Area
On Day 117-1105, as operatives [[asher|Asher]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] entered the busy preliminary Green Area concourse disguised in lab coats, Cindy greeted them and offered assistance and directions as they cased the facility (Session 38).
