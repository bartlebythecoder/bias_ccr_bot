# House Tauribi

**Summary**: One of the sixteen Imperial noble houses, ruling the Acigne subsector under Duke Ishuggi, with its largest corporate stake in Tukera Lines.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-25

---

## Subsector Governance

House Tauribi is one of the sixteen [[noble-houses|Noble Houses]] of the Imperium, holding hereditary rule over the **Acigne subsector**. The house is headed by **Duke Ishuggi** ([[noble-houses|Noble Houses]]).

## Megacorporate Holdings

- **[[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]]**: Owns an 8% equity stake, its most significant corporate holding.
- **Minor Stakes**: Holds 1% each in [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], and [[susag|SuSAG]].
