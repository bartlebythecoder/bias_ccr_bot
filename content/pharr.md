# Pharr

**Summary**: System 1130 in Solo Sector Subsector J, currently dealing with a major humanitarian crisis.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Pharr (1130) is a world situated in Subsector J of the Solo Sector.

## Humanitarian Crisis

In early 1105, Pharr was designated as experiencing a critical humanitarian crisis, prompting regional intervention and an emergency MCr 45.0 relief package dispatched from the [[rimward-circuit-collective|Rimward Circuit Collective]] via [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## Demographics & Notable Natives

Recognized as the poorest planet in the Sanlucar subsector, severe poverty has driven native talents to fight for educational escape, including chemist and elite hacker [[asher|Asher]] (Sessions 1–5).
