# Pleasant Hill

**Summary**: System 1218, capital of the Pleasant Hill Subsector, governed by the Children of the Psalms.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Pleasant Hill (1218) carries the UWP designation B2009EF-A and serves as the capital world of the Pleasant Hill Subsector ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).

## Theocratic Transition

In 353-1104, the planetary authority of Pleasant Hill officially ceded governance to the [[children-of-the-psalms|Children of the Psalms]], an ideological transition backed directly by [[duke-dulinor|Duke Dulinor]] ([[news-1105-vol-2|News Dispatch — Volume 2]]).
