# Julie (Telepathic Assassin)

**Summary**: Psionic assassin who posed as a colleague of [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] to infiltrate BIAS CCR and abduct [[sarik|Sarik]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**"Julie"** is an unidentified psionic assassin and operative who infiltrated [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s operations on [[freeport|Freeport]] by impersonating a coworker of slain [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] designer [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] (Sessions 47–48).

---

## Operational History

### The Infiltration & Abduction of Sarik (Day 131-1105)
On the morning of Day 131-1105, the assassin arrived at BIAS CCR's Freeport townhouse posing as "Julie," Louise Soto's grief-stricken graphics colleague (Session 47). Displaying fear of the team's uplifted simian companions, she pleaded for an escort to her apartment to retrieve medication. When [[sarik|Sarik]] escorted her inside, she deployed a potent [[psionics|psionic Suggestion]] that instantly paralyzed and incapacitated him, allowing her to abduct him from the premises.

Subsequent photographic verification by [[asher|Asher]] against Wu-Ketai corporate databases confirmed that the real Julie was an entirely different individual, exposing the visitor as a telepathic operative.

### Extortion & Negotiation with Ridpath (Day 131-1105)
After sedating Sarik and packing him into a shipping crate, the assassin coordinated with [[ridpath|Mr. Ridpath]] and BIAS CCR via a three-way encrypted link (Session 48):
- She cited the **Cr 1,000,000** dark web bounty on Sarik's life, demanding ransom.
- Escalate threats: She warned that any interference with her transit would result in lethal harm to Sarik.
- The Counter-Offer: In an unexpected twist, she proposed returning Sarik unharmed if BIAS CCR agreed to complete a specialized job on her behalf, an arrangement that Attorney Ridpath agreed to broker while BIAS CCR narrowed down her tram station hiding place.

### The Dead-Man Switch Standoff & Agreement (Day 131-1105)
When [[beck|Beck]] and [[sky|Sky]] breached her 8th-floor condo apartment, Julie confronted them holding a handheld dead-man switch trigger, having already ordered a hover shipping container vehicle to the loading dock below (Session 49). 

Rather than detonating the trigger or escalating to violence, Julie agreed to listen to Beck's counter-proposal:
- Beck leveraged intelligence that tied her to the starship ***[[belladonna|Belladonna]]*** and threatened to feed her location to anti-psionic activist [[cam-decardo|Cam Decardo]] and port police.
- In lieu of the Cr 1,000,000 bounty, Beck offered Julie a **one-sixth share** (approx. Cr 333,333) of [[yandee|Demon Prince Yandee's]] MCr 2.0 contract to assassinate the leader of [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]], backed by a Cr 100,000 escrow if the hit was delayed past two months.
- Julie accepted the terms, agreed never to bounty Sarik again, and turned him over immediately, conditioned on [[ridpath|Attorney Ridpath]] formalizing the legal paperwork.

After facing down a brief standoff when Sky drew a combat blade, Julie was allowed to leave cleanly. She traveled under [[asher|Asher's]] cyber tracking to Freeport's starport, where [[isambard|Isambard]] watched her pass through a security gate toward her ship (Session 49).
