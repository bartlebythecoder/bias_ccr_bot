# Marco

**Summary**: Young Romentino street urchin who guided BIAS CCR through the Backways beneath Startown Liberty and salvaged an illegal AI spider.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role & Activities

Marco is a resourceful street urchin inhabiting the subterranean ruins and refuse processing districts beneath [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 19). Like [[sydney|Sydney]] and [[paolo|Paolo]], Marco navigates the forgotten infrastructure and maintenance passages of the planetary starport (Session 19).

## Guiding BIAS CCR

On Day 47-1105, when planetary authorities under [[the-banks|The Banks]] and [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] broadcasted arrest warrants for corporate espionage against the members of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]], [[sarik|Sarik]], [[beck|Beck]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[asher|Asher]] hired Marco to guide them to [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s yacht at the starport via [[the-backways|The Backways]] (Session 19).

Marco successfully led the operatives through forgotten drainage shafts and rust waterways, remaining with them even after the party was ambushed by a violent mob of desperate scavengers (Session 19). During the traverse, Marco discovered a concealed cache containing packaged food, clean water, and an [[ai-spider|AI Spider]]—a device he identified as powerful, valuable, and highly illegal (Session 19). Impressed by his aid and loyalty, the operatives permitted Marco to keep the AI spider and the supplies before safely reaching the refuse sector (Session 19).
