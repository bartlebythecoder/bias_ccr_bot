# Isabel

**Summary**: Housemaid at Marie [[orslo-jollynn|Orslo Jollynn]]'s [[freeport|Freeport]] mansion who revealed a secret passage during BIAS CCR's raid.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Isabel** (also referenced as **Isabelle**) was a domestic housemaid employed at the private gated mansion of [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]] CEO [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn]] on [[freeport|Freeport]] (Sessions 56–57).

## Role in the Mansion Raid (Day 133-1105)

* **Breach & Subdual**: During BIAS CCR's armed breach of Jollynn's mansion, [[sky|Sky]] entered an interior room and surprised Isabel alongside a security guard. When the guard reached for his weapon and Isabel screamed, [[thomas|Thomas]] and Sky neutralized the guard, while uplifted simian [[isaiah|Isaiah]] grappled and subdued Isabel, keeping her restrained and quiet (Sessions 56–57).
* **Secret Passage Disclosure**: While being guarded by Isaiah during the ongoing firefight outside, Isabel shared domestic servant gossip revealing the layout of the house, including the maid's spiral staircase and the existence of a secret tunnel and hidden room behind a library bookcase (Sessions 56–57).
* **Bribe & Safe Departure**: Following the execution of Jollynn and the safe-cracking heist, [[beck|Beck]] negotiated with Isabel, providing her with **Cr 10,000** in cash to guarantee her complete silence and facilitate her immediate, safe departure from Freeport (Sessions 56–57).
