# Ripley

**Summary**: [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]]'s bodyguard on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], judged by [[beck|Beck]] to be a sociopath.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Ripley** is a personal bodyguard on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] employed by gangster and gambler [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]] (Session 42).

## Sargon Murder Investigation
On Day 119-1105, following the violent death of Sargon, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] questioned Ripley regarding the homicide (Session 42). Ripley asserted that Teamster leader [[reidmar|Reidmar]] had hired an assassin—describing the suspect as a woman in her forties wearing an industrial jumpsuit—who allegedly murdered Sargon.

During questioning, Ripley displayed a chilling emotional detachment and total indifference regarding his employer's death. Psychological profiler [[beck|Beck]] clinically assessed Ripley as a sociopath, noting that his shifting testimony and callous demeanor made his accusations highly unreliable (Session 42).
