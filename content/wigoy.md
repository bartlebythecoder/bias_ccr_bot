# Wigoy

![[wigoy.png]]

**Summary**: Highly intelligent, eyeless herbivore native to [[tutin|Tutin]], blue-gray and bipedal, with four anemone-like tentacle arms and a body covered in sensory cilia. Now almost extinct.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-21

---

## Biology & Behavior

The wigoy is a bipedal herbivore, blue-gray in colour, with a body covered in fine sensory cilia. It has four arms ending in anemone-like tentacles and possesses no eyes, relying instead on its ciliated surface to perceive its surroundings.

The species is regarded as highly intelligent.

## Habitat & Range

The wigoy is native to [[tutin|Tutin]] (1624), one of only two breathable worlds in the subsector. Since the [[tutin-pacification|Tutin Pacification]] of 1077–1083, Tutin has been administered by the [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] government as a restricted nature reserve with a permanent population of roughly one hundred residents.

## Conservation Status

The wigoy is almost extinct. The cause of its decline is not recorded.
