# Elin Butler

**Summary**: Young aristocrat on [[romentino|Romentino]] and younger brother of [[christine|Christine]] Butler, whose abduction by a Red Hand faction triggered an MCr 1.0 ransom crisis.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Background & Disappearance

Elin Butler is a young high-status resident of [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] and the younger brother of [[christine|Christine Butler]] (Sessions 11–12). Prior to his abduction in early 1105, Elin had grown estranged from family circles, falling in with a rough group of local youths and frequenting [[rickeys-casino|Rickeys Casino]] in the Entertainment district alongside gambling companion [[marcus|Marcus]] (Session 12).

## The Abduction & Investigation

Elin was kidnapped by a militant insurgent cell claiming affiliation with [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]] (suspected to be the [[real-red-hand|Real Red Hand]] breakaway) (Sessions 11–12). Although Christine paid an initial ransom demand, the kidnappers escalated their extortion, demanding an additional MCr 1.0 (Session 11). Operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] undertook an investigation to locate him, tracking his personal effects and gambling contacts to Rickeys casino (Session 12).

## The Staged Kidnapping Hoax

On Day 44-1105, investigation by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] and revelations from [[tres-capulet|Tres Capulet]] unmasked Elin's disappearance as an elaborate con (Session 14). Elin had staged his own abduction and dictated the MCr 1.0 extortion demand against his sister [[christine|Christine Butler]] simply because he felt his living allowance was too small (Session 14). 

When operatives [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[asher|Asher]] breached his luxury glass tower apartment, Elin was found completely relaxed and unharmed (Session 14). Shamelessly, he proposed that the operatives join his con to extract additional wealth from his sister; the operatives rejected his offer, physically restrained him, and handed him over to authorities (Session 14).
