# Lars

**Summary**: Young scavenger-diver in a tricorn hat, abandoned by [[saltpeter|the Saltpeter]]'s crew on [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] and rescued by BIAS CCR.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Lars** was a young scavenger-diver wearing a distinctive tricorn hat discovered stranded in Meeting Room B on Level 1 of [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 61).

## Discovery & Station Lore
On Day 188-1105, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] breached Meeting Room B, holding Lars at gunpoint before operative [[isambard|Isambard]] disarmed him (Session 61). Lars claimed to have arrived aboard the salvage vessel ***[[saltpeter|Saltpeter]]*** and was abandoned by his crewmates a week earlier.

During debriefing, Lars shared critical station layout intelligence:
- **Kevyn Barker**: Warned that the IT intern [[kevyn-barker|Kevyn Barker]] in the gym locker was an advanced, human-seeming android planted to fool divers.
- **Level 2 (Android Kingdom)**: Described an eerie realm populated by androids convinced they are human, ruled by a childlike monarch, surrounded by organic "skin" bulkheads that bleed a milk-like substance.
- **Level 3 (Darkness & The Minotaur)**: A dark sector roamed by aggressive androids and a taboo entity referred to as the "Minotaur."
- **The Golden Rat Taboo**: Warned against stealing the **[[golden-rat-statue|Golden Rat Statue]]** from the kitchen shrine, explaining that removing it halts the automated replenishment of food and ammunition caches across the station.

## Execution as a Monarch Clone
After guiding the team and witnessing [[beck|Beck]] secure a sparkling syringe from an EMP booby trap, Lars accompanied BIAS CCR to the reception area. Although diagnosed with dehydration and malnutrition, when [[arkady|Arkady]] arrived at the airlock, Arkady summarily shot Lars twice in the face and vented his body into the vacuum of space (Session 61).

Arkady revealed that any scavenger returning alone from the depths is a biological duplicate created by [[monarch|Monarch]], noting that this was the **third "Lars"** he had executed in two weeks (Session 61).

## Magnus's Search & Continued Vigil (Session 62)
Back aboard *The Bell*, Lars's former crewmate and partner **[[magnus|Magnus]]** was desperately waiting for him (Session 62). Unaware of Lars's breakdown, cloning, and subsequent execution by Arkady, Magnus implored the *BIAS CCR* crew to relay the message that he would never depart *The Bell* without him.
