# Danny Conti

**Summary**: Prospector at [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] revealed to be an illegal clone duplicate of a deceased miner.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Encounter & Clone Discovery

Danny Conti was a registered mineral prospector operating out of [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 17). On Day 47-1105, Conti approached undercover operative [[isambard|Isambard]], offering to sell insider coordinates to a high-value mineral claim he claimed was worth millions (Session 17).

Simultaneously, operative [[asher|Asher]] obtained internal station casualty manifests from junior technician [[anya|Anya]], which explicitly documented that **Danny Conti was formally deceased** (Session 17). This discrepancy confirmed [[tres-capulet|Tres Capulet]]'s allegations that corporate interests are operating an illegal [[cloning|Cloning and Memory Units]] pipeline to replace dead miners with synthetic duplicates (Session 17).
