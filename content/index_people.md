## BIAS CCR & Core Operatives

- [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]]: Licensed corporate consulting and private security firm established on Romentino by Beck, Asher, Isambard, and Sarik.
- [[asher|Asher]]: Chemist and hacker from Pharr educated at Nesvorny, cyber-intrusion and scientific operative.
- [[beck|Beck]]: Psychological profiler from Kidderminster, cellular rejuvenation beneficiary, undercover medic and interrogator.
- [[isambard|Isambard]]: Former pirate, merchant marine, and Imperial scout, team mechanic, grappler, and Type-S Scout claimant.
- [[sarik|Sarik]]: Former Imperial marine veteran of the Sanlucar Civil War and Tutin colonist, frontline heavy operative.
- [[sky|Sky]]: Commercial flyer pilot at K.C. Station, former Imperial Marine, and Cooper's local asset recruited into the pyramid raid.
- [[cooper|Cooper]]: Covert corporate espionage patron who recruited the crew to infiltrate K.C. Station on Mirassol.
