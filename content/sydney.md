# Sydney

**Summary**: Resourceful 11-year-old street guide and urchin fixer in Startown Liberty who guided the crew through the starport green zones and tracked down Harry Proud.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role & Street Network

Sydney Antonelli is an eleven-year-old child (Session 12) living in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on the frigid, corrosive world of [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 10). Supporting herself through independent street enterprise, Sydney offers specialized navigation and guide services to off-world travelers disembarking at Starport Administration, steering clients through authorized "green zones" to avoid local cartel violence and muggings (Session 10).

Starport personnel explicitly recommend that incoming off-world travelers retain Sydney and remain in her company at all times while exploring the settlement (Session 10).

## Assistance to the Crew

On Day 38-1105, Sydney was hired by [[sarik|Sarik]], [[beck|Beck]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[asher|Asher]] (Session 10):
- **Tactical Advisement**: Instructed the operatives to purchase sidearms to project deterrence across the starport settlement (Session 10).
- **Underworld Tracking**: Following [[harry-proud|Harry Proud]]'s theft of the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], Sydney mobilized her network of street urchins, pinpointing Proud's presence at the [[blue-parrot|Blue Parrot]] in GAS town (Session 10).
- **Narcotics Infiltration**: After Proud's murder, Sydney brokered a direct introduction to local [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] dealer [[lisa|Lisa]], leading the team directly to the Barrikday hideout of [[screetch|Screetch]] (Session 10).

- **Protective Ties**: Sydney maintains a close personal bond with local merchant and Red Hand connector [[hal-the-grocer|Hal the Grocer]], who treats her with paternal protectiveness (Session 12).

## Police Detention and Orphan Origins

On Day 47-1105, when [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] pressed [[the-banks|The Banks]] to raid BIAS CCR's rented condo in the Noble Quarter, Sydney was detained and interrogated alongside [[sky|Sky]] and [[tina|Tina]] regarding corporate espionage allegations (Session 19). She was released without charges later that day (Session 19).

During the operatives' passage through [[the-backways|The Backways]], fellow street child [[paolo|Paolo]] inquired after her, revealing to [[sarik|Sarik]] and the crew that Sydney was an orphan living in the squalid refuse processing sector beneath Startown Liberty (Session 19). Sarik specifically instructed Sky to ensure Sydney was fully compensated for her guide work and loyalty before departing Romentino (Session 19).
