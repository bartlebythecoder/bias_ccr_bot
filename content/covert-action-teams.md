# Covert Action Teams

**Summary**: Specialized 5-person corporate black-ops intelligence units and mercenary sweep-and-clear strike teams executing clandestine warfare in Subsector J.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Corporate Covert Action Teams (CAT)

Covert Action Teams (CAT Teams) are elite, deep-cover intelligence and paramilitary squads maintained by megacorporations and major subsector firms:
- **Structure**: Standardized 5-person cells of highly trained, specialized operatives armed with military-grade weaponry.
- **Mission Profile**: Deployed for industrial espionage, asset extraction, targeted assassinations, sabotage of competitor facilities, and black-budget reconnaissance ([[megacorporations|Megacorporations]]).

## Sweep and Clear Mercenary Doctrine

A specialized category of planetary assault mercenaries deployed in corporate proxy wars:
- Tasked with violently purging enemy resistance from landing zones, industrial refineries, or asteroid outposts to establish a secure operational foothold for follow-on forces.
