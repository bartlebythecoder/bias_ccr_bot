# Ella

**Summary**: Friendly android on Level 2 of Metropolis Station who mistook BIAS CCR for newly born androids and guided them to the Child King's court.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## The Welcome & Guided Tour
- **Greeting the "Newly Born"**: On Day 192+, Ella intercepted the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] strike team upon their arrival on Floor 2. Mistaking the armored operatives for newly synthesized android constructs, Ella greeted them as "newly born" and volunteered to guide them through the deck's society.
- **Tour of Level 2**: Ella escorted the team through the *Visitor Shrine* and the *Bot's Playground*, where she innocently observed [[the-chosen|The Chosen]] androids playing games while defective or unranked droids were dismantled and harvested for replacement parts.
- **Escort to the Court**: Ella ushered the crew directly into the royal audience hall of the **[[child-king|Court of the Chosen King]]** to seek permission to traverse the lower labyrinth.
- **Audience, Stunner Ambush & Imprisonment**: In the Court, Ella stood among [[the-chosen|The Chosen]] android guards (who closely resembled her physical chassis and wielded stunners). After [[sarik|Sarik]] detonated an EMP grenade at the Child King's feet and hostile Chosen units were subdued, the crew secured Ella and the incapacitated Child King together inside the galley for containment (Session 71).
