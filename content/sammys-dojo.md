# Sammy's Dojo

**Summary**: Combat sports and martial arts training facility in Basin City on Csabai, owned by Sammy Petit.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Facility Overview

Sammy's Dojo is a popular athletic gym and unarmed martial arts dojo situated in [[basin-city|Basin City]] on [[csabai|Csabai]], operated by instructor [[sammy-petit|Sammy Petit]] (Session 29). Frequented by local working-class fighters and students—including Psalmist courier [[jacob-godard|Jacob Godard]]—the gym features regulation combat rings and sparring mats (Session 29).

## The Match of Day 94-1105

On Day 94-1105, operatives from [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] tracked Godard's habits to the dojo (Session 29). In an unsanctioned wagering bout, operative [[isambard|Isambard]] locked proprietor Sammy Petit in a submission choke on the mat, extracting the lead that pointed the operatives to [[freeport-university|Freeport University]] and [[chloe-vinit|Chloe Vinit]] (Session 29).
