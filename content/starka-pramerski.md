# Starka Pramerski

**Summary**: [[freeport|Freeport]] administrator who fronted [[omni|Omni]]'s money laundering for the Vipers and [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]], later murdered.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Starka Pramerski** was a corporate administrator on [[freeport|Freeport]], listed as the sole employee of [[omni|Omni]], a corporate shell used by the [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]] and [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] for laundering illicit narcotics proceeds and coordinating dock logistics (Sessions 52, 54).

## Activities & Role

* **Money Laundering Cutout**: Digital forensics on the laptop exfiltrated from [[cafe-dimitri|Cafe Dimitri]] revealed that the Zero-G Vipers channeled dirty revenues through Omni under Pramerski's name (Session 52).
* **Dockside Customs Payoffs**: Pramerski met regularly with union dock supervisor [[irquez|Irquez]] at [[orion-docks|Orion Docks]], delivering recurring cash payoffs to ensure union dockworkers facilitated smooth customs clearance for syndicate freight (Session 54).
* **Abduction by Hunter's Heroes**: On the evening of Day 132-1105, rival mercenaries [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] broke into Pramerski's home, ransacked the premises, and abducted him. An anonymous Tipster alerted BIAS CCR: *"Save Starka Pramerski. Park 32. Hurry."*, prompting a race to Park 32 where Pramerski was held (Sessions 52–53).
* **Homicide in Freeport Mall (Day 133-1105)**: Hours after escaping or being released from the park standoff, Pramerski was tracked to a Freeport shopping mall by [[the-man-with-the-gun|The Man With The Gun]]. The operative followed him into a restroom and strangled him, scattering unusual pills to stage the death as a drug overdose. Although [[inspector-coker|Inspector Coker]] initially treated it as an accidental overdose, [[beck|Beck]] discovered telltale neck bruising, confirming foul play as part of an internal syndicate purge (Session 54).
