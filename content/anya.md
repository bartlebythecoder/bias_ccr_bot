# Anya

**Summary**: Junior IT technician at Morella Station who provided Asher with critical power telemetry and the deceased employee manifest.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## IT Operations

Anya is a junior computational technician employed in the central computer and systems bay of [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 17). 

On Day 47-1105, when operative [[asher|Asher]] arrived inspecting the systems, Anya cooperated willingly, supplying station power consumption logs and an internal list of deceased facility employees before her senior IT supervisor arrived on shift (Session 17). Her casualty records revealed that prospector [[danny-conti|Danny Conti]] was listed as dead, proving he was an illegal clone (Session 17).
