# Rico Mathers

**Summary**: Small-time narcotics dealer in Jonestown on [[freeport|Freeport]] and a close friend of [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Rico Mathers** is a low-level narcotics user and dealer residing in the Homestead Apartments government housing project in the Jonestown district of [[freeport|Freeport]], and the close personal friend of independent dealer [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]] (Session 50).

## Role in the Borbon Investigation

* **Stadler's Lead**: On Day 132-1105, underworld broker [[stadler|Stadler]] sold the identity and location of Rico Mathers to [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] as Borbon's closest confidant (Session 50).
* **Interrogation by BIAS CCR**: When Beck and Sarik tracked down Rico in his disheveled apartment, Rico revealed that he had already been questioned earlier that day by a "big guy in an orange suit" claiming to represent the [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]]—an operative deduced to be from rival mercenary outfit [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] (Session 50).
* **Intelligence on Borbon**: Rico disclosed that Conde Borbon had severed ties with the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]] to distribute narcotics as an independent dealer, and expressed confidence that Borbon was not in hiding and would return to Freeport within a day or two (Session 50).
* **Bonding & Cafe Dimitri Disclosure**: At 4:15 PM on Day 132, Beck bonded with Rico by playing video games for several hours (Session 51). Rico revealed that Borbon had left his phone behind and gone completely off-grid after witnessing Louise Soto's murder. Rico detailed the turf rivalry between the established Primero Cartel and the aggressive Zero-G Vipers, noting Borbon feared the Vipers wanted him dead for refusing to buy from them. Rico also gave them the lead on [[cafe-dimitri|Cafe Dimitri]], a fortified Vipers clubhouse tied to operative [[montanez|Montañez]] (Session 51).
