# Admiral Grace Inn

**Summary**: Inn in [[hampton|Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]], run by the elderly innkeeper Mackey Falk, where [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] booked three rooms for two weeks on Day 230-1105.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-02

---

## Overview

The Admiral Grace Inn is an inn in [[hampton|Hampton]], the elevator town on [[tutin|Tutin]]. It is run by Mackey Falk.

BIAS CCR knew of the inn from their database before arriving on Tutin.

## BIAS CCR's Stay (Days 230–231-1105)

On the evening of Day 230, [[jiro|Jiro]] took [[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isaiah|Isaiah]] and [[tomas|Tomas]] to the inn. Mackey questioned them about the simians and their plans. Beck gave their cover: a trip to [[sundance|Sundance]] for hunting and outdoor work, with a local contact expected to meet them (Session 83).

They booked three rooms for two weeks, giving the crew a place to come back to, store belongings or leave someone in town. Beck set a miniature audiovisual device in the hallway, and [[sarik|Sarik]] wedged a chair under his door handle because he was still being hunted. The night passed without suspicious traffic or disturbance, though Asher and [[isambard|Isambard]] both dreamed of [[rock-squid|rock squids]]. The crew discussed what they had learned over breakfast the next morning (Session 83).
