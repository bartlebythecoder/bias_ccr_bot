# Dr. Eva Smart

**Summary**: Chief Medical Officer at Morella Station on Romentino who concealed a secret laboratory door and was attacked by Sarik during a psionic psychotic break.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Medical Authority at Morella

Dr. Eva Smart is the Head Physician and Chief Medical Officer at [[morella-station|Morella Station]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 17). Overseeing the installation's primary infirmary, clinical laboratories, and biological supplies, Dr. Smart operates under the administrative authority of Rig Manager [[gunther-charm|Gunther Charm]] (Session 17).

## Concealment of Clone Facilities & Assault

On Day 47-1105, during an undercover inspection by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] operative [[beck|Beck]], an unidentified individual was observed peering out from behind a concealed panel in Dr. Smart's laboratory (Session 17). When operative [[sarik|Sarik]] breached the panel to reveal a heavy secondary locked door and demanded an explanation, Dr. Smart denied any knowledge of the secret entrance (Session 17). Her evasive response triggered Sarik's latent psionic neural trauma, causing him to lapse into an uncontrollable berserk rage and charge the doctor (Session 17).

## Erebus Power Employment & Interrogation

Following her injury and surgical stabilization, Dr. Smart was abducted by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] and subjected to interrogation during transit to Startown Liberty (Session 18). She confessed that her direct employer was [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]] (operating Morella under an outsourcing contract from LSP) and that her primary scientific breakthrough was eliminating neurological psychosis in fast-clones, insisting her genetic experiments were legitimate (Session 18). In Startown, she was remanded to the custody of Real Red Hand leader [[tres-capulet|Tres Capulet]] (Session 18).
