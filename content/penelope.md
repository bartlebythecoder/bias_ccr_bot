## The Elevator Descent

On Day 230-1105, artists Penelope and Archie introduced themselves to BIAS CCR during the ninety-minute elevator descent to [[hampton|Hampton]] (Session 82). When Archie asked about the simians, [[isambard|Isambard]] passed them off as shy "monkey butlers" and warned that eye contact could be taken as a dominance challenge. Penelope touched [[tomas|Tomas]], who did not become angry (Session 82).
