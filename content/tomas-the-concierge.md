# Tomas the Concierge

**Summary**: Concierge at the luxury glass apartment tower in Startown Liberty who resisted a bribe and was subdued by Isambard during the raid on Elin Butler.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Incident at the Glass Tower

Tomas (pronounced *Toe-Mass*) is a slender, fragile residential concierge working the front reception desk at a luxury high-rise glass tower in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 14). 

On Day 44-1105, when operatives [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[asher|Asher]] arrived seeking [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]], Tomas refused a cash bribe and reached for the console alarm to alert building security (Session 14). Although Isambard's attempt to disable him with pepper spray failed comically, Asher jammed the alarm console while Isambard placed Tomas into an effective sleeper choke-hold, rendering him unconscious so the team could ascend to Elin's apartment (Session 14).
