# The Stratemeyers

**Summary**: Militarized mining syndicate based in the lawless Dark Crescent of the Arico belt, secretly commanded by banker [[orslo-jollynn|Orslo Jollyn]] until her 1105 assassination.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Origins and Structure

The Stratemeyers (or Stratemeyer Syndicate) originated as a loose confederation of heavily militarized belters and miners operating in the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] asteroid belt ([[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]]). While initially avoiding formal designation as a criminal cartel, the syndicate routinely operated outside the law:
- Attacked any unauthorized starships approaching their claimed asteroid stakes without warning.
- Staged armed raids against rival mining installations to seize claims and industrial equipment.

## Escalation and High Finance Infiltration

Over time, the syndicate expanded into extortion, kidnapping, and interplanetary racketeering ([[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]; [[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]):
- **Freighter Fleet Hijacking**: On Day 119-1105, the syndicate captured [[reidmar|Reidmar]]'s entire merchant freighter fleet in the Dark Crescent, prompting Reidmar to petition the Tempest Company for armed escort and setting the stage for subsequent escalations (Session 41).
- **Eternity Station Crisis**: In 131-1105, the syndicate executed hostages on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], triggering a total general strike led by [[reidmar|Reidmar]] that paralyzed outer belt commerce ([[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).
- **The Jollynn Unmasking & Decapitation (Day 133-1105)**: In a coordinated raid on her gated estate, [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] ambushed and executed Marie Orslo Jollynn, her assistant, and chief bodyguard Richard. Decrypted mobile files conclusively proved Jollynn commanded the syndicate, directing operations through bank president [[tora-takanabe|Tora Takanabe]] ("The President") to field managers like Starka Pramerski (Sessions 56–57; [[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
- **Internal Leadership Cleanup (Day 133-1105)**: Amidst mounting investigations into [[omni|Omni]] and precursor research, syndicate assassin [[the-man-with-the-gun|Richard (The Man With The Gun)]]—who served as Jollynn's personal bodyguard—murdered money-laundering administrator [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]] in a Freeport mall restroom, staging it as a drug overdose before being uncovered by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] (Session 54).
