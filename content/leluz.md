# Leluz

**Summary**: An underworld figure and gun-for-hire who leaked intelligence regarding an assassination contract on narcotics dealer Conde Borbon.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Leluz is an operative in the regional criminal underworld. In 131-1105, an encrypted communique originated by Leluz (forwarded through [[ridpath|Ridpath]]) leaked that three weeks earlier, the owner of **The Stardust** approached Leluz with an assassination contract targeting drug dealer [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]]. Leluz declined the hit citing insufficient payment and high risk ([[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).
