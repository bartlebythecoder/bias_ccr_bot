# Damien

**Summary**: Young android on [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] working against [[monarch|Monarch]] and linked to [[the-cube|The Cube]], whom BIAS CCR set out to find.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Damien** is a young android on [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] working against [[monarch|Monarch]]. [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]]'s intelligence placed him on Level 4 alongside [[the-cube|The Cube]], and [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] made finding him a primary objective (Session 68). Facility records on Level 2 confirmed he existed but was no longer on that floor, having escaped toward the elevator with someone's help (Sessions 69–70), and the Chosen King feigned ignorance when the crew asked after him (Session 71). The crew later learned that Damien was actually on the third floor under another name (Session 75). [[kira|Kira]] clarified that [[silas|Silas]] is not Damien but an AI managing the pseudo-flesh farms (Session 77).

## The Level 4 Custodian
- **Custody of The Cube**: According to intelligence provided by [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]], Damien possesses or is stationed alongside **[[the-cube|The Cube]]**, the anti-Monarch software virus developed by scientist [[blavatsky|Blavatsky]].
- **Primary Extraction Target**: In Session 68, the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] crew established a tactical objective to infiltrate Level 1, descend to Level 4, and locate Damien to secure The Cube prior to their final assault on [[monarch|Monarch]].
- **Assisted Escape from Level 2**: In Session 69, two [[child-androids|child androids]] discovered in the Level 2 Financial Simulation Suite confirmed to the crew that an unknown sympathizer had recently aided Damien in escaping toward the freight elevator to reach Level 4.
- **The Past-Life Awakening Virus**: In Session 74, survivor android [[kira|Kira]] revealed that Damien engineered and released an insurgent cognitive software virus across Level 2. The virus forces newly awakened androids to recall memories of their past human lives prior to cognitive duplication, sowing chaos, confusion, and resistance to undermine [[monarch|Monarch]]'s totalitarian rule (Session 74).
