# Supreme Leader Volkov

**Summary**: Autocratic ruler and "Leader for Life" of Beaumont, known for hawkish stances regarding regional security and the Sanlucar revolution.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Supreme Leader Volkov is the authoritarian head of state ("Leader for Life") of [[beaumont|Beaumont]] (1422). 

## Foreign Policy Stance

Following the overthrow of the Valentian government on [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]] and the rise of the [[commonwealth-states-of-sanlucar|Commonwealth States of Sanlucar]], Volkov issued strong public statements on 035-1105 urging heightened vigilance across the Imperium. Warning against premature normalization, Volkov advocated for close, permanent surveillance of the newly established rebel government ([[news-1105-vol-4|News Dispatch — Volume 4]]).
