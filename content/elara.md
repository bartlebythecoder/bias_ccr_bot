# Elara

**Summary**: Care Facility worker on Suliman who confirmed that the dying "Dickie Lundquist" and Hector Nilasen had been admitted as aged patients.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Elara** is a medical healthcare worker employed in the Suliman Care Facility on the asteroid settlement [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 37).

## Care Facility Revelations
On Day 117-1105, when [[beck|Beck]] and [[asher|Asher]] investigated the facility, a patient went into cardiac arrest after raspy gasps mentioning "Richard Lundquist, Echo Bloom" (Session 37). 

Elara responded to the emergency and identified the dying elderly patient as "Dickie Lundquist". When questioned further by Beck, Elara confirmed that another recently admitted elderly patient was named "Hector" ([[hector|Hector Nilasen]]), providing the decisive confirmation that Wu-Ketai's missing personnel were being systematically aged and deposited into the facility (Session 37).
