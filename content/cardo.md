# Cardo

**Summary**: Underworld fixer on Suliman who sold BIAS CCR covert access to the restricted Apex Zenith sector for Cr 6,000.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Cardo** is an underworld fixer and transit broker operating on the asteroid settlement [[suliman|Suliman]] in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system (Session 35).

## Apex Access Broker
On Day 116-1105, [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] contacted Cardo to arrange covert access to the restricted Apex Zenith sector of Suliman. While Cardo initially demanded a fee of Cr 10,000, the operatives successfully negotiated the price down to Cr 6,000 before proceeding with their investigation (Session 35).
