# Foust Refinery

**Summary**: Kino Yuba Foust's rare metals refinery in Hollow on Marcolandia, which BIAS CCR defended against Rainford Ruby's heist.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The Foust Refinery is a private metals refining and processing facility owned by [[kino-yuba-foust|Kino Yuba Foust]], situated in the remote settlement of Hollow on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] (Session 30).

## Operational Context & Heist Threat
Due to regional logistics breakdowns, processed rare metals accumulated in massive quantities within the facility's warehouses, turning it into a lucrative target for raiders and industrial thieves. In response, Foust contracted [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] alongside the mercenary team [[elliotts-eagles|Elliott's Eagles]] to fortify the perimeter and monitor threat vectors (Session 30).

### The Ruby Heist Defense
On Day 96, Year 1105, the refinery was attacked in a coordinated operation funded by [[rainford-ruby|Rainford Ruby]]. After [[kino-yuba-foust|Kino Yuba Foust]] was poisoned, rogue security officer [[larry-mckarney|Larry McKarney]] armed a block of prisoner workers to create a diversion in the south against [[silvio-warren|Silvio Warren]] and [[isambard|Isambard]]. Meanwhile, McKarney led an armored strike team against the eastern approach to reach the warehouse. The defense was held by [[sky|Sky]] and [[sarik|Sarik]], with the five mercenaries of [[elliotts-eagles|Elliott's Eagles]] guarding the interior, completely protecting the stockpiled metals (Sessions 31–32).
