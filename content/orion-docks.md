# Orion Docks

**Summary**: [[freeport|Freeport]] cargo port and warehouse district used by [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] as a smuggling corridor.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Orion Docks** is a commercial shipping facility, cargo port, and warehouse district located on the habitat of [[freeport|Freeport]] (Sessions 53–54).

## Role in Underworld Logistics

The docks serve as a primary transit hub for freight passing through Freeport customs. Intelligence recovered from a decrypted burner phone at Park 32 revealed that [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] maintained an active distribution and smuggling corridor through the facility (Session 53).

On Day 133-1105, [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] investigated the docks, interrogating dockworking supervisor [[irquez|Irquez]]. Irquez disclosed that late syndicate administrator [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]] made routine payments to ensure local union cooperation when slipping sensitive or contraband shipments through customs inspections (Session 54).
