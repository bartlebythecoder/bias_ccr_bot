# Shailok

**Summary**: Elderly, severely aged patient in [[suliman|Suliman]]'s Care Facility whom [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]] often visited.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

**Shailok** is an elderly patient institutionalized in the Suliman Care Facility on the asteroid settlement [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 37).

## Link to Richard Lundquist & Echo Bloom
According to technician [[david-orana|David Orana]], missing geneticist [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]] frequently visited Shailok prior to his own disappearance (Session 37). When [[beck|Beck]] and [[asher|Asher]] visited the facility on Day 117-1105 to interview him, Shailok was severely aged, fragile, and unable to speak coherently. The visit directly preceded the discovery that Lundquist and [[hector|Hector Nilasen]] had also been admitted to the ward after being artificially aged through [[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]] under [[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]], indicating Shailok was likely an earlier victim of the experimental process (Session 37).
