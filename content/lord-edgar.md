# Lord Edgar

**Summary**: Curt and unfriendly younger brother of Viscount Rittenhouse, implicated in the conspiracy to steal the Beaugard painting inside [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Noble Standing & Personality

Lord Edgar is the younger brother of [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]], the ruler of [[csabai|Csabai]] (Sessions 22–23). Characterized by household staff and peers as curt, arrogant, and distinctly unfriendly, Edgar maintains private quarters within [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] (Sessions 22–23).

## Implication in the Beaugard Theft

On Day 57-1105, during the internal investigation into the theft of the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]], electronic surveillance timestamp analysis conducted by [[asher|Asher]] and [[sky|Sky]] uncovered an unrecorded meeting: [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]] had spent ten minutes inside Lord Edgar's private apartments immediately before entering the gallery to drug [[lord-julian|Lord Julian]] with sedated coffee (Sessions 22–23). 

While the operatives successfully recovered the stolen artwork from Vivienne's suite, the exact punitive measures or disciplinary actions handed down by Viscount Rittenhouse against Lord Edgar for his complicity were handled confidentially within the royal family (Sessions 22–23).
