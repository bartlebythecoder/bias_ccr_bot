# Marion Rampagne

**Summary**: President of the [[romentino|Romentino]] regional office of [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], who actively monitors the activities of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Corporate Leadership

Marion Rampagne serves as the regional President of [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] (LSP) for the [[romentino|Romentino]] system (Session 11). As the supreme commercial authority over LSP's substantial mining, fuel refining, and research operations on Romentino and [[mirassol|Mirassol]], Rampagne commands vast corporate resources and private security divisions (Session 11).

## Confrontation at Baltimore House

Attending Lord Baltimore's formal gala on Day 40-1105 alongside her Chief of Corporate Security [[jin|Jin]], Rampagne confronted the newly incorporated [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] operatives ([[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isambard|Isambard]], [[sarik|Sarik]]) (Session 11). Rampagne made it explicitly clear that LSP was aware of their deceptive stowaway story and intended to keep them under close corporate scrutiny (Session 11).
