# Ruby Refinery Heist

**Summary**: 1104 mercenary heist on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] targeting stockpiled super-metal shipping crates at the independent refinery of [[rainford-ruby|Rainford Ruby]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Incident Overview

In year 1104, the independent Ruby Refinery on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] was struck by a meticulously coordinated armed heist. The refinery, owned by Rainford Ruby (a distant relation of Lord Blackthorne), specializes in refining rare super-metals harvested from the atmosphere of the gas giant Vilnohirsk.

## Execution and Fall-Out

- **The Raid**: A mercenary assault team, working with inside conspirators, landed a large contra-grav cargo lifter at the refinery warehouse and successfully extracted millions of credits worth of stockpiled super-metal crates.
- **Internal Suspicions**: While no official public charges were pressed, Rainford Ruby privately blamed his cousin, Yuba Foust (tied to [[youst-refining|Youst Refining]]), for orchestrating or facilitating the theft to feed rival industrial refining pipelines.
