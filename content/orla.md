# Orla

**Summary**: Warrens guide and fixer on [[suliman|Suliman]] hired by BIAS CCR for Cr 1,500.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Orla** is a resourceful guide, fixer, and courier operating throughout [[the-warrens|The Warrens]] on the asteroid settlement [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 35).

## Collaboration with BIAS CCR
On the morning of Day 116-1105, following the team's release from corporate detention, union organizer [[carl-parker|Carl Parker]] dispatched Orla to assist [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] (Session 35). 

After negotiations with [[isambard|Isambard]], Orla accepted a fee of Cr 1,500 to provide discrete navigational services, secure black-market goods, establish an entry contact into the high-end Apex Zenith sector, and investigate leads regarding missing geneticist [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]] (Session 35).
