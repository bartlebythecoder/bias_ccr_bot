# News Dispatch — Volume 9

**Summary**: Dispatches spanning days 134 to 170 of 1105 across Subsector J, featuring the assassination of financier [[orslo-jollynn|Orslo Jollynn]] and her unmasking as leader of the Stratemeyer Syndicate, major Megacorporation contract shifts between [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], and [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]], a subsector-wide Psalmist Watch report, and rumors of an abandoned [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] facility holding illegal Ancient technology and AI security robots.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Dispatches and Timeline

### 134-1105: Murder of Orslo Jollynn and Unmasking of Stratemeyer Syndicate
- **Location**: [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] (1324)
- **Actors**: [[orslo-jollynn|Orslo Jollynn]], [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]]
- Renowned financier and bank owner Orslo Jollynn was found executed gangland-style at her private mansion.
- Subsequent investigative breakthroughs sensationally revealed that Jollynn was the clandestine mastermind and head of [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] (Stratemeyer Syndicate), exposing the syndicate's deep infiltration into elite planetary banking.

### 170-1105: Megacorporation Interstellar Contract Battles
- **Actors**: [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]], [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]]
- **Csabai**: Hortalez et Cie dealt a major commercial blow to Ling Standard Products by winning the long-term planetary government technology management contract, which LSP had controlled for 12 years.
- **Kidderminster**: In a shocking military upset, Hortalez et Cie secured the Kidderminster Navy's 'Senior Operations Initiative' contract, beating out Tukera Lines whose Subsector President is married to [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]].
- **Marcolandia**: Ling Standard Products successfully fended off an aggressive takeover bid by Hortalez et Cie for ownership of the [[youst-refining|Foust Refinery]] and three gas giant harvesting operations at Vilnohirsk, cementing LSP's expansion into super-metal extraction.
- **Arico Stock Market Impact**:
  - Hortalez et Cie: Up 7 to 186
  - Ling Standard Products: Down 1.5 to 141.5
  - Tukera Lines: Down 5.5 to 97.5

### 170-1105: Subsector-Wide Psalmist Watch
- **Actors**: [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]], [[orslo-jollynn|Orslo Jollynn]], [[darlene-suitor|Darlene Suitor]], [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]
- **Arico**: The unmasking of billionaire Orslo Jollynn's criminal empire invigorated the populist Psalm 134 movement, which leveraged the scandal as proof of the corruption of wealth and celebrity. Participation surged to record levels in Freeport and the Belt, cementing Arico as the informal subsector hub for Psalmism.
- **Csabai**: Following the arrest of [[darlene-suitor|Darlene Suitor]] for racketeering, narcotics, and murder, local Psalmist leaders maintained a low profile, disavowing Suitor as an "unfortunate militant" fringe element.
- **Marcolandia**: A Psalmist center closed due to the rising dominance of Ling Standard Products, which actively limits Psalmist influence and bars adherents from managerial roles.

### Rumors: The Lost Wu-Ketai Ancient & AI Facility
- **Location**: [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] (1324)
- **Actors**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]]
- Underground rumors report the existence of an apocryphal, abandoned Wu-Ketai manufacturing facility that allegedly utilized illegal Ancient artifacts to create an autonomous, illegal super-AI tasked with constructing security robots.
- The subterranean or hidden warehouse is said to house extensive stockpiles of illicit Ancient relics, which would face summary orbital destruction by Imperial Navy "black shirts" if discovered.
