# One-Handed Man

**Summary**: Armed stranger who came to [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] hunting for [[sarik|Sarik]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The **One-Handed Man** is an armed adversary who arrived at **[[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]** in the **[[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]]** on Day 190-1105 actively searching for **[[sarik|Sarik]]**.

*(Session 65)*

---

## Identity and Background
On Day 190-1105, **[[dana-westby|Dana Westby]]** sent an urgent comms message to **[[sky|Sky]]** warning that a suspicious individual described as a "One-Handed Man" had touched down on Eternity Station and was aggressively asking locals for Sarik's whereabouts.

The crew of **[[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]** identified two primary hypotheses regarding his identity:
1. **[[ruffian|Ruffian]] from Suliman**: On Day 115-1105 in The Warrens of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] mining asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]], Sarik was attacked by a laborer with electrified knuckles; Sarik counter-attacked by driving his combat blade completely through the assailant's hand, leaving him severely mutilated.
2. **Dark Web Bounty Hunter**: An assassin seeking to collect the **Cr 1,000,000** (MCr 1.0) bounty placed on Sarik by syndicate or corporate interests following the events on Freeport.

---

## Crew Response
Upon receiving the warning, **[[sky|Sky]]** advocated launching a preemptive strike to track down and eliminate the One-Handed Man on Eternity Station before he could trace BIAS CCR to **[[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]**. The crew deliberated her aggressive proposal while balancing their ongoing operations in The Deep.
