# Isidora

**Summary**: Professional steward aboard [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s luxury yacht, second cousin to [[isambard|Isambard]], and expert informant on the politics and society of [[csabai|Csabai]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Kinship & Shipboard Role

Isidora serves as the head steward aboard [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]]'s private [[gsbag|GSbAG]] luxury yacht (Session 20). She is the second cousin of [[isambard|Isambard]], the retired Scout intelligence agent and co-founding director of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] (Session 20).

## Briefing on Csabai

During the Jump voyage from [[romentino|Romentino]] to [[csabai|Csabai]] in early 1105, Isidora reconnected with Isambard and the operatives (Session 20). Drawing upon extensive personal and professional travel throughout the subsector, Isidora demonstrated comprehensive familiarity with Csabai, providing BIAS CCR with detailed insights into the planetary culture, the court of [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]], and the socio-economic divisions defining the high-population world (Session 20).
