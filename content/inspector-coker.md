# Inspector Coker

**Summary**: [[freeport|Freeport]] homicide detective leading the [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] murder investigation, who treated it as a domestic killing.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**Inspector Coker** is a senior homicide investigator with local law enforcement on [[freeport|Freeport]], assigned as the primary detective investigating the high-profile slaying of [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] graphic designer [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] in The Lux (Session 45).

---

## Operational History

### The Soto Murder Investigation (Day 130-1105)
On Day 130-1105, through arrangements coordinated by syndicate attorney [[ridpath|Mr. Ridpath]], Coker granted an official interview to [[beck|Beck]] and the operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] (Session 45):

- **Suspect Assessment**: Coker viewed the homicide as a domestic dispute, focusing on [[david-parker|David Parker]], Louise Soto's estranged ex-boyfriend who possessed an established record of violence and had fled into hiding following the shooting.
- **Ballistics & Weapons Law**: Coker confirmed that a ballistic slug had been recovered from Soto's skull during the autopsy. He emphasized Freeport's strict public security ordinances, noting that all non-stunner ranged weapons are strictly outlawed on the habitat. With Ridpath's legal filing, he facilitated access to the official coroner's report.
- **Surveillance Review**: Coker noted that while dozens of low-quality holovids had been filmed by panicked clubgoers outside [[club-strata|Club Strata]], the masked shooter's face was completely obscured, leaving the identity unconfirmed.

### The Starka Pramerski Mall Death (Day 133-1105)
On Day 133-1105, Inspector Coker responded to a Freeport shopping mall where a patron discovered the body of [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]] in an unlocked public restroom (Session 54).
- **Preliminary Finding**: Coker indicated to the arriving team that initial findings suggested an accidental drug overdose based on pills found at the scene.
- **Beck's Counter-Finding**: When [[palmas|Palmas]] vouched for [[beck|Beck]], Coker permitted Beck to inspect the corpse. Beck pointed out neck bruising indicative of manual strangulation and staged overdose, estimating time of death within twelve hours and securing scene evidence before municipal CCTV was accessed (Session 54).
