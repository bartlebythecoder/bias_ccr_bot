# Kino Yuba Foust

**Summary**: Owner of the Foust Refinery on Marcolandia who hired BIAS CCR to protect his stockpiled rare metals.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Kino Yuba Foust is the industrialist owner and operator of the [[foust-refinery|Foust Refinery]], an isolated metals processing facility located in Hollow on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] (Session 30).

## Security Contract with BIAS CCR
Faced with severe transport and delivery bottlenecks that left his storage warehouses overflowing with refined rare metals, Foust feared an impending corporate heist. Upon the arrival of the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] at Marcolandia, Foust retained [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] to oversee facility security and conduct intelligence operations:
- **Terms**: Cr 100,000 for one week of security management.
- **Expense Account**: Cr 50,000 (used to retain [[elliotts-eagles|Elliott's Eagles]]).
- **Bounty**: Cr 250,000 bonus if a planned heist was successfully intercepted and foiled (Session 30).

### Feud with Rainford Ruby and Refinery Defense
Foust's fear of a robbery stemmed from having hired mercenaries to rob his cousin [[rainford-ruby|Rainford Ruby]]'s refinery a year earlier. During the subsequent attack on his own facility orchestrated by Ruby, Foust was poisoned at dinner by an infiltrated kitchen worker. He survived through prompt medical treatment provided by [[beck|Beck]] and the staff doctor. Following the successful defense of the warehouse and neutralization of [[larry-mckarney|Larry McKarney]] by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]], Foust paid the agreed Cr 100,000 security fee alongside the Cr 250,000 heist thwart bonus (Sessions 31–32).
