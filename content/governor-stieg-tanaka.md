# Governor Stieg Tanaka

**Summary**: Colony administrator for [[hampton|Hampton]] on [[tutin|Tutin]] and the surrounding area.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-20

---

## Administration of Hampton

Governor Stieg Tanaka serves as the colony administrator for [[hampton|Hampton]], the town on [[tutin|Tutin]] that serves as the entry and exit point for all workers on the planet, and for the surrounding area. Tutin is administered as a restricted nature reserve by the [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] government.
