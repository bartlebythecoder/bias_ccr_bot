# Joey

**Summary**: Full member of The Red Hand and associate of Hal the Grocer who struggled under the syndicate's ban on kidnappings.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Role in The Red Hand

Joey is an active "made" soldier within [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]] in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] and a close personal contact of [[hal-the-grocer|Hal the Grocer]] (Session 12). Following the truce negotiated by [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]], Joey experienced severe financial difficulties meeting his required upward quota payments to senior syndicate leadership due to the organizational ban on profitable kidnappings (Session 12).

His financial predicament exemplifies the systemic rank-and-file dissatisfaction that spurred the creation of the [[real-red-hand|Real Red Hand]] splinter group (Session 12).

## Rogue Smuggling to Nesvorny

In direct violation of the syndicate peace and at risk of summary execution by [[eminshi-moth|Eminshi Moth]], Joey coerced street dealer [[tina|Tina]] under threat of death to act as an off-world drug mule, ordering her to smuggle [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] to [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] (Session 13).

## Alliance Offer with BIAS CCR

Facing mortal danger from [[eminshi-moth|Eminshi Moth]], Joey approached [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] on Day 044-1105, proposing to arrange a direct meeting with the [[umdala|Umdala]] (supreme head of The Red Hand) to nullify Moth's vendetta, on the condition that the operatives help him escort [[tina|Tina]] and her smuggled [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]] cargo onto the star liner to [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] (Session 15).

## Surface Infiltration Scheme & Bobby's Death

On Day 044-1105, Joey organized an overland vacuum suit trek across Romentino's toxic surface to bypass starport security, assigning his brother [[bobby|Bobby]] to escort [[tina|Tina]], unaware that BIAS CCR operatives intended to rescue her (Session 16). During the crossing, Bobby was killed by [[sarik|Sarik]], and Tina was taken into protective custody by the crew, leaving Joey's smuggling operation shattered (Session 16).
