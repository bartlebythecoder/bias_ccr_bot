# Ricardo

**Summary**: Street cyberneticist in the Icebox on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] who referred BIAS CCR to the surgeon [[solon|Solon]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Ricardo** is a street cyberneticist and bodyshop technician operating in [[eternity-station#Key Sectors & Black Markets|The Icebox]] on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] (Session 41).

## Bodyshop Consultation
On Day 119-1105, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] were directed to Ricardo's cluttered workshop by [[katarin|Katarin]] while investigating the poker winning streak of [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]] (Session 41). Ricardo welcomed the operatives, discussing the steep expenses and risks associated with installing neural wafer slots. Recognizing that Sargon's cybernetic capabilities exceeded typical street-surgery work, Ricardo directed the team to an elite, high-end practitioner named [[solon|Solon]] (Session 41).
