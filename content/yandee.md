# Yandee

**Summary**: Demon Prince who rules Eternity Station and leads the Meinhoff Brigade.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Demon Prince Yandee** is the supreme criminal overlord of the [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]] and the de facto sovereign ruler of [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] in the [[dark-crescent|Dark Crescent]] of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico Belt]] (Session 43; [[demon-princes|Demon Princes]]; [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]).

## Governance of Eternity Station
Operating from her fortified court on Eternity Station, Yandee rules the station through an iron-fisted syndicate bureaucracy supported by the heavily armed **Tempest Company** mercenaries ([[eternity-station|Eternity Station]]). Under her dominion, the Meinhoff Brigade commands the interstellar trafficking of the narcotic **Sycorax** across the Arico belt inward toward [[freeport|Freeport]], while systematically infiltrating the leadership of Teamsters Local 315F (Session 40).

## Summit with BIAS CCR & The Stratemeyer Bounties
On Day 119-1105, following the murder of gambler [[harvon-sargon|Harvon Sargon]], Teamster head [[reidmar|Reidmar]] brought operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] before Yandee (Session 43). The crew presented decrypted video footage extracted from Sargon's neural "black box" by [[asher|Asher]], conclusively exposing **[[mosh|Mosh]]** as Sargon's murderer.

Impressed by the operatives' efficiency and forensic proof, Yandee granted BIAS CCR a formal syndicate pardon for their armed forced entry into the clinic of cyber-surgeon [[solon|Solon]], conditional upon an apology and compensation for property damage (Session 43). Furthermore, seeking to crush syndicate rivals threatening outer belt commerce, Yandee offered BIAS CCR two massive multi-million credit contracts:
- **MCr 2.0 (Cr 2,000,000)**: To identify, hunt down, and eliminate the clandestine overall leader of [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyer Syndicate]].
- **MCr 2.0 (Cr 2,000,000)**: To locate, assault, and recapture Reidmar's hijacked merchant freighter fleet.

## Settlement of the Stratemeyer Contract (Day 133-1105)
On Day 133-1105, BIAS CCR and Hunter's Heroes submitted conclusive proof of [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn's]] execution and syndicate leadership to Yandee's attorney, [[ridpath|Mr. Ridpath]] (Sessions 56–57). Satisfied by the corroborated evidence, Yandee approved the fulfillment of the MCr 2.0 bounty, yielding an 800,000 credit payout share to BIAS CCR.
