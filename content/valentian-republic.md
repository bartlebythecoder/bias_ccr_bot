# Valentian Republic

**Summary**: The governing regime on the moon Sanlucar that controlled vast gas-mining infrastructure before its overthrow in early 1105.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

The Valentian Republic was the dominant political authority on the gas-mining moon [[sanlucar-de-barram|Sanlucar de Barram]]. Following the Sanlucar Civil War, the Republic annexed the neighboring Khanate of Aridush after key raids decimated the Aridush leadership ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## Governance and Ideological Alignment

The Republic retained centralized control over Sanlucar's extensive gas extraction industry, causing intense friction with other local states and workers. In recent years, Republic leaders closely allied themselves with the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement, an ideological alignment that alienated the moon's predominantly working-class populace ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).

## Collapse

Under increasing pressure from the [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]], Republic defenses cracked during a coordinated rebel offensive, leading to a full military surrender in 003-1105 and the displacement of the Republic by a provisional administration under [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]] ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).
