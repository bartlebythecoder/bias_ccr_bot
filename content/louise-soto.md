# Louise Soto

**Summary**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] graphic designer shot dead outside [[club-strata|Club Strata]] on [[freeport|Freeport]], whose murder BIAS CCR investigated.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**Louise Soto** was a 24-year-old graphic designer employed by the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai Corporation]] on [[freeport|Freeport]] in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system. On Day 113-1105, she was murdered by a masked gunman while standing in line outside [[club-strata|Club Strata]] in The Lux (Session 45; [[news-1105-vol-7|News Dispatch — Volume 7]]).

---

## Murder and Investigation

### The Shooting at The Lux (Day 113-1105)
While queuing outside [[club-strata|Club Strata]] in The Lux entertainment sector within the Humboldt District, Soto was shot in the head with a solid-slug projectile by an unidentified masked assailant (Session 45; [[news-1105-vol-7|News Dispatch — Volume 7]]). The assassination sparked widespread panic across the district and was recorded by dozens of bystanders, although the killer's face was completely concealed.

### The Cryptic Tip & Syndicate Nexus (Days 127–130-1105)
On Day 127-1105, upon emerging from their medical convalescence on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] received an anonymous informant transmission regarding the contract against [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]]: *"Louise Soto did not have to die"* (Session 45).

Arriving on Freeport, BIAS CCR investigated her death:
- **Domestic Cover vs. Syndicate Hit**: Police Inspector [[inspector-coker|Inspector Coker]] considered her violent, fugitive ex-boyfriend [[david-parker|David Parker]] the prime suspect. However, BIAS CCR deduced that the acquisition and discharge of an illegal ballistic firearm under Freeport's strict weapons ban pointed to professional planning, suggesting Soto had uncovered or designed proprietary material connecting Wu-Ketai to the Stratemeyer Syndicate.
- **Associates**: Inquiries at Club Strata with bouncer [[andre|Andre]] and bartender [[cadre|Cadre]] corroborated Parker's violent history, while the operatives initiated searches for her Humboldt District apartment and her Wu-Ketai colleagues, Sarah and Julie.

### Breakthrough: Soto as Collateral Damage (Day 132-1105)
On Day 132-1105, cyber-operative [[asher|Asher]] uncovered local surveillance recordings capturing the shooting outside Club Strata from a fresh angle (Session 50). The footage revealed that independent narcotics dealer [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]] was standing directly in front of Louise Soto when the fatal shot was fired. 

This critical evidence proved that Louise Soto was not targeted for corporate espionage or domestic violence, but was tragic collateral damage in an attempted underworld hit on Conde Borbon, explaining the cryptic clue: *"Louise Soto did not have to die"* (Session 50).
