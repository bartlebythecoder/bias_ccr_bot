# Elena Dumont

**Summary**: Chief Medical Officer at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]], whose professional anxieties and career ambitions were manipulated by [[beck|Beck]] to authorize the secret pyramid expedition.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Station Administration

Dr. Elena Dumont served as the Head Doctor and Chief Medical Officer at [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]], overseeing all clinical facilities, medical personnel, and emergency biological care in the Redshed complex (Session 6). Highly conscious of corporate standing, Dr. Dumont was intensely driven by protecting her reputation within the parent corporation and advancing her administrative career (Session 6).

## Manipulation by Beck

Circa Day 32-1105, following the disappearance of Field Support supervisor [[sophia|Sophia]] and the onset of debilitating psionic shockwaves across the area, undercover operative [[beck|Beck]] targeted Dumont's psychological vulnerabilities (Session 6). By framing the recovery of Sophia and discovery of the wave origin as a career-defining triumph, Beck persuaded Dr. Dumont to grant confidential authorization for an ad-hoc, nighttime search party, providing the crew legitimate cover to leave the station and raid the [[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]] (Session 6).
