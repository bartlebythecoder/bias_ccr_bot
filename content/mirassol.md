# Mirassol

**Summary**: Extremely frigid, low-oxygen moon in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system hosting [[kc-station|K.C. Station]], dangerous indigenous cryo-fauna, a crashed battle cruiser, and a buried Ancient pyramid.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Environmental Conditions

Mirassol is a satellite orbiting in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system (1428) (Sessions 1–5). The moon features an inhospitable climate characterized by:
- **Sub-zero Cryogenic Temperatures**: Frigid planetary surface covered in permafrost, methane snow, and ice plains ([[mursa|Mursa]]).
- **Thin / Deficient Atmosphere**: The atmosphere contains severely restricted oxygen concentrations, forcing all personnel outside sealed habitats to wear environmental suits and oxygen-supplemented breathing gear (Sessions 1–5).

## Native Ecology

Mirassol supports an aggressive, specialized cryogenic ecosystem:
- **[[mursa|Mursa]]**: Massive apex predators colloquially known as "space bears," featuring hollow nitrogen-insulated fur and a liquid methane plant diet, known to aggressively hunt warm-blooded organisms (Sessions 1–5; [[mursa|Mursa]]).
- **[[mirassol-spider|Mirassol Spiders]]**: Indigenous predatory arachnid fauna adapted to freezing temperatures ([[mirassol-spider|Mirassol Spider]]).

## Surface Installations & Anomalies

- **[[kc-station|K.C. Station]]**: A three-structure scientific and maintenance research outpost (Sessions 1–5).
- **Crashed Battle Cruiser**: An ancient warship wreckage located near K.C. Station from which [[cooper|Cooper]] hired operatives to extract sensor readings (Sessions 1–5).
- **[[mirassol-pyramid|Mirassol Pyramid]]**: A prehistoric, subterranean precursor structure of [[the-ancients|Ancient]] origin protruding through the frozen lunar surface (Sessions 1–5).
