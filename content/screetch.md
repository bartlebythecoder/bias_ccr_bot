# Screetch

**Summary**: Starlight-addicted son of shuttle pilot [[harry-proud|Harry Proud]] who fatally stabbed his father and experienced precognitive visions through the purple-glowing [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Background & Addiction

Screetch was the estranged son of commercial shuttle pilot [[harry-proud|Harry Proud]], residing in a dilapidated shack within the Barrikday slum district of [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 10). Screetch was heavily addicted to [[starlight-narcotic|Starlight]], an illicit psionically active hallucinogen synthesized from local Noavarite (Session 10). Unknown to Screetch, his father had been accumulating bribe money and stolen assets in a desperate endeavor to relocate Screetch off-world for medical treatment and rehabilitation (Session 10).

## The Patricide & The Precognitive Orb

On Day 38-1105, after Harry Proud stole the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] and Cr 20,000 cash from [[sky|Sky]], Proud met Screetch at the [[blue-parrot|Blue Parrot]] in GAS town (Session 10). When Proud refused to provide funds for Screetch to purchase Starlight, Screetch flew into a violent narcotic rage, stabbing his father repeatedly in an alleyway and fleeing with the stolen bag back to his shack, unaware that his father had died (Session 10).

When the operative team tracked Screetch to his shack, [[sarik|Sarik]] kicked in the entrance to find Screetch high on Starlight and brandishing an assault rifle (Session 10):
- Resting beside Screetch, the **Ancient Orb shifted from azure blue to vibrant purple**, which Screetch revealed had shown him a precognitive glimpse of the future wherein he shot Sarik and was subsequently cut down (Session 10).
- Sarik broke the predicted cycle by putting down his combat blade, prompting Screetch to surrender his weapon peacefully (Session 10).
