# Black Globe Generator

**Summary**: Advanced defensive force screen technology generating an impenetrable energy-absorbing black sphere, prone to lethal internal explosive overload.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Defensive Mechanics

The Black Globe Generator is a specialized defensive system that projects a pitch-black spherical force screen around a starship:
- **Impenetrable Barrier**: Physical matter is incapable of penetrating the surface boundary.
- **Energy Absorption**: Directed energy attacks (lasers, particle beams, plasma) impinging upon the barrier are absorbed directly into the generator's internal capacitor banks.
- **Heat Dissipation**: Once the screen is lowered, accumulated energy is radiated away into vacuum as thermal heat.

## Operational Vulnerabilities

- **Tactical Blindness**: The barrier completely blocks electromagnetic sensors and communications; vessels operating with an active globe must navigate blindly via inertial guidance and cannot maneuver while the field is raised.
- **Catastrophic Overload**: If absorbed energy exceeds generator capacity, the system fails catastrophically—releasing the entire accumulated energy load as an internal explosion within the ship's hull. This failure mode is suspected in the destruction of the cruiser *Ninkur Sagga* at the [[battle-of-liberty|Battle of Liberty]] ([[kinunir-battle-cruiser|Kinunir-Class Battle Cruiser]]).
