# Ling Standard Products

**Summary**: Imperial Megacorporation dominating the Lower Circuit from its [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]] headquarters, operating Frontier Lines, manufacturing weapons, and funding anti-Psalmist lobbying.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Corporate Overview & Holdings

Ling Standard Products (LSP) is one of the massive thirteen Imperial Megacorporations, holding an industrial monopoly spanning heavy mining, micro-electronics, ground and grav vehicles, advanced weaponry, and consumer goods.
- **Major Stockholders**: [[house-aledon|House Aledon]] (18%), [[house-alkalikoi|House Alkalikoi]] (8%), [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]] (2%).

## Regional Dominance in Subsector J

LSP represents the single largest corporate power along the Rimward (Lower) Circuit:
- **Subsector Headquarters**: Anchored on the industrial world of [[nesvorny|Nesvorny]].
- **Core Worlds**: Exercises dominant market influence on [[romentino|Romentino]], [[lachat|Lachat]], [[pharr|Pharr]], and Hutchison.
- **Frontier Lines**: Wholly owned commercial shipping line catering exclusively to the Lower Circuit trade network.

## Security, Politics & Conflicts

- **Romentino Bombing**: In 051-1105, LSP's Morella Noavarite mining outpost was obliterated in a catastrophic attack by [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]], killing all 200 personnel ([[news-1105-vol-5|News Dispatch — Volume 5]]).
- **Anti-Psalmist Lobbying**: Connected to covert anti-Psalmist lobby groups; systematically curbs [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] expansion and bars adherents from corporate management on worlds like [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
- **Tech & Refining Battles**: Lost its 12-year Csabai government contract to [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]], but repelled Hortalez's takeover attempt of the [[youst-refining|Foust Refinery]] on Marcolandia ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).

## Operations on Mirassol & Security Interdiction

LSP maintains proprietary research installations in the Romentino system, including [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on the frozen moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]] (Session 9). Following an unauthorized breach of an unrecorded Ancient precursor site in early 1105, LSP dispatched internal corporate investigators to Mirassol and mobilized an armed security interdiction squad on [[romentino|Romentino]] to detain and interrogate inbound personnel from the station shuttle (Session 9).

## Regional Leadership on Romentino

LSP's regional planetary division on [[romentino|Romentino]] is headed by President [[marion-rampagne|Marion Rampagne]], with corporate security enforcement directed by Chief of Security [[jin|Jin]] (Session 11). Following the Mirassol infiltration, both executives confronted the newly formed [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] consultancy at Lord Baltimore's soiree, confirming LSP's active surveillance of the group (Session 11).

## Espionage Warrants Against BIAS CCR

Following the breach of Morella Station on Day 47-1105, LSP leadership accused BIAS CCR of corporate espionage, demanding that [[the-banks|The Banks]] broadcast planetary arrest warrants and raid their private quarters in Startown Liberty (Session 19). LSP additionally petitioned [[commodore-koroleva|Commodore Koroleva]] for an Imperial warrant, though the Commodore rebuffed the request (Session 19).

## The Witchfinder General and the Apex Detention

In late 1105, LSP's regional headquarters in [[apex|Apex]] on Csabai became the center of security tension involving [[darnes|Darnes]], known as the "Witchfinder General" (Session 26). Deducing that Darnes was a covert corporate psionic telepath, operative [[beck|Beck]] sought an appointment with him on Day 92-1105, only to be detained in a locked interrogation chamber by LSP Corporate Security Agents (Session 26).

## Outsourcing Internal Inquisitions to Erebus Power

Cyber intelligence recovered by [[asher|Asher]] on Day 93-1105 revealed that LSP's regional division in [[apex|Apex]] had spent two years subcontracting internal security purges to [[darnes|Darnes]], who was secretly an operative of rival energy megacorp [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]] (Session 27).
