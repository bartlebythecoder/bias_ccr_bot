# Anderton

**Summary**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] security officer on [[suliman|Suliman]] who detained BIAS CCR after [[the-warrens|the Warrens]] street brawl and warned them against further disturbances.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Anderton** is a corporate security officer serving the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation on the asteroid settlement [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 35).

## Warrens Street Incident
On Day 115-1105, Officer Anderton led the security detachment and paramedics responding to a violent confrontation in [[the-warrens|The Warrens]] involving [[sarik|Sarik]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and a local thug armed with shock knuckles (Session 35). 

Anderton confiscated the operatives' weapons, checked their papers, and placed the team in light handcuffs for overnight detention while surveillance footage was reviewed. With Security Chief [[marcus-fortune|Marcus Fortune]] absent, Anderton managed the incident and formally warned the party that any further disturbances on private corporate property would result in their expulsion from Suliman (Session 35).
