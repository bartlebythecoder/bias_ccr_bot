# Romero

**Summary**: Underworld technician on Suliman who helped BIAS CCR tap the colony's surveillance cameras.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Romero** is an underworld technician and information broker active on the asteroid settlement [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 35).

## Surveillance Intermediary
On Day 116-1105, Romero offered his technical services to [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] for a fee, assisting [[asher|Asher]] and [[beck|Beck]] in tapping internal surveillance camera networks to track the movements of missing persons [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]], [[hector|Hector Nilasen]], and Security Chief [[marcus-fortune|Marcus Fortune]] (Session 35). Crucially, Romero informed the team that Wu-Ketai's central core laboratories lacked surveillance monitoring entirely, highlighting a deliberate security blind spot (Session 35).

## Extraction to Freeport
Having assisted BIAS CCR with surveillance intelligence, Romero secured a commitment from the team to help him flee the asteroid (Session 39). On Day 117-1105, during the climactic facility breach and simian breakout, the team contacted pilot [[sky|Sky]], instructing her to locate Romero and escort him onto an evacuation shuttle bound for [[freeport|Freeport]] before security sealed the station (Session 39).
