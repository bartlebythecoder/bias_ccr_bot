# Joanna Lundquist

**Summary**: Wu-Ketai employee on Freeport who hired BIAS CCR to find her missing husband, Richard Lundquist.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Joanna Lundquist is an employee of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation residing on [[freeport|Freeport]] in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system (Session 33).

## Commissioning BIAS CCR & The Echo Bloom Bounties
Following the disappearance of her husband [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]] on the corporate asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]], Joanna grew convinced that corporate authorities were engaged in a cover-up. She retained [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]], offering Cr 100,000 for intelligence on his whereabouts and Cr 250,000 for his rescue, while connecting the team with sympathetic union leader [[carl-parker|Carl Parker]] (Session 33).

On Day 117-1105, after BIAS CCR confirmed Richard had been found in the Care Facility suffering from artificial aging, Joanna released their initial **Cr 100,000** fee (Session 38). She subsequently offered two major new bounties:
- **Cr 100,000**: For obtaining and extracting a verified physical DNA sample of Richard from the facility.
- **Cr 1,000,000 (MCr 1.0)**: For securing verified scientific telemetry, photographs, or video evidence documenting Wu-Ketai's [[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]] apparatus under [[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]] (Session 38).
