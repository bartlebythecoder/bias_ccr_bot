# Isambard

**Summary**: Former pirate, Imperial scout, and merchant marine who operates as the crew's mechanic, close-quarters combatant, and pilot-in-waiting for a surplus Type-S Scout ship.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Background & Career

Isambard possesses a diverse and checkered past across space, having served variously as an Imperial Scout, an armed merchant mariner, and a corsair/pirate (Sessions 1–5). 

Through his service in the Imperial Interstellar Scout Service (IISS), he earned official entitlement to the use of an older surplus Type-S Scout ship (Sessions 1–5). Securing operational possession of this starship serves as a core long-term objective for his crew (Sessions 1–5).
