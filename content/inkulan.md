# Inkulan

**Summary**: Irklan cultural go-between and intermediary based in Theos bar on Csabai who provided BIAS CCR with Irklan cultural insights and Csabaipede antidote.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Intermediary Role & Theos Bar

Inkulan is a respected member of the martial ascetic [[irklan|Irklan]] sect who resides within the cosmopolitan environs of [[csabai-starport-alpha|Csabai Starport Alpha]], primarily holding court at [[theos-bar|Theos Bar]] (Session 25). Operating as a diplomatic and cultural bridge between the reclusive mountain sect and local municipal authorities, Inkulan frequently assists planetary constabularies in interpreting Irklan traditions (Session 25).

## Dealings with Sarik

On Days 90–91-1105, following the assassination of [[calmer-singh|Calmer Singh]], operative [[sarik|Sarik]] sought out Inkulan at Theos bar (Session 25):
- **Irklan Martial Ethics**: Inkulan elucidated that Irklan operatives undertake contracts—including wetwork—strictly as tests of personal martial prowess and honor; they operate entirely alone, meaning no blood feud or retaliation would target [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] for eliminating the assassin at Berth 17 (Session 25).
- **Antidote Transaction**: Inkulan sold Sarik doses of specialized [[csabaipede|Csabaipede]] venom antidote (Session 25).
- **Rationale for Crew Slaughter**: Inkulan explained that Irklan assassins are obligated to eliminate anyone who possesses dangerous secrets they were hired to suppress; because Singh's crew had spent a week in Jump space with him, they were systematically slaughtered to ensure total operational silence (Session 25).
