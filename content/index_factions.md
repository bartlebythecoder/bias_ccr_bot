# Solo Subsector J — Factions, Houses & Megacorporations

**Summary**: An index of noble houses, megacorporations, regional governments, and underworld syndicates operating within Solo Sector Subsector J.

## Noble Houses & Governance

- [[house-aledon|House Aledon]]: Great House ruling Pilar subsector (Duke Norris), major LSP shareholder, anti-Psalmist, rival to Ilethian.
- [[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]: Great House ruling Pleasant Hill subsector (Duke Dulinor), 19% Hortalez, patron of Psalm 134, anti-psionic.
- [[house-liashay|House Liashay]]: Ruling house of Hess subsector (Duke Shiral Vash Liashav), elevated post-Hessian Revolt, anti-Psalmist.
- [[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]]: Ruling house of Kidderminster subsector (Duchess Margaret), allied with Tukera Lines, anti-Psalmist.
- [[noble-houses|Noble Houses]]: Overview of the sixteen Imperial subsector dynasties, House Alkalikoi crown, and feudal authority.
- [[commonwealth-states-of-sanlucar|Commonwealth States of Sanlucar]]: Sovereign government on Sanlucar formed by the Aridush coalition.
- [[the-banks|The Banks]]: Ruling financial oligopoly of Romentino exercising executive governance, policing, and arrest authority.
- [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]]: Former governing power on Sanlucar overthrown in 1105.
- [[rimward-circuit-collective|Rimward Circuit Collective]]: Regional cooperative coordinating humanitarian and economic initiatives.
- [[ouro-correction-department|Ouro Correction Department]]: Penal agency supplying convict labor to Youst Refining.

## Megacorporations & Industry

- [[delgado-trading|Delgado Trading]]: Youngest Imperial megacorporation (founded 997), miniaturization, mining, publishing, toys.
- [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]]: Lachat technology and energy firm, creator of Alexandria AI, aiming for megacorp status by 1112.
- [[general-products|General Products]]: Heavy machinery and starship builder, plagued by disasters in its Kinunir battle cruiser division.
- [[gsbag|GSbAG]]: Elite starship builder constructing vessels for the Imperial family and capital combat naval warships.
- [[hexham-mining|Hexham Mining]]: Mining enterprise on Marcolandia outsourcing refining to Youst.
- [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]]: Ancient banking and insurance megacorp (founded 730), subsector HQ on Arico, mercenary bonding house.
- [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn Trust]]: Major independent bank based on Freeport, official bank of the Starport Workers Union.
- [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]: Dominant megacorp in Lower Circuit (HQ Nesvorny), mining, weapons, Frontier Lines, anti-Psalmist lobbying.
- [[megacorporations|Megacorporations]]: Overview of the colossal commercial entities operating at Imperial scale across Charted Space.
- [[oberlindes-lines|Oberlindes Lines]]: Major subsector shipping line founded post-Hessian Revolt, majority-owned by SuSAG.
- [[sternmetal-horizons|Sternmetal Horizons]]: Foundational industrial megacorp producing power generators and the Imperium's food synthesis machinery.
- [[susag|SuSAG]]: Chemical and geneering giant, primary Imperial anagathics producer (DeLeon), protected by TL 12–14 private armies.
- [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]]: Interstellar passenger and cargo giant dominating xboat routes, closely tied to Duchess Margaret.
- [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]]: Specialized biotechnology corporation breeding uplifted simians as organic labor alternatives to AI.
- [[youst-refining|Youst Refining]]: Industrial refining firm on Marcolandia holding monopoly deal with Hexham Mining.
- [[omni|Omni]]: Corporate shell company on Freeport used by the Zero-G Vipers for money laundering.

## Underworld, Cartels & Militant Factions

- [[arico-gangs|Gangs of Arico]]: Overview of the Arico underworld: Jonestown street gangs on Freeport and the Dark Crescent syndicates.
- [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]]: Revolutionary rebel movement that seized control of Sanlucar.
- [[children-of-the-psalms|Children of the Psalms]]: Political and governing arm of Psalm 134 that assumed control of Pleasant Hill on 353-1104.
- [[covert-action-teams|Covert Action Teams]]: Corporate 5-person undercover intelligence and black-ops mercenary assault doctrine.
- [[demon-princes|Demon Princes]]: The supreme criminal warlords commanding multi-system underworld empires across the subsector.
- [[meinhoff-brigade|Meinhoff Brigade]]: Underworld syndicate controlling Eternity Station under Demon Prince Yandee, trafficking Sycorax.
- [[nesvorny-university|Nesvorny University]]: Academic research institution investigating Ancient civilization ties to local fauna.
- [[petow|PETOW]]: Animal advocacy front (People for the Ethical Treatment of Uplifted Workers) used by BIAS CCR.
- [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]]: Dominant criminal cartel in Jonestown on Freeport, trafficking Meinhoff Sycorax under Kaelen Varr.
- [[real-red-hand|Real Red Hand]]: Militant splinter faction of The Red Hand seeking to revive kidnappings and illicit Starlight exports.
- [[the-doves|The Doves]]: Clandestine militant Psalmist cell on Csabai targeting Darnes and hunting BIAS CCR for the Ancient Orb.
- [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]]: Violent anti-mining insurgent group operating out of Romentino.
- [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]]: Militant mining syndicate led by Orslo Jollynn, staging raids from the Dark Crescent.
- [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]]: Aggressive street gang in lower Jonestown on Freeport engaged in turf wars with the Primero Cartel.
- [[elliotts-eagles|Elliott's Eagles]]: Five-person mercenary fire team hired to defend Foust Refinery.
- [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]]: Independent mercenary outfit pursuing the Stratemeyer assassination bounty in competition with BIAS CCR.
