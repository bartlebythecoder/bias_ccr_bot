# Vince

**Summary**: Head of security at the Stardust Casino, working for Ortega Frey.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Vince** is the head of security at the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]] in North Lux on [[freeport|Freeport]], serving under owner [[ortega-frey|Ortega Frey]] (Session 50).

## Activities & Role

* **Handling BIAS CCR**: On Day 132-1105, when [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] arrived at the Stardust Casino seeking Ortega Frey, Vince initially informed them she was unavailable and monitored their wait in the coffee shop (Session 50).
* **Conference & Context**: After the operatives knocked on the penthouse fire door, Vince escorted them to meet Frey in a conference room. Following the discussion, Vince assured Beck and Sarik that no other investigative teams had approached Frey, vouching for the casino's reputation for honesty despite its dated appearance (Session 50).
