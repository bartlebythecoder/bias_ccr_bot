# Heavy Marbles

**Summary**: Dense black spheres of unknown purpose recovered by BIAS CCR from [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] and sellable to fences such as [[fagan|Fagan]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Heavy Marbles** are dense black spheres of unknown function recovered by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] from [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]. The crew extracted a bag of them from a corpse's chest cavity near the Level 2 executive bathroom, while [[monarch|Monarch]] remained conspicuously silent as they debated taking them (Session 66). Four more turned up in a manila envelope beneath a cafeteria table on Level 1, and [[arkady|Arkady]] confirmed the marbles could be sold to buyers such as [[fagan|Fagan]] on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], though no one could say what they do (Session 67). [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]] later asked to see them when she demanded the crew's recovered artifacts; [[sarik|Sarik]] showed her the marbles but did not let her handle them (Session 68).

## Characteristics & Discovery
- **Location**: Found inside the ribcage of an ancient deceased human scavenger ("green boots") slumped in a restroom on Metropolis Station Level 2.
- **Tripwire Trap**: The pouch was tethered to a fine tripwire running into the chest cavity, rigged to detonate or trigger an electrical/explosive disturbance trap if disturbed clumsily.
- **Rope Extraction**: On Day 191, [[beck|Beck]] successfully lassoed and pulled the pouch free with a climbing rope from outside the restroom doorway without setting off the primary explosive trap.
- **Anomalous Activation**: Upon being dislodged and dragged into the restroom doorway, the marbles coincided with a sudden energetic surge: the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] pulsed vivid blue, station emergency lighting strobed, and [[sarik|Sarik]]'s radio-frequency anomaly detector shrieks an alert for an incoming station "ghost".
- **Cafeteria Cache**: On Level 1, the team discovered a manila envelope tucked beneath a table in the cafeteria containing four additional heavy marbles.
- **Underworld Buyer**: [[arkady|Arkady]] identified [[fagan|Fagan]], a black market broker operating on [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], as an established buyer for the marbles.
