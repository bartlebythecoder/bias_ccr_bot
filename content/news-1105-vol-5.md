# News Dispatch — Volume 5

**Summary**: Dispatches spanning days 051 to 093 of 1105 across Subsector J, featuring the Red Hand terrorist attack on Ling Standard Products on Romentino, the first public appearances of Wu-Ketai's uplifted apes on Arico El Nuevo, and musical pop icon Sabrina touring Csabai under Duchess Margaret's Cultural Exchange Tour.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Dispatches and Timeline

### 051-1105: Red Hand Terrorist Attack on Ling Standard Products Mining Outpost
- **Location**: [[romentino|Romentino]] (1428)
- **Actors**: [[the-red-hand|The Red Hand]], [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]
- The Red Hand, an anti-mining extremist terrorist group, carried out a brazen suicide assault against LSP's Morella Mining outpost on Romentino.
- Operatives infiltrated the facility posing as auditors before detonating explosives and firing indiscriminately. The outpost was completely destroyed; all ~200 employees mining rare mineral Noavarite and the attackers were killed.
- Law enforcement launched immediate retaliatory tactical sweeps in **Startown Liberty** (the group's operating base), killing 45 Red Hand members, including top leaders **Umdala Kinchasa**, **Emnishi Moth**, and **Trey Capulet**.

### 079-1105: Wu-Ketai Uplifted Apes Enter the Field
- **Location**: [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] (1324)
- **Actors**: [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]]
- Sightings of man-like bipedal apes across Arico El Nuevo prompted Wu-Ketai to confirm that their uplifted organic servants are officially "out in the field".
- While general public sales remain at least a year away, deliveries to an exclusive, closed circle of private clients have commenced. The beings are marketed as compliant, organic labor avoiding the legal, physical, and Imperial taboos of AI-driven robotics.

### 093-1105: Pop Star Sabrina Tours Csabai
- **Location**: [[csabai|Csabai]] (1626) / [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] (1226)
- **Actors**: [[sabrina|Sabrina]], [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]]
- Kidderminster musical sensation Sabrina announced a multi-concert tour across Csabai as a headline performer under [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]]'s Cultural Exchange Tour.
- Famed across the subsector capital for her lavish lifestyle and autobiographical pop songs, this tour marks her debut system-wide performance on Csabai.
