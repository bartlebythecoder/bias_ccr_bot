# Mysterious Woman

**Summary**: Unidentified operative on [[romentino|Romentino]] who warned BIAS CCR of an imminent kidnapping ambush organized by [[eminshi-moth|Eminshi Moth]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Interventions in Startown Liberty

The Mysterious Woman is an unidentified, discreet operative active across [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] whose loyalties and sponsors remain unconfirmed (Sessions 14–15):
- On Day 44-1105, she delivered a handwritten note to the [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] operatives upon their return to the Noble Quarter, warning that an organized abduction attempt was being mobilized against them (Session 14).
- Later that afternoon in GAS Town, she intercepted the operatives in person, pinpointing an active four-man ambush lying in wait two blocks ahead, which enabled the crew to deploy [[sydney|Sydney Antonelli]] to confirm the threat and evade [[eminshi-moth|Eminshi Moth]]'s enforcers (Session 15).
