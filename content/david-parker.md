# David Parker

**Summary**: Violent ex-boyfriend of [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] and the police's prime suspect in her murder, later found to have been abducted.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**David Parker** is a resident of [[freeport|Freeport]] and the estranged ex-boyfriend of murdered [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] graphic designer [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]]. Following Soto's fatal shooting outside [[club-strata|Club Strata]] on Day 113-1105, Parker became the primary suspect pursued by local police (Session 45).

---

## Background & Investigation
- **History of Violence**: According to lead investigator [[inspector-coker|Inspector Coker]], Parker had a documented history of domestic violence and abusive behavior toward Soto (Session 45).
- **Bartender Corroboration**: During field inquiries at Club Strata, bartender [[cadre|Cadre]] corroborated that she had personally counseled Louise to separate from Parker due to his volatile nature.
- **Flight & Suspicions**: Parker disappeared into hiding immediately following the shooting. While police considered the case an open-and-shut crime of passion, [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] noted that the assassin's use of a strictly banned, military/civilian ballistic firearm in a crowded entertainment district suggested premeditation and professional syndicate involvement, potentially framing Parker or leveraging his history as a convenient cover.
- **Abduction by Hunter's Heroes (Day 130-1105)**: Rather than evading capture on his own, Parker was abducted from the street by rival mercenary unit [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]], who believed capturing him would resolve the Stratemeyer contract (Session 46). Footage of the kidnapping was acquired by [[isambard|Isambard]] from broker [[stadler|Stadler]]. Held captive in their Freeport townhouse, Parker was interrogated by [[beck|Beck]], who concluded from his psychological profile that he was innocent of the murder. However, Beck deliberately told Hunter's Heroes that Parker was guilty to mislead their investigation.
