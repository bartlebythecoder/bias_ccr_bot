# Alexandria AI

**Summary**: Advanced supercomputer knowledge repository and AI advisor developed on [[lachat|Lachat]] by [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]], strictly built to adhere to Imperial AI advisory regulations.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## System Overview

Unveiled on 018-1105 on [[lachat|Lachat]] by [[byron-falcon-power|Sir Byron Falcon Power]], Alexandria is an immense planetary supercomputer developed by [[erebus-power|Erebus Power]] ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]). It functions as both an exhaustive knowledge archive and an advanced analytical decision-support advisor.

## Imperial AI Compliance

To comply with stringent Imperial artificial intelligence restrictions, Alexandria is architected with strict operational guardrails:
- Strictly advisory-only with no autonomous implementation authority or direct control over infrastructure or military systems.
- Designed as a model of Imperial AI safety, contrasting with forbidden autonomous machines or rogue synthetic experiments.

## Psionic Pathology Consultation

In early 1105, researchers [[beck|Beck]] and [[asher|Asher]] identified Alexandria as one of the few computational knowledge databases within Sector Solo-6 capable of diagnosing and recommending treatments for acute precursor psionic neurological degradation (Session 12).
