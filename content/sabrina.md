# Sabrina

**Summary**: Popular musical superstar from Kidderminster, headline performer of Duchess Margaret's Cultural Exchange Tour.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview

Sabrina is an acclaimed musical artist and cultural icon originating from the subsector capital of [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] (1226). She is renowned for her extravagant public lifestyle and autobiographical pop songs chronicling her day-to-day exploits ([[news-1105-vol-5|News Dispatch — Volume 5]]).

## Grand Tour Appearances (1105)

- **Csabai**: In 093-1105, Sabrina was announced as a centerpiece act for [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]]'s Subsector Cultural Exchange Tour, scheduling a series of high-profile concerts across [[csabai|Csabai]] (1626) ([[news-1105-vol-5|News Dispatch — Volume 5]]).
- **Arico El Nuevo**: In 129-1105, Sabrina's itinerary expanded to [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]], planning an arrival on 145-1105 for a two-week stay in Freeport, including at least one performance in the elite Torus habitat ([[news-1105-vol-8|News Dispatch — Volume 8]]).
