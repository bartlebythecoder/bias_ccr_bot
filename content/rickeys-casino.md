# Rickeys Casino

**Summary**: Prominent gambling house in the Entertainment district of Startown Liberty frequented by Elin Butler and his associate Marcus.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Establishment

Rickeys Casino is a gaming venue operating within the neon-lit Entertainment district of [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 12). Catering to high-stakes gamblers, off-world merchants, and reckless youths, it provides an active social hub for Startown nightlife (Session 12).

## The Butler Investigation

During inquiries into the abduction of [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]], operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] discovered Elin spent extensive time at Rickeys casino prior to his disappearance (Session 12). On Day 43-1105, operatives [[sarik|Sarik]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] visited the casino to collect Elin's forgotten effects, learning that his teenage companion [[marcus|Marcus]] was actively gambling at the tables (Session 12).
