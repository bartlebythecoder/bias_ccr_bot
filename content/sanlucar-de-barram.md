# Sanlucar de Barram

**Summary**: System 1022 in Solo Sector Subsector J, an arid gas-giant moon with underground and orbital cities, vital for pressure crystal harvesting and defined by decades of revolutionary conflict.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Physical Geography and Demographics

Sanlucar de Barram (1022) carries the UWP profile B200676-A ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]). The primary world is an airless, arid moon orbiting the gas giant Nazca along the Spinward Circuit. Despite lacking an atmosphere, it supports approximately 4 million residents housed across extensive subterranean dome complexes and pressurized orbital cities.

Economically, Sanlucar is an extraction hub specializing in hydrogen gas skimming from Nazca and the mining of **pressure crystals**, a critical component for contra-grav drives and gravity-manipulation machinery ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).

## The 1089–1090 Civil War

Sanlucar suffered extensive devastation during the [[sanlucar-civil-war|Sanlucar Civil War]] (1089–1090), which erupted between the dominant [[valentian-republic|Valentian Republic]] and the fiercely independent Khanate of Aridush ([[sanlucar-civil-war|Sanlucar Civil War]]):
- Combat was constrained to structure-to-structure battles in near-vacuum conditions, yet dome breaches resulted in mass civilian casualties and ruined agricultural biodomes ([[sanlucar-civil-war|Sanlucar Civil War]]).
- Covert Imperial Marine intervention on the side of Valentia broke the Khanate, leading to its total abolition and the execution of its senior leadership, leaving deep humanitarian scars and unresolved trauma ([[sanlucar-civil-war|Sanlucar Civil War]]).

## The 1105 Revolution & Commonwealth Settlement

In early 1105, mounting working-class resentment over resource distribution and the Valentian government's adoption of the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] movement ignited a popular revolution:
- The [[aridush-freedom-fighters|Aridush Freedom Fighters]], commanded by civil war survivor [[giuliana-weber|Giuliana Weber]], executed a coordinated guerrilla campaign that forced the complete military surrender of the Valentian Republic ([[news-1105-vol-1|News Dispatch — Volume 1]]).
- Following high-stakes diplomatic talks with [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]], Weber secured formal recognition for the newly proclaimed [[commonwealth-states-of-sanlucar|Commonwealth States of Sanlucar]] in exchange for dropping war crimes prosecutions against Imperial officers and granting amnesty to Psalmist detainees ([[news-1105-vol-3|News Dispatch — Volume 3]]).

## Reconstruction and Economic Boom

While the Imperial Navy restored Sanlucar's travel advisory to Green, the moon faced a profound reconstruction crisis:
- Severe shortages of contra-grav lifters, atmospheric processors, and modular housing delayed refugee resettlement throughout mid-1105 ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).
- Concurrently, political stabilization triggered an aggressive speculative boom in pressure crystal futures on the [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] Stock Exchange ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).
