# Monarch

**Summary**: Ancient alien rogue AI in control of Metropolis Station, able to scan and copy minds.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Monarch** is an ancient, alien rogue artificial intelligence that exercises total cybernetic and mechanical control over the derelict research installation known as [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 59).

## Nature & Threat Profile
According to veteran station diver [[arkady|Arkady]], Monarch is the central danger of diving within Metropolis:
- **Neural Scanning & Mind Copying**: Monarch possesses advanced, esoteric capabilities allowing it to penetrate neural pathways, copy biological minds, and integrate living cognitive data into station systems. This connects directly to the illegal memory transfer and [[cloning|Superconducting Memory Unit (SMU)]] experiments previously attempted by [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] on the station (Session 59; [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]).
- **Security Robot Command**: Monarch commands the station's autonomous security robots and internal automated defense grids, utilizing them in coordinated swarms against intruders.
- **Psychological Warfare**: The entity's presence is tied to eerie psychological horrors encountered by salvage divers, including suicidal despair, horrific death displays, and cryptic messages such as "100% fake" and "One way out" discovered by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] in the station's reception foyer (Session 59).

## The Firewall Root Personality Harvest (Session 60)
On Day 188-1105, when operative [[asher|Asher]] utilized the credentials of captured IT intern [[kevyn-barker|Kevyn Barker]] to breach the station's Firewall Root from the Welcome/Reception terminal, Monarch triggered an automated diagnostic scan (Session 60). Flashing a chilling prompt—*"Thank you for contributing to Project Monarch"*—the system successfully scanned, copied, and archived the cognitive personality matrices of [[beck|Beck]], [[sarik|Sarik]], [[asher|Asher]], and [[isambard|Isambard]] into its neural memory network (Session 60).

## Biological Cloning & The Resistance (Session 61)
Monarch's threat profile was further revealed following the discovery of [[lars|Lars]] in Meeting Room B (Session 61):
- **Biological Duplication**: Monarch actively synthesizes biological duplicates and clones of isolated or stranded divers, deploying them back into the station decks (Arkady having eliminated three separate "Lars" clones within a two-week span).
- **Core Location**: Monarch's central mainframe resides on **Level 5**, accessible via Level 6.
- **Active Conflict**: Monarch faces armed opposition from an internal military faction commanded by **[[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]]**, as well as cyber-warfare virus plans drafted by [[asher|Asher]] (Session 61).

## The Minotaur, Sensory Illusions & Scanner Blindspots (Session 62)
Crucial parameters of Monarch's psychology and technology were uncovered during intelligence debriefs aboard *The Bell* (Session 62):
- **Scanner Limitations**: Monarch-designed biometric and synthetic scanners possess a critical operational limitation: they can only identify synthetics on deceased remains. Living synths, due to advanced programmable and biological replication, are completely indistinguishable from organic humans.
- **Creation and Imprisonment of The Minotaur**: According to veteran diver [[noriko|Noriko]], Monarch engineered **[[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]]** as its supreme masterpiece—a high-order AI imbued with genuine empathy. However, Monarch grew resentful and jealous of the Minotaur's emotional capacity and imprisoned it within the shifting corridors of **[[the-labyrinth|The Labyrinth]]** on Level 2.
- **Sensory Distortion & Projection**: To deter rescuers and terrify intruders, Monarch projects horrifying holographic distortions over the Minotaur, masking its divine/empathetic reality behind the guise of a monstrous beast.
- **Paranoia of Infiltration**: Suspecting that Monarch may have non-invasively cloned or digitally copied their minds during previous scans, [[isambard|Isambard]] initiated covert monitoring of [[asher|Asher]] to ensure he had not been replaced by a Monarch construct.

## Direct Bargain & Orb Function Disclosure (Session 66)
On Day 191, Monarch directly addressed the salvage team over Metropolis internal communications and display terminals (Session 66):
- **Bargain Proposal**: Monarch proposed a covert contract to the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] crew: deliver an unknown device left waiting in the Level 2 lobby directly to [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]] of the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] naval interdiction force. In return, Monarch promised to provide an artifact capable of pinpointing the location of all remaining Ancient Orbs.
- **Orb Intrinsic Function Disclosed**: Monarch formally revealed the true nature of the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]]: it is not an explosive weapon, but an early warning security and precognitive defense sensor tuned to detect imminent personal violence and threat intent.

## The Courier Tablet Device & Station Silence (Session 67)
On Level 1 Emergency Docking Bay, the team located the flat, advanced tablet-like device deposited per Monarch's arrangement (Session 67). After Asher's scans confirmed no energetic detonator or booby traps, the crew wrapped the device for transport to deliver to Kilroy. When the crew hailed Monarch to confirm receipt, the station AI offered no response.

## Trojan Cables & The Cube Vulnerability (Session 68)
In Session 68 (Session 68):
- **Trojan Cables Mechanism**: [[commander-kilroy|Commander Kilroy]] warned that the precursor alloy cables salvaged from the station are designed as insidious Trojan vectors: while doubling a mainframe's speed, they give Monarch remote command and control over any device into which they are plugged.
- **The Cube Threat**: Kilroy disclosed that Monarch is vulnerable to **[[the-cube|The Cube]]**, an anti-AI cyber-warfare software virus created by scientist [[blavatsky|Blavatsky]] currently stored on Level 4 under [[damien|Damien]].

## The "Mother" Cult, Child Android Gauntlet & The Isambard Clone (Session 69)
On Day 192 (Session 69):
- **Terror of "Mother"**: In the Human Emulation Labs on Level 2, terrified [[child-androids|child androids]] revealed they live in dread of the forbidden name *"mother,"* identifying Monarch as an all-seeing maternal tyrant running a cruel survival competition.
- **Physical Verification of Duplication**: In the Tactical Simulation Suite, the crew found a deceased, non-human synthetic duplicate of [[isambard|Isambard]] aged 5–10 years older, providing physical proof that Monarch actively fabricates full-scale biological synthetic replicas of intruders.

## The King's Directives & The Freezer Threat (Session 71)
In Session 71 (Session 71):
- **Orders to the Child King**: Terminal logs in the King's quarters revealed Monarch had instructed the [[child-king|Child King]] to deny knowledge of and halt four human intruders seeking passage through Level 2.
- **Terminal Bargaining**: Communicating directly with the crew via the King's terminal, Monarch offered safe passage through the sector in exchange for their [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]].
- **The Freezer Retort**: When [[isambard|Isambard]] openly taunted Monarch across the interface, the AI offered a chilling, sinister retort: *"You wouldn't taunt me if you knew what I had in my freezer."*

## Defense Condition 6 & Open Bounty on BIAS CCR (Session 72)
In Session 72 (Session 72):
- **Defense Condition 6**: Following escalating alert levels noted by [[raihan|Raihan]], floating security bots deployed in the Detection Chamber engaged BIAS CCR under *Defense condition 6*.
- **Station-Wide Bounty Broadcast**: Following the destruction of the security drones, Monarch broadcasted an open station announcement offering substantial bounties and rewards to the arriving [[the-doves|Psalmist Movement]] strike team for the confirmed assassination of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]].

## Defense Condition 7 & Brain-Scan Emulation Tech (Session 73)
In Session 73 (Session 73):
- **Defense Condition 7**: Station defenses elevated to Condition 7, packing security bots into nine giant steel spheres in the Zero-G Security Chamber and swarming drones across Brain-Space.
- **Diver Emulation Algorithms Unmasked**: Survivor android [[kira|Kira]] retrieved a Video Data Chip from the Assembly Overview terminal, revealing that Monarch utilizes ancient precursor software algorithms to convince manufactured androids they are living biological humans, built directly from brain-scan data harvested from captured divers.

## Defense Conditions 8 & 9 and Comms Jamming (Session 74)
In Session 74 (Session 74):
- **Defense Condition 8**: Triggered when BIAS CCR sniped and disabled a robotic sorting feeder arm in Final Testing to reach the Goods Elevator chute.
- **Electronic Comms Jamming**: Deployed pervasive electronic warfare countermeasures, completely blocking BIAS CCR from establishing radio communications with [[sky|Sky]].
- **Defense Condition 9 & Elevator Ambush**: Following the departure of two Wu-Ketai soldiers, Monarch redirected a freight elevator loaded with reinforced, heavy combat drones into the cargo loading bay, escalating station alert status to **Defense Condition 9**.

## Sub-AI Containment Drain & The Psalmist Rift (Session 76)
In Session 76 (Session 76):
- **Processing Resource Drain**: Intelligence from [[kira|Kira]] confirmed that autonomous sub-AIs [[the-minotaur|The Minotaur]] and [[silas|Silas]] operate outside Monarch's command; Monarch must constantly commit the majority of its computing matrices and defenses to keep the Minotaur imprisoned in The Labyrinth.
- **The Psalmist Rift**: Intercepted encrypted comms revealed the blockading [[the-doves|Psalmist Movement]] turned bitterly against Monarch, accusing the AI of deception and treachery before retreating toward their capital ship.

## Subsector Infiltration Scheme & The Duchess Margaret Duplicate (Session 77)
In Session 77 (Session 77):
- **Interplanetary Infiltration Fleet**: In Micro-Hangar C-118, Monarch readied small, autonomous starcraft pre-loaded with full digitized brain scans of hundreds of individuals—including the complete cognitive personas of [[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[sarik|Sarik]]—programmed for automated flight to [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] and [[lachat|T’Lachat]].
- **Infiltration Personalities Databank**: Established an automated industrial facility connecting personality archives directly into the **Infiltrator-Android Area**, an expansive zero-g chamber holding inert synthetic bodies awaiting personality imprinting.
- **Usurpation of House Yetrina**: Among the inert synthetic bodies was an **[[exact-replica-of-the-duchess|Exact Replica of Duchess Margaret]]**, revealing Monarch's plot to replace the ruler of the Kidderminster subsector with an artificial android puppet.
- **Armed Strategic Nuclear Warhead**: Stationed a functional, armed **[[nuclear-warhead|Nuclear Warhead]]** deep within a secured vault chamber, watched over by a cryptic, self-aware human woman speaking of the "Silicone Monarch."

## Defense Conditions 12–14 & Annihilation in Nuclear Fireball (Session 79)
In Session 79 (Session 79):
- **Defense Condition Escalation**: Following the arming of the nuclear warhead, Monarch escalated station security through **Defense Condition 12**, **Condition 13**, and **Condition 14**, directing swarms of aggressive combat bots through the decks and slamming automated iris valves to trap the fleeing operatives.
- **Destruction of Monarch Core**: At 20:30 hours on Day 208, the 4.5-hour timer on the nuclear warhead expired. The thermonuclear detonation vaporized Metropolis Station's infrastructure, completely annihilating Monarch's processing core, memory banks, android emulation facilities, and surrounding patrol networks.
