# Andre

**Summary**: Bouncer at Club Strata on Freeport who unknowingly carried Hunter's Heroes' surveillance bug after Beck slipped it into his pocket.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

## Overview
**Andre** is a bouncer employed at [[club-strata|Club Strata]], a popular nightclub located in The Lux within the Humboldt District on [[freeport|Freeport]] (Session 45).

---

## Operational History
On Day 130-1105, during [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]'s investigation into the assassination of [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]], operatives [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] interviewed Andre at the entrance of Club Strata. Andre provided them with insights into the local gambling scene and district movements (Session 45).

Later that evening, when [[beck|Beck]] discovered that operatives from rival mercenary squad [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] had slipped an electronic surveillance bug onto her person, she subtly reverse-pickpocketed the tracker into Andre's pocket, misdirecting Hunter's Heroes' surveillance assets (Session 45).
