# Cooper

**Summary**: Covert espionage patron who recruited the crew to infiltrate [[kc-station|K.C. Station]] on [[mirassol|Mirassol]] and retrieve sensor telemetry from a crashed battle cruiser.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Mission Contract

Cooper operates as a secretive corporate or independent handler, maintaining flyer pilot [[sky|Sky]] as his primary local liaison and asset on [[mirassol|Mirassol]] (Session 6). In 1105, Cooper hired four down-on-their-luck operatives—[[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[sarik|Sarik]]—to infiltrate [[kc-station|K.C. Station]], a corporate research post located on the frozen moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]] in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system (Sessions 1–5).

Cooper's designated objective was for the crew to covertly infiltrate the wreck of an ancient downed battle cruiser and secure sensitive telemetry, diagnostic measurements, and historical sensor logs (Sessions 1–5).

## Evacuation Order

Following the team's successful retrieval and transmission of the battle cruiser data, Cooper dispatched an urgent priority transmission instructing the team to immediately abandon the post on the next scheduled outbound shuttle, warning that corporate investigators were incoming to initiate an audit (Sessions 1–5).

## Aristocratic Network on Romentino

Unknown to the crew during their initial employment, Cooper maintains personal ties to the high nobility of [[romentino|Romentino]], having specifically requested that [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]] maintain surveillance and offer discreet protection for operative [[beck|Beck]] during her stay in Startown Liberty (Session 11).
