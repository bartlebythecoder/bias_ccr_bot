# Hampton

**Summary**: Town on [[tutin|Tutin]] near the orbital elevator, serving as the entry and exit point for all workers on the planet.

**Last updated**: 2026-10-21

---

## Location & Purpose

Hampton is a town on [[tutin|Tutin]], the breathable world in the subsector (1624) administered as a restricted nature reserve by the [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] government. It is located near the planet's orbital elevator.

## Gateway for Workers

Hampton serves as the entry and exit location for all workers on Tutin. Every worker arriving on or departing from the planet passes through the town.

The town sits at the ground terminus of Tutin's orbital elevator, which links it to the Class C orbital starport. The descent takes about 90 minutes, and each car carries fewer than two dozen people.

## Subsector Navy Presence

The town hosts a Subsector Navy contingent commanded by marine colonel [[colonel-marshall-moses-clark|Colonel Marshall Moses Clark]].

## Administration

Hampton and the surrounding area are administered by colony administrator [[governor-stieg-tanaka|Governor Stieg Tanaka]].
