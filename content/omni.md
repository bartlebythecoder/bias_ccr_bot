# Omni

**Summary**: Freeport shell company used by the Zero-G Vipers to launder narcotics money, with Starka Pramerski as its only employee.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Omni** is a corporate front and shell entity registered on [[freeport|Freeport]], utilized by the [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]] street gang to launder illicit narcotics revenue and syndicate earnings (Session 52).

## Background & Operations

* **Corporate Front**: Decrypted accounting files recovered from the laptop seized at [[cafe-dimitri|Cafe Dimitri]] exposed Omni as a legitimate-appearing corporate conduit for Vipers street cash (Session 52).
* **Personnel**: Official corporate filings identified [[starka-pramerski|Starka Pramerski]] as Omni's sole registered employee, leading both [[hunters-heroes|Hunter's Heroes]] and [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] to track down his residence in search of deeper financial and gang intelligence (Session 52).
