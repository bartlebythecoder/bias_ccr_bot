# Sterling

**Summary**: Arrogant and patronizing head butler of Csabai Palace who managed domestic staff and summarily dismissed security chief Morgan following the Beaugard theft.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Position & Conduct

Mr. Sterling is the senior majordomo and head butler of [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]], overseeing domestic operations, staff administration, and ceremonial decorum for [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]] (Sessions 22–23). Renowned for an arrogant, haughty, and patronizing disposition, Sterling exercises broad administrative authority across household personnel (Sessions 22–23).

## Role in the Theft Crisis

On Day 57-1105, following the disappearance of the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] from the palace gallery, Sterling reacted with punitive haste, summarily firing the palace head of security, [[morgan|Morgan]] (Sessions 22–23). Sterling was subsequently interviewed by [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] as an initial suspect during their internal audit of the palace staff (Sessions 22–23).

---

## Family Ties to Guillaume Sterling

During the violent raid on the [[csabai-psalmist-center|Csabai Psalmist Center]] on Day 93-1105, the rogue Psalmist operative who hired the [[irklan|Irklan]] assassin was unmasked as [[guillaume-sterling|Guillaume Sterling]], pointing to deeper, illicit connections between the palace butler's family circle and [[the-doves|The Doves]]' network on Csabai (Session 28).
