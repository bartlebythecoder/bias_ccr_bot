# Saltpeter

**Summary**: Salvage starship whose diving crew worked inside [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] and left [[lars|Lars]] behind.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The ***Saltpeter*** is an independent salvage starship whose diving crew operated within the restricted perimeter of [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 61).

## Station Operations & Abandonment
According to the tricorn-wearing diver [[lars|Lars]], the *Saltpeter* delivered a salvage team into Project Metropolis before abandoning Lars aboard the station approximately one week prior to the arrival of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] (Session 61).
