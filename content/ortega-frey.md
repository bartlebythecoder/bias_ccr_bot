# Ortega Frey

**Summary**: Owner of the Stardust Casino on Freeport who secretly funded Winslow Leach's research into Project Metropolis.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Ortega Frey** is the proprietor and owner of the [[stardust-casino|Stardust Casino]], an aging gaming establishment located in North Lux on [[freeport|Freeport]] (Session 50).

## Activities & Role

* **Assassination Contract Allegations**: On Day 132-1105, rogue telepath [[julie|Julie]] sent an encrypted message to [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] claiming that the owner of the Stardust Casino had attempted to hire her to assassinate independent narcotics dealer [[conde-borbon|Conde Borbon]] (Session 50).
* **Interview with BIAS CCR**: [[asher|Asher]] identified Frey as the casino owner, noting her background held no obvious criminal affiliations. When [[beck|Beck]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] visited the Stardust Casino and met with Frey in her conference room alongside security chief [[vince|Vince]], Frey acknowledged having been requested to arrange a meeting with Conde Borbon in the past, but asserted that she had no ongoing interest or involvement in his whereabouts (Session 50).
* **Physical Surveillance (Day 132-1105)**: Later that afternoon, [[sky|Sky]] conducted mobile surveillance on Frey, discovering she moved across North Lux in a small personal grav car with a single bodyguard, making solo retail visits while the guard waited outside (Session 51).
* **Funding Project Metropolis Research (Day 132-1105)**: Cloned databanks from researcher [[winslow-leach|Winslow Leach]] revealed that Frey was secretly bankrolling his university research team. Leach had recently contacted Frey's private line and transmitted the precise coordinates of the Ancient precursor archaeological site upon which the classified [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] was built, exposing Frey's deep interest in lost precursor artifacts (Session 53).
* **Stardust Charity Gala (Day 133-1105)**: Frey hosted and attended the high-society fourth-floor charity gala at the Stardust Casino alongside [[orslo-jollynn|Marie Orslo Jollynn]], coordinating in the secured VIP area while guarded by planetary police officers (Session 55).
