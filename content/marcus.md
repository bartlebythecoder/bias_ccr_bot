# Marcus

**Summary**: Teenage gambler at Rickeys casino in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] and social companion of kidnapping victim [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Association with Elin Butler

Marcus is a young resident of [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] who belonged to the close social circle of [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]] (Session 12). A frequent patron of [[rickeys-casino|Rickeys Casino]] in the Entertainment district, Marcus was identified by casino personnel as actively playing at the gaming tables on Day 43-1105 when [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] operatives arrived investigating Elin's kidnapping (Session 12).
