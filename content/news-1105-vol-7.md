# News Dispatch — Volume 7

**Summary**: Dispatch covering day 113-1105 on [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]], reporting the targeted assassination of [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]], a 24-year-old graphic designer for [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai Corporation]], outside a club in The Lux within the Humboldt District.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Dispatches and Timeline

### 113-1105: Murder in The Lux (Humboldt District)
- **Location**: [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] (1324)
- **Actors**: [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]], [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]]
- A 24-year-old graphic designer employed by [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]], Louise Soto, was murdered by an unidentified masked gunman while waiting in line outside a popular nightclub at "The Lux" in the Humboldt District.
- The shooting caused mass panic in the premier entertainment zone. The perpetrator escaped and remains at large.
- Graphic footage of the assassination was captured by bystanders and widely circulated across planetary social networks.
- Occurred on the exact same date as the anti-telepath lynching of Dario Caminante in the neighboring Freeport Cairo district, accentuating a sharp rise in violent crimes across Arico El Nuevo ([[news-1105-vol-6|News Dispatch — Volume 6]]).
