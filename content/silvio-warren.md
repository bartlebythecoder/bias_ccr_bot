# Silvio Warren

**Summary**: Honest, cantankerous security chief at the [[foust-refinery|Foust Refinery]] who held the south perimeter during the heist.

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

Silvio Warren is the cantankerous, honest chief of security at the [[foust-refinery|Foust Refinery]] on [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] (Sessions 31–32).

## Defense of the Southern Perimeter
During the inside assault on Day 96, Warren held the perimeter south of the warehouse against an armed diversion of rioting prisoners. Reinforced by [[isambard|Isambard]] and his stockpile of grenades, Warren's team contained the assault and forced the prisoners to capitulate (Sessions 31–32).
