# Ruffian

**Summary**: Warrens laborer on [[suliman|Suliman]] who picked a fight with [[sarik|Sarik]].

**Last updated**: 2026-11-09

---

**Ruffian** is a pugnacious resident and laborer in [[the-warrens|The Warrens]] on the asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 34).

## Confrontation with BIAS CCR
Following [[asher|Asher]]'s cyber-penetration of the Wu-Ketai corporate intranet and the group's inquiries regarding working conditions and the canceled care center, Ruffian aggressively confronted the undercover team. Accusing them of inciting trouble and snooping into local affairs, Ruffian escalated the encounter into physical violence by throwing a punch at [[sarik|Sarik]] (Session 34).
