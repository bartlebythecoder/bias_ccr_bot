# Nuclear Warhead

**Summary**: Strategic nuclear device in a vault deep inside Metropolis Station that BIAS CCR armed to destroy the station and Monarch.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The **Nuclear Warhead** is a massive strategic nuclear device discovered inside a reinforced vault chamber within the deep infrastructure of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] (Sessions 76–77).

## Discovery & Reconnaissance

First identified on recovered station schematics by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] during their Loading Dock refit in Session 76, the warhead was initially proposed by [[isambard|Isambard]] as a catastrophic failsafe option to obliterate the rogue AI [[monarch|Monarch]].

In Session 77, after traversing Micro-Hangar C-118, the Infiltrator-Android Area, the Seminar Room, and the Inspection Booth, the team used laser cutting gear to slice through the final reinforced blast door. Inside the vault room, they found the colossal nuclear weapon standing in the center.

## The Woman on the Bomb & Station Ghost (Sessions 77–78)

In Session 77, seated directly atop the nuclear warhead, the squad discovered an unidentified woman murmuring poetic verse in a state of existential detachment:
> *"A king is crowned and I am but a jester. In the court of the Silicone Monarch, a mere fleeting thing, dead and living, a wasted chance."*

In Session 78 (Session 78):
- **Holographic Ghost of Wu-Ketai Crew Member**: The woman was revealed to be an agitated holographic station ghost—a corrupted psychic memory loop of a deceased [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] crew member demanding immediate station destruction. Bullets passed harmlessly through her form, though EMP grenades temporarily disrupted her projection.
- **Arming the Warhead**: After Beck calmed the entity by pledging to detonate the bomb, the ghost provided access protocols. Beck manually engaged the arming sequence on the terminal, triggering sirens and station-wide alarms.
- **The 4.5-Hour Countdown Override**: Demanding instant detonation, the ghost launched traumatic psychic memory assaults against Asher and Sarik. While Isambard pacified the ghost with EMPs and Beck's paperback book (passed off as the ghost's lost **Diary** from Disassembly), Asher hacked and modified the detonation routines under bot fire, successfully programming a **four-and-a-half-hour (4.5-hour) delay**.
