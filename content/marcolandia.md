# Marcolandia

**Summary**: Small airless icy rock moon 1425 orbiting gas giant Vilnohirsk, housing 700,000 residents in subterranean cities, balkanized into Ouro and Paraty states, extracting super-metals.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Physical Environment & Demographics

Marcolandia (1425) carries the UWP designation C2000577-A ([[news-1105-vol-5.1|News Dispatch — Volume 5.1]]). The primary world is a small, airless, frozen rock moon orbiting the massive gas giant Vilnohirsk:
- **Harsh Surface**: Characterized by extreme cold, low surface gravity, and complete vacuum.
- **Subterranean Society**: All **700,000** residents live in pressurized subterranean cities, deep tunnel networks, and rotating orbital habitats.

## Economy
- **Super-Metals**: The economy centres on extracting and refining valuable atmospheric "super metals" from the gas giant Vilnohirsk.
- **Upper Circuit Fuel Stop**: Marcolandia serves as a vital refuelling stop along the Upper Circuit trade route.

## Political Balkanization & Megacorporate Hegemony

- **Sovereign States**: Divided into two main cooperative political entities, **Ouro** and **Paraty**, alongside numerous independent industrial company factory towns.
- **Corporate Dominance**: Megacorporation [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]] is the dominant financial and commercial power, though fiercely contested by [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] ([[news-1105-vol-9|News Dispatch — Volume 9]]).
- **Penal Workforce**: Refining firm [[youst-refining|Youst Refining]] signed contracts with the [[ouro-correction-department|Ouro Correction Department]] to employ convict labor in heavy super-metal processing ([[news-1105-vol-5.1|News Dispatch — Volume 5.1]]).

## Heists and Security Crises

- **The Ruby Refinery Heist (1104)**: Armed mercenaries raided Rainford Ruby's warehouse, stealing millions in super-metal crates ([[ruby-refinery-heist|Ruby Refinery Heist]]).
- **Foust Refinery Attack**: A foiled armed raid and poisoning of the owner at the Foust Refinery saw intervention by the BIAS CCR team.
