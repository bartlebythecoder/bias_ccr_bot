# GaS Town

**Summary**: Industrial and vice district of Startown Liberty on Romentino, known for illicit transactions, public holoscreens, and proximity to older starport docking sectors.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Overview & Geography

GaS Town is a prominent industrial and commercial district situated within [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on the corrosive frontier world of [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 10). Located adjacent to older, decommissioned starport docking bays and disused transport sectors, GaS Town serves as a major hub for Startown's working-class population, illicit underworld commerce, and commercial dives (Session 19).

Notable establishments within GaS Town include the [[blue-parrot|Blue Parrot]], an underworld drinking establishment where thief [[harry-proud|Harry Proud]] sought refuge after stealing the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] (Session 10).

## Operational Significance to BIAS CCR

- **The Stolen Orb Tracking**: On Day 38-1105, following the theft of the Ancient Orb, [[sarik|Sarik]], [[beck|Beck]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[asher|Asher]] tracked Harry Proud through GaS Town with the aid of street urchin [[sydney|Sydney]] and local Starlight distributor [[lisa|Lisa]] (Session 10).
- **The Espionage Broadcast**: On Day 47-1105, after returning from their covert assault on [[morella-station|Morella Station]], the operatives returned to Startown Liberty via an old port section outside GaS Town (Session 19). Walking through GaS Town alongside [[lord-baltimore|Lord Baltimore]], the team discovered that [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] had pressured [[the-banks|The Banks]] into plastering their faces across every public holoscreen in the district, branding them as dangerous corporate espionage fugitives (Session 19). This public broadcast forced BIAS CCR into the subterranean [[the-backways|The Backways]] to reach Baltimore's yacht (Session 19).
