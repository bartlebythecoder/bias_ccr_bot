# The Warrens

**Summary**: Wu-Ketai-controlled tunnel network where most of Suliman's residents live and work under heavy surveillance.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**The Warrens** is a vast, subterranean residential and industrial tunnel network on the asteroid settlement of [[suliman|Suliman]] in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system (Session 34).

## Demographics & Living Conditions
The majority of Suliman's 70,000 residents reside in the Warrens. Living conditions are cramped and austere, with limited amenities and heavy air recyclers. The workforce consists of both human laborers and articulate uplifted simians who perform varied mechanical and industrial duties (Session 34).

## Surveillance & Corporate Control
The entire Warrens complex is owned and heavily controlled by the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation. Residents and visitors are subjected to ubiquitous electronic tracking systems, and corporate security officers patrol key nodes to suppress unrest. The district is organized into labor units led by figures like union organizer [[carl-parker|Carl Parker]], who frequents the local tavern, [[the-rusty-bolt|The Rusty Bolt]] (Session 34).
