# Rose and Crown

**Summary**: Tavern in Startown Liberty on Romentino used as the ransom drop location for Elin Butler and linked to a missing child disappearance.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Establishment & Underworld Nexus

The Rose and Crown is a public house and tavern situated in [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 13). Patrolled by local workers, spacers, and syndicate couriers, the establishment serves as an informal clearinghouse for clandestine meetings and underworld transactions (Session 13).

## Investigative Connections

On Day 43-1105, operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] visited the Rose and Crown, uncovering two significant investigative threads (Session 13):
- The pub served as the physical transfer point where the initial ransom payment for [[elin-butler|Elin Butler]] was delivered to extortion couriers (Session 13).
- Patron reports indicated the establishment was also the last confirmed location where an unidentified missing child had been seen prior to vanishing, suggesting an ongoing predator or abduction pattern in the area (Session 13).
