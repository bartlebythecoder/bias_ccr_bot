# Ishmael

**Summary**: Unknown figure inside [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] who left a dead-drop of strange cables and a signed note behind a bathroom mirror.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Ishmael** is an unidentified, clandestine individual or operative active within **[[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]]**.

*(Session 63)*

---

## The Bathroom Mirror Dead-Drop
In Session 63 (Day 189-1105), while the **[[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]]** crew was investigating the vandalized Executive Bathroom in the Level 2 Lobby of Metropolis Station, **[[sarik|Sarik]]** discovered a loose screw behind a graffiti-inscribed mirror (*"the Minotaur extends beyond the Minotaur"*). 

Behind the mirror was a concealed dead-drop containing:
1. Coiled, disconnected cables assessed by **[[asher|Asher]]** as possessing unusual properties linked to ancient/precursor technology.
2. A handwritten note reading:  
   > *"Ishmael sends their regards."*

---

## Identity & The Bonsai Memorial (Session 64)
In Session 64 (Session 64), the crew questioned veteran diver **[[noriko|Noriko]]** aboard *The Bell* regarding Ishmael:
- Noriko revealed that Ishmael was an experienced expedition diver who vanished into the depths of Metropolis long ago and never returned.
- In his memory and honor, Noriko maintains and cultivates hundreds of delicate memorial bonsai trees aboard *The Bell*.
- Whether Ishmael survived in secret among the station droids or his legacy was adopted by an anti-Monarch insurgent cell remains an active mystery.
