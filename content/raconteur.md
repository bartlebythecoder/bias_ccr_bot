# Raconteur

**Summary**: Imperial Scout Service vessel on loan to [[isambard|Isambard]], used as BIAS CCR's main transport.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The *Raconteur* is an Imperial Scout Service vessel on long-term loan to [[isambard|Isambard]] as a benefit of his previous service. Following their operations on [[csabai|Csabai]], the crew of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] adopted the vessel as their primary means of transport across the Spinward Marches (Session 30).

## Operational Characteristics & Loan Conditions
- **Scout Telemetry Tether**: The ship contains hardened, tamper-proof transponders that log and transmit telemetry and sensor data directly back to the Scout Service whenever in communication range.
- **Reserve Service Call-Up**: Under the terms of the vessel loan, Isambard and the *Raconteur* remain on inactive reserve and can be mobilized for temporary scout or recon assignments at any time.
- **Flight Dynamics**: The ship possesses an unusual handling configuration that required initial adjustment from pilot [[sky|Sky]].

## Operational History
- **Voyage to Marcolandia**: Transported the crew and passengers [[jean-luc-cartier|Jean Luc Cartier]] and [[jocelyn-gauthier|Jocelyn Gauthier]] from [[csabai|Csabai]] to [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] on Day 94, Year 1105 (Session 30).

- **Jump to Arico El Nuevo**: Following the completion of the security contract at the [[foust-refinery|Foust Refinery]], the ship departed [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]] bound for [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] (Sessions 31–32).

- **Encounter with Mystery Vessel**: Leaving [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]], the ship was intercepted by an untransponding 1,000-ton ghost ship emitting psionic pulses before jumping to [[freeport|Freeport]] at [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] (Session 33).
- **Turret & Laser Drill Refit (Session 58)**: While docked at [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]], the crew installed a double turret armed with an industrial laser drill to cut entry points into unmonitored hull plates during boarding actions (Session 58).
- **Wu-Ketai Fighter Interception & Docking at The Bell (Session 59)**: On Day 188-1105, as the *Raconteur* approached [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]], [[beck|Beck]] disabled the ship's transponder. A [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] fighter-class patrol craft engaged the vessel with pulse laser fire; however, pilots [[sky|Sky]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] held course, and the attacker broke off upon nearing the station perimeter. The *Raconteur* successfully docked with ***[[the-bell|The Bell]]*** (Session 59).
