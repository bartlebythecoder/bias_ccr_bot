# Henry

**Summary**: Holographic "ghost" of a Wu-Ketai maintenance technician encountered on Level 2 of Metropolis Station.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Henry** was a human maintenance technician stationed aboard [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]] during the late era of [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] research operations who manifested as a station "ghost".

## Encounter on Level 2 (Eden)
- **Holographic Loop**: On Day 191, the [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] crew encountered Henry in the Level 2 Executive Lounge. He appeared as a looping, distressed holographic apparition kneeling on the floor, attempting to repair a broken door while lamenting broken station rules and ordering the operatives back to reception.
- **Orb Reaction & Memory Discharge**: When [[asher|Asher]]'s [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]] pulsed with energy in his vicinity, Henry shrieked and discharged lightning-like filaments of electrical/psychic energy from his eyes into the eyes of [[sarik|Sarik]].
- **Eden Intel**: The surge overwhelmed Sarik with Henry's living memories, unmasking that Metropolis Level 2 was formally designated *Eden*, and that Henry had perished during the corporate collapse of late Wu-Ketai operations when the installation was consumed by [[monarch|Monarch]].
- **Suppression**: [[isambard|Isambard]] disrupted the apparition with an EMP grenade, enabling the crew's emergency elevator retreat to Level 1.
