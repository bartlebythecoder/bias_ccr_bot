# Noble Houses

**Summary**: The sixteen aristocratic dynasties entrusted with feudal governance of the Imperium's subsectors, centered on the royal crown of House Alkalikoi and the rival Three Great Houses.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Feudal Governance of the Subsectors

In the Third Imperium, a Noble House is an elite dynastic confederation of patrician families invested by Imperial charter with sovereign hereditary stewardship over an entire subsector. Established formally around year 1011, these houses govern planetary systems, command regional naval assets, and control massive equity portfolios in the thirteen Imperial megacorporations ([[imperial-timeline|Imperial History Timeline]]).

## The Royal Crown and the Three Great Houses

- **House Alkalikoi**: The Imperial Royal House holding the throne of the Imperium. Holds substantial equity across major megacorporations (22% SuSAG, 8% LSP, 8% Hortalez) ([[susag|SuSAG]]; [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]]; [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]]).
- **The Three Great Houses**: While all sixteen subsector houses are equal on paper, two aristocratic houses match the royal family in sheer wealth, fleets, and interstellar clout:
  - **[[house-aledon|House Aledon]]**: Rulers of Pilar subsector, led by Duke Norris, heavily integrated with [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] ([[house-aledon|House Aledon]]).
  - **[[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]**: Rulers of Pleasant Hill subsector, led by [[duke-dulinor|Duke Dulinor]], patrons of [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] and major owners of [[hortalez-et-cie|Hortalez et Cie]] ([[house-ilethian|House Ilethian]]).

## Regional Noble Dynasties

- **[[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]]**: Rulers of the Kidderminster subsector, commanded by [[duchess-margaret|Duchess Margaret]]; allied with [[tukera-lines|Tukera Lines]] ([[house-yetrina|House Yetrina]]).
- **[[house-liashay|House Liashay]]**: Rulers of the Hess subsector, granted their duchy following the [[hessian-revolt|Hessian Revolt]]; led by Duke Shiral Vash Liashav ([[house-liashay|House Liashay]]).
- **[[house-tauribi|House Tauribi]]**: Rulers of the Acigne subsector, led by Duke Ishuggi ([[house-tauribi|House Tauribi]]).
- **Former Houses**: **House Gaval**, stripped of the Hess subsector and outlawed following the failed 11th-Century rebellion ([[hessian-revolt|Hessian Revolt]]).
