# Major Samson

**Summary**: Imperial noble and former military officer residing on Romentino who openly praised the crew for outmaneuvering Ling Standard Products.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Aristocratic Military Veteran

Lord Major Samson is a retired Imperial military officer and peer of the nobility residing in the Noble Quarter of [[startown-liberty|Startown Liberty]] on [[romentino|Romentino]] (Session 11). Known for his forthright demeanor, Samson maintains an independent posture that frequently clashes with corporate dominance (Session 11).

## Interaction with the Crew

At Lord Baltimore's soiree on Day 40-1105, Major Samson met operatives [[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[sarik|Sarik]] (Session 11). Samson openly commended the operatives, remarking how thoroughly they had flummoxed and embarrassed [[ling-standard-products|Ling Standard Products]] during their escape from Mirassol (Session 11).
