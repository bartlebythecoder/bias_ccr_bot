# Kevyn Barker

**Summary**: Apparent Wu-Ketai IT intern found locked in a gym locker on Metropolis Station, who passed a blood test but was later confirmed to be an advanced synthetic.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Kevyn Barker** is a young [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporate IT intern discovered trapped within a locker in the Employee Rec Center on Level 1 of [[metropolis-station|Project Metropolis]] (Session 60).

## Discovery & Interrogation
On Day 188-1105, during their exploration of the derelict station's gymnasium, the operatives of [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] heard banging and searched the rows of lockers, opening one to find Kevyn Barker (Session 60). Kevyn claimed that co-workers had shoved him into the locker as a cruel workplace prank.

## The Temporal Anomaly & Blood Test
When questioned about the station's status and current timeline, Kevyn stated an operating year that diverged sharply from the current Imperial calendar date (1105), suggesting a temporal displacement, synthetic impersonation, or cryogenic stasis:
- **Biological Verification**: Operative [[asher|Asher]] drew a blood sample and conducted a field analysis, confirming Kevyn possessed authentic human biology rather than synthetic or android construction (Session 60).
- **Tactical Detention**: Despite confirming his biological humanity, persistent doubts regarding his psychological stability, potential [[monarch|Monarch]] infiltration, or temporal origin prompted [[sarik|Sarik]] to lock Kevyn back into the locker for his own safety and operational security.
- **Credential Exploitation**: The crew utilized Kevyn's corporate user credentials and passwords to log into the Logistics Hub at the Welcome/Reception terminal, reviewing station docking arms and cargo lifts before triggering the Firewall Root personality scan (Session 60).

## Synthetic Status & Shipboard Containment (Session 62)
During debriefings and scanner analysis in Session 62 (Session 62), the crew verified that Kevyn Barker was indeed an advanced, programmable **synthetic** rather than an organic human, explaining how Monarch scanners fail to distinguish living synths until cellular death. He was extracted from the locker and remained securely locked aboard the *BIAS CCR* starship under observation.

## Smashed Locker Discovery (Session 63)
On Day 189-1105, upon returning to the Level 1 Employee Rec Center to verify the scene, the operatives found that the gym locker where Kevyn had initially been trapped had been violently pried open and forced, with evidence of struggle and no sign of Barker remaining (Session 63).
