# Hector

**Summary**: Wu-Ketai security officer on Suliman and friend of Richard Lundquist who secretly briefed BIAS CCR, then disappeared himself.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**Hector Nilasen** is a corporate security officer employed by the [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] corporation on the mining and research asteroid [[suliman|Suliman]] (Sessions 34–35).

## Friendship with Richard Lundquist
Hector was a trusted personal friend of [[richard-lundquist|Richard Lundquist]], the research technician whose mysterious disappearance sparked an investigation. Unlike many hardened corporate enforcers, Hector maintained genuine regard for Lundquist and was aware of his inquisitive nature (Session 34).

## Meeting with BIAS CCR
Advised by union leader [[carl-parker|Carl Parker]], [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] tracked Hector to [[derry|Derry]], a sushi bar in the Derry sector. Although terrified of corporate reprisals and surveillance, Hector agreed to talk privately after the operatives invoked Parker and Richard's wife, [[joanna-lundquist|Joanna Lundquist]] (Session 34).

### Revelations
- **Ancient Simians**: Hector revealed that Richard had discovered evidence of uplifted simians who were *older than officially recorded*, indicating Wu-Ketai's secret genetic experiments extended back much further than public corporate statements claimed.
- **Security Exoneration**: Hector stated that regular security was not responsible for Richard's disappearance, suggesting that Richard stumbled into something illicit and dangerous within the high-security research sectors.
- To protect Hector from suspicion, the party devised a cover narrative framing their discussion as routine inquiries into worker safety (Session 34).

## Mysterious Disappearance & Artificial Aging
On Day 116-1105, corporate security officer [[anya-suliman|Anya]] interrogated [[asher|Asher]] and [[beck|Beck]], revealing that Hector Nilasen had mysteriously vanished shortly after his interaction with the party (Session 35). Subsequent surveillance hacking by Asher and Beck revealed Hector was last observed entering a packed cantina with extensive camera blind spots, joining Richard Lundquist and Security Chief [[marcus-fortune|Marcus Fortune]] among the missing (Session 35).

On Day 117-1105, when Beck and Asher investigated the Suliman Care Facility following the cardiac arrest of Richard Lundquist, care worker [[elara|Elara]] confirmed that Hector had also been recently admitted as an elderly, senile patient (Session 37). Hector had been subjected to [[chrono-energetic-siphoning|Chrono-Energetic Siphoning]] under [[project-echo-bloom|Project Echo Bloom]], having decades of his biological lifespan forcibly extracted to preserve corporate secrecy and rejuvenate recipients.
