# Lord Julian

**Summary**: Son of Viscount Rittenhouse, who was drugged by his girlfriend [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]] while guarding the Beaugard painting in [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Lineage & Role

Lord Julian is the son of [[viscount-rittenhouse|Viscount Rittenhouse of Csabai]], the planetary ruler of [[csabai|Csabai]], and the brother of [[lady-eleanor-finch|Lady Eleanor Finch]] (Sessions 22–23). Julian struggles under deep feelings of personal inadequacy, acutely aware of his father's chronic disappointment in his capabilities and character (Sessions 22–23).

## The Painting Theft

On Day 57-1105, Julian was assigned the mandatory family vigil to personally watch over the [[beaugard-painting|Beaugard Painting]] on loan from [[kidderminster|Kidderminster]] in the [[csabai-palace|Csabai Palace]] gallery (Sessions 22–23). 

During his watch, Julian's girlfriend, [[vivienne-lacroix|Vivienne LaCroix]], brought him coffee (Sessions 22–23). Unknown to Julian, Vivienne laced the drink with a fast-acting sedative to render him unconscious, stolen the painting, and then administered a stimulant to awaken him (Sessions 22–23). Julian believed he had only blinked or remained awake throughout the entire hour, leaving him terrified and bewildered when the painting was discovered missing (Sessions 22–23). His reputation and innocence were vindicated when [[bias-ccr|BIAS Corporate Conflict Resolution]] exposed Vivienne's plot and recovered the painting (Sessions 22–23).
