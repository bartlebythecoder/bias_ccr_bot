# Freeport

**Summary**: One-million-resident Stanford Torus habitat and capital of the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

Freeport is a massive 20-kilometer Stanford Torus orbital habitat located in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] system, boasting a population of over one million permanent residents (Session 33).

## Structure & Demographics
- **Scale**: A 20 km-diameter rotating Torus habitat in the [[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]] asteroid belt. Its living ring measures 1.4 km wide by 60 km in circumference.
- **Population**: 1,045,200, served by a Class A starport. Freeport is the capital of the Arico system ([[arico-el-nuevo|Arico El Nuevo]]).
- **Ecology**: Artificially seeded with self-sustaining terrestrial wildlife, including insects, fish, birds, and grazing herbivores.
- **Religious Life**: Home to the *Sons and Daughters of the Psalms*, an ascetic monastic branch of the [[psalm-134|Psalm 134]] faith.

## Districts
- **Cairo District**: Urban centre facing unrest following a governor replacement and anti-psionic riots.
- **Humboldt District**: Prosperous civic and financial district housing the Tagarro, Montanes, and [[jollynn-trust|Jollynn]] banks, alongside the Celestial Observatory and Gardens.
- **Jonestown District**: Commercial entertainment and nightlife area effectively ruled by organised crime cartels such as the [[primero-cartel|Primero Cartel]] and [[zero-g-vipers|Zero-G Vipers]].

## Infrastructure & Amenities
Serving as the primary commercial and transport nexus for the system, Freeport features expansive multi-tier environments ranging from bustling industrial docks to luxury residential zones within its Starport District. It maintains advanced medical and psychiatric facilities capable of assessing and treating exotic psionic trauma (Session 33).

## Operational History
On Day 105, Year 1105, the crew of the [[raconteur|Raconteur]] arrived at Freeport following a harrowing psionic encounter near [[marcolandia|Marcolandia]]. On the station, [[sarik|Sarik]] was cured of his psionic illness, [[isambard|Isambard]] received a Cr 700,000 treatment estimate for his severe psionic condition, and the crew was contracted by [[joanna-lundquist|Joanna Lundquist]] to investigate [[suliman|Suliman]] (Session 33).

On Day 130, Year 1105, BIAS CCR returned to Freeport to pursue their MCr 2.0 contract against [[the-stratemeyers|The Stratemeyers]] (Session 45). Meeting with syndicate attorney [[ridpath|Ridpath]] and [[inspector-coker|Inspector Coker]], the operatives investigated the assassination of [[wu-ketai|Wu-Ketai]] designer [[louise-soto|Louise Soto]] in The Lux entertainment district outside [[club-strata|Club Strata]], identifying illegal projectile ballistics and suspecting syndicate involvement behind the purported domestic violence cover of [[david-parker|David Parker]] (Session 45).
