# Child Androids

**Summary**: Juvenile-looking androids bred and stress-tested in the Human Emulation Labs on Level 2 of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]], terrified of "Mother".

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

The **Child Androids** are a population of juvenile-appearing synthetic constructs engineered and maintained within the Human Emulation Labs on Level 2 of [[metropolis-station|Metropolis Station]].

## Characteristics & Social Order
- **Appearance & Condition**: Possessing the physical form of young children, these androids lack individual names, exhibit distinct biological/synthetic exhaustion, and live in constant terror of station systems.
- **The Terror of "Mother"**: The children express intense fear of the forbidden word *"mother,"* understood to be an internal appellation for the rogue AI [[monarch|Monarch]] or its primary cognitive overseer.
- **The Emulation Gauntlet**: In the Human Emulation Labs, dozens of child androids are subjected to grueling cognitive routines, puzzle-solving competitions, and exhausting labor. Those who fail or collapse are subjected to suffering or recycling.
- **Assisting Damien**: Two child androids sheltered under an overturned server rack in the Financial Simulation Suite revealed to [[beck|Beck]] and [[isambard|Isambard]] that a sympathetic ally had recently aided [[damien|Damien]] in escaping toward the freight elevator to Level 4.
