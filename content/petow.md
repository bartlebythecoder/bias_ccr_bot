# PETOW

**Summary**: Fictitious uplift-rights group, People for the Ethical Treatment of Uplifted Workers, used by BIAS CCR as a cover identity.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-30

---

**PETOW** (*People for the Ethical Treatment of Uplifted Workers*) is a fictitious animal and uplift advocacy group utilized by [[bias-ccr|BIAS CCR]] as a cover identity (Session 40).

## Operational Use on Eternity Station
On Day 118-1105, following their escape from [[suliman|Suliman]] with liberated uplifted simians [[isaiah|Isaiah]] and [[thomas|Thomas]], BIAS CCR docked at [[eternity-station|Eternity Station]] (Session 40). When dockmaster [[loshi|Loshi]] expressed suspicion that the team might be corporate spies or militant simian liberationists, the operatives presented credentials identifying themselves as representatives of PETOW, successfully securing a 24-hour entry visa and docking clearance (Session 40).
