# Mirassol Pyramid

**Summary**: Subterranean precursor structure of Ancient origin on the moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]], targeted for artifact theft by [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]].

**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

---

## Discovery

The Mirassol Pyramid is an uncataloged, pre-human architectural structure on the frozen moon [[mirassol|Mirassol]] in the [[romentino|Romentino]] system (Sessions 1–5). The bulk of the structure remains buried deep beneath lunar permafrost and rock, with only the pinnacle projecting above the ground surface (Sessions 1–5). It was located by station workers and subsequently investigated by operatives [[isambard|Isambard]] and [[sarik|Sarik]] (Sessions 1–5).

## Criminal Expedition

Off-world crime syndicate figure [[blue-lou-boyle|Blue Lou Boyle]] discovered the site's existence and leveraged blackmail or coercion against K.C. Station field supervisor [[sophia|Sophia]] and workshop mechanic [[liam|Liam]] to illicitly breach the pyramid and recover [[the-ancients|Ancient]] artifacts (Sessions 1–5).

The unauthorized foray met with sudden disaster:
- [[liam|Liam]] was discovered dead near the excavation site (Sessions 1–5).
- [[sophia|Sophia]] disappeared into the structure or surrounding badlands (Sessions 1–5).
- The four espionage operatives ([[beck|Beck]], [[asher|Asher]], [[isambard|Isambard]], and [[sarik|Sarik]]) were left weighing whether to breach the pyramid to investigate or evacuate as ordered by [[cooper|Cooper]] (Sessions 1–5).

## Architectural Features & Subterranean Anomalies

Exploration by the five-person operative cell circa Day 32-1105 revealed remarkable architectural and physical characteristics (Session 6):
- **Artificial Illumination**: Despite millennia of abandonment, the subterranean halls are brightly lit by functioning ceiling light panels (Session 6).
- **Floating Suspended Floors**: The walkways and chamber floors hover freely without direct connection to the perimeter walls, suspended over a layer of thick vapor or mist concealing unidentified mobile entities beneath (Session 6).
- **Central Shaft**: A large circular rotunda connects deeper vertical levels via an open central shaft (Session 6).
- **Subterranean Fauna**: The complex is inhabited by packs of dog-sized hybrid reptile-rodents, possessing characteristics of both rats and alligators (Session 6). Forensic tracking by [[sarik|Sarik]] at the surface entrance also confirmed that an unknown non-human organism had exited the facility following Sophia's breach (Session 6).

- **Deep Cable Shaft & Flying Predators**: Exploration deeper into the complex revealed a vertical shaft with a hanging overhead cable inhabited by aggressive, miniature dragon-like winged creatures that ambush intruders (Session 7). Lower passageways are also infested with meter-long predatory serpents (Session 7).
- **The Artifact Vault**: Contained the [[ancient-orb|Ancient Orb]], a glowing blue crystalline sphere secured and extracted by the operative team (Session 7). Following the orb's extraction, sensor readings indicated the overall psionic emissions did not travel with the artifact, confirming the primary psionic generator resides permanently within the pyramid facility itself (Session 7).

## Psionic Field & Cognitive Hazard

The pyramid generates a persistent, rhythmic psionic field that acts as a cognitive hazard to living minds (Session 6):
- Operative [[asher|Asher]] tracked the energy using a field-modified Geiger counter (Session 6).
- Exposure induces acute psychological degradation and violent insanity; operational safe duration was calculated by [[beck|Beck]] at 60 to 120 minutes depending on prior exterior exposure (Session 6).
